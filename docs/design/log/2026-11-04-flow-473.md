# FLOW-473: "מעט נתונים" below 60 days; the closing row only on a loan paid out at closing

- PR: #969
- Kind: screen and copy
- Changed: `app/src/screens/project-returns-info.ts` (`MIN_EARNING_DAYS`, `FEW_DAYS`, `fewDays`, `fewDaysText`; the metric values, row hints and page captions), `app/src/screens/project-returns-cards.tsx` (the total's per-cent), `app/src/screens/loan-closing-paid-sheet.tsx` (`showsClosingPaid`), `app/src/screens/loan-detail-screen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/loan-detail-store.ts` (`paidAtClosing` from the project's returns); stories `Screens/Project investment` (new: Fewer than 60 days), `Screens/Property returns` (new: Cap rate, fewer than 60 days), `Screens/Loan page` (new: Project loan, not paid out at closing).
- Rule:
  - A figure scaled to a year from a first rent inside the period needs 60 days (`MIN_EARNING_DAYS`). Below that it reads "מעט נתונים" as a muted note in the figure's place, and its hint or caption says why: "מהשכירות הראשונה · N ימים · מחושב לשנה מ־60 ימים". A figure that is not scaled keeps its value.
  - A row that only matters for some records of a kind shows only on those, and on any other record that holds a value in it, so the value can be seen and cleared.
