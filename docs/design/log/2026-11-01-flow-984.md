# FLOW-984: Review loads its open items in pages

- PR: #901
- Kind: behavior
- Changed: `app/src/screens/review-queue.tsx`, `review-lists.tsx`, `review-shared.tsx`, `app/src/ui/review-count.tsx`.
- Rule:
  - The next page of the queue loads by itself, ahead of the owner: when 15 cards remain in the card flow, or one screen before the end of the full list. There is no "load more" button.
  - Every count on Review is the server's total of open items ("פריט 12 מתוך 1,287", "1,287 ממתינות", the list's subtitle, the minutes estimate). A count over a thousand carries a comma.
  - A page that fails to load leaves a quiet row, "לא הצלחנו לטעון עוד פריטים" with "ניסיון חוזר", in the place of the next card (at the end of the list in the full list). A skipped card wraps back only once every page is loaded.
- Source: the owner's answers to the FLOW-984 plan, 2026-10-31.
