# FLOW-404: the השקעה card on the project page

- PR: #223
- Kind: component
- Changed: `investment-card.tsx` (new `InvestmentCard`), `ui/css/24-investment.css` (new, the `.ui-invest-*` block, imported last in `ui.css`), `investment-card.stories.tsx` and `investment-card.stories-support.ts` (new), `ui/index.ts` (exports), `screens/project-investment.tsx` (new: `ProjectInvestmentSection`, the edit, rehab and loans sheets), `screens/project-investment.stories.tsx` (new), `project-detail-screen.tsx` (the card under the categories), `project-detail-screen.stories.tsx` (the page with the card)
- Rule: An equity that needs a missing figure names the figure ("חסר שווי אחרי שיפוץ", in the link colour for the owner, muted for a viewer); one that another currency blocks says so ("לא מחושב, יש הלוואה בדולר", muted). It never shows 0.
- Rule: An amount in another currency than the project's is listed apart ("ועוד $120,000 בדולר") and never added in.
- Rule: An investment figure is edited one at a time in a bottom sheet that saves on שמירה or on close (0075); מחיקה clears it. A viewer sees no chevrons and no הוספה, and nothing on the card opens.
- Rule: The card's head says מתחילת הפרויקט; the screen's period and basis never change it. Under 19rem of card width the two equities stack, so a seven-figure amount never wraps.
- Source: [0143](../../decisions/0143-project-investment.md); owner approved the mockup as drawn, 2026-10-08 (`plans/flow-404-investment-mockup.html`)

## Where the build differs from the approved mockup (design review r1)

- Kind: pattern
- Changed: `RehabSheet` and `LoansSheet` in `screens/project-investment.tsx`; `.ui-invest-loans`, `.ui-invest-link`, `.ui-invest-retry` in `ui/css/24-investment.css`
- Rule: The rehab list and the loans list open as bottom sheets, not pushed pages: they are short read-only lists that belong to one card, and a sheet keeps the project page under them (DESIGN-RULES §3.1, template B).
- Rule: Rehab rows show no line count: no read returns a per-project, per-category count on rehab's cash basis, and a count from another basis would not match the row's amount ("the numbers add up to the line they came from").
- Rule: Rehab rows carry no minus: the list is titled שיפוץ, a figure already labelled as a cost, like the project's category rows (§3.5, FLOW-328), and the rows add up to the total above them.
- Rule: The edit sheet does not focus the amount when it opens: sheets focus their title (`Sheet`'s open focus), and a focus the user did not tap would not raise the keyboard on iOS anyway.
- Rule: When the counted categories do not add up to rehab's total, the sheet shows the total and "הפירוט לפי קטגוריה לא זמין." instead of rows that disagree with it.
- Rule: A link or retry inside a sheet uses the default text-link size, so its target is 44px; a retry sits on its own line under its sentence, as `LoanReadError` does.
- Rule: The loans row is static (no chevron) when there is no open loan in any currency.
- Source: design review r1 on #223, 2026-10-08
