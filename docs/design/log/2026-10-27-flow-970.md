- Rule:
  - Every forecast graphic reaches 3:1: a category's bar has its empty part as a `--color-track-outline` hairline, not a 1.2:1 fill; the view chips' edge is `--color-control-edge`. The "out" bars (FLOW-961's `--color-chart-expense`) and the dashed card edges (FLOW-969's `--color-outline-forecast`) come with those tasks.
  - A failed read is Home v2's card: "הצפי לא נטען", "התזרים של היום לא נטען" (the list stays) or "הקטגוריות לא נטענו", what happened, נסו שוב and צריך עזרה?. Offline with figures on screen, a line says "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM".
  - Every toast hangs under the header; an undo stays 5s and a note 4s (decision 0220). A change's write adds no "הצפי עודכן" over the screen's toast with ביטול.
  - The chart's heading is its takeaway, the big figure's amount: "אוקטובר צפוי להיסגר ב־−$5,741" (the amount isolated left to right, so the minus stays before the currency), "אוקטובר צפוי להיסגר באיזון" at 0. The chart's title and its months sit above it as one label.
  - The view chips and the category view's links are 44px; the chart has no link here.
  - The row page's button says what it saves: "שמירת השינוי בצפי".
  - The type is on the 13/15/17/20/32 scale: the big figure and the row page's amount 32, the page's title 20, section totals and the page's lines 15, a row's second line and the status 13 (the row line ellipsizes less than at 14); amounts tabular. The weights stay the tokens' (FLOW-959 owns an app-wide weight change).
