# FLOW-1254, FLOW-1255, FLOW-1249: Home's late row draws once and hides the right item

- PR: pending (draft)
- Kind: UI, behavior (no new look)
- Changed: `forecast-skip.ts` (`useLateBillsQuery` gives no list until the month's overrides are in, and waits for them when the screen turns them on later); `HomeScreen.tsx` (says its overrides are due once the books show); `attention-rows.tsx` (a named late row's hide key carries its item); `project-attention.tsx` (the late rows keep that key).
- Rule: a loading state does not show content that is about to change. The late row draws once, at its final count and name, a moment later when the overrides are slow. If the overrides read fails, the whole list draws: an error never hides an alert. A read that will not run on a screen is never waited on.
- Rule: a hide applies to the item the person hid. A named late row's key is `missing:1:<party>:<project|none>` (income: `missing-income:1:...`); two or more keep `missing:<count>`. The old `missing:1` no longer matches a named row, so a row hidden before this shows once more (an alert showing again is the safe side, feedback-and-states section 4).
