# FLOW-1277: a splash screen, the icon fills with liquid on every cold start

- PR: #1267
- Kind: new component, motion, behaviour
- Changed: `ui/splash.tsx` (`SplashMark`: a 3-unit violet outline tile, a violet F, liquid rising inside it with a moving wavy surface, the F white where the liquid covers it through an SVG mask, then the `Wordmark` (sign-in size, logo tone) fading in; `Splash`: the overlay with its timer, tap and key skip), `splash-boot.ts` (when it plays; the cover; the mount in a root of its own), `main.tsx` (one call after the app's render), `css/67-splash.css` (loaded with `lazy.css`), the `--motion-splash-*`, `--ease-splash-fill` and `--size-splash-icon` tokens, `index.html` (the page colour before any CSS, light and dark), `vite.config.ts` (`background_color` `#F6F4FA`).
- Rule: the splash is an overlay above the mounted app, so its reads and first render never wait for it. Only transform and opacity animate (the SVG mask and wave by transform). It never plays under an automated browser, so no test click lands on it. It is decorative and never takes focus.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-23: picked concept N3 (מילוי) from the design canvas, asked for it slower, on the page colour, on every cold start. Task `docs/backlog/tasks/FLOW-1277.md`.
- Timing: liquid rise 2400ms on `--ease-splash-fill`; surface wave 600ms linear, 4 cycles; wordmark 400ms from 2300ms; fade out `--motion-toast-out` (160ms), so about 2.9s in all. Reduced motion: the end state for 400ms, then the fade.
- Exception to DESIGN-RULES §2.7 ("nothing loops for attention"): the wave runs 4 cycles during the rise and stops. The owner's explicit pick, 2026-11-23.
- Colour: the page colour (`--color-bg`) behind, the tile and the liquid `--color-band` in both themes, the F white (`--color-on-band`) where covered, the wordmark `--color-logo`.
- Known limit: the manifest's `background_color` takes one colour, so the system's own launch screen of the installed app is light on a dark phone. The page itself paints dark from its first byte (`index.html`).
- Unchanged: Home's first render and its reads (`startHomeReads`), route changes, the e2e suite (an automated browser never sees the splash).
- Design review: shots of `components-splash--start`, `--mid-fill`, `--done` and `--dark` at 320 and 393, light and dark.
