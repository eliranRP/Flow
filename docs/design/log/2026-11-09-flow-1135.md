  - New lazy List screen `utility-refunds-screen.tsx` (`/utility-refunds`, `?project=<id>` for one property): back link, title, "N לא הוחזרו · M לבדיקה", the figure (the sum not refunded, per currency), the 30-day line, the הכול / לבדיקה / לא הוחזרו segment docking into the 44px list bar, then one `GroupHead` (one-line, with summary) per property. Every answer toasts with ביטול, which calls the write's take-back path (`answer_utility_refund` with null, or matched with `p_keep_category` false after להשאיר; `unmark_utility_refunds_seen`, `undo_utility_refile`, `set_utility_payer` back to the prior).
  - New sub-page `project-utilities-screen.tsx` (`/projects/:projectId/utilities`, beside `/investment` and `/profit`): the band with the property's back link, "חשבונות · מי משלם", then each utility with its category and usual amount ("חשמל ומים · בדרך כלל $80") and a שוכר / חברה `SegmentedControl` (a radiogroup), and הוספת ספק (a sheet of the property's recurring expenses that are not a utility yet, `project-utilities-add.tsx`).
  - The property page (`project-detail-screen.tsx`, `project-attention.tsx`, `project-menu.tsx`): the same row-card for the property's bills (opening the list with `?project=`), a "חשבונות" link row ("מי משלם · N ספקים · M לא הוחזרו") and a "חשבונות" item in ⋯ (`BoltIcon`, new in `icons-later.tsx`), on a rental or any project with utilities.
  - CSS: `65-utility-refunds.css`, imported by `lazy.css` (the two screens' own classes, `ui-urf-*` and `ui-utl-*`).
  - #1100 r1: the who-pays head waits for the bills read before it says "הכול הוחזר" ("בודקים…", or "לא נטען · ניסיון חוזר" when it failed); a utility's line and a card's payment line wrap and are never cut (they carry the usual amount, the day and the gap); the payment picker's window starts the same day a month before the bill, clamped to that month's last day, and leaves out a payment already another bill's refund unless that bill is of the same property and month; the bill's sheet opens on its actions each time. The stories feed the screens through `UtilityClientProvider` (a stand-in client for the utility reads and writes) instead of `sample` props on the screens.
- Rule:
  - A list of suggestions that each ask a yes or no draws them as surface cards (the transfers' anatomy: two legs, a why line, two 44px pill answers); an answer that leaves something to fix keeps the card, with the fix on a raised panel inside it.
  - A group head that needs an action for the whole group (סימון הכל כנראה) keeps the one-line `GroupHead` and puts the action as a link button right under it: at 320 the title, the summary and the action do not fit one line.
  - A per-row two-way choice (שוכר / חברה) is a `SegmentedControl` at the row's end, each option at least 64px; a viewer sees it disabled, with the current choice.
- Changed from the boards:
  - סימון הכל כנראה sits under the property's head, not at its end (rule above).
  - הוספת ספק offers the property's recurring expenses (the schedule the page already reads); the boards draw the button only.
