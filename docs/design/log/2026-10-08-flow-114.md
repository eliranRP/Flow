# FLOW-114: a matched loan payment is one row on the transaction card

- PR: #252
- Kind: component
- Changed: new `LoanPartsSheet` (app/src/ui/loan-parts-sheet.tsx, css/26-loan-row.css) with stories; `LoanCategoryRow` (screens/loan-match-row.tsx) takes the category row's place on a matched payment; `LoanSplitPanel` became `LoanMatchOffer` (screens/loan-match.tsx), only the "שיוך להלוואה" row and its picker; transaction-screen.tsx wraps its category row and passes `loan_split`
- Rule: A payment matched to a loan shows one row in the category slot: the "קטגוריה" eyebrow, "תשלום הלוואה · <loan>", and "N חלקים" (or "ממתין לבדיקה" in the warning tone) under it. It opens the split sheet: the loan's name as the title, one amount field per part in the order קרן, ריבית, מסים וביטוח, עמלות, a "סה״כ" row, one primary "שמירה", and a quiet ghost "ביטול השיוך" under it. שמירה stays off, with one line saying the total it needs, while the parts don't add up to the line. Unmatch has no confirm step: an undo toast "השיוך בוטל · ביטול". The category of a matched line cannot be changed anywhere on the card. A viewer gets the row static, with no sheet. The unmatched offer row carries the eyebrow "הלוואה". This replaces the loan split list on the card in DESIGN-RULES §4 screen 10 ("פיצול התשלום", "נספר ברווח", "מחוץ לרווח").
- Source: owner pick of option B on a card (2026-10-08, plans/flow-114-loan-match-B.png); decision 0136

Deviations from mockup B, each following an existing rule:
- The undo toast sits under the header, not at the bottom: the app's toast rule (DESIGN-RULES §2.8, Toast).
- The amount fields are the shared `MoneyField`, 52px tall with ₪ or $ inside the box, so the rows are a little taller than the mockup's 44px boxes (§2.8 Text input; §3.4 amount fields).
- The "הלוואה" label sits on the "שיוך להלוואה" row as its eyebrow, like the פרויקט and קטגוריה rows, instead of a separate section label; the row keeps its existing hint with the one loan that fits (FLOW-134).
- The card keeps what the mockup did not draw: the status pills, the "פיצול" section (its "לפי קטגוריות" off while matched) and the bank details.
- What counts in profit ("נספר ברווח", "מחוץ לרווח" on the principal) is no longer on the card or in the sheet (§2.1 light and clean: it does not change what the owner does on this screen).
