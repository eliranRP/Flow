Status and tags are coloured text or a 6px dot. Bars are 6 to 8px. Side margin 20px; flat rows about 64px with no dividers; cards radius 20px; primary buttons are 52px pills. Motion comes from the tokens only; under reduced motion every `--motion-*` is 0ms (the two sheet tokens stay 1ms for Vaul). Screen CSS sets no raw hex, size, weight, duration, easing or radius (`scripts/check-css-tokens.mjs`).
