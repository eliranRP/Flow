- Rule:
  - The bar's open part and its legend dot are `--color-track-outline` (#6F6787 dark, 3.2:1 on the card), not the 1.4:1 track. The bar is one image named with both amounts ("שולם $x, פתוח $y").
  - A marked row keeps full contrast (no 60% opacity); its line "סומן כשולם · ממתין לסנכרון" says the state.
  - "סימון כשולם", "ביטול", the match line and the refresh are 44px (`--touch-min`), and so is the sort row.
  - The match box's "לא זה" edge is `--color-control-edge` (#8F879F dark, 3:1 and more).
  - Every toast hangs under the header, an undo 5s and a note 4s (decision 0218). Clearing a mark offers ביטול, which marks it again; "לא זה" offers ביטול, which takes back that dismissal (`undismiss_invoice_match`).
  - A failed read keeps the title (the list) or the bar (the invoice page): "החשבוניות לא נטענו" / "החשבונית לא נטענה", what happened, נסו שוב and צריך עזרה? (`PageLoadFailed`). Offline with invoices on screen: "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM · מתחבר מחדש…" (`BandOfflineLine onPage`).
  - The PDF card's note says what it does: "PDF מ־SUMIT · נפתח בחלון חדש".
- Source: owner approval on 2026-10-26; the Invoices row on the design canvas, page "Ready for dev", and its findings note.
- Already as the board, no change: the status chip is 13px (`--type-caption-size`); a new invoice's age line is the secondary text (#B1A8C4, above 4.5:1); the tab bar's pill came with FLOW-958.
- Departures from the approved board:
  - The overdue reminder ("שליחת תזכורת ל־<לקוח>", sent through SUMIT after a 10s ביטול; owner call) is not built here: Flow has no SUMIT call that sends a document or a reminder, only the sync that reads documents. It waits for the owner's call on how to send it.
  - The full-screen invoice viewer: `document_url` is SUMIT's document page, which the card opens in a new tab; Flow holds no PDF of its own to draw in a viewer.
