- Rule:
  - FLOW-1014: a screen's add action shows on its empty state too, as the empty state's one action, and on a populated list it sits under the summary card, not after the last group.
  - FLOW-1015: a coach mark shows only to the owners it speaks to (the קבועים tip: an owner with recurring rows), says only what is on screen (the dot's sentence only while the dot is on), and never blocks the controls under it: a press outside it or a scroll closes it, and the press still reaches its control.   - FLOW-1016: a count read from loaded pages only is a floor while pages remain, and says so ("לפחות 12 בלי פרויקט"); it is never dropped. While the next page loads, a list's end says "טוענים עוד…" (`t-caption t-muted`, `role="status"`), and the card flow keeps its frame with the card's place as a skeleton, not the whole-screen loader.
