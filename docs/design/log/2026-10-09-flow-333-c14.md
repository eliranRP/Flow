# FLOW-333 C14: the loud flag is one line on a short phone

- PR: #296
- Kind: component
- Changed: `css/06-review-card.css`, the review card's short-phone block (`max-height: 720px`). A loud flag is one 44px line: the icon, the title in `text`, 8px, then the hint in warning, cut with an ellipsis at its end when the line is full (the title stays whole); the icon gap is 8px. The row's own air stands in for the card's bottom padding when the flag ends the card. On לאישור the slim banner's לרשימה keeps its 44px target but overlaps the title line and the banner's edge by 8px, so the banner is 44px tall (was 52) and 61px when it wraps at 320 (was 77). Measured from the card's bottom to the pinned bar's top: at 375x667 a Jev card with a loud duplicate flag and the slim banner ends 7px above the bar (it ran 19px under), the hint whole; the loud spike card without the banner 35px above (18); at 320x667 the Jev card with a quiet flag 8px above (it ran 8px under). At 320x667 a Jev card with a loud flag and the wrapped banner still runs 10px under (43) and the hint is cut, so it scrolls. Above 720px tall nothing changes: the loud flag keeps two lines (390x844, 52px above the bar). Screen readers still hear "לבדיקה:", the title and the whole hint. Stories: Screens/Routes Review Jev Fit Loud 320, Review Jev Fit Loud 390.
- Rule: On a short phone (up to 720px tall) a loud review flag is one 44px line: the title whole, then the hint in warning, cut at its end when it does not fit; taller phones keep the hint on a second line.
- Source: FLOW-333 C14 (follow-up from #278).
