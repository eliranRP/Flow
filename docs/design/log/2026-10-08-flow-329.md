# FLOW-329 and FLOW-124: The P&L switch row on the transaction card

- PR: #248
- Kind: screen
- Changed: Transaction card. Taking a line out of the P&L moved from the ⋯ sheet (top corner, unlabelled) to a "ברווח והפסד" switch row right under the category row, with the hint under it; undo stays on the toast. A loan line shows the row locked ("תשלום הלוואה · נספר לפי הפיצול"). ⋯ now shows only on a manual line and holds מחיקה alone; elsewhere its slot stays empty so ˄ ˅ don't move. The pill and hint follow the server's `pnl_state`: a line split by category with some parts kept out shows "חלקית ברווח" and a hint naming the split's categories, and a line kept out by its parts shows "מחוץ לרווח". On a line split by category the switch always sets the whole line (all parts in, or all out), since its parts' own categories decide otherwise. The פיצול section was already at the bottom of the card.
- Rule: A reversible per-item setting is a labelled switch row next to what it overrides, not an item in an overflow menu. ⋯ appears only when it holds something.
