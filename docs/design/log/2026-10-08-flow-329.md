# FLOW-329 and FLOW-124: The P&L switch row on the transaction card

- PR: #248
- Kind: screen
- Changed: Transaction card. Taking a line out of the P&L moved from the ⋯ sheet (top corner, unlabelled) to a "ברווח והפסד" switch row (neutral chart icon) after the VAT line; undo stays on the toast. On, the row has no hint; off, one line of scope ("רק השורה הזו. הקטגוריה לא משתנה.", or the kept-out category it follows). A loan line shows the row locked ("תשלום הלוואה · נספר לפי הפיצול"), and so does a split whose parts differ ("לפי הקטגוריות בפיצול"), which opens the split by category where the parts are set. On a line split by category whose parts agree, the switch sets the whole line. ⋯ now shows only on a manual line and holds מחיקה alone; elsewhere its slot stays empty so ˄ ˅ don't move. The pill follows the server's `pnl_state`: "חלקית ברווח" for a mixed split, "מחוץ לרווח" for a line kept out by its parts. ⊘ stays only where it means kept out (the pill). The פיצול section was already at the bottom of the card. Design lead review on #248.
- Rule: A reversible per-item setting is a labelled switch row, not an item in an overflow menu; it carries a hint only in the state that needs one, and is locked with one reason when something else decides it. ⋯ appears only when it holds something.
- Source: FLOW-329 (cycle 1, U5 and D15), FLOW-124 item 2 (#105 review).
