# FLOW-464: a newly let rental's metrics, a year from the first rent

- PR: the FLOW-464 claim PR
- Kind: copy
- Changed: `app/src/screens/project-returns-info.ts` (`metricCaption`, the metric page's caption and "=" row, `NO_PAYMENTS`, `ANNUALIZED`, `perYear`), `project-returns-data.ts` (`annualized_from`, `earning_days`), `app/src/dev/returns-sample.ts` (`birchLaneReturnsAnnualized`), `project-returns.stories.tsx` (CapRateAnnualized).
- Rule:
  - A per-year figure that rests on less than the period says so where it is read: the hint under the metric row ends "· מחושב לשנה"; the metric page's meta names where it starts and how long ("מהשכר הראשון ב־01/04/2026 · 215 ימים, מחושב לשנה") in place of "12 חודשים".
  - The "=" row divides the figure for a year (the NOI or cash flow since the first rent × 365 ÷ the days), so the division on the page gives the figure in the hero.
  - A ratio with nothing to divide by names what is missing in plain words, not a dash: "אין תשלומים עדיין".
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-03, on a rental let three weeks before (a cap rate of 1.6%); decision 0249.
