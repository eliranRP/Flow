# FLOW-1039: pull to refresh on every screen (ld-07)

- PR: #977
- Kind: component
- Changed: new `app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx` (`PullRefresh`, mounted once in `App.tsx` beside `EdgeSwipeBack`; `PullRefreshMark`), CSS `62-pull-refresh.css` (`.ui-pull-refresh`; in the installed app `html` gets `overscroll-behavior-y: none`), `ui/index.ts`, DESIGN-RULES ld-07, CONTROLS.md. Story: Components/Pull to refresh (idle, pulling at 320 and 393, armed, refreshing at 320 and 393).
- Rule:
  - Pull to refresh works in the installed app only (a browser tab keeps its own), by touch, from scroll top 0. It follows the FLOW-314 gesture rules: decided after 10px, sideways goes to the swipes, never from either 24px edge zone, a sheet or dialog, a field, a revealed swiped row, a toast, or a list scrolled down from its own top.
  - The mark is the edge-back mark's round 40px `raised` circle with the inset ring and the shared `.ui-spinner`, centred under the top safe area. It comes down at half the move (up to 96px), turns and fades in with the progress, and takes the accent at 64px; released there it rests at 64px and spins (`--motion-spin`) until the reads settle, at least 400ms, then goes up and fades on `--motion-sheet-out` with `--ease-exit`. Released before, it springs back the same way and nothing is read.
  - Reduced motion: the mark rests in place and only fades in; no follow, no turn, no spring.
  - A refresh re-reads every active query. A failed read shows "הרענון נכשל. נסו שוב." once. A screen reader hears "מרענן" from a status line; the mark itself is hidden from it.
  - A pull re-reads Flow's own data only; it starts no bank sync, since opening the app starts none (FLOW-1040 asks the owner whether it should).
  - While it refreshes, the mark rests 24 to 64px under the top safe area, over the middle of the screen's top row (on Home, the band's action row) for the refresh's 400ms or more. Accepted: the owner asked for a spinner at the top as other banking apps show it, the mark takes no taps (`pointer-events: none`), and moving the screen down instead would shift every screen's layout. Revisit with the board frame for ld-07.
  - The installed app has no rubber band at the top or end of a page (`overscroll-behavior-y: none`), so the pull is the only thing that moves.
