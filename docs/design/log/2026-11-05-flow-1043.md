- Kind: component

Stories: Components/CompactBar (band and list bars, dock closed and docked, with a sticky head), Components/DockFilter, Components/SegmentedControl (Slides, SlidesOnBand), Components/GroupHead (OneLine), Components/AttentionCards, Components/StatusMark. No screen uses the new parts yet (FLOW-1036 and waves B and C).
- Rule:
  - CompactBar `dock` and `docked`: the dock row under the bar is always rendered when `dock` is set; closed it is 0px, transparent, `inert` and `aria-hidden`; open it is 60px (48px of segment track and 12px under it), its height over `--motion-page` with `--ease-enter`, its opacity over `--motion-toast-out` (160ms). An open dock sets `html[data-compact-dock="on"]` (counted like the bars), which adds 60px to `--compact-bar-h` for every kind of bar.
  - A bar with `centre` (Home's band bar) reports `html[data-compact-bar="band"]`, 64px. The sticky offsets that follow (`--month-sticky-top`, `scroll-padding-block-start`, both plus the safe area and the 1px line): 0, 64, or 124 docked on an Overview screen; 44 or 104 under a list bar; 56 or 116 under a detail bar.
  - `useDockedFilter({ barShown, bar })` docks once its mark (a `ui-compact-mark` right above the in-page control) has scrolled under the bar while the bar shows; `scrollUnderBar()` after a pick while docked puts the mark 1px above the bar's edge (smooth; instant under reduced motion), so the filter stays docked. `DockSlot` fades the in-page copy out over 160ms, keeps its height, and takes it out of the focus order and the reader's tree while docked. One state in the screen feeds both copies.
  - SegmentedControl draws its selected state as one `aria-hidden` indicator measured from the selected option (`offsetLeft`, so it is right under `dir="rtl"`, and right when options size to their words), sliding by `transform` over `--motion-page` with `--ease-enter`; a jump under reduced motion. Until it is laid out, or with nothing selected, the option keeps its own fill. Page tone: `--color-tint-strong`, as before. Band tone: the indicator is `--color-band-pill` (white in light) and the selected label `--color-accent`.
  - GroupHead `summary`: one line, the title at the start (`t-section`) and the summary at the end (`t-meta`, `--color-text-secondary`) on its baseline, 48px (14 + 26 + 8), a 1px `--color-line` under it, the page background behind; `inset-block-start` transitions over `--motion-page` so the head follows the dock. Without `summary` the two-line head is unchanged.
  - AttentionCards: 56px cards (surface, line border, `--radius-card-tight`, the row's icon, a one-line `t-body` title, a chevron, a 44px X named "הסתרה: <title>"); the review row (a row with `button`) is first and has no X, and so has any row without a `dismissKey`. The X slides the card 24px toward itself (`--rise-push`) and folds it (`max-height` over `--motion-sheet-out` with `--ease-exit`, opacity over 160ms). "N הוסתרו · הצגה" restores; with every row hidden the section is one 44px line, "אין מה לטפל · הכול נסקר" with a check in `--color-income`, and ביטול. `aria-live="polite"`. Hidden rows are kept in `flow.attention.<storageKey>` as `{ rowId: dismissKey }`, every read and write in try/catch; a row shows again when its `dismissKey` changes.
  - StatusMark: 24px, `aria-hidden`, a ListRow `icon`: `due` a dashed 2px ring in `--color-text-muted`, `late` the same ring in `--color-signal`, `paid` a check on a `--color-tint` disc, `once` a 6px dot. The row's words say the state.
  - Reduced motion turns every new transition off through the `--motion-*` tokens and `--rise-push`.
