# FLOW-1043: pull to refresh that feels native (ld-07)

- PR: #981
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx` (new `pullMove`, `pullSpokes`, `PULL_REST_PX`, `PULL_SPOKES`; `pullTravel` is now the rubber band; new phases `returning` and `closing`; the spinner is an inline SVG), CSS `62-pull-refresh.css` (the round raised mark and its accent are gone; new `.ui-pull-spinner`), two motion tokens in `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (`--motion-pull-spin`, `--motion-pull-return`), DESIGN-RULES 2.7 and ld-07, CONTROLS.md. Story: Components/Pull to refresh (idle; pulling at 25% and 50% at 320 and 393; a notched iPhone at 100% at 320 and 393; a past month's band; refreshing at 320 and 393, with and without a notch; returning at 320 with a notch and at 393; a plain screen pulling and refreshing). E2e: `app/e2e/pull-refresh.spec.ts`.
- Reference: the owner's screen recording of a native iOS app's pull to refresh (not in the repo). What it shows is written in FLOW-1043; the numbers below were read off its frames at 4, 10, 30 and 60 frames a second.
- Rule:
  - The spinner is the iOS activity indicator: 12 thin rounded spokes (an inline SVG, `currentColor`), 20px across, with no circle or background. Its colour is `--color-text-secondary`, and `--color-on-band-secondary` over the band (Home, a project, a past month's band), where the secondary text colour would not read on the purple.
  - While pulling the spokes appear one by one, clockwise from 12 o'clock, standing still: `round(progress × 12)` of them, none at the start and all 12 at the 64px threshold. In the reference the brightest spoke goes round once in about 0.88s, one spoke per tick, so refreshing it turns in 12 steps on `--motion-pull-spin` (880ms), the spokes fading from full at the head to a quarter behind it.
  - The rubber band: `travel = 64 × ln(1 + move / 64)`. Its slope is 1 at the start (8px of move gives 7.5px), it falls as the pull goes on, and it never stops (300px gives 111px). The move counts from where the pull is decided (10px), so the screen starts at 0 with no jump; the threshold comes at 110px after that, 120px from touch-down. Crossing it plays `navigator.vibrate(10)` once per pull, where the platform has it (iOS Safari does not, so nothing happens there).
  - The spinner is centred in the gap's part under the status bar until the gap reaches the rest, and stays there as the pull goes on, as in the reference.
  - Released at 64px or more, the screen and the gap ease to a 56px rest (the spinner with 18px above and below it, plus the safe area on a notched iPhone) on `--motion-pull-return` (400ms) with `--ease-enter`, and hold while the spinner turns (at least 400ms, at most 15s, as before). When the reads settle, the spinner fades and shrinks to 0.7 where it stands (`--motion-fade`, `--ease-exit`), still turning, and the screen glides back to the top on `--motion-pull-return` with `--ease-enter`, a soft ease-out. Released before, it glides back the same way and the spokes fade where they stood, without turning.
  - A touch during the glide back catches the screen where it is: the screen's live `top` is read from the running transition and turned back into a pull (`pullMove`), so the finger pulls on from there with no jump and no 10px decision. A tap lets it go on returning.
  - The gap runs 1px under the screen's top: the rubber band's travel is fractional, and `top` and the gap's transform round it to device pixels apart, which left a hairline of the page between them.
  - Smoothness: a Playwright check on a 393 × 852 touch screen (3x) logs the screen's top on every animation frame over a 180px pull in 30 moves, the release, the hold and the return, and over a return caught mid-way; no frame moves more than 12px (9.4px and 9.2px at most, over three runs each).
  - Kept from FLOW-1042: the whole screen moves by `top` over a gap in its top's colour, the fixed bars stay put, the notch geometry, the 400ms minimum and 15s cap, every cached read goes stale with no bank sync, and the gesture guards. Reduced motion: the screen does not move and there is no gap; the spinner rests 18 to 38px under the top safe area with all its spokes, still, and only fades in with the pull.
  - The reference's indicator measures about 28px across on its 393pt-wide screen; FLOW-1043 asks for about 20px, which this build follows.
