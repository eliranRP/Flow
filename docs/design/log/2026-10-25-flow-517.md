# FLOW-517: the weekly summary sheet over Home

- PR: #800
- Kind: sheet, copy, motion
- Changed: `app/src/screens/weekly-summary-sheet.tsx` (new: WeeklySummarySheet and its route, `57-weekly-summary.css`, imported by it so it stays out of Home's first load), `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (opens it from `?week=`), `app/src/weekly-summary.ts` (the words and the week).
- Rule: The Sunday push opens Home on the current month with the weekly summary sheet over it (`/?week=<Sunday>`). The sheet's head is "סיכום שבועי" over the week ("ראשון 4 עד שבת 10 באוקטובר"; across two months each day names its month) with ✕. Its blocks, in order: "רווח השבוע" (15px secondary) over the 40/400 figure (the signal colour with its minus when negative, muted at $0, the text colour above) with the change pill beside it ("▲|▼ $X מול השבוע הקודם", 13/500 on the raised tone, secondary text when profit got better, the signal colour when worse; none with no lines or no week before), then when some of the week's lines wait a 13px muted "כולל N שעוד מחכות לאישור"; the inner box on the raised tone, 16px rows with hairlines (נכנס green with +, יצא, and "לא נספר ברווח" over "שיפוץ והשבחה, הון בעלים ועוד" only when not zero); at most one project alert on the tint (16px-round, a 6px signal dot, "<project> עבר את התקציב" 16px, "$spent מתוך $budget · N%" 13px secondary, "ועוד N פרויקטים מעל התקציב" 13px accent, a chevron), which opens the project; a centred 13px muted count ("N תנועות נכנסו השבוע · M אושרו אוטומטית"); and the 52px primary "לאישור N פריטים · כ־X דקות" (about 20 seconds an item; "פחות מדקה", "כדקה"), or when nothing waits a bordered 52px "✓ הכול מאושר" with the check in the income colour. A week with no lines keeps "$0" muted and shows the empty state (64px tint circle, calendar icon, "לא נכנסו תנועות השבוע", one line) and the outline "בדיקת החיבור לבנק" to the connections page. Loading shows skeleton blocks after 300ms; a failure is one line with "נסו שוב". Motion: the sheet's own entrance and exit, and its blocks rise 8px and fade, 40ms apart from 120ms (stagger tokens, so nothing moves under reduced motion).
- Source: owner approval of the board's Weekly summary frames on 2026-10-25 (prototype from the push, 23 lines with 7 waiting, 0 lines, 1 line auto-approved, 3 lines with 1 waiting and no alert, 50 lines with 12 waiting, loading, failed). Decision [0211](../../decisions/0211-weekly-summary-sheet.md).
- Departures from the approved board (the canvas was corrected to match on 2026-10-25): the note under the profit reads "כולל N שעוד מחכות לאישור", not "לפי N תנועות מאושרות · M עוד לא נספרו": lines waiting for review count in the sums, as on Home, so the board's words would be wrong. The alert reads "<project> עבר את התקציב", not "עבר את תקציב השיפוץ": a project has one budget, for all its spending. The button counts the whole open review queue, as the push and Home do, while the note counts the week's lines that wait; on the boards they are the same number. The board's dashed "↺ הצגה חוזרת" is a review control on the canvas only.
