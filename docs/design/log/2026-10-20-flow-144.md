# FLOW-144: תזרים צפוי, cash words on the forecast card and chart

- PR: #746
- Kind: screen and component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/month-forecast.tsx` (`ForecastCard` and `MonthChart` take `kind`; the card's `title`, `start` and `strong`; the chart's month in progress), `app/src/ui/forecast-words.ts` (new: the two sets of words), `app/src/ui/month-chart-scale.ts` (`ahead`, `capHeight`), `app/src/ui/css/51-next-month.css` (`.ui-fc-start`, `.ui-fc-net-strong`, `.ui-fc-neg`, `.ui-fc-stack`, `.ui-fc-cap`, the head wraps at 320), `app/src/screens/forecast-screen.tsx` (title, the card and chart under the figure), `app/src/ui/css/28-forecast.css` (deleted `.ui-forecast-today`; `.ui-forecast-fc`), `app/src/screens/home-parts.tsx` (the band row's title), `app/src/screens/next-month-block.tsx` (`kind` in place of `netLabel`).
- Rule: The forecast card and the month chart are one part with two sets of words. A cash view (the project page, תזרים צפוי) says צפוי להיכנס / צפוי לצאת / תזרים צפוי and "נכנס ויצא לפי חודש" with the legend נכנס · יצא · צפי; a profit view (project profit, company profit) says הכנסות צפויות / הוצאות צפויות / רווח צפוי and "הכנסות והוצאות לפי חודש". The month-end forecast is titled "תזרים צפוי" (header, compact bar, the item page's back link); its big figure is the month's end with nothing under it. Under it, 16px down, the card shows the whole sum: "עד סוף <month>" with the צפי tag and a `text-muted` caption "N פריטים · פירוט למטה" (no link; it drops under the title at 320), a first row "תזרים היום" (15px, label `text-secondary`, figure `text`, `bad` when negative) with a `line` divider under it, the two columns with counts ("3 פריטים · 2 באיחור", "8 פריטים, מהם 6 קבועים"), and a strong last row "תזרים בסוף <month>" at 17/500 in `text`, its figure the big one in the same colour (`bad` when negative). The profit and project cards keep the grey 15px net. The chart, 14px under the card, has the cash months from five months back as they ended and the current month in progress: solid bars so far with a dashed cap (1.5px `accent` or `chart-expense` dash, no fill, 4px top radius, the solid bar's top corners square) for what is still expected, on one scale of so far plus expected; its label "אוק׳ · צפי" is in `accent-text` and wraps under itself like next month's. Home's band row reads "תזרים צפוי לסוף <month>".
