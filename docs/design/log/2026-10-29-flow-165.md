# FLOW-165: Reports: open a balance-sheet account's movements

- PR: #860
- Kind: new screen (`/reports/account`); the balance sheet's account rows become links; the category drill-down reads one project by id
- Changed: `app/src/screens/report-account-screen.tsx` (new), `app/src/screens/reports-parts.tsx` (`BalanceView` takes `accountHref`), `app/src/screens/reports-screen.tsx` (`reportAccountPath`; `reportLinesPath` writes the project id), `app/src/screens/report-lines-screen.tsx` (reads one project with `p_project_id`; the read-every-page and the name filter are gone), `app/src/App.tsx`, `app/src/screen-loaders.ts`, `app/public/_redirects`. No CSS change: the screen uses the reports screen's and the month lines' parts.
- Rule:
  - On מאזן, every asset, liability and equity account row opens its page; "רווח שנצבר" (the running profit) is not an account and stays a plain row.
  - The page keeps the balance sheet's date and project. Its header is the account's name with "דוחות" to go back. The figure is the balance, labelled "יתרה היום" (or "יתרה ב־<date>"), with the account type and the project under it.
  - Two rows follow: "יתרה ב־1/1/<year>" (the opening balance) and the year's change: "שולם השנה" with "<n> תשלומים" on a loan's principal, else "שינוי השנה" with "<n> תנועות".
  - "תנועות" is a collapsible section with the count on its head. Inside, the year's movements by month, newest first, each opening the transaction page. A row's title is the party (or description). Its hint is the loan part first ("קרן"), then the date and the project, as whole parts, so a narrow row drops the project and keeps the part. When the account holds only part of the line, a line under the hint says "מתוך תשלום <line amount>"; at 320 px it wraps onto two lines rather than being cut. Amounts are signed as the balance sheet shows them, in the text colour. More pages load with "עוד <n> תנועות".
  - A loan's principal links "לדף ההלוואה" (its one loan) or "להלוואות" (several loans, or none recorded on the parts).
  - States: skeleton while reading, "החשבון לא נטען" with a retry (offline wording when offline), the offline line when the page shows what it read before, "החשבון לא נמצא" with "חזרה למאזן", and "אין תנועות מתחילת <year>." when the year has none.
- Source: the approved reports design, board ReportAccountDrill, 2026-10-28.
- Departures from the approved board:
  - The board's header chips ("היום", the project) are not drawn. The page has no date or project picker of its own: it keeps the balance sheet's, which the user changes there. A pressed chip that opened nothing would be a control that does nothing.
  - The header's share button is not drawn: `export_report` has no one-account file yet.
  - The movements are grouped by month under the "תנועות" section, as on the category drill-down. The board shows a flat list.
  - The board's "10 תשלומים · קרן בלבד" reads "10 תשלומים": the account holds only the principal parts, so "קרן בלבד" adds nothing.
  - The board puts "Mr. Cooper · קרן" in the title and "<date> · מתוך תשלום $1,350" in the hint. At 320 px the title cut off the part and the hint lost its last part (review r1), so the part leads the hint and "מתוך תשלום" has its own line under it.
  - With no movement this year, the section head and the change row show no "0".
- The category drill-down's summary is the whole group's total and count from `get_breakdown_lines` (`total_minor`, `count`), not the sum of the loaded pages.
