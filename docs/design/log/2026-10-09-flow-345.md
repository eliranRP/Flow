Built shots: the project's `mockups/cycle-7/flow-345-built/` (390, 320, 390 dark: middle, first, last, mid-drag peek both ways, end resistance; 390 and 390 dark with content scrolled under the row).
- Rule: A card opened from a list walks it from a quiet row pinned at the bottom of the screen (the thumb zone, on the safe area; the transaction screen has no tab bar): הקודמת on the start side and הבאה on the end side, accent text links in `label` with no arrow glyphs and a 44px hit area, and a muted "N מתוך M" in `meta` with tabular digits between them. The row is filled with `bg` (the page colour), so content never shows through it. The top bar keeps Back and ⋯ only. A card opened from a link, or a one-row list, shows no row.
- Rule: At a list end that side's word is hidden with `visibility: hidden` through a class (not removed, not disabled), so the counter stays centred. A button move keeps focus on the pressed word on the next card; when that word is hidden there, focus waits on the counter (`tabindex=-1`, no ring).
- Rule: The row draws a 1px `line` hairline on top only while card content is scrolled under it (a mark at the end of the card, watched against the row's top edge); a card that ends above the row leaves it flat (design lead, 2026-10-09).
- Rule: The counter, which takes focus at a list end, is heard as the plain place ("12 מתוך 24"); the drawn digits with their reserved width are hidden from screen readers.
- Rule: Mid-drag the neighbour's edge peeks in from the side it will enter (next from the left, previous from the right): a `surface` panel with a `line` border and `--radius-card`, `--space-4` from the card, with the neighbour's name in `title-3` `text-secondary` on the edge nearest the card when the prefetch already holds it, otherwise a plain edge. Toward a list end the card gives a quarter of the move, at most 32px, nothing peeks, and it springs back on release. Reduced motion: no follow, no peek, swap on release.
- Source: owner pick on the FLOW-345 card, option D (2026-10-09 07:17Z), mockup `mockups/cycle-7/flow-345-d.png`; design lead's spec.

## Notes for the design lead (DESIGN-RULES is yours; not edited here)

- §3.7 "Headers and navigation", the FLOW-314 swipe line: "stay put toward an end" no longer holds; the card now gives up to 32px and springs back (the Rule above). The same line's "A sideways swipe repeats a control already on screen" still holds: הקודמת / הבאה are that control.
- §3.7, the FLOW-335 line "A swipe on a band figure follows the direction of the arrows it duplicates" (finger toward the start-side arrow = that arrow's step): the card has no arrows now, and its swipe works the other way round. A finger moving right, toward הקודמת, opens the next card, which enters from the left where הבאה sits, like turning a page (FLOW-314, unchanged here). That matches the words' places by where the card comes from, not by where the finger goes. Worth one sentence in §3.7 so the band rule is not read as applying to the card.
- §4 "10 Transaction detail", the "Prev and next (FLOW-303)" bullet describes the ˄ ˅ top bar, `aria-disabled` ends with a hidden hint, and "No position on screen". Option D replaces all three: the words sit at the bottom, an end hides its word, and "N מתוך M" is on screen. The loading and error part ("⋯ keeps its slot and the title sits under the bar") no longer applies: a loading card now uses the plain header, and the row stays below it.
- §3.4 "Steppers and earlier / later arrows are SVG chevrons": not affected; the card's step control is words, with no chevrons.
