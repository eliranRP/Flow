# FLOW-1064: a report row's share as a percent

- PR: (this PR)
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/screens/reports-parts.tsx` (`reportHint` takes a share number in place of a bar; `viewBar` is now `viewShare`), `reports-balance-screen.tsx`. CSS: `61-reports-v3.css` (`.ui-rep-share`, `.ui-rep-share-fill` gone).
- Rule:
  - A report row's share of its group is a whole percent in the row's line under its name, in the line's caption type and secondary colour, never a bar.
  - With no line of its own the row reads "85% מהקבוצה"; with one, the percent leads it: "40% · הכנסות $X · הוצאות $Y". The line stays on one line, cut at its end.
  - No percent on income rows, on a group of one row, or on the balance sheet's equity (as the bar before it).
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-06 ("the bar looks childish and not related, I prefer a percentage number"), option A of the mockup they approved.
