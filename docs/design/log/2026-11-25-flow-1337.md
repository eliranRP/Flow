# FLOW-1337, FLOW-1338: the Loans list keeps its loans offline on a new day; a row hint keeps its date

- PR: see the tasks, `docs/backlog/tasks/FLOW-1337.md` and `FLOW-1338.md`
- Kind: states, layout
- Changed: `screens/loans-host.tsx` (rows draw from the kept list when today's outlooks have no data), `screens/loans-list.tsx` (the next payment's "הבא dd/mm · amount" is one hint part), `ui/list-row.tsx` (`WholeHint`: a part with letters that shows whole or drops), stories "Offline, new day" and "Loans, attention, hint at 393".
- Source: UX rules review 2026-11-25. Offline on a new day the list showed "אין הלוואות" while the loans were kept; a row's hint cut "הבא 15/11" to "הבא…".
- Rule: offline is never empty. When the list is kept and today's outlooks are not (paused offline), the rows draw with no next payment and no late mark, under the offline line; a failed outlooks read still fails the list (FLOW-1316).
- Rule: a date and its amount in a row's hint are one part that shows whole or drops together; the date is never cut to its label.
- Band: the summary reads the outlooks too and is null without them, so the band falls back to the rows' own balances (no next payment, no paid line) until they are read.
- Unchanged: every other `hintParts` user (only the new `WholeHint` opts in), the CSS, the failed state, online loading.
