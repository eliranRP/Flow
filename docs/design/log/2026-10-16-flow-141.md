## Proofs and known mismatches

Proofs: `/mnt/project-files/proofs/flow-141/` (01 to 05, board left, dev-fixture app right at 390 px). The app side is the dev server (`vite --mode development`), because a production build strips the `/e2e` routes.

Mismatches seen in proof 01 (dark, month forecast), by element:
- Currency: the board shows `$` figures, the app shows `₪` (the app is ILS-only, as the decision says).
- Data: the board shows six small recurring charges and the app fixture shows one, so the totals differ by the same amount. The big number is therefore not comparable across the two sides.
- Names: the board uses different invented names from the fixture.
- Footnote: the app's footnote is hidden behind the tab bar at 390 px, while the board shows it.
- Sync strip: the board has no sync strip in this frame and the app does not show one either (loading uses DelayedSkeleton/ListSkeleton, not the FLOW-371 strip).
