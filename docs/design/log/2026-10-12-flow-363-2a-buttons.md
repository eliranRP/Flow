# FLOW-363 (2a): Button and IconButton on the redesign

- PR: FLOW-363 (2a), Button and IconButton (the foundation is in 1/4, 0181).
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/button.tsx` (new `link` and `danger-link` variants, a `chevron` prop, a `disabled` prop on the link form, press motion on the four new variants), `app/src/ui/icon-button.tsx` (`m-press`), `app/src/ui/css/01-base.css` (the button roles: primary 52px pill at 17/500, secondary 52px outline at 1.5px `--stroke-strong` in `--color-tint-strong`, 16/500, text in `--color-text`; link 44px, 15/400, `--color-accent-text`; danger-link in `--color-signal`; the `:active` colour flashes removed from primary, secondary and IconButton; the old variants keep their look at weight 500), `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (dark `--color-on-accent` is `#15111E`, the dark background, so text on the accent matches the board), `app/src/ui/button.stories.tsx` (one args-based story file with autodocs; 11 stories become 9, and the old variants share one `Legacy` story). The 61 TextLink call sites, `text-link.tsx` and the old variants stay until their screen PRs.
- Rule: Buttons are 52px pills at 17/500 on the accent (primary), or a 52px outline with a 1.5px tint-strong border at 16/500 in the text colour (secondary). A link is text only, 15/400 in the accent-text colour, at the 44px target, with an optional chevron after the label (it points left in RTL). Danger-link is the same in the signal colour. Every button presses to opacity .72, and primary and secondary also scale to .985, over 120ms, with no colour flash. IconButton presses to opacity .72 only. Dark text on the accent is `#15111E`.
- Source: the approved Design canvas "Buttons" page (FLOW-363 redesign); REDESIGN-PLAN §2.2 rows 1 and 2.
