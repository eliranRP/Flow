# FLOW-1139: skeletons in the content's shape on the forecast, invoices, loans, reports and Home's parts

- PR: #1099
- Kind: refinement
- Approved: the owner, 2026-11-09: "Can you make sure we add skeleton instead of loading…..", the app-wide ask behind FLOW-1137 to FLOW-1140.
- Changed:
  - `app/src/ui/chart-skeleton.tsx` (new): a month chart's place while its months load: MonthChart's card with a title bar over six pairs of bars on the chart's 108px scale, each with its label; `mini` is the reports hub's 40px strip of ten bars. Used by the forecast, the company's and a project's next month, and the hub's P&L card.
  - `forecast-screen.tsx`: the page loads as the display figure, the forecast card, the chart and two sections of rows, not list rows; the loaded page's chart holds a chart skeleton while its six months load; a row's page shows a skeleton in place of a project or category name still loading, not a blank.
  - `unpaid-screen.tsx` and `filed-today-screen.tsx`: the lines skeleton (the total, its line, the chips, rows under heads). `invoice-screen.tsx` and `recurring-charge-screen.tsx`: the detail skeleton; the charge's history holds its place (the head, six bars, three rows) while its charges load.
  - `transfers-screen.tsx`: the lead line and two pair cards, each two legs, the reason line and the two answers.
  - `loans-summary-screen.tsx`: the band holds the period switch, the label, the figure and its two rows as band skeletons; the body draws the paid-parts card (title, parts bar, legend, note) and a row per loan with its bar, not the loans list's shape.
  - `member-report-screen.tsx`: while the report loads, the band with Back, band skeletons for the label, the figure and its line, and the real scenario switch; under it two calculation cards and the property rows. The forecast tab's own skeleton (FLOW-1136) is unchanged.
  - `month-drawer.tsx`, `home-month.ts`: each month with data shows a small skeleton bar in place of its net while the year is read, never ₪0; a failed read shows a dash.
  - `weekly-summary-card.tsx`: while the week's figures load, a skeleton in the card's shape holds its place on Home, so the card no longer pushes the page down when it lands. A failed read or a hidden card still draws nothing.
  - `search-parts.tsx`: "מחפש…" / "מחפש בכל התקופות…" is read to screen readers only; `search-start.tsx`: a check's count still loading is a skeleton, not a blank.
- Rule: a loading state draws grey blocks in the content's own shape, a chart included: never a line of loading text, a blank where content will land, a placeholder figure (₪0, $0), or list rows standing in for a page of another shape.
