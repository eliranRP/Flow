# FLOW-332 · Swipe back from the start edge

- **Lane:** UI lane 3 · **Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Approval:** owner approved FLOW-332 (small UI); no mockup.
- **What changed:** in the installed app, a swipe from the right (start) edge goes back on every screen with a Back button. A 40px round mark with the back chevron slides in from the edge at half the pull (up to 48px) and fills with the accent once a release would go back (past 30% of the width). A flick also goes back.
- **Rules (as FLOW-314):** touch only; starts within 24px of the edge; decided after 10px, and a mostly vertical move stays with the page scroll; never inside a sheet, a field, or a sideways list; tab roots have no Back, so nothing happens there. A browser tab keeps its own edge gestures.
- **Shared components:** new `EdgeSwipeBack` and `EdgeBackMark` (app/src/ui/edge-back.tsx); `BackButton` registers its action, so the swipe and the button always agree.
- **Rule:** an app-wide gesture borrows the screen's own control for its action, never a second route of its own.
- **Stories:** Components/Edge swipe back → Pulling, Armed (light and dark). Screenshots: the project's reviews/flow-332 folder.
