# FLOW-332 · Swipe back from the start edge

- **Lane:** UI lane 3 · **Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Approval:** owner approved FLOW-332 (small UI); no mockup.
- **What changed:** in the installed app, a swipe from the right (start) edge goes back on every screen with a Back button. A 40px round mark with the back chevron sits at the height where the touch started (clear of the safe areas), starts with 4px showing, slides in at half the pull (up to 52px, moved with transform) and fills with the accent once a release would go back (past 30% of the width), with a short vibration where the device has one. A flick also goes back. The resting mark has a 1px inset ring in the control border colour, not a drop shadow (DESIGN-RULES 2.6).
- **Rules (as FLOW-314):** touch only; starts within 24px of the edge; decided after 10px, and a mostly vertical move stays with the page scroll; never inside a sheet, a field, or a sideways list; tab roots have no Back, so nothing happens there. A browser tab keeps its own edge gestures. On iOS a diagonal swipe can still scroll the page a little before the swipe is decided; that is accepted.
- **Shared components:** new `EdgeSwipeBack` and `EdgeBackMark` (app/src/ui/edge-back.tsx); `BackButton` registers its action, so the swipe and the button always agree.
- **Rule:** an app-wide gesture borrows the screen's own control for its action, never a second route of its own.
- **Stories:** Components/Edge swipe back → Pulling, Armed (light and dark). Screenshots: the project's reviews/flow-332 folder.
