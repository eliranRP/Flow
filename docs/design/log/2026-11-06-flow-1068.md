- Rule:
  - The מדדים tab is groups, not one list: each group is a `section.ui-month` under a one-line `GroupHead` with its title and no count. A rental reads תשואה, סיכון, מחיר; a renovation תשואה, מחיר. The groups are data (`RENT_GROUPS`, `FLIP_GROUPS`), so a new metric is a key in one of them.
  - A metric row reads a loss (a figure under zero, in money or per cent) in the loss colour with its minus.
  - A renovation's band figure is the net profit; under it, "ROI X% על הכסף בעסקה" when the ROI is a per cent.
  - A part of ₪0 is not drawn (a renovation with no holding costs has no holding part).
  - A percent the owner types is one `PercentSheet`, with its own range, empty-field line and clear label: the tax bracket's מחיקה, the selling cost's ברירת מחדל (back to 8%).
  - Direction (the owner, 2026-11-06): a row that opens points left (`ChevronIcon`, an SVG, never a mirrored text character such as ‹ or ›), and back points right.
