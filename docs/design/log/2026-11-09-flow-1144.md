# FLOW-1144: a property's utility bills by month, with a sheet per month

- PR: the FLOW-1144 PR (the UI half; the server half adds `list_utility_months`)
- Kind: screen
- Changed:
  - New List screen `project-utility-months-screen.tsx` at `/projects/:projectId/utilities/months` ("לפי חודש"), opened from a link row near the top of the property's חשבונות · מי משלם page. Back to that page, the title, "<property> · האם החשבונות הוחזרו", then one `ListRow` per month, newest first: a `StatusMark` (✓ when every bill is back, else the late dashed ring), the month ("אוקטובר 2026"), "שכירות $X · חשבונות $Y" (the rent in the income colour, "—" with none), and the status: "כל החשבונות הוחזרו" (income colour), "חסר $X" (the sum not refunded, a bill still waiting included) or "לא נכנסה שכירות" (signal colour). Both lines wrap at 320.
  - Tapping a month opens the app's `Sheet`: the month as title and its status under it, then two `GroupHead` sections with their totals, "נכנס מהשוכר" (the rent lines; "לא נכנס תשלום החודש" when there is none) and "חשבונות ששולמו" (each bill with its `StatusMark` and "הוחזר" or "לא הוחזר" in its line). Every row links to `/transactions/:id`.
  - Data: `useUtilityMonthsQuery` and `utilityMonthsSchema` read `list_utility_months(p_project)`; a month's currency is its lines'. An answer on החזרי חשבונות refreshes it (`utility-months` in `UTILITY_KEYS` and the ledger keys).
  - Only existing parts: `ListRow`, `StatusMark`, `GroupHead`, `Sheet`, `CompactBar`, the skeletons, `EmptyState` and `PageLoadFailed`; its few classes are in `65-utility-refunds.css` (`ui-utm-*`).
- Rule:
  - A per-period summary that the owner drills into is a list of rows that open the app's `Sheet` with the period's lines grouped under `GroupHead`s with totals, every line opening its transaction.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-09 ("From each month we got a rent payment we need to see utilities payments as well"); board `V3-Project-UtilityMonths`, approved by the owner on 2026-11-09; FLOW-1144.
