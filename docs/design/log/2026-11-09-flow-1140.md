# FLOW-1140: a cold open and a screen's code load in the screen's own shape; offline says so

- PR: to come
- Kind: refinement
- Approved: the owner, 2026-11-09, answering the FLOW-1140 plan: the screen's own skeleton on a cold open; a full-screen route's title bar and grey blocks after 300ms while its code loads; and offline, "Please add a check for internet connection that recognizes when offline only then use the offline ui".
- Changed:
  - `app/src/cold-open-route.ts` and `app/src/cold-open.tsx` (new): while the session loads, a signed-in route draws its own screen's loading frame, picked by its path, in the outlet it lands in. A tab route sits over the tab bar (Home keeps its skeleton, now with the tab bar under it); Projects and Loans draw their band and title, Settings, invites, the journal composer, the line split and the review queue their title, and any other page Back's place. The bar is on the first frame; the blocks (the existing light skeletons: projects, loans, grouped settings rows, קבועים, profit, month lines, detail, form, list) wait 300ms.
  - `app/src/App.tsx`, `app/src/screen-suspense.tsx`: a full-screen route opened from a tab (search, invites, setup, a line's split and journal entry, install) shows the same frame without the tab bar, after 300ms, while its code loads, in place of a blank page. A boundary already on screen still keeps the previous screen up.
  - `app/src/connection.ts` (new), `app/src/main.tsx`: the app's online state starts from the browser's own and follows its events, so the app knows it is offline from launch and a read sent then waits for the connection.
  - `app/src/ui/offline-wait.tsx` (new), `app/src/ui/skeleton.tsx`: only while offline, a loading place waiting on such a read shows the app's offline line, "אין חיבור · ננסה שוב כשהחיבור יחזור", in place of its skeleton, under the screen's bar; when the connection returns the read resumes and the content fills in by itself. Every ScreenState's loading does this, and the page bodies that mark their DelayedSkeleton `offline` (loans, the loans summary, unpaid invoices, a recurring charge, unassigned lines, a report's account and lines, the forecast). Online nothing changes.
- Rule: while a screen loads, its own bar or band shows on the first frame and its blocks after 300ms, never another screen's skeleton or a blank page; offline, a load that waits for the connection says so in one line instead of a skeleton.
