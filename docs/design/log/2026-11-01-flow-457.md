- Rule:
  - A formula row that adds or takes away shows one sign: its operator by the figure's sign, then the figure without a sign ("− $8,400", never "− −$8,400"). The first row keeps its own sign; ÷ and × rows are as they were.
  - A section's count names what it counts, with its unit, from the rows drawn ("6 שורות", "שורה אחת"). A section of one row does not fold: its head is a plain line, the title in the same column as the others.
  - ∞ in a per-cent figure's place has the accessible name "כל ההון חזר".
  - An input has one name on its row, its sheet and a "חסר · …" hint: "שווי הקרקע", "מספר יחידות" (the names in `INVESTMENT_LABELS`). Every row of נתוני הנכס that opens something has the press state.
  - A total that leaves out a missing source says so under the figure: "חלקי · חסר X" or "חלקי · חסרים X וY" (caption, secondary).
  - A source row on the total card is dot | label | ⓘ | gap | amount, with no per-cent of the money in. A missing source shows הוספה (when an input adds it) in the amount track, and what is missing on its hint line ("חסר · שווי הקרקע"), so the label keeps one line at 320.
  - The bar's four shades are the accent mixed with the card at 100/86/73/62%, strongest first in the legend's order; the lightest is 3:1 against the card in both themes (3.16:1 light, 3.54:1 dark).
  - השקעה והלוואות reads: the total card (the total, its sources, then הון בנכס: הון עצמי היום, השבחה צפויה, כסף בעסקה), נתוני הנכס, מדדים, הלוואות. Only the sources carry an ⓘ; an equity row is itself the button to its sheet, with a chevron. The money in the deal is named once, in its row; under the total the meta reads "על הכסף בעסקה".
  - A metric or equity row whose input is missing shows only הוספה, and its hint says what is missing ("חסר · שווי היום"). A viewer sees "חסר".
