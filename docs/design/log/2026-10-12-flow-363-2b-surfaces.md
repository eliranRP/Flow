# FLOW-363 (2b): Card, StatusDot, ChangePill and ProgressBar on the redesign

- PR: FLOW-363 (2b), Card, StatusDot, ChangePill and ProgressBar (the foundation is in 1/4, 0181; 2a is Buttons).
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/card.tsx` (new, `as` section or div, `tone` default or tint, passes its labels through; `css/40-card.css`), `app/src/ui/prompt-card.tsx` (renders as a Card; its CSS keeps the width and margin only), `app/src/ui/status-dot.tsx` (new, replaces `StatusPill` in `chip.tsx`: tone neutral, accent or signal, optional 6px dot, role meta for the words), the eight `StatusPill` call sites (list-row, loan detail, profit months, transaction screen), `app/src/ui/change-pill.tsx` (new `upHurts` prop; band pill tones from `pill-helps` and `pill-hurts`), `app/src/ui/progress-bar.tsx` (`BudgetBar` is `variant="split"`; the fill is `m-bar-grow` in every variant; the unused `slim` variant is gone; the thin bar is the default 6px bar), `app/src/screens/project-detail-screen.tsx` and `app/src/ui/bidi-counts.stories.tsx` (the split call). The CSS: `03-controls.css` (`.ui-status` is text or a dot; `.ui-bar` is 6px), `04-banners-lists.css` (`.ui-change` in chip type with no weight 600; the band fills; `.ui-delta-*` are text, the one signal colour for a fall), `05-search-toast.css`, `14-review-change.css` and `setup/setup.css` (the `.ui-bar-thin` and `.ui-bar-slim` overrides, deleted). Stories: `card`, `status-dot` (new), `change-pill` and `progress-bar` (one args-based file each, autodocs); `chip.stories` loses its status cases. Tests: card, status-dot (moved from chip), change-pill (classes per tone and `upHurts`), progress-bar (the grow class, the split percent and overage).
- Rule: A status is coloured text or a 6px dot, never a filled chip. A change is text off the band; on the band a helping change is a grey fill and a hurting one a red fill. A card has a 16px padding and a 20px radius and no border. A progress bar is 6px and grows from the right on enter.
- Source: the approved Design canvas "Ready for dev" boards (FLOW-363 redesign); REDESIGN-PLAN §2.2 rows 4, 16 and 17, §3 and §4.1; DESIGN-RULES §2.8.
- Left for later PRs: the old `.ui-delta-*` readers in `related-charges` and `missing-bill-list` now show the new text colours (helps neutral, hurts in signal), which the screen PRs will check against their boards. `review-card`'s spike keeps its own tinted pill, since it is a warning, not a status.
