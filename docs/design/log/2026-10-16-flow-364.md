## Decisions made while building

- **The drawer's pick moves the month after the sheet's history pop.** A navigation sent before the pop
  is undone by it, so the pick sets the month in an effect once the drawer has closed.
- **The band's + is in Home's band, not the tab bar.** It follows `useAllowAdd`, so a viewer sees no
  + and preview keeps it. The sheet returns focus to it on close (`addTriggerRef`).
- **The tab bar's active tab scrolls to the top** when tapped (smooth, or instant with reduced motion).
- **The loading skeleton keeps the band real.** The company, the icons and the month pill are real; the
  figures are band bars, and the split card and the box are blocks.
- **Dead code removed with the tab bar:** the count badge, the FAB, `SettingsIcon`, `withSheetBackground`,
  and the badge and tab-slot tokens nothing else used.

## Board comparison (FLOW-364 proof)

Changes made to match the boards: the band body spacing and the forecast row (a chevron is added, which
the brief did not have: a deliberate change); the month drawer's cell size (60px) and alignment; the
past month's white back pill and no attention box; the attention box's dividers and row height; the
compact bar's month and figure, centred between the arrows; the tab bar's icon size and the bank icon;
the `--tabbar-min-inset` of 32px (see DESIGN-RULES §3.3).

Differences that stay open, with the reason:

- **Fixture data, not layout.** The dev fixture is ILS (₪) and the board is USD ($); the figures, the
  sign of the month (so the split card reads התזרים or המינוס and the bar colours change), the months'
  amounts in the drawer and the attention rows' sub-lines all come from the sample data. The layout
  matches. The board's sample names are not in the repo; the dev company is an invented name.
- **Empty first run (StatesHomeEmpty) is not rebuilt.** The board's empty band, the "חברו את הבנק" card and
  the "מה יופיע כאן" list differ in copy and structure from `NoBooksYet`. The current copy is asserted by
  App.test, the smoke and controls e2e specs and the viewer-writes test ("עוד אין נתונים", "חיבור בנק או
  SUMIT"). Rebuilding it changes that copy in five test files that other lanes also touch, so it is left
  for the owner to decide.
- **Drawer footer (override).** The board has no link under the grid. The app keeps "לכל החודשים ברשימה" as an
  override: `/cash/history` has no other entry, so removing the link would leave it reachable only by URL.
- **Drawer year arrows.** The board's year arrows sit in grey circles and both are enabled in the frame.
  The fixture's year range disables them, so they show faded.
- **Forecast chevron.** Added on purpose (see above).

## Empty first run and the dollar fixture (FLOW-364, proof pass)

- **Empty first run follows StatesHomeEmpty.** The band holds the month pill, a zero figure and "עוד אין תנועות. הן יגיעו מהבנק.", with no preview label on this state. The bank card (חברו את הבנק, חיבור Mercury, the manual link) and "מה יופיע כאן" with two bullets follow, all right-aligned. Viewers keep the card without the action (FLOW-601). The copy in App.test, auth-guard, viewer-writes, smoke, controls, smoke-readonly, sumit-live and pages follows this copy.
- **Band pills.** On the band both change pills are white with the helps text; the arrow gives the direction, as on the board (`.ui-band .ui-change-amount.ui-delta-bad`).
- **Split swatch.** The profit row's swatch takes the bar segment's colour when the profit is a loss.
- **Dollar negative month (`?state=usd-loss`).** Invented figures in cents, with three named categories in the split subline ("X, Y ועוד"). It is the fixture for the board's negative frames; the default ILS state is unchanged.
- **Preview route (not the proof).** The preview route still shows ILS and its preview label; the proof uses `/e2e/cash-empty` (USD, signed in).
- **Storybook layout.** `storybook-layout.spec.ts` passes (26 tests) on the built Storybook; its selectors needed no change.
- **Proof route.** `/e2e/cash-empty` renders the empty first run signed in: a company name with an initial avatar, USD as the base currency, not in preview. The proof uses it, so the empty band's preview label is gone for real users too.
- **Zero figure and title.** The empty band's zero figure sits at 60% opacity (the board's dim lavender), and "חברו את הבנק" uses the heading weight (600).
- **Picker values.** The cells' figures are one step under the list size (label size, 15px), as the board's are. `?state=usd-loss` passes the year's January-to-current months to the drawer (`drawerYearMonths`, dev only). Losses are coral with a minus. The year arrows are grey circles.
- **Empty bullets.** The "מה יופיע כאן" rows use a 4px gap between them, measured 12px tighter than the 16px gap; the rows' centres now sit within about 3 css px of the board's.
- **Band height.** Measured on the proof images at the centre column: the empty band ends at 570 px (2x) against the board's 572 px.
