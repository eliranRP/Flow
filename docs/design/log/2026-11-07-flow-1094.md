- Why these icons (FLOW-1095): הגדרות וחשבון is the least used: the owner opens settings rarely, and the band's other icons are the day's actions (+ adds, קבועים carries the red count of late bills, search finds a line). But the ⋯ takes the avatar's 44px slot, so moving the avatar alone gives the name 4px (the avatar's margin), not the 96px asked: at 320 the row is 288px, the actions 4 × 44 + 4, so the name had about 64px ("Acm…"). One more icon has to go to free a slot. Search is the next least used on Home: it is one more tap in the sheet, it keeps its focused field (the row passes the same state), and the Projects band still has it. + is the add flow's only entry on Home, and קבועים's count is a status that must stay seen. With two icons in the ⋯, the name has over 96px at 320 ("Acme Tes…"; the story checks 96 or more).
- Rule:
  - Under 360px, a band row keeps its title at least 96px wide: the least used icons move into a 44px ⋯ sheet, never a status icon (one with a count) or the screen's add action. From 360px up nothing moves.
  - Hiding a card offers ביטול for 4s in the app's toast ("הוסתר"); the ביטול brings back that card only. The screen's "N הוסתרו · הצגה" stays as the way back after.
