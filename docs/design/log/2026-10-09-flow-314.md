# FLOW-314: swipe between transactions on the card

- PR: the FLOW-314 PR (built by UI lane 4 in closed draft #279, handed over)
- Kind: pattern
- Changed: new `CardSwipe` (app/src/ui/card-swipe.tsx) wraps the transaction card's body under the top bar when the card was opened from a list. A finger moving right opens the next card, which slides in from the left; moving left opens the previous one, the way ˄ ˅ and the arrow keys already move. `useTxnNav` gains `enter` (the side a swiped-to card enters from). Stories "CardSwipe" (middle, dark, 320, last)
- Rule: A sideways swipe repeats a control already on screen and never replaces it. It shares one set of rules (touch only; 24px edge zones, the 24th px included, left to swipe-back; nothing inside a field, a sheet or a sideways list; decide after 10px and give vertical moves to the page; follow the finger and commit past 30% or a flick; stay put toward an end; reduced motion swaps on release; no swipe while the page is pinch-zoomed, so a sideways pan moves the zoomed view). In RTL the finger moving right goes forward (the next card enters from the left, like a push) and moving left goes back, matching ArrowLeft/ArrowRight and the start-edge swipe-back.
