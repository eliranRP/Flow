# FLOW-1339: offline on a new day, a loan row does not say "בלי תשלום קבוע"

- PR: #1305
- Kind: states, copy
- Changed: `screens/loan-list.tsx` (`outlooksUnknown` on the row), `screens/loans-host.tsx` (sets it when today's outlooks have no data), `screens/loans-list.tsx` (`loanRowHint` / `loanRowHintParts` leave the next-payment part out), story "Offline, new day".
- Source: the #1303 review (FLOW-1337). Offline on a new day every row said "בלי תשלום קבוע", though the loans have a schedule and only the outlooks are missing.
- Rule: an unknown is not a "none" (feedback-and-states § 4). When today's outlooks are not known, a row's hint and spoken label leave the next-payment part out; a demand loan keeps "בלי תשלום קבוע", since it never has a schedule; with the outlooks known, a loan with no next payment still says it.
