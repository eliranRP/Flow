  - A figure's ⓘ sheet defines it once: the body above the calculation, then איך לקרוא and בשביל מה only. The guide's heads are captions (caption size and weight, secondary), not a second title level. A sheet whose body scrolls ends it above the foot's button (FLOW-471).
  - A card title keeps one line at 320 with its ⓘ beside it: the period goes to a caption under it ("לשנה"). A section's count counts the rows that add up, not the "=" row (FLOW-471).
