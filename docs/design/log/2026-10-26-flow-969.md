- PR: #829
- Kind: screen, component, pattern
- Changed: `app/src/ui/order-chips.tsx` (new, `LinesOrderChips` from `cash-lines-parts.tsx` made shared), `app/src/screens/company-profit-screen.tsx`, `company-profit-period.tsx`, `company-next-month-block.tsx`, `project-profit-screen.tsx`, `project-month-profit-screen.tsx`, `next-month-block.tsx`, `project-next-category-sheet.tsx`, `cash-lines-screen.tsx`, `app/src/next-month-chart.ts` (`profitTakeaway`), `app/src/project-forecast-writes.ts`, `app/src/use-company-profit.ts` (`readAt`), `03-controls.css`, `36-cash.css`, `45-project-profit.css`, `47-company-profit.css`, `50-forecast-edit.css`, `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (`--color-outline-forecast`).
- Rule:
  - The order chips on company profit, project profit and a project's month are one choice: a radio group ("סידור") of 44px chips with a 3:1 edge, arrow keys, Home and End, one tab stop. The month lines' chips are the same component. The period switch (אוקטובר · 2026 · מההתחלה) is 44px.
  - Every profit chart heads with a one-line takeaway (owner card): the metric and its span are the small label ("הכנסות והוצאות לפי חודש · מאי עד אוקטובר וצפי"), the takeaway the heading ("ספטמבר היה החודש היחיד שבו יצא יותר משנכנס", "בכל חודש נכנס יותר משיצא", "2 חודשים יצא יותר משנכנס"; by year on the all-time chart). With nothing in or out the metric stays the heading.
  - A failed read hides the figures: Home v2's failed card ("רווח אוקטובר לא נטען", "הרווח של <name> לא נטען", "רווח <month> לא נטען", or "אין חיבור") with נסו שוב and צריך עזרה?. Offline with figures on screen, "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM" under the figure.
  - הסרה מהצפי removes the row at once; its toast hangs under the header with ביטול for 5s, a save's note 4s (decision 0219).
  - Edges at 3:1: the forecast's dashed outline (`--color-outline-forecast`, light #7A6E92, dark #8F879F), the forecast edit page's history bars (`--color-track-outline`) and its name underline (`--color-control-edge`), and the category sheet's search edge. Its placeholder and the removed rows' meta are muted text at 4.5:1.
  - Type on the scale: the profit pages' 14px and 12px off-scale sizes move to line2, section and caption.
- Source: owner approval of the Profit boards on 2026-10-26 (design canvas, "Ready for dev", the Profit row and its "PROFIT v2" note), and its owner cards (takeaways on all profit charts; "At once, 5s undo"). Decision [0219](../../decisions/0219-profit-toast-under-header.md).
- Departures from the approved board:
  - The expense bars were already at 3:1 (`--color-chart-expense`, #823).
  - "לכל הפרויקטים" already draws 44px (`.ui-fc-chart-link`).
  - The year chart's twelve month labels stay 11px: at 13px they clip on a 360px screen.
