- PR: #835
- Kind: screen, copy
- Changed: `app/src/connection-undo.ts` (new: `holdForUndo()`), `app/src/screens/connection-pages.tsx` (SUMIT), `app/src/screens/mercury-connection-screen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/mercury-connected-view.tsx`, `app/src/screens/mercury-accounts-step.tsx`, `app/src/screens/assistant-settings.tsx`, `app/src/screens/assistant-setup.tsx`, `app/src/use-mercury-connect.ts`, `app/src/use-sumit-connect.ts`, `app/src/use-mercury-accounts.ts`, `app/src/ui/css/10-settings-onboarding.css`, decision 0223.
- Rule:
  - Disconnecting Mercury or SUMIT: at once, with no confirm, and a toast under the header with ביטול for 5s (the owner's call). The disconnect is sent once the 5s end, so the key stays on the server until then and ביטול sends nothing. Leaving the app ends the wait at once.
  - A refused key: "Mercury לא קיבל את המפתח. בדקו שהוא Read Only ושהעתקתם אותו כולו." / "SUMIT לא קיבל את המפתח. בדקו את מספר החברה ואת המפתח." under the key field, no toast, and "חיבור מחדש".
  - "חיבור" is never greyed out (it was #6A627A on #2E2740 and a tap did nothing). A tap with something missing says "צריך את המפתח המלא, לפחות 8 תווים" (Mercury) or "צריך מספר חברה (6 ספרות לפחות) ומפתח מלא" (SUMIT). The account picker's save is never greyed out; with nothing checked it reads "בחרו לפחות חשבון אחד".
  - Every toast on the connection pages hangs under the header (`useTopToast()`, `topToast()`, decision 0222): connect, refresh, the accounts' saves, the AI keys. Setup's connect sheets keep their place.
  - The key toggle (הצגה / הסתרה) is 44px tall (it was 36), on every key field (setup too).
  - The key, company and date fields' edges are the 3:1 control edge, which FLOW-972 made app-wide.
- Source: owner approval of the Connections v2 boards on 2026-10-26 (design canvas, Ready for dev, the Connections sheets on the Settings row and the findings note "CONNECTIONS v2"). Decision [0223](../../decisions/0223-connections-disconnect-with-undo.md).
- Departures from the approved board: a refused key is cleared from the field, as before (the board keeps it showing); the note says to paste it again. The tab bar pill is FLOW-958's.
