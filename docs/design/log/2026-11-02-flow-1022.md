# FLOW-1022: the total-return card lines up its ⓘ icons and its amounts

- PR: (this PR)
- Kind: look
- Changed: `app/src/ui/css/26-returns.css`.
- Rule:
  - Icons that follow labels in a list of rows sit in one column, as wide as the longest label, not right after each label.
  - On the תשואה כוללת card, amounts line up on their last digit (the column's start, right in RTL), so a minus stands outside the digits and amounts of the same length share their "$". A word in an amount's place (הוספה) keeps the column's far edge.
  - This deliberately amends FLOW-454 for this card only, where the figures are long and can be negative. The same screen's נתוני הנכס and מדדים rows keep their amounts at the far edge (left in RTL): each sits beside its own chevron and title, not in a stacked column of signed sums.
