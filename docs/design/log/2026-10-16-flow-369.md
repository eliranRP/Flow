## Differences from the board

1. **Currency.** The board shows `$`. The app is in ₪, so the form shows `₪`.
2. **Bank row.** The board moves the bank connection to Settings › חיבורים. The row stays in the + sheet for now: its test (`add-sheet.test.tsx`) belongs to PR #633, so it is not edited here. The move follows #633.
3. **Camera.** The board's full-screen camera with a scan line and a reading step is not built. The camera tile opens the phone's camera (`capture="environment"`) and then the form, with nothing read from the photo (FLOW-306).
4. **Suggestion line.** The board's supplier suggestion is not built.
5. **Overhead.** "בלי פרויקט" is a chip on the form, not a project; it saves the line with no project.
