# FLOW-449: Next month's forecast card and month chart (project screens)

- PR: #741
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/month-forecast.tsx` (new: `ForecastCard`, `MonthChart`), `app/src/ui/month-chart-scale.ts` (new: the bar scale), `app/src/ui/css/51-next-month.css` (new), `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (new tokens `--color-chart-expense`, light #A69EB8, dark #564E66, and `--radius-chart-bar`, 4px), `app/src/screens/next-month-block.tsx` (new), `app/src/screens/project-profit-screen.tsx` (the card and chart replace the one-line forecast row in לפי חודש), `app/src/screens/project-cash-screens.tsx` (the card and chart replace חודשים קודמים), `app/src/ui/screen-skeletons.tsx` (`NextMonthCardSkeleton` takes the new card's box); deleted `app/src/screens/project-next-month-card.tsx`, `ProjectMonthsCard`, `.ui-pn-card*`, `.ui-pn-tag` and `.ui-project-months-card`.
- Rule: Next month's forecast is one shared card and one chart on every screen that shows it. The card: a 1.5px dashed `outline-forecast` outline, radius `card`, the month (17/500) with an outlined צפי tag, "פירוט ועריכה ›" in `accent-text` (or a `text-muted` caption with no link), two columns of הכנסות צפויות (28/400, `income` with +, `text-secondary` at zero) and הוצאות צפויות with 13px `text-muted` captions, and under a `line` divider the net label and figure in `text-secondary`, never green. The chart: a `surface` card, title 15/500 with a 13px sub, two bars a month on one scale (108px max, 4px floor, 2px for zero, 4px radius; income `accent`, expenses `chart-expense`), oldest on the right, next month dashed and unfilled (income `tint` with an `accent` dash) with its label in `accent-text`, legend 12px הכנסות · הוצאות · צפי, one `role="img"` label with next month's numbers. The net label is רווח צפוי on a profit page and תזרים צפוי on the project page, where the card's expenses, its net and the chart's forecast pair are cash: the rest of next month's loan payments that the profit leaves out is added (decision 0196). The card's figures are `Amount size="column"`: 28/400, stepping down together (24, 20, then 17) until both fit their columns (`.ui-amount-fit`). The chart's pairs align from the top, and next month's label wraps centred under itself when wider than its column (320 with seven months).
