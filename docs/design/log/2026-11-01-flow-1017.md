# FLOW-1017: קבועים by name on Home and a count on its icon; Projects rows' צפי as plain text

- PR: #TBD
- Kind: pattern
- Changed: `app/src/screens/home-parts.tsx` (`HomeBandActions`: a red count badge `.ui-band-count` replaces the 8px `.ui-band-dot`, 9+ past nine, and the tip's sentence says "המספר האדום: קבועים שלא הגיעו."; `AttentionBox`: a "כל הקבועים" link at the box's foot), `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (the link's path), `app/src/screens/projects-screen.tsx` (`nextFigure`: "צפי לחודש הבא" over the amount as plain secondary text, no `ui-fc-tag` and no coloured `Amount`; the company card's slot keeps its height while the forecast is read); CSS `07-band-home.css` `.ui-band-count` (was `.ui-band-dot`), `43-home.css` `.ui-home-attention-foot`, `51-next-month.css` `.ui-projects-next-loading` and `.ui-proj-next`.
- Rule:
  - A status on an icon is a number, never a bare coloured dot: a count badge in `--color-signal` with text in `--color-bg` (readable in both themes), 9+ past nine, and the icon's accessible name says the count.
  - A destination reached only by an unlabeled icon also gets a link by name where its items surface (קבועים: "כל הקבועים" at the foot of Home's דורש טיפול box).
  - A row's second figure is plain secondary text that names what it is ("צפי לחודש הבא ₪X"), not a tag and a second coloured amount; when it would widen the row's end column at 320, the words and the amount stack on two lines (the amount never breaks).
  - A card that loads after the list above it holds its height while its reads are out, so the rows under it never jump.
