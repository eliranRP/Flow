Batch A of the five-token plan (T2). Mechanical only: M1 (the 1.5px borders become `--stroke-strong`), M2 (raw sizes equal to a token of the same meaning: touch targets to `--touch-min`, fields to `--field-height`, the search field to `--search-height`, rows to `--row-height`, the segment to `--size-seg`, bars to `--bar-thin` or `--bar-job`), M3 (the two font sizes that equal a role become that role), and M4 (the half-step and whole-step spacing spellings). Values that have no token of the same meaning are left alone, including the 12px label sizes and the 1.5-step spacing. Every replacement has the same computed value.

Proof: 140 Storybook shots (35 stories at 320 and 393, light and dark) are byte-identical before and after the sweep.
