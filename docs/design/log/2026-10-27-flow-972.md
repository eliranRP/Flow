- Rule:
  - A text field's edge and the dashed "+ עסק חדש" are `--color-control-edge` (#8F879F dark, #7A6E92 light), 3:1 and more on the card; the tint was 1.35:1. The field edge is app-wide (`.ui-field-control`). The switch's off edge was already 3:1 (FLOW-965).
  - "+ עסק חדש" opens the first-use setup for a new business (the owner's call); it already did.
  - יצירת הקטגוריה is never greyed out. A name the kind already has says "כבר יש קטגוריה בשם הזה" under the field as it is typed; a tap with no name says "כתבו שם לקטגוריה" there and focuses the field; a one-letter name says "שם קצר מדי – לפחות 2 תווים". The note is announced.
  - Saves say what they save: "שמירת השם" on the business and a category, "יצירת הקטגוריה".
  - Offline, each page (the menu, העסק, קטגוריות, צוות) says under its head "אין חיבור · שינויים יישמרו כשהחיבור יחזור" with "מתחבר מחדש…". התראות says "אין חיבור · אפשר לשנות התראות כשהחיבור יחזור" instead, and its switches, this device and the test push refuse offline with those words. התנתקות and deleting the company refuse offline too, so neither runs by itself later.
  - Every toast hangs under the header; an undo stays 5s and a note 4s (decision 0222). The team page's toasts leave its bar (the last `place: "bar"`, now removed with 0137), and the theme's undo is no longer 3s. החלפת עסק keeps its toasts under the header on Settings and the default place from Home's band.
  - The הוצאות / הכנסות switch on קטגוריות is 44px at 15px; the other links and rows were already 44px.
  - The type is on the 13/15/17/20/32 scale: the category count line 13, a business's name in החלפת עסק and an invite's name 17, the choice sheets' rows 17 with a 13px note (on every screen that uses them), the team page's back link 17.
