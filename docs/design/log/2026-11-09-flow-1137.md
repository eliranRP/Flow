# FLOW-1137: skeletons in the content's shape, for review, forms, sheets and settings

- PR: (to be opened)
- Kind: refinement
- Approved: the owner, 2026-11-09: "review the app and make sure we follow the skeleton concept for loading across the app".
- Changed:
  - `app/src/ui/loading-shapes.tsx` (new) and `app/src/ui/css/65-loading-shapes.css` (new): the shapes these screens load in. A form (the line's figure, then a label bar over a control bar per field), a choice sheet's radio rows, a chip field's chips, the + sheet's tiles and rows, and a settings-style page (per group, a head bar over an inset card of icon rows; קטגוריות starts with its segmented control). Each whole-screen or whole-sheet shape says "טוען…" in one sr-only line.
  - לאישור loads as its one review card's skeleton (`ReviewCardLoading`, which now says "טוען…" there), not list rows. The full list keeps the list skeleton.
  - The journal composer, the line split, the change-assignment sheet, the next-month edit and the + sheet wait for the user's role with their skeleton, not a blank. The journal composer and the line split load as a form. The change-assignment sheet draws radio rows under its one title (the second header is gone). The journal composer also waits for the bank account's name, so its bank leg never says "בנק" first.
  - The add form's פרויקט and קטגוריה fields draw skeleton chips while their lists load. The recurring charge's pick sheet draws skeleton radio rows (`ChoiceSheet` takes `loading`).
  - The transaction's קבוע row has skeletons for its value, hint and pace tag while its read is out, not "לא קבוע" and a spinner. The הערה וקבצים section keeps its title and a block where the note goes; the review card's thread button keeps its place as a pill.
  - The loan split's fee category is a label over a control block, not "טוען…" as an option. The notifications page's device row has a skeleton hint, not a switch that reads off. Settings' צוות count and החלפת עסק row hold skeleton values while they are read.
  - Settings, חיבורים, קטגוריות and חיבור ל־AI load in their grouped shape.
- Rule: a loading state draws grey blocks in the shape of what loads: a form as label and control bars, a sheet's choices as rows or chips, a settings page as its groups. It never shows a blank where content will appear, a placeholder value as if read, visible "טוען…", or list rows for a page of another shape; a screen that waits for the user's role shows its skeleton meanwhile.
