Then the head "מה לקבל · לכל המכשירים" over the three switches, which always work, and the head "מכשירים מחוברים" over a static list: a short device name, "נוסף dd/mm" under it, and "המכשיר הזה" as the value of this device's row. Heads are the settings section head (secondary, 15px) at the card's edge, 12px above their card.
