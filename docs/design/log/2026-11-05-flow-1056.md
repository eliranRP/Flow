Stories: Screens/Routes Home on next month, its loading, failed, nothing expected and scrolled states, and next month picked in the drawer. E2e: `app/e2e/home-redesign.spec.ts`.
- Rule:
  - Home has three month states: past (the darker band, "חזרה ל…"), current, and future (next month only). A future month keeps the current band's shape and tone: the month pill with its arrows, the title over the big figure, two 44px rows, the caption and the foot row. Its figures are the forecast: title "תזרים צפוי · <month>", the expected net, "צפוי להיכנס" and "צפוי לצאת", each with its line under the label in the caption's type (on-band secondary), and the foot row "פירוט ועריכה" to `/forecast/next`.
  - The rows on a future month are not links: nothing has moved yet, so there are no lines to open.
  - Next month is a pick, not a card: the drawer's dashed cell and the band's later arrow both move Home there with the same history and scroll handling as any month. The earlier arrow from next month goes to the current month; there is no later month. A preview has no forecast, so it stops at the current month.
  - Under the band on a future month: no דורש טיפול, no weekly card, no setup checklist, and no filter (הכול, קבועים and נותר לשלם sort what has moved). A section title "הצפוי ב<month>" (`t-section`) stands where the filter sits, then the forecast's rows by project (the צפי page's order, כללי last) under one-line `GroupHead`s whose sums read "לתשלום" and "להיכנס", each row with `StatusMark` kind "due"; a loan's principal kept out of profit is faded (`setAside`) and opens nothing.
  - Loading: bars for the big figure (the hero's height) and the two amounts, and three skeleton rows. Failed: the band stays with dashes for its figures; the list says "הצפי ל<month> לא נטען" with נסו שוב. Nothing expected: the band at zero and an `EmptyState` "אין עוד צפי ל<month>".
  - A past month's body is its activity in the current month's composition: a section title "הפעילות ב<month>" (`t-section`) with the month's close under it in a group summary's style (`t-meta`, secondary: "נכנס $X · יצא $Y"), then the filter (הכול / קבועים / חד-פעמי: nothing is due in a closed month, so no נותר לשלם), which docks into the compact bar as on the current month, then קבועים (recurring lines, `StatusMark` paid) and חד-פעמי (one-off) under one-line `GroupHead`s whose sums read "יצא" and "נכנס". The month's lines only, never the schedule. When the lines run past their first page the list ends with "כל התנועות ב<month>" to the month's lines page; no כל הקבועים there. No new card for the close: the title's summary is it. (Also in Changed: `home-activity.tsx` `pastActivityGroups`, `monthFilter`, `PastMonthClose`; CSS `.ui-home-act-head`, `.ui-home-act-close`; stories Home past, its loading, failed and no lines.)
