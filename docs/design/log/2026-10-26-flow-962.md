# FLOW-962: Loans v2, the UX/UI rules review of הלוואות

- PR: #824
- Kind: screen, component, pattern
- Changed: `app/src/screens/loan-detail-screen.tsx` (the status row, סגירת ההלוואה, החזרה לפעילות, the closed head, the toasts), `loan-close-sheet.tsx` (new: the close sheet and `LoanDateField`), `loan-rate-sheet.tsx` (moved out of `loan-detail-sheets.tsx`: "מתי השינוי חל?"), `loan-states.tsx` (new: the failed card and the offline line), `loan-detail-cards.tsx`, `loans-list.tsx`, `loan-summary-card.tsx`, `loan-payments-view.tsx`, `loan-setup.tsx` (שמירת ההלוואה), `transaction-top-bar.tsx` (`toastCeiling`), `app/src/ui/css/26-loan-row.css`, `29-loan-detail.css`, `46-loans.css`, `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (`--color-loan-interest`; `--color-track-outline` as FLOW-961 adds it).
- Rule:
  - Every part of a loan bar reads at 3:1 or more: principal is the one accent, interest is ink (`--color-loan-interest`, 11.7:1 dark, 11.5:1 light), escrow is the outline grey (`--color-track-outline`, 3.2:1 dark, 3.4:1 light). The balance card's empty track is a hairline outline in that grey, not a 1.2:1 fill. The summary bar's label names each part and its amount.
  - The summary card says its takeaway over the bar ("רוב התשלום החודשי הולך לקרן", or "החלק הגדול…" when no part passes half). The next payment is its own row ("התשלום הבא", the lender and dd/mm, the amount), then the 12 months. The link to the summary page is ink with a chevron: one accent per card.
  - A closed loan's row is at full contrast, with a "נסגרה" tag (hairline, muted edge) and a chevron. The נסגרו toggle reads הצג/הסתר with a chevron that turns. Amounts are tabular.
  - The status row only reads. An open loan has סגירת ההלוואה (outline, full width) with "לפירעון מוקדם או למימון מחדש." under it; the sheet asks למה נסגרת? (נפרעה במלואה, מימון מחדש, each with a line), תאריך הסגירה (today, or the last payment when later) and says what follows. A closed loan leads with "נסגרה · <why> · DD/MM/YYYY" and a line, and has החזרה לפעילות. "שינוי" beside the pill is gone.
  - The rate sheet asks "מתי השינוי חל?" first (מהיום / מתאריך…), with one line on what that changes; its button is שמירת הריבית. The new-loan sheet's button is שמירת ההלוואה.
  - The delete confirm says what goes and what stays; its button is מחיקת ההלוואה.
  - On the loan pages every toast hangs under the top bar, and an undo stays 5s (decision 0214).
  - A failed read is Home v2's: an alert icon, "<thing> לא נטענה/נטענו", what happened, נסו שוב (filled) and צריך עזרה? (/help), on a card. Offline with figures on screen, a line says "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM".
  - A loan with no payments says what will appear and links "איך מחברים את חשבון הבנק" (44px). The paid card already waits for the first payment.
  - The paid card's period switch and the $/₪ marks are 44px targets. The payments page's purple tag is a plain label.
- Source: owner approval of the Loans v2 boards on 2026-10-26 (design canvas), with the owner's picks on cards the same day. Decision [0214](../../decisions/0214-loan-page-toasts-and-close.md).
- Departures from the approved board:
  - The status row does not say "נסגרת לבד כשהיתרה מגיעה ל־0": the app does not close a loan at $0 yet. Left to the owner.
  - "מתי השינוי חל?" sits on the rate sheet, where the app keeps dated terms (`loan_rates`); the kind, term and payment stay in the סוג sheet and apply to the whole loan. For a past day the line says the payments attached since then keep their parts (0132), not that they are worked out again. Left to the owner.
  - The close sheet has no link to the new loan: the app has nowhere to store it yet. Left to the owner.
  - The type stays on the app's tokens (FLOW-959 is the app-wide weight change); the tab bar pill is FLOW-958's.
