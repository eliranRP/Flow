# FLOW-455: an account's page keeps its content while the period changes; the share sheet says why it is blocked beside its button

- PR: #TBD
- Kind: screen
- Changed: `app/src/screens/report-account-screen.tsx` (FLOW-455), `app/src/screens/report-share-sheet.tsx` and `app/src/ui/css/61-reports.css` (`.ui-rep-share-why`, FLOW-456).
- Rule:
  - A choice that changes a screen's read (a period, a span) keeps the screen, its header and the control just tapped, on the last data until the new read is in (`aria-busy` on the content meanwhile); only a first load shows the skeleton.
  - A count over loaded pages is not shown while more pages remain unless it is whole: newest-first groups show their count, and the oldest group shown has none until the end is loaded.
  - A sheet's disabled action says why in its pinned foot, above the button, as a `role="status"` caption (empty, it takes no room), not at the end of the scrolling body.
  - Turning off an "all" switch starts the pick list from the screen's own item, else every item, so the first tap never blocks the sheet.
- Source: UX rules review 2026-11-01 (the owner asked for the fixes)
