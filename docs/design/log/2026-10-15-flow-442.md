# Cash lists: a loan part row names its part and the whole payment, kept-out rows too

- PR: FLOW-442 (review fixes on #598).
- Kind: screen
- Changed: On the cash month's נכנס, יצא and לא נספר ברווח lists (`CashMonthLines` and `CashLineRows` in `app/src/ui/cash-line-rows.tsx`), a loan payment's part row names its part ("קרן" or "ריבית") with "מתוך <whole payment> · date" in its hint. This holds on both the counted and the kept lists. A kept-out loan part (the principal, today) reads like a counted one: it uses the same split hint (`SplitPartsHint`, wrapping) as a counted row, not the one-line kept hint, so the "מתוך" figure stays visible at 320 and 393. The kept-out label ("לא נספר ברווח", FLOW-438) leads that hint on a line of its own (`lead` prop, `.ui-split-hint-lead`), so the month list still marks the line as left out; the date drops first when a wide amount leaves no room for it, as on a counted row. The one-line kept hint (`KeptOutHint`) stays for the other kept-out rows. The unused `KeptOutHint` fragment, the `SplitAmount` export and the `ReactNode` type are removed.
- Rule: A loan payment's part row names its part ("קרן" or "ריבית") and the whole payment ("מתוך $3,500 · date"), on every cash list, counted or kept out; a kept-out loan part uses the same split hint as a counted one.
- Source: FLOW-442, the owner's report that the loan rows on the cash lists lacked context. The intended wording is the same on the counted and kept lists, so the reader sees which part is left out and how it relates to the whole payment.
