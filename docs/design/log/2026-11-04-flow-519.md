- Changed: `app/src/screens/legal-links.tsx` (`LegalLinks` renders its own `nav` "מידע משפטי" with a list, no "·"), `SignInScreen.tsx` (the consent sentence above the Google button; the links under the profile line), `settings-screen.tsx`, `LegalScreen.tsx` (the contact address as a mailto link; "בעמוד הזה"; section ids), CSS `04-banners-lists.css` (`.ui-legal-links`), `09-sign-in-project.css` (`.ui-signin-consent`, `.ui-signin-legal`), `10-settings-onboarding.css` (`.ui-settings-legal`), `60-help-legal.css` (`.ui-legal-section`, `.ui-legal-toc`, `.ui-legal-mail`), `scripts/check-prod-bundle.mjs` (the service worker must precache the legal chunk). Stories: Screens/Routes Sign in, Settings menu, Terms, Privacy.
- Rule:
  - A set of three or more footer links is a grid of 44px targets with no separators (the legal links: 2×2), never a "·" line that can wrap.
  - A consent the tap implies sits right above that button, in `text-secondary` at body size.
  - A reading page with five or more sections opens with "בעמוד הזה" under its date: one 44px accent link per section that scrolls to it and focuses its heading; sections are split by a `line` hairline with 24px either side.
  - A contact address inside running text is a mailto link in `<bdi dir="ltr">`, the sentence's punctuation outside it.
- Source: UX rules review 2026-11-04 (the owner asked for the fixes)
