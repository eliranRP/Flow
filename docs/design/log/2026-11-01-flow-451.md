- Rule:
  - A figure made of other figures has an ⓘ (a 44px button, the 18px info icon in `text-muted`) that opens one shared sheet: title and figure, one or two lines on what it is, the parts as rows with an operator column (÷ × + − =), each row opening where its part comes from (a line, a part's page, Loans, or the input's edit sheet), the "=" row in 500, an optional muted caption, and סגירה. Figures taken away are drawn with their minus too.
  - A figure whose input is missing reads "חסר" with an accent "הוספה" that opens that input's sheet. An input no button can add (a value a year back) reads "חסר · שווי לפני שנה" with a one-line muted hint and no button. Never 0.
  - A part's or metric's page: Back, the title, "12 חודשים אחרונים · name" (a part: "מאז הקנייה · name") under it, the 40/400 figure and its meta line, then collapsible sections (the report drill-down's head) for the calculation and the transactions by month, 50 at a time, "עוד N תנועות". A transaction opens its own page; its category changes there.
  - The total return's bar is 8px in four shades of the one accent (`color-mix` with the surface), strongest first; only positive sources take a share. The one signal colour on the page is a negative total; elsewhere a negative figure keeps its minus in the text colour.
