# FLOW-1108: a report row's line too long for it shows its short figures

- PR: #1055
- Kind: pattern
- Changed:
  - `app/src/screens/reports-parts.tsx` (`reportHint`): a line with figures is written twice, whole: in full ("פחות מ־1% · הכנסות $120,000,000 · הוצאות $119,990,000") and short ("<1% · הכנסות $120M · הוצאות $120M"), the short one hidden from a screen reader. A line whose two forms are the same is written once.
  - `app/src/ui/css/61-reports-v3.css`: `.ui-rep-hint-fit`, a box one line tall in which the full line wraps away when it doesn't fit and the short line takes the whole line; below 374 the full line is hidden as before. `.ui-rep-hint-short`'s `display: none` is gone.
  - Stories: `Screens/Reports` "P&L · by project, nine digits under 1% at 393"; the 360 and 374 nine-digit stories carry the same row; `Screens/Reports/Rows` (was "Balance rows") "Report row · an icon in the lead column at 320".
- Why: FLOW-1098 left one row cut: a nine-digit line led by "פחות מ־1%" is 337px, so from 374 to 408 it ended in "…". Moving the 374 switch would have made every row short on the owner's 393 phone; now only that row is. At 320 its short line was 1px too wide, so the short line writes the share "<1%".
- Rule: From 374 a report row's line that is still too long for its row shows its short figures; the other rows keep their full ones. A short line writes "פחות מ־1%" as "<1%". No hint with figures ends in "…".
- Source: UX rules review follow-up 2026-11-07 (the owner asked to fix the open items).
