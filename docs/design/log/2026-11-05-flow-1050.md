# FLOW-1050: pull to refresh that feels native (ld-07)

- PR: #981
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx` (new `pullMove`, `pullSpokes`, `PULL_REST_PX`, `PULL_SPOKES`; `pullTravel` is now the rubber band; new phases `returning` and `closing`; the spinner is an inline SVG), CSS `62-pull-refresh.css` (the round raised mark and its accent are gone; new `.ui-pull-spinner`; the gap moves inside the screen box), two motion tokens in `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` (`--motion-pull-spin`, `--motion-pull-return`), DESIGN-RULES 2.7 and ld-07, CONTROLS.md. Story: Components/Pull to refresh (idle; pulling at 25% and 50% at 320 and 393; a notched iPhone at 100% at 320 and 393; a past month's band; refreshing at 320 and 393, with and without a notch; returning at 320 with a notch and at 393; a plain screen pulling and refreshing). E2e: `app/e2e/pull-refresh.spec.ts`.
- Reference: the owner's screen recording of a native iOS app's pull to refresh (not in the repo). What it shows is written in FLOW-1050; the numbers below were read off its frames at 4, 10, 30 and 60 frames a second.
- Rule:
  - The spinner is the iOS activity indicator: 12 thin rounded spokes (an inline SVG, `currentColor`, stroke 1.4 of 20 units, about 2px), 28px across as in the reference, with no circle or background. Its colour is `--color-text-secondary`, and `--color-on-band-secondary` over the band (Home, a project, a past month's band), where the secondary text colour would not read on the purple.
  - While pulling the spokes appear one by one, clockwise from 12 o'clock, standing still: `round(progress × 12)` of them, none at the start and all 12 at the 64px threshold. In the reference the brightest spoke goes round once in about 0.88s, one spoke per tick, so refreshing it turns in 12 steps on `--motion-pull-spin` (880ms), the spokes fading from full at the head to a quarter behind it. With reduced motion the turn stops: a refreshing spinner keeps that fade on still spokes, and a pulling one shows all 12.
  - The rubber band: `travel = 0.5·move + 0.5·21.5·move / (21.5 + move)`, half straight and half a knee. Its slope is 1 at the start (8px of move gives 6.9px), falls toward 0.5, and never stops. The move counts from where the pull is decided (10px), so the screen starts at 0 with no jump; the threshold comes at 110px after that (120px from touch-down), and 290px gives 155px. The reference reaches about 170px there, but it runs close to straight from the start (slope about 0.57): a curve that slows down and starts 1:1 cannot pass both 64px at 110 and 170px at 290, since that needs travel(290) / travel(110) above 290 / 110. This one keeps the 1:1 start and stops about 9% short. Its inverse (`pullMove`) is a quadratic's root. Crossing the threshold plays `navigator.vibrate(10)` once per pull, where the platform has it (iOS Safari does not, so nothing happens there).
  - The spinner is centred in the gap's part under the status bar until the gap reaches the rest, and stays there as the pull goes on, as in the reference.
  - Released at 64px or more, the screen eases to a 64px rest (the 28px spinner with 18px above and below it, plus the safe area on a notched iPhone) on `--motion-pull-return` (400ms) with `--ease-enter`, and holds while the spinner turns (at least 400ms, at most 15s, as before). When the reads settle, the spinner fades and shrinks to 0.7 where it stands (`opacity` and the individual `scale`, on `--motion-fade` with `--ease-exit`), still turning, and the screen glides back to the top on `--motion-pull-return` with `--ease-enter`, a soft ease-out. Released before, it glides back the same way and the spokes fade where they stood, without turning.
  - A touch during the glide back catches the screen where it is: the screen's live `top` is read from the running transition and turned back into a pull (`pullMove`), so the finger pulls on from there with no jump and no 10px decision. The spinner's fade and shrink are on a transition in every phase, so it comes back from where the fade had got to instead of snapping. A tap lets the screen go on returning.
  - The gap is the screen's own first child, drawn just above it, so it moves with the screen in every frame and has no animation of its own. As a separate transform it ran on the compositor ahead of the screen's `top` while the refreshed screen rendered, and bars of the page showed between them during the glide back (seen in a frame strip of this build); a 1px seam also showed at fractional travel.
  - Smoothness: a Playwright check (Chromium with an iPhone viewport, 393 × 852 at 3x, and touch, not WebKit, so the safe area, `lvh` and Safari's touch handling are not covered) logs the screen's top with the frame's time on every animation frame over a 180px pull in 30 moves, the release, the hold and the return, and over a return caught mid-way. The screen never moves faster than 12px per 60Hz frame (0.72px/ms between two samples): 10.8px and 9.0px at most, over three runs each.
  - Kept from FLOW-1042: the whole screen moves by `top` over a gap in its top's colour, the fixed bars stay put, the notch geometry, the 400ms minimum and 15s cap, every cached read goes stale with no bank sync, and the gesture guards. Reduced motion: the screen does not move and there is no gap; the spinner rests 18 to 46px under the top safe area with all its spokes, still, and only fades in with the pull.
