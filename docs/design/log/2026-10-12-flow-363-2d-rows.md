# FLOW-363 (2d): ListRow and List layouts (flat and inset), row title on the body role, list-row stories on args

- PR: FLOW-363 (2d), the row layouts (the foundation is in 1/4, 0181; 2a is Buttons, 2b is Card and the status parts, 2c is Amount).
- Kind: component
- Changed: `ListRow` and `List` take `layout?: "flat" | "inset"` (`ListLayout` in `app/src/ui/list-row.tsx`). The row adds `ui-row-flat` or `ui-row-inset` on every variant's branch (static, button, danger, selectable, and the project, transaction and item branch) and on the skeleton row. `List` adds `ui-list-flat` or `ui-list-inset` on `.ui-project-list`. No prop draws today's row. New `app/src/ui/css/41-rows.css` (imported after `40-card.css`): flat is `min-height: var(--row-height)` with its `.ui-avatar` at `var(--avatar)`; inset is a Card surface (`--color-surface`, `--radius-card`, `overflow: hidden`) with rows at `padding-inline: var(--space-inset-row)` and a 1px `--color-line` divider between rows, and the pressed and focus tint runs the card's width with square corners. `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` gets `--space-inset-row: 14px`. `css/01-base.css`: `.ui-row-title` reads the body role (`--type-body-size`, `--type-body-weight`, `--type-body-line`) instead of title-3 size and line with the meta weight. Stories: `list-row.stories.tsx` is rewritten on args (21 stories, was 61; the `*Dark`, `*320`, `*390` and `*SE` copies are gone, and theme and width come from the toolbar). New kept stories: `FlatList` and `InsetList`. Tests: `list-row.test.tsx` gets a layout block (default draws neither class; flat and inset classes on every variant and on the List; a busy button with a layout keeps `aria-busy` and ignores taps).
- Rule: A long list's row is flat: on the background, 20px page sides, no divider, about 64px tall. A row inside a card is inset: 14px inside, and a 1px `--color-line` hairline between rows, none after the last. Row titles read the body role (17/400).
- Source: the approved Design canvas "Long data lists → flat rows" and "Focused items and summaries → cards" (FLOW-363 redesign); REDESIGN-BUILD "Spacing"; REDESIGN-PLAN §2.2 row 3 and §3; DESIGN-RULES §2.8 (the "List row" entries and the list-height row).
- Left for later PRs: no screen opts in here, so every list keeps its default row until its screen PR passes `layout`. The statement row (`ListRow variant="statement"`) takes no layout yet. Radio, check, person, connector and cash-line rows are not folded or deleted. The row title's line height moves from 1.3 to 1.45 with the body role, so a default row is 3px taller (settings account rows: 50px to 53px; the test expectation moved with it).
- Press (review fix): tappable rows (button, danger, selectable, link and project-button branches) get `m-press` without `m-press-scale` (opacity .72, 120ms, no scale); static and non-link rows get neither. Test: `list-row.test.tsx` "ListRow press (FLOW-363 2d)". The inset tint comment in `41-rows.css` now says "selected".
