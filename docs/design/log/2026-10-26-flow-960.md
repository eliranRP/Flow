- Rule:
  - אישור is never a dead button. It is disabled only while the card settles (under a second). With a field missing, a tap opens that field's picker; the project sheet then says "בוחרים פרויקט, ואז אפשר לאשר", and after a pick the card comes back with the field filled (no auto-approve). The line under the bar reads "חסר פרויקט · אישור יפתח את הבחירה" (or "חסר קטגוריה · …") in `--color-warning`.
  - A missing value on the card reads in `--color-warning`, with a 16px alert icon before "חסר" beside the chevron, and the box gets a 1px warning outline (inset), so the gap is not told by colour alone.
  - Jev's reason heads the project and category box, padded as a row with a hairline under it. On a split or a split mismatch there is no box, and the reason stays under the parts.
  - Up to 12 items the visit meter draws a segment each; above 12 it is one bar filled with the items done. Both are a `meter` with their values.
  - In כל הפריטים a row with no project ends its meta with an alert icon and "חסר פרויקט" in the warning tone.
  - The review project picker shows its search from 10 projects.
  - On לאישור every toast hangs under the header (decision 0212): `--space-2` under the meter, with the card's sides. The approve undo stays 5s; a failed approve stays until ניסיון חוזר, a tap or a swipe.
