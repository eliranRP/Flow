# FLOW-454: property returns on real deals: aligned rows, before the first rent, one unit by default, the loan taken over

- PR: #925
- Kind: screen
- Changed: `app/src/screens/project-returns-data.ts` (units always set, `units_default`, the loans taken over at closing), `project-returns-info.ts`, `project-returns-cards.tsx`, `project-returns-parts.tsx`, `project-investment-cards.tsx`, `project-investment-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/26-returns.css`, `app/src/dev/returns-sample.ts`, `app/src/dev/investment-sample.ts`.
- Rule:
  - A source row on the total-return card is a grid of fixed tracks: dot | label | ⓘ and a flexible gap | amount (fixed, at the per-cent's side, tabular figures) | per-cent (fixed). Every row keeps its amount and per-cent in the same columns. A figure that is not an amount (a missing note, "לא מחושב") is one cell across the amount and per-cent tracks, from the amount track's start. A hint under a row is its own grid row, from the label's track to the end.
  - The values of הון בנכס and מדדים sit in a fixed-width column at the row's end, in tabular figures (the metrics' column is narrower: a per-cent or a ratio).
  - Before the first rent (no rent yet in the read), a per-cent figure reads "לפני השכר הראשון" in caption style, muted: the total return, each source (on its own line under the amount, at the per-cent's edge), cash-on-cash and cap rate on מדדים, and the metric page's hero, which then has no result row. The amounts stay; DSCR keeps its own words.
  - Units are 1 until the owner sets them: the יחידות row reads the number, with the caption "ברירת מחדל · עריכה" while it is the default; it opens the units sheet as before. Nothing reads "missing units".
  - A loan taken over at closing (Sub2) splits a purchase formula row in two: "רכישה וסגירה (מהתנועות)" (the purchase less the loan, opening the purchase lines) and "+ הלוואה שנלקחה בסגירה" (captioned with the loans' names, opening the loan, or the loans list for several). The two add up to the price. נתוני הנכס reads "מהתנועות + הלוואה שנלקחה · לפירוט". The money in has no loan row: it is cash only.
