- Rule:
  - An investment-style Overview picks its band by kind: a rental's band is a per-cent with "$X בשנה על $Y" under it and two rows (the money in the deal, today's equity); a renovation's is the expected profit in money (`size="hero" compact`, as on the project page) with the ARV and the total cost. When the figure cannot be worked out, the band says "חסר" and the attention cards say what to add; it never reads $0.
  - A missing input is an attention card whose link is the page itself with `?edit=<field>`; the page opens that field's sheet when such a link lands while it is open. Its `dismissKey` is the input (`arv`, `land`), or `history:<value date>` for a second value, so updating the value brings the card back.
  - The parts of a figure are one card: the 8px bar of shades (`--motion-bar-grow`), then one row per part (swatch, name, a line under it, figure, chevron). A part with its own page links to it; a part whose calculation is short opens its ⓘ sheet; a part whose input is missing opens that input's sheet.
  - "איך מחושב" on a band is a button on the band (16% on-band tint, 44px) that opens a `Sheet` with the figure's formula rows (`FormulaList`), then how to read it, and הבנתי.
  - A calculation page's transactions: no section card; one `section.ui-month` per month with `GroupHead summary` (count · signed sum), on the page's gutter.
  - Pixel pass (decision 0257, board V3-Calc): a calculation page's head is the name at 24/600 with its ⓘ, the period line 14 secondary 8px under it, the 40px figure, and how it is made in 14 secondary. Each block of the calculation is a `CalcSection` (it does not fold): a 20px surface card, 4px 16px inside, a 44px head over a hairline (its name 15/500 and its figure 14, both secondary), then 60px rows with the operator 20px in a 20px column; an "=" row, mid-list (the NOI) or last, is 600 with a 20/600 figure. The statement is one list of + − = rows, as the board draws it; `ReportSection` is no longer used on these pages.
  - Pixel pass (board V3-Investment): the band's label is 17 secondary 14px under the kind line, its two rows 44px, 14px under the figure's line and 6px apart; the parts card is 16px 18px inside with 10px between the title, the 10px bar and the rows, the swatches 10px, the hint in the secondary colour, the fourth part in the muted colour. A typed ARV gets its own card ("ARV מבוסס על הערכה שלכם", `dismissKey` `estimate:<arv>`).
