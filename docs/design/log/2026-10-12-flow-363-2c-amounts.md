# FLOW-363 (2c): Amount, "+" on income, list cents 11px raised 5px

- PR: FLOW-363 (2c), Amount (BigNumber renamed), "+" on income, list cents (the foundation is in 1/4, 0181; 2a is Buttons, 2b is Card and the status parts).
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/big-number.tsx` renamed to `app/src/ui/amount.tsx` (component `Amount`, type `AmountProps`; `formatAmount`, `showsMinus`, `splitCents` and `heroStepClass` stay there). `formatAmount` passes `plus` for `direction: "income"`, and `Amount` draws the "+" for `income` on a non-zero figure with no loss. Every import and JSX use in `app/src` now reads `Amount`. The one local `Amount` wrapper in `investment-card.tsx` is now `CardAmount`, and the two split screens import the UI one as `AmountFigure`. Stories: `amount.stories.tsx` (7 stories, was 10; args hold strings, the view makes the bigint). Tests: `amount.test.tsx`, and the "+" expectations in the screen and list-row tests. `design/system/implementation-tokens.css` gets `--cents-raise: 5px`; `css/01-base.css` gives `.t-amount .ui-num-cents` the cents size and that raise.
- Rule: Money in is `income` green with a "+" in front of the figure, inside its `dir="ltr"` isolate, so a screen reader reads it (decision 0181 amends the no-plus rule of 0120). Money out is `−` on the left, U+2212. A figure shown as zero has no sign and no green. A list amount draws its cents at 11px, raised 5px (Design guide: "List amount · 17 / 400, cents 11 raised 5px").
- Source: the approved Design canvas "Ready for dev" boards (FLOW-363 redesign); REDESIGN-PLAN §2.2 row 5; decision 0181; DESIGN-RULES §2.8 and §3.5.
- Left for later PRs: the hand-written `aria-label` sentences such as "הכנסה ₪5,000" keep their figure without the "+", because the word already names the direction. Plain-text money (`formatIls` in the reviewer preview's home rows, and text built without `Amount`) is not drawn with a plus here; screen PRs that draw money in as an `Amount` get it for free.
