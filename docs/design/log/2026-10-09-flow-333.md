# FLOW-333: Split by category editor follow-ups

- PR: #261
- Kind: screen
- Changed: Split by category editor. A part's ₪ or % field now takes its own full-width line, so ₪9,999,999.99 fits at 320, and a share past 1,000% reads with separators ("25,720.16%"). The ₪ and % buttons are 44px wide. "הוספת חלק" moved from under the parts into the sticky bar, at the start of the totals line; with eight-figure sums the totals wrap under it. After a part's category or project pick, focus lands in that part's value field, and a new part takes the unit of the last part typed (3 taps per part). On a refund line the reversal section of the category picker opens expanded. The line's own project shows once in a part's project list, as the "פרויקט השורה" row. The hold sentence and "ביטול השינוי" show only after the first ✕, once: the part's own copy of the same sentence then drops. Design lead review on #261.
- Rule: An entry field that can hold a long amount gets the row's full width; a screen's add action sits in its sticky bar when the list it grows pushes it down. A warning repeats in one place only.
- Source: FLOW-333 C1, C3a, C3b, C3c, C4, C5, C7, C9 (mobile UI/UX review cycle 3).
