- Rule:
  - An Overview screen's attention rows are `AttentionCards` under the band in `ui-page-pad`, 20px under it; each row's `dismissKey` is `<id>:<count>[:<amount or ids>]` (`unpaid:3:460000`, `changes:2:t1+t2`), so a hidden card returns when what it counts changes. The review row has none.
  - An Overview screen's list under the cards: a zero-height `ui-compact-mark`, the `SegmentedControl` (hidden label) inside `DockSlot`, then one `section.ui-month` per group with `GroupHead summary` ("N · $X יצא · +$Y נכנס"; a due group says לתשלום / להיכנס) and a flat `List` of `transaction` rows with a `StatusMark` icon. The same control, tone band, goes in the bar's `dock`; `useDockedFilter({ barShown, bar: 64 })` on the band bar; a pick calls `scrollUnderBar()`.
  - A due row's amount is the usual one, drawn muted (`.ui-home-act-due`); a late row uses `tone="warning"` and says "איחר"; a due one "צפוי ב־<day>". Income rows sign + in green (`sign="in"`), expenses carry no minus (`sign="cost"`).
  - A failed list read says "רשימת התנועות לא נטענה" with `FailureLine` and keeps whatever else loaded; the band stays. An empty filter says so in words (`EmptyState`).
  - The month drawer's next-month cell (state `next`, dashed, `aria-pressed`) shows that month's forecast card above the grid; months beyond it stay "עוד לא".
