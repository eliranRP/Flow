# Project page opens on the project's cash

- PR: #504 (Project page thread).
- Kind: screen
- Changed: a project opened on its profit with a period pill, and its cash, investment and loans each took more taps. It now opens like Home: the band shows this month's תזרים for the project, then נכנס, יצא and רווח החודש rows, one "השקעה והלוואות" row, and חודשים קודמים. נכנס or יצא opens the project's own lines (a shared bill shows the project's share only); an earlier month opens its own page; רווח החודש opens the profit page, which keeps the period pill. The investment page is now "השקעה והלוואות" and lists the loans under the investment figures.
- Rule: a project's main page is its cash, laid out as Home's; profit, investment and loans are one tap away, never on the first screen.
