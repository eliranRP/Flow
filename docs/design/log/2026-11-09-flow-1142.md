# FLOW-1142: utility refunds: each bill and payment opens its transaction

- PR: the FLOW-1142 PR
- Kind: navigation
- Changed:
  - `/utility-refunds`: each leg of a suggestion card (each bill, then the payment) is a link to `/transactions/:id`, the whole leg is the tap target (`--touch-min`), with a 16px chevron in `--color-text-muted` at its end, press scale 0.97 over `--motion-press`, and a focus ring.
  - A payment that refunds several bills shows one leg per bill (supplier, category · dd/mm/yy, amount) in place of one joined leg, so each bill can be opened.
  - The bill's sheet: פתיחת התנועה (a link button) under הוחזר · בחירת התשלום.
  - A viewer's bill row links to the bill's transaction.
  - The payment leg's line names the project the payment is filed to ("<project> · <category> · dd/mm/yy · N ימים אחרי"; "בלי פרויקט" when it has none). A payment split by category lists its parts under its leg, inside the card: a "פיצול" head, then one caption line per part in the secondary colour ("<category> · <project, only when it is not the payment's> · <amount>"), the amount in an LTR `bdi`; the lines wrap at 320 and are never cut. The new `project_name` and `parts` of list_utility_refunds' payment are optional in the schema (null and []), so the screen works before the server sends them.
  - Each leg link's accessible name is what the leg shows (name, line, amount) plus "פתיחת התנועה" (WCAG 2.5.3, label in name).
- Rule:
  - A payment split by category shows its parts where the payment is shown, as caption lines under it; a part names its project only when it differs from the payment's.
  - A link's aria-label contains its visible text.
  - A row that names one transaction on a review screen opens that transaction's page, so the owner can verify before answering.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-09, on the live החזרי חשבונות screen; FLOW-1142.
