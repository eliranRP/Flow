- Rule: A line's journal entry is "פקודת יומן": a quiet accent link with the book icon and a turning chevron at the bottom of the line page opens it in place as a 16px surface card; a line with a leg outside the P&L shows the card open with the book in its head and no link. The card lists חובה, then זכות (13px muted side heads over hairlines), each leg a 52px row: the account (16px, an English bank name left to right with its last 4 never cut) over "type · project" (13px muted), the amount at the end unsigned; a leg outside the P&L fades its name and amount to 0.6 and adds "· [eye-off] לא נספר ברווח" to its meta. The footer is a check icon and "מאוזן · $X בחובה ובזכות". An entry an MCP write booked carries "✦ נרשם ע״י Jev", a 12px accent-text badge with a tint-strong outline, in the card's head. A part of a split that nets against the line is a credit, "זיכוי" (never "גרוס-אפ"): off, the quiet "סמנו כזיכוי"; on, a filled tint-strong "✓ זיכוי" chip and the part's card in the tint-strong outline; its note says "יורד מהסכום" and, when its category also has a normal part, "נטו ב<category>: +$X זיכוי" (the normal part's note then starts "עלות ·"); the header says "✓ תואם לשורה · a − b + c = line" once such a split adds up. Money in that is not income (a loan received) reads "+" in the text colour; green stays for income. Money terms: escrow (אסקרו) is an asset kept out of profit, not an expense — the "Loan parts" row becomes "Principal and escrow are not expenses (principal a liability, escrow an asset). Interest is." In the P&L by category, the kept-out categories are a "לא נספר ברווח" group after the counted ones (its head names how many categories), each row "<category> · <נכס | התחייבות>" with the name and amount faded and the type muted, no share bar, opening to its lines faded as kept-out rows.
