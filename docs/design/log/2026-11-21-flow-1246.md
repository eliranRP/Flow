# FLOW-1246: A late row names its one bill or income

- PR: #1238
- Kind: copy
- Changed: `forecast.ts` (`lateItemName` returns the party's name when a list holds exactly one late item of a direction; `partyName`'s fallback moves into a helper both use), `screens/attention-rows.tsx` (`missingName` and `missingIncomeName`; with a count of 1 and a name, the title reads "החשבון של {party} לא הגיע" or "התנועה הקבועה של {party} לא הגיעה", the party in `<bdi>`), `screens/HomeScreen.tsx` and `screens/project-attention.tsx` (pass the names from the same list the count comes from).
- Rule: hierarchy/copy: a row says what it is. Two rows that read alike ("חשבון אחד לא הגיע", "תנועה קבועה אחת לא הגיעה") name their item when there is exactly one. Two or more keep the count, so the wording does not change when the number grows.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-21 (the screenshot story: a utility bill and a rent payment looked like the same item). Task `docs/backlog/tasks/FLOW-1246.md`.
- Unchanged: `short` ("לא הגיעו"), `tag` ("חשבון אחד", "הכנסה אחת"), `dismissKey` (`missing:N`, so existing dismissals still hold), and the row's link.
- Name wording: "של {name}" follows the app's existing "של {name}" titles (the business in invites, the company on the band). The name is an invented sample in stories and tests ("מים טובים", "שוכר לדוגמה"). The name is wrapped in `<bdi class="ui-attn-name">`.
- Long names (the owner's decision, 2026-11-21, "Shorten the name"): the name is one line in its own box that ends in "…" when it is long, capped at the text's width less 5.5em (the verb's), so the title is 2 lines at most and always ends with "לא הגיע" / "לא הגיעה". The 2-line row rule in DESIGN-RULES (a row whose name is its identity wraps up to 2 lines) holds. A named row's card grows to 2 lines and its list item's cap is the finite `touch-min + space-8 + space-1` (80px, the 2-line card plus its gap), so the dismiss fold still animates from a height to 0; a cap of `none` cannot. The count rows and the under-360px short title are unchanged.
- Design review: done after shots at 360 and 393, light and dark, of `screens-routes--home-late-named` and `screens-routes--home-late-named-long`: worst-case names (32 wide letters, 38 digits, long words, Latin) stay at 2 lines with the verb visible; the dismiss fold samples 70px to 0 over about 230ms.
