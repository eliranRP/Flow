# FLOW-1124: דוח שותף counts each property's all-in and each member's own loans

- PR: #1083
- Kind: pattern
- Changed:
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-format.ts`:
    - `saleFormula` takes the member's whole share. Its rows are now:
      - value − selling − the banks' loans;
      - "החזר הלוואות שותפים" when any member lent the company;
      - = הון נקי, then the member's part;
      - when members lent the company, "+ הלוואות של X" and "= סה״כ ל־X".
    - The band shows the member's total ("סה״כ ל־X אם מוכרים הכל"). When the member lent the company, its line under the figure shows their equity and their loans apart.
    - `profitFormula`: value − selling − עלות כוללת (purchase, renovation, holding, as the investment page's net profit), then = רווח and the member's part. It counts only the properties with a purchase.
    - Each property's sheet shows its member loan apart, and `propertyProfitFormula` its profit against the all-in.
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-parts.tsx`: new drills.
    - The members' loans: each loan with its member and property.
    - Each member's total: their equity part + their loans.
    - The profit's value, selling, all-in (purchase + renovation + holding), profit and each member's part.
    - The banks' loans leave a member's loan out, and say so.
    - The net lists a member's loan to the company as a minus line.
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-screen.tsx`: "רווח מול העלות הכוללת", a second calculation card on מכירת הכל. When properties have no purchase, it names them under the card ("לא ברווח: … (אין מחיר קנייה)"). A property's row hint ends with its profit.
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-sheets.tsx`: the members sheet lists the company's open loans under "הלוואות שותפים לחברה", demand loans first. Each loan has a select: "לא של שותף" or a member. A loan is one member's.
  - `app/src/screens/reports-hub-screen.tsx`: the hub card shows the first member's total, and the members' equity as "הון נקי לכולם".
