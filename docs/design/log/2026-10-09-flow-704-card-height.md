# FLOW-704 and FLOW-352 1: The review card keeps its height when Jev settles, and values wrap at 320

- PR: #380
- Kind: component
- Changed: `ui/review-card.tsx` (the held ✦ line while `pending`), `css/17-action-bar.css` (`.ui-review-reason-slot`; in the narrow-card rule the pill wraps under a long value, the value clamps at two lines, and the ✦ line drops its indent), `screens/jev-review-card.tsx` (the wait-and-settle harness: a fill with a reason, an auto fill with ביטול, one row filled, stored rows). Stories: Screens/Jev review "Wait and settle" at 390, 320 and dark 320. e2e: `jev-review.spec.ts` checks waiting and settled heights match at 390 and 320.
- Rule: while Jev's read waits, a review card holds the "✦" line under its rows (hidden, one hint line) whenever a row shows a skeleton, so the reason or "מולא ע״י Jev" line lands without moving the card or the action bar. A card whose rows are all stored holds nothing, since Jev fills nothing there. A split_mismatch card never holds it.
- Rule: on a phone-width card (container under 25rem) the ✦ line starts at the card's start edge, under the labels, instead of in the value column. Jev's longest reason ("כמו ב־10 מתוך 12 הפעמים האחרונות") then stays on one line at 320, the line the card holds while it waits; in the value column it took two lines and the card still grew 19px. From 25rem up it keeps the value column.
- Trade-off: when Jev answers nothing for a waiting row, the held line goes and the card closes up by one hint line. That case is rarer than a fill, and with both fields empty the "בחרו פרויקט וקטגוריה" line takes the space.
- Rule: At phone width, a value that doesn't fit beside its pill takes the row's width, and the pill drops under it on the chevron side.
- Detail (FLOW-352 1): on a phone-width card (the review container under 25rem) a value that doesn't fit beside its pill takes the row's width and the pill drops under it, still on the chevron side; the value wraps to a second line before it ends in an ellipsis. Beside a הצעה pill at 320 only about 52px were left, so a plain two-line wrap still cut "וילה הדקלים" to "וילה / הדקל...". A short value keeps its pill on the same line. Stories: Components/ReviewCard "Stress: value on two lines beside הצעה" at 320 and dark 320. The 320 e2e for a long Jev value checks two lines, then the cut.
- Trade-off (FLOW-352 1 with FLOW-704): a skeleton row is one line, so at 320 a Jev value long enough to wrap (about 14 letters beside the pill) still grows the card by one title line when it lands. Cutting it to one line instead was the cycle 11 finding, so the wrap wins. A shared cost holds no ✦ line, since Jev fills no project there.
