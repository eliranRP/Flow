- Rule: The setup is seven steps: business, connect the bank or books, projects, loans, smart tagging, the first approval, and Home screen install. Each step has one main button, a back link (not on step 1), the counter "שלב n מתוך 7", skip-all in the top row, and an optional step skip. Welcome and done are full screens with no counter. Skip-all opens a sheet and goes to Home. Motion: the movie loops three scenes every 2.4 seconds, pauses on request or when hidden or off screen, and under reduced motion shows the last scene without a loop or a button.
