# FLOW-1303: the splash ends when Home's figures are drawn

- PR: see the task, `docs/backlog/tasks/FLOW-1303.md`
- Kind: behaviour, motion
- Changed: `ui/splash.tsx` (`Splash` ends on a ready signal instead of a fixed clock; the rest of the fill is played faster when the data is early), `ui/splash-ready.ts` (new: the DOM watch for Home's first figures), stories "Data ready early" and "Data late (failsafe)".
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-24. The question: "On a real phone the splash screen covers about 2.9 s of every cold start, which hides kept data that is ready at about 0.3 s. Should the splash change?" The owner chose **"End when data is ready"**: the splash fades as soon as Home's kept data has drawn, with a minimum of about 0.8 s.
- Rule: the splash is ready when Home's figures (`.ui-band-body .ui-num`) are in the DOM with no skeleton, or the app shows a route other than Home (a signed-out start lands on the login screen), or an error state is up. It stays at least 800ms, then fades for 160ms (`--motion-toast-out`). The 3s failsafe is the most: the splash is gone by then, data or not.
- Motion: when the data is ready before the animation's own end (2.7s), the rest of the fill, the wave and the wordmark are played faster, so they arrive in 400ms (or by the 800ms mark), then the fade starts. Only the playback rate changes, so the motion is continuous and never cut off mid-wave. Still only transform and opacity animate; the tokens are unchanged.
- Numbers: data at 0.3s: the fade starts at 0.8s, the splash is gone at 0.96s. Data at 1.5s: the fade starts at 1.9s, gone at 2.06s. No data: the fade starts at 2.84s, gone at 3.0s. Before: about 2.9s every time.
- Exception to DESIGN-RULES §2.7 unchanged: the wave runs 4 cycles during the rise and stops (the owner's pick of 2026-11-23); a faster fill plays fewer of them.
- Kept: a tap or a key skips it with the quick fade, reduced motion (the finished icon for 400ms, and it does not wait for data), `aria-hidden`, no focus, never under `navigator.webdriver`, the page-colour cover and its 3s failsafe for a chunk that never arrives.
- Cost: one `MutationObserver` on the body, with no polling and no timer; its callback runs one class lookup per batch of DOM changes (the busy scan only once figures exist), and it is disconnected when the splash ends. It lives in the splash's lazy chunk, so Home's entry carries nothing for it. An event from Home was rejected: it would add bytes to the Home entry and code to Home's first render.
- Known limits: a persistent `aria-busy=true` on Home (none today) would hold the splash to the failsafe. An empty Home (a company with no entries yet) draws its hero figure and counts as drawn. Under reduced motion the splash keeps its 400ms hold and does not wait for data.
- Unchanged: Home's first render and reads (`startHomeReads`), `splash-boot.ts`, the CSS and the motion tokens, route changes, the e2e suite.
