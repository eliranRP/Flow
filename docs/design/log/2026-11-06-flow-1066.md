- Rule:
  - The forecast covers 12 months after the current one (decision 0260). Every month of it is a future month on Home, with next month's composition: the band (title "תזרים צפוי · <month>", the expected cash net, צפוי להיכנס and צפוי לצאת with their lines), פירוט ועריכה, and "הצפוי ב<month>" by project.
  - The band's later arrow stays on through the forecast's last month and is off there; the earlier arrow on a future month goes one month back. A preview has no forecast and stops at the current month.
  - On a month after next the caption reads "הערכה · לפי קבועים, הלוואות וממוצע 6 חודשים" (the band's caption style), and the line under צפוי לצאת reads "קבועים וממוצעים": there is no comparison with this month's cash.
  - On every future month the פירוט ועריכה row shows the month's expected profit (profit terms: income less expenses, no loan principal) in the forecast row's value slot, `Amount` size list, a band skeleton while it is read and nothing once the read failed. Its line reads "רווח צפוי · הצפי של כל פרויקט ל<month>"; the link's accessible name is "פירוט ועריכה, רווח צפוי ב<month> <amount>". The band's big figure stays the cash net.
  - In the month drawer, every month of the forecast is a dashed צפי cell that picks like any month, the year arrows reaching the last month's year; a month past the forecast keeps "עוד לא", now with a solid line and no fill, disabled.
  - Every arrow on a future month (the pill's, the compact bar's, the פירוט ועריכה chevron, the drawer's years) is `OutwardChevron` as on the current month, pointing outward; never a text character.
  - `/forecast/next`, a project's צפי page and its edit and add pages take `?month=` for a month of the forecast; their titles name it ("צפי מרץ 2027") and their links carry it. Next month needs no parameter.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-06 ("I want to see the forecast revenue on any future month"), approved with the mockup: 12 months, build as the mockup, editing on any future month; and "Expected profit (Recommended)" for the פירוט ועריכה row, with the arrows pointing outward as on the current month.
