# FLOW-1024: a sheet stays on the screen with the iPhone keyboard up

- PR: #953
- Kind: behaviour
- Changed: `app/src/ui/sheet.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/08-tabbar-empty.css`, `app/src/ui/css/02-fields-sheets.css`, `app/src/ui/keyboard-inset.ts`.
- Rule: an open sheet's bottom edge sits on the top of the keyboard, on every platform. The app lifts it by `--kb` (the space below the visual viewport), never by the keyboard's full height, so iOS panning the page to a field does not lift it a second time.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-03, iPhone screenshots of a money sheet whose top went above the status bar.
