- PR: #826
- Kind: screen, component, pattern
- Changed: `app/src/screens/cash-lines-screen.tsx` (the side tabs, the order chips, the review button, the failed card, the offline line, the empty card), `app/src/screens/cash-lines-parts.tsx` (`LinesOrderChips` new, `CashLinesEmpty`, `LinesCount` without the waiting part), `app/src/screens/transaction-screen.tsx` (toasts under the bar, the rule offer), `app/src/screens/transaction-rule-offer.tsx` (new), `app/src/screens/transaction-top-bar.tsx` (`toastCeiling`, as #824 adds it), `app/src/ui/band-states.tsx` (`PageLoadFailed`, `PageOfflineLine` new: a page without the band), `app/src/ui/screen-state.tsx` (`failed`), `app/src/ui/segmented-control.tsx` (`role="tab"`), `app/src/ui/css/03-controls.css` (chip edges, the selected tab), `10-settings-onboarding.css` (the off switch's edge), `36-cash.css`, `07-band-states.css`, `59-txn-rule-offer.css` (new).
- Rule:
  - Edges meet 3:1. An unselected chip's edge is `--color-control-border` (3.7:1 dark, 3.2:1 light on the page; the tint was 1.4:1), app-wide. An off switch keeps its fill and gains a 1.5px inset edge in `--color-control-off` (3.4:1 on the card both themes; the fill alone was 1.4:1), app-wide.
  - The lines page's order chips are 44px (were 36px), 15/600, and one choice: a radio group with arrow keys and one tab stop. The נכנס / יצא switch is a tab list: the two sides are two views of the month.
  - Lines waiting for approval are Home v2's filled button under the total ("לאישור 2 פריטים · כדקה"); the count line keeps "N תנועות · $X לא נספרים ברווח".
  - A failed read hides the figures: a card says "יצא של <month> לא נטען" (or "אין חיבור"), what happened, נסו שוב and צריך עזרה?. Offline with lines on screen, a line under the total says "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM".
  - An empty side says "עוד לא נכנס כסף ב<month>" while the month runs ("לא נכנס…" for a past one), with one filled button to the other side ("מה יצא ב<month>").
  - On the transaction page, the project and category toast, the counts-in switches' toasts and the rule's toasts hang under the bar with ביטול for 5s (decision 0216).
  - After a category change on a supplier's expense line, one card under the rows offers the supplier's next lines: "להכניס גם את השורות הבאות מ־<supplier> לקטגוריה <category>? שורות קודמות לא משתנות." with כן, מעכשיו and רק השורה הזו, outlined 44px answers of equal weight on the tint (edges in `--color-text-muted`: 4.9:1 dark, 4.1:1 light; the control border is 2.8:1 there).
- Source: owner approval of the Lines v2 boards on 2026-10-26 (design canvas, the Lines row), and its owner call on the rule offer. Decision [0216](../../decisions/0216-lines-toasts-and-supplier-rule.md).
- Departures from the approved board:
  - The type sizes and weights stay the app's tokens (FLOW-959 owns the app-wide change); only the chips' size moves.
  - The rule offer comes after a category change only. A sync never gives a new line a remembered project, so a project offer would promise nothing (0216 §5).
  - The invoice card is not a new viewer: the app's card already opens the document (a SUMIT page, not an image), so it was never dead; the board's "tap to zoom" was the board's own. The bank description is already 13px and not monospace in the app. The touch targets of the side switch and the chips already reached 44px through their hit areas; the chips now draw 44px too.
  - The transaction page's history chart (its takeaway title, its label and its past bars) and "לא קבוע" last in the frequency sheet are FLOW-963's files (#825, `related-charges.tsx` and `pace.ts`), which already make those changes; this PR does not touch them.
  - The tab bar's pill is FLOW-958's, already in.
