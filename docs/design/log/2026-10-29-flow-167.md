# FLOW-167 + FLOW-168: a description a search found, and a long project in a row's hint

- PR: #865
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/list-row.tsx` (the one-line hint's parts; `wrapTitle` and `titleFull` props), `app/src/ui/css/02-fields-sheets.css` (`.ui-row-title-wrap`, the row hint's `.ui-hint-part`, `.ui-hint-sep`, `.ui-hint-text`), `app/src/screens/report-lines-screen.tsx`.
- Rule:
  - A report's category lines, searched: a line found only by its description shows the description as its title and the date and supplier in the hint. The title wraps up to three lines, so the matched word stays in view at 320; a Latin description reads left to right, a Hebrew one right to left. When the first matched word starts more than 24 characters in, the title starts at the word boundary two words before it, after "…" ("…parts, labour, inspection 120"); screen readers get the whole description, in a hidden span after the title. Before, the description was one hint part, and a Latin one was cut at its start ("…lacement, unit 2 · 18/10").
  - A list row's one-line hint ("18/10 · <project>"): whole parts in order, then the parts past the first that do not fit are cut with "…" while a few letters of each still fit (4em, the "·" included), or dropped when less room is left. With three parts whose 2nd and 3rd both have letters, both can be cut on one line (story ListRow · Two Cut Parts, at 393). Before, that part wrapped to the hidden second line, so a long project name vanished and only the date showed. A part with less room than that still drops whole; a date or an amount never shortens; a first part too long on its own is cut, as before. Each part's text takes its own direction, so a Latin project name is cut at its end.
  - For the design lead:
    - DESIGN-RULES still says a transaction row's hint part "drops with its ·" (FLOW-124) and "a hint part shows whole or not at all" (FLOW-339 Search, DESIGN-RULES line 413); both need this rule's exception when they are folded in.
    - The cut can leave a stub of 2 to 4 letters and "…" at 320: "השקע…" (month lines, a kept line's category), "חשמל ו…", "תיקונים ות…", "הנהלת חשבונ…" (the project month by date), "משרד החברה…" (by amount), "תשלום ה…" (ListRow · Transaction Tags). DESIGN-RULES 413 said a part shows whole or not at all, so decide whether 4em is the right floor, or whether a later part should drop when an earlier one was cut.
    - Two cut parts on one line is the current behaviour; if only one part should be cut, the parts after the cut one need to drop.
    - The three-line cap and the snippet that starts near the match are the coordinator's call for the owner (keep building, 2026-10-29); DESIGN-RULES caps a name at 2 lines (FLOW-339 loans) and places text by layout, not by counting characters (FLOW-310), so both need a ruling.
