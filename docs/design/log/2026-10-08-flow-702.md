# FLOW-702: Jev mode and threshold, and the auto fill on the review card

- PR: #231
- Kind: screen
- Changed: the Jev card's אפשרויות panel (app/src/screens/jev-settings.tsx) now holds a "מצב" segmented control (הצעות בלבד / מילוי אוטומטי) with its hint, and, in auto mode only, a "סף ביטחון" segmented control (80% / 85% / 90% / 95%, 90% by default). A stored threshold that is not one of the four selects none and says "הסף כרגע N%". Both controls are disabled while a save runs and for a viewer. The status line reads "פעיל · הצעות בלבד" or "פעיל · מילוי אוטומטי". New `.ui-jev-options-panel` block in css/10-settings-onboarding.css; stories OnAuto (+ dark, 320) and OnAutoCustomThreshold
- Changed (card, agreed with UI lane 2): `ReviewCard` (app/src/ui/review-card.tsx) gains `jevFilled`: when the auto job's fill still stands on the stored row, a line "✦ מולא ע״י Jev" sits under the rows, above the reason line, with a בטל TextLink at its end (44px target, busy while the undo runs; a viewer gets the label alone). בטל calls `undo_jev_prefill`; the line stays in לאישור with its values before and Jev fills nothing more on it. The undo toast and its refusals sit above the bar. A flagged line the job held shows its flag and Jev's suggestion, with no label. New `.ui-review-filled` block in css/17-action-bar.css; ReviewCard stories "Jev filled" (undo, dark, 320, running, viewer, with a quiet flag at 320)
- Rule: A setting with two to four short fixed values that sits inside a card uses SegmentedControl, applies on tap and saves at once (no confirm). A dependent setting appears under the control that enables it and is hidden, not disabled, while that control's value makes it meaningless. A filled value that an automatic job wrote says so on the card in one hint line with its undo as a text link at the end; the undo needs no confirm, and its toast says what happened. The plan's "chips" for the threshold are a SegmentedControl, for radio semantics and to match the mode control.
