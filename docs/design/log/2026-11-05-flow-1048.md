- Rule:
  - A Detail page has its own Back in the page (`BackButton`, wrapped in `data-toast-ceiling` so top toasts hang under it) and a `CompactBar size="detail"` with the name and the figure, shown by `useScrolledPast(head, true, DETAIL_BAR)` on the page's head. No screen keeps a bar of its own.
  - A List screen whose segments dock renders them once in the page in a `DockSlot` under a `ui-compact-mark`, and the same nodes in the bar's `dock`; two segments (side and currency) dock together.
  - A late recurring charge is both a "לא הגיעו" card (hidden per device, keyed `late:<alert key>`) and a row in its group with `StatusMark late` and its lateness in words, so hiding the card never hides the charge.
  - The recurring-or-one-off order needs FLOW-1035's flag on every line (`linesKnowRecurring`); without it the chip is hidden and `?order=recurring` orders by date.
  - Decision 0257 (the board's px win): קבועים has the board's two month cards (עוד לשלם, עוד לקבל: 20 corners, 14/16 inside, a 14 label, a 28 figure, a 13 note) and its הכול / הוצאות / הכנסות segment, which docks into the 56px bar; a List or Detail head is one column 4px apart under the safe area plus 8px (Back 44, title 32/600 or name 24/600, line 14); a Detail status line is 14/500 and its figure 40; the lines' count joins the month line.
  - A reversible write on a Detail page shows the 5s toast with ביטול (`topToast`): approve reopens the review, a match answer takes the answer back, an ignore restores the alert.
