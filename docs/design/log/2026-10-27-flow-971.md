- Rule:
  - Search: the field is a 48px pill; its clear, the active chips, the sheet's segments and project chips are 44px targets. The placeholder is muted text at 4.5:1. The sheet's project chips are one choice, a radio group with arrow keys and one tab stop. The review switch's off track is the 3:1 outline colour.
  - Search: a failed search shows Home v2's failed card ("החיפוש לא הצליח", נסו שוב, צריך עזרה?). Offline, a line under the field says "אין חיבור · מחפש רק במה שכבר נטען", and the search runs on the lines earlier searches read, by the server's rules.
  - Every sheet's grab handle is at 3:1 (`--sheet-grab`, light #8F879F, dark #6F6787). The token also draws the onboarding chip's dashed border (`setup.css`), which moves with it.
  - כל הפריטים לאישור: the title at 32 and its line at 15 ("6 פריטים · 1 בלי פרויקט"), Home v2's filled button ("לאישור הפריט הראשון · כ־2 דקות לכולם"), day heads with each day's signed total, tabular; a row has the initials, the payee over "project · category", and the signed amount. A line with no project keeps Review v2's mark. As the board draws it, the row drops the old source icon (בנק/חשבונית), the date on the meta line (it moved to the day head) and the chevron; a name with no letter still shows the source icon in the initials circle. Jev's bookings sit under their own day, first in it. A day or month of another year carries its year. Totals are one figure per currency, shekels first; on שויכו היום a line kept out of profit adds nothing.
  - A project's לאישור: Back names the project, the line under the title says how many wait, the total waiting is the page's figure, and the filled button opens the first line. The month head shows its count and total even when it is the only month.
  - שויכו היום: Back reads לאישור, the line says "N שורות שויכו היום · אפשר לשנות כל אחת", the day's total, the group heads tabular.
  - The three lists keep their page on a failed read (Home v2's card) and say offline which moment they show; an empty list is a card with one way on.
  - The change sheet on לאישור saves on the pick with ביטול for 5s (its writes and ביטול run in one scope, one after another; ביטול brings the line back as the queue's head), names each missing field under its row with a red edge, and its remember line names the supplier and the category (decision 0221). Its links (פרויקט חדש, the split links, פיצול לפי קטגוריות) are 44px.

  - The remember line does not promise the project or "אפשר לבטל בהגדרות": a sync applies a remembered category only, and no settings page lists supplier rules (decision 0221, open for the owner).
  - The day heads read "היום • 11/10" with the bullet the month lines use (FLOW-965), not the board's "·".
  - A whole amount keeps no ".00" (owner, 2026-10-10), so the rows read "+$100", not the board's "+$100.00".
  - The filed groups keep their count and income and expense figures (FLOW-347), now tabular, rather than one signed figure.
  - The full list's empty card does not name Mercury: not every company has it connected. It replaces the queue's done frame (ReviewEmpty) on the list, as the board draws it; the queue keeps its done frame.
  - The tab pill (L8) was already done in Home v2 (#819).
