# FLOW-1045: Reports v3: the hub, profit and loss by category, project and month with a drill-down, the balance sheet by project

- PR: #984
- Kind: screen
- Changed: `/reports` is the hub (`reports-hub-screen.tsx`, Hub pattern); `/reports/pnl` (`reports-screen.tsx`) and `/reports/balance` (new `reports-balance-screen.tsx`) are the two reports (Overview pattern with a drill-down); their routes and views in the new `app/src/report-views.ts`; `ReportShell` in `reports-parts.tsx` composes `TopBand`, the band `CompactBar` with `dock`, `useDockedFilter` and `DockSlot`; lists are `GroupHead summary` and `ListRow`; the review count is an `AttentionCards` row. CSS: the v3 blocks at the end of `61-reports.css` (`.ui-rep-v3`, `.ui-rep-band*`, `.ui-rep-pill*`, `.ui-rep-page`, `.ui-rep-hub*`); removed the old switch, chips, tools, by-project cards and kept-out row blocks. Removed: the two-report segmented control, the projects chip and its sheet (`ReportProjectSheet`), `ByProjectView`, `StatementView`, `BalanceView`, the collapsed-section memory (`flow.reports.collapsed.*`).
- Rule:
  - A report's band (Overview, BUILD-PLAN §1.1): `TopBand` with no wordmark, Back (`‹ דוחות`, or `‹ לפי פרויקט` in a drill-down) as `leading`, share as `trailing`; in the hero the name (`t-name`), the period or date pill (the band track, 48px, opens its sheet), the figure (`ui-band-title` label, hero `Amount`, compact from 100K), one secondary line, then two 44px rows (income and expenses, or assets and liabilities). The whole hero fades with `ui-band-fade`.
  - The band bar is `CompactBar tone="band"` with `start` Back, `centre` the name · period and the figure (opens the same sheet) and `end` share or a 44px spacer; it shows on `useBandGone()`. The view control is one `SegmentedControl` drawn twice, `tone="band"` in the bar's `dock` and `tone="page"` in `DockSlot`, through `useDockedFilter({ barShown, bar: 64 })`; a pick calls `scrollUnderBar()`.
  - A drill-down stays on the screen: the URL gains `project=` or `month=` (pushed, with `state.reportDrill`), the band takes the row's name and figure with "מתוך <whole>" under it, the view control goes, the list is the row's own groups, and the last row goes on ("לדף הפרויקט", "תנועות החודש"). Back pops when it came from the list, else replaces to the view.
  - A breakdown adds up to its band: the general expenses that belong to no project are their own group ("כלליות · לא פרויקט"), and the balance sheet by project ends with "בנק ושאר" (the company less its properties). The bottom line under the groups is their own sum.
  - Hub cards are `Card` with one `Link` inside (the whole card opens the report): name and chevron, the label and figure (`t-card`), an `aria-hidden` mini chart (month bars, the last in the accent; or the assets split, loans against equity), two facts over a hairline. Then "פירוקים", `ListRow variant="item"` with an icon, hint and chevron.
