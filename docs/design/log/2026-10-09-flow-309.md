# FLOW-309: a steady review counter, focus that follows the queue, and the short-phone check

- PR: #304
- Kind: component
- Changed: `screens/review-count.tsx` (new) and `css/06-review-card.css`: each number of the queue counter ("1 מתוך 12") uses tabular, lining digits and reserves the total's digits, two at least, in a hidden `::before`, with its digits hugging "מתוך" and the spare room on the outer side. At 320 the counter and the progress bar stayed put from 1 to 10 of 12 (the bar's edge jumped 8px at 9→10 before). Only a total that grows from 99 to 100 (new lines arriving) still widens it once. `screens/review-queue.tsx`, `review-focus.ts` (new): after a successful אישור or דלג with focus in the action bar, focus goes to the next card's first button, or to the empty state's לדף הבית when the queue empties; ביטול pressed on the toast brings it back to the returning card's אישור. A tap that never focused the bar (iPhone) moves nothing, and on Android a tap's focus draws no ring (`:focus-visible` stays off). The header's own title focus no longer counts as "focus left the bar". `css/06-review-card.css`: the filed-today banner sits 8px under the counter row (was 4) on phones taller than 720px; the short-phone block keeps 4. `screens/review-swap.ts` (new): a card that comes back while it is leaving settles with אישור enabled (it stuck in "out" and disabled), a change to any field of the card on screen shows at once (only eight fields did), and no ref is written during render. Measured at 320x693 with the worst case (a supplier on two lines, Jev's reason on two lines, the plural banner on two lines): the card ends 21px above the pinned bar, and אישור, שינוי and דלג end 68, 8 and 8px above the tab bar; 375x667: 47px, and 68, 8, 8px. Both unchanged by this PR: the pinned bar (FLOW-327) and the short-phone trims (#278, #296) already fit it; the page scrolls 10px at 320x693 under the bar. Stories: Screens/Routes Review Fit 320 Short (+ dark), Review Counter 10 At 320 (+ dark); a `flow320-short` (320x693) viewport.
- Rule: A counter whose digits change in place (the review queue's "N מתוך M") uses tabular digits and reserves the total's digits, two at least, so nothing beside it moves. When a queue's card leaves with focus in its action bar, focus goes to the next card's first button, or to the empty state's action; a tap that did not focus the bar moves nothing. Spacing between the review queue's blocks stays on the 8px step; only the short-phone block (up to 720px tall) trims it.
- Source: FLOW-309 (follow-ups from the FLOW-327 reviews); design session in UI lane 2.
