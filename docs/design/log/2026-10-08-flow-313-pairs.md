# FLOW-313 follow-up: Month bar currency pairs stay together

- PR: #246
- Kind: component
- Changed: `MonthList` month bar (design lead sign-off after #242). Each currency's pair of figures stays on one line with 8px inside it, and the currencies are 24px apart, so ₪ and $ pairs read apart. When both pairs don't fit, the $ pair wraps under the ₪ pair, start-aligned. When the totals don't fit beside the month name (an ordinary ILS month at 320px, or nine-digit figures), they drop to a row under the name instead of splitting a pair, starting under the name (beside the name they sit at the far end): 65px at 320 for a one-currency month (was 61px with a split pair), 87px for nine-digit figures in two currencies (was 84px). No figure overflows at 320, 360 or 390. Scroll padding now allows for the tallest bar (month name plus three currency rows, about 110px).
- Rule: A currency's figures are one unit: never split a pair; currencies sit 24px apart (8px inside a pair), and a pair that doesn't fit wraps whole to the next line, start-aligned.
- Source: FLOW-313 item 4 follow-up, design lead review of #242.
