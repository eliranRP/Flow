# FLOW-169 + FLOW-170: report search rows, the drill-down snippet, the balance date caption

- PR: #868
- Kind: pattern
- Changed: `app/src/search-reports.ts`, `app/src/screens/search.tsx` (the report rows), `app/src/screens/report-lines-snippet.tsx` (new: `SearchSnippet`), `app/src/screens/report-lines-screen.tsx` (`lineText`), `app/src/ui/list-row.tsx` (a snippet title takes its whole text's direction), `app/src/screens/reports-sheets.tsx` (`BalanceDateSheet`).
- Rule:
  - Search's report rows: a report's word with a Hebrew prefix letter (ה ב ל ו מ, one or two) or with punctuation around it still gives its row ("המאזן", "מאזן?", "רווח והפסד, Cedar"), and so does a project's name with one ("מאזן לשיפוץ"). English "balance" gives the balance sheet only as the whole query or in "balance sheet". With a project chip on, the rows open on that project ("מאזן · <project>"), unless the text names another one.
  - Reports drill-down, a line found by its description: the title (three lines) is placed by layout, not by counting characters (FLOW-310). The whole description shows when every matched word is within the three lines; otherwise a cut before a match, from the earliest (two words before it, then at its own word), and the first cut that shows every matched word wins; if none does, the earliest one that shows the most. A description with no spaces is cut after the nearest / , - _ . : ; | or at the match. This replaces FLOW-167's 24-character threshold.
  - Same screen: when a word matched the project, the hint keeps it, after the supplier, which names the line: "18/10 · Quick Fix Plumbing · Cedar St" (coordinator's call in the review of #868, under the owner's "keep building"; the first build put the project first, and at 320 the supplier then dropped, undoing FLOW-167). Each part follows FLOW-168: whole, or cut with "…" while 4em of it fits, else dropped. At 393 both show. At 320 beside a dollar amount the hint is about 110px, the date and the supplier at 4em fill it, so the project drops to the hidden line and the matched word is not drawn there (screen readers still get it).
  - The balance sheet's date sheet (נכון לתאריך): one caption under the dates and under the calendar for another date, in the reports' caption style (`.ui-rep-note`, as "דוח ניהולי, לא דוח כספי מבוקר."; under the calendar the column's gap spaces it), linked to the dates with `aria-describedby`: "המאזן כולל את כל התנועות מההתחלה ועד התאריך שבוחרים". No "from the start" choice: every date already counts from the start.
  - With the search's "בלי פרויקט" (no project) chip on, the report rows open the all-projects report: the reports have no "no project" view.
