# FLOW-1183 · Five-token foundation

- **Kind:** system
- **Rule:** Two shadows only: `--shadow-sm` for cards and surfaces, `--shadow-lg` for floating layers (toasts, the sync pill, menus, sheets, popovers); no other shadow.
- **Rule:** Every spacing value is on the 4-pt grid through a space token, with no `calc()` arithmetic; the 2, 6, 10, 14 and 18px steps are retired; hairlines, borders and focus rings are not spacing.
- **Rule:** The 12px label is `--type-label-size`; the role scale stays.
- **Rule:** Where a rounded control sits in a tight padded rounded track, outer radius = inner radius + padding.

- **Pages:** none yet (tokens, rules and the skill only; each screen area adopts them in its own PR).
