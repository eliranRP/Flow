# FLOW-171: The account page's period, and long Latin names that wrap

- PR: #869
- Kind: screen, component
- Changed: `app/src/screens/report-account-screen.tsx` (the period choice, the year headings), `app/src/screens/reports-parts.tsx` (`ReportRow`'s name), `app/src/ui/screen-header.tsx` (the stacked title), `app/src/ui/slash-breaks.tsx` (new), `app/src/ui/css/61-reports.css` (`.ui-rep-account-span`, `.ui-rep-account-year-*`, the reports' stacked title).
- Rule:
  - A balance-sheet account's page puts a two-option `SegmentedControl` (input radius, 44px segments) between its balance and its summary rows: "השנה", or the year itself when the as-of date is in an earlier year ("2025", read as "שנת 2025"), and "מההתחלה". The choice lives in the URL (`span=all`) with a replace, as the reports' own choices.
  - From the start there is no opening row; the one summary row is "שינוי מההתחלה" with the count, and the movements are grouped by month under a heading per year (the year and its count on a hairline; under it the months are named alone, their regions keep the year).
  - A name with a slash between letters ("Contributions/Distributions") gets a `<wbr>` after the slash in a stacked screen title and a report row, so it breaks after "/" and at spaces before it breaks inside a word; a date's slashes ("1/1/2026") stay as they are. The reports' stacked titles also wrap anywhere as a last resort, so a long Latin word never runs past the screen in right-to-left.
  - The account page's title alone (`.ui-rep-account-head`, not every reports title) clamps at three lines instead of the pages' two, so an account's long name shows whole. This departs from DESIGN-RULES (a name wraps to two lines before the ellipsis, lines 409 and 497) and waits on the design lead's sign-off.
  - From the start, a month's heading under its year is lighter than the year's (`t-line2`, muted, the group-title pattern), so the year reads as the level above.
- Decided: the owner approved the three-line title and the period choice on 2026-10-30 ([decisions 1 and 2](2026-10-30-owner-decisions.md#1-the-account-pages-title-wraps-to-three-lines)).
- Source: the owner's report on 2026-10-29 (phone screenshots), "keep building without waiting"; the period choice uses the reports screen's existing segmented control (the board's ReportAccountDrill draws no period choice).
