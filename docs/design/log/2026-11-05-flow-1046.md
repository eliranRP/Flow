- Rule:
  - An Overview screen's band bar is `CompactBar tone="band"` with `centre` (a 13px title and the 20/600 figure) and 44px `start` / `end` slots; a missing action keeps an empty 44px slot so the centre stays centred. Its mark (`ui-compact-mark`) sits at the end of the band's hero, and the bar shows once it passes 64px (`useScrolledPast(mark, true, 64)`); the docking hook uses the same 64.
  - A List screen's docking segment uses the detail bar (`size="detail"`, `bar: DETAIL_BAR`), its mark under the page's figure.
  - A loan's band rows follow its kind: regular, תשלום חודשי and ריבית; interest only, ריבית חודשית and ריבית; on demand, ריבית and הוחזר השנה; with no schedule left, ריבית alone (`loanBandRows`).
  - Rows by kind: פריסה רגילה, ריבית בלבד, בלון, לפי דרישה, in that order, a group only when it has a row; the head's summary is the count and the balances, one per currency.
  - The next payment's mark is `StatusMark due` (`late` once its date passed); a closed loan's row and a paid payment carry `paid`; an upcoming payment carries `due` and the word צפוי.
  - A "check this" card on a detail page (the loan waiting for a check) keeps its hidden state in the attention store under `flow.attention.<screen>:<id>`, keyed by what it counts, and its confirm shows the 5s toast with ביטול.
