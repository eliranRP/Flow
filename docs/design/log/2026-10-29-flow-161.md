# FLOW-161: Reports, the P&L and the balance sheet, from a card on Home

- PR: #855
- Kind: screen, new component
- Changed: `app/src/screens/reports-screen.tsx`, `reports-parts.tsx`, `reports-sheets.tsx`, `report-lines-screen.tsx`, `home-reports-card.tsx` (all new), `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (CashHome's `reports` slot, the last card), `app/src/reports.ts`, `app/src/use-reports.ts`, `app/src/report-schemas.ts` (new), `app/src/ui/css/61-reports.css` (new, on demand), `app/src/ui/css/43-home.css` (`.ui-home-rep`).
- Rule:
  - Reports open from the last card on Home ("דוחות", under "ממה מורכב המינוס"): two halves, רווח והפסד (this year's net profit) and מאזן (today's equity). No tab, nothing in Settings.
  - One reports screen: a רווח והפסד / מאזן switch, then two chips: the period (or, on the balance sheet, the date) and the projects. Both chips open a sheet; the choice lives in the URL.
  - Period: presets first (החודש, חודש שעבר, הרבעון, השנה, שנה שעברה, מההתחלה), then "תאריכים אחרים", which turns the same sheet into from/to and a month grid with the range's length ("16 ימים"), and a back arrow to the presets. The balance sheet: היום, סוף חודש שעבר, סוף רבעון שעבר, סוף שנה שעברה, תאריך אחר. One sheet at a time.
  - Projects: כל הפרויקטים יחד, לפי פרויקט (P&L only: a card per project, closed, with "הדוח המלא של …"), or one project; a search once there are ten or more.
  - A report leads with its number (רווח נקי with its share of income, or הון עצמי with "מאוזן"), capped at the total step. Every section collapses with its total on its header; a parent category is a group inside its side; "כיווץ הכל" / "פתיחת הכל"; what is closed is remembered per report.
  - A category row opens its lines (`/reports/lines`): the total, the split by project, the lines by month, each opening the transaction page; the period, the currency and one project carry over.
  - The balance sheet warns when חשבון מעבר is not zero, with an icon, the amount and "סידור הקטגוריות"; the bottom line is התחייבויות והון, with רווח שנצבר in הון.
  - States: a skeleton after 300 ms, the empty period with one action, the app's failed copy, the offline line, "הפרויקט לא נמצא".
- Source: owner approval on 2026-10-28 of the reports boards (design canvas, page "In design · for approval": rows "דוחות", "How the user gets to דוחות" Option 2, the states board), after a review against the UX/UI rule set; the owner picked the Home card only.
- Departures from the approved board: the P&L's sections are the company's own categories (a parent with its sub-categories is a group); the board drew two fixed expense sections, which the books do not hold. The balance-sheet account drill-down and the share icon come in follow-ups (FLOW-165, FLOW-162).
