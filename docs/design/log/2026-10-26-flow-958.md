- Rule:
  - On the current month, דורש טיפול comes right after the band (and the weekly card), before the split card. The review row is the screen's one filled button, worded as the weekly sheet's: "לאישור 7 פריטים" with "· כ־2 דקות" in 13/400 after it, so it holds one line at 320. The other rows follow as lines; a single one sits as a row, not as a card of its own.
  - The attention box is a neutral card (surface, 1px line border, card radius), its icons secondary and its chevrons muted. The accent on Home is spent on the button, the selected tab and the selected month only.
  - The split card's title is its takeaway ("רוב המינוס הלך לנכסים", "רוב מה שנכנס הוא הון", "רוב התזרים הוא רווח"); the metric name ("ממה מורכב המינוס") sits over it as a 13px muted label. The note line under the rows is gone.
  - The band's נכנס and יצא rows are 44px. The hero and the split card's amounts use tabular figures and never wrap.
  - The active tab draws a 56×32 pill (tint-strong) behind its icon and a 600 label, beside the accent colour.
  - The month drawer draws no year arrows when the books hold one year. A future month's name and "עוד לא" are muted text (4.5:1), not disabled text.
  - Offline with figures on screen: a 44px line in the band, "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־HH:MM" (Israel time, when the figures were read).
  - A failed read keeps the band: an alert icon, "<month> לא נטען" (offline: "אין חיבור"), one line on what happened, a white "נסו שוב" button and a "צריך עזרה?" link to /help.
