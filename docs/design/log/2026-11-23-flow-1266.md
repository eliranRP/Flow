# FLOW-1266: a recurring charge can have an end date

- PR: #1257
- Kind: new control, behaviour, copy
- Changed: `ui/recurring-charge-parts.tsx` (`EndDateRow`: "תאריך סיום" with the flag icon, the date or "ללא" on the end in the usual amount's tag style, quiet when there is no end), `screens/recurring-end-date.tsx` (the row, a ChoiceSheet of presets with their dates as descriptions, "התאריך שבחרת" when the stored date is none of them, "ללא תאריך סיום", the "תאריך אחר" link to the DateSheet from today on), `screens/recurring-charge-screen.tsx` (the row under the usual amount), `css/48-recurring-charge.css` (`.ui-charge-rec-tag-off`).
- Rule: few words, icons first; one decision per sheet. The common ends are one tap each and show the date they mean, so nothing needs to be worked out; an exact date is one step further. A new setting gets its own row and sheet on the charge's page, not Settings.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-23, on the charge page. The owner chose presets plus an exact date, and that the charge leaves קבועים once its end date passes. Task `docs/backlog/tasks/FLOW-1266.md`.
- Copy: "תאריך סיום"; hint "ממשיך עד שתסמנו" with no end, "אחריו לא נצפה לחיוב" with one; presets "אחרי החיוב הבא", "בעוד 3 חודשים", "בעוד 6 חודשים", "בסוף השנה"; the note under the sheet "אחרי התאריך החיוב לא ייצפה, וכשיעבור הוא ייצא מהקבועים."; toasts "מסתיים ב־{date}: {name}" and "תאריך הסיום הוסר: {name}".
- Unchanged: the pace row, the usual amount, the skip-month switch and the history.
- Design review: shots of `screens-recurringcharge--end-date-sheet-open` and `--with-end-date` at 320 and 393, light and dark.
