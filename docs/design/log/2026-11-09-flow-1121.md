# FLOW-1121: דוח שותף's calculation opens its parts; the selling cost names its percent and can be changed

- PR: #1077
- Kind: pattern
- Changed:
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-format.ts`: every row of החישוב carries a sheet target (`{ kind: "sheet" }`, a new `Target` kind the investment pages ignore). The member's share is now the formula's last row instead of its result, so it opens too. `sellingHint` writes the selling cost's percent: "7% · תיווך, סגירה ומסים", a range "7%–8%" when the properties differ, and "8% · ברירת מחדל" when every property takes the default.
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-parts.tsx` (new): `DrillSheet`, one row opened.
    - Each property's figure with how it is made, and the total; every number comes from `computeMemberShare`'s result:
      - value and its date;
      - percent × value;
      - open loans;
      - value − selling − loans;
      - LTV × value;
      - the payoff;
      - closing % × the new loan;
      - the cash freed.
    - A property that is not refinanced is marked "לא ממוחזר, לא נספר" and left out of the total.
    - The share lists every member's ownership × the total.
    - A property row links to its investment page. On the selling cost, the owner's rows open the percent editor instead, with "שינוי האחוז לכל הנכסים" under them.
    - `TreasuryUpdated`: "עודכן ב-09:30 · רענון" (or "עוד לא עודכן · רענון"), and for a viewer the time alone.
  - `app/src/screens/member-report-screen.tsx`:
    - החישוב's resolver opens the row's sheet.
    - The selling cost is edited with the investment page's `PercentSheet`, with its own help: a percent of the value. It saves through `set_project_investment`'s `selling_cost_bp`, for one property or all.
    - The rate card:
      - with no quote and no yield, the date button is today's;
      - the missing-yield line reads "תשואות האג״ח מתעדכנות אוטומטית מאתר משרד האוצר. אפשר גם להקליד ידנית." (a viewer gets the first sentence);
      - "עוד אין הצעות. הוסיפו הצעה כדי להשוות." stands before "+ הוספת הצעה" while there are no quotes;
      - the updated line with רענון sits under the source note and at the top of the yields sheet.
  - `app/src/use-member-report.ts`:
    - `useSellingCostWrite` invalidates `member-report` and `project`, so the investment page reads the new percent too.
    - `useRefreshTreasury` calls `refresh_treasury_yields` (#1079) and answers with a toast:
      - "מבקשים את התשואות מהאוצר…", then the report is read again after about 20 s and 60 s;
      - "כבר התבקש לפני רגע";
      - "לא ניתן לרענן כרגע".
    - `treasury_fetched_at` and `treasury_requested_at` are read as optional.
  - `app/src/ui/css/64-member-report.css`: the parts' total line and the updated line.
  - Stories: `Screens/Member report/Parts`:
    - every row on both tabs, and a property not refinanced;
    - the selling cost for one property and for all;
    - a viewer's parts;
    - the rate card before any quote or yield (owner and viewer), and with yields but no quotes;
    - the refresh: never fetched, fetched today, recent, unavailable, and a viewer.
- Why: the owner, 2026-11-09, on החישוב: "Each part of the composite number should be clickable and informative so the user can see how we got this number. Also selling cost should include the assumption percentage and can be changed". The rate card with nothing yet read as broken; and "Allow the user to refresh manually the us bond rate".
- Rule:
  - Every figure in a calculation the owner reads opens how it was made, down to each property.
  - An assumption's percent is named where it is used, and is changed where the owner reads it, through the same write as on its own page.
- Source: the owner, 2026-11-09.
