# FLOW-1265: A row's one-line hint shows none of its hidden second line

- PR: #1256
- Kind: fix (visual)
- Changed: `ui/css/02-fields-sheets.css` (`.ui-hint-parts` gets `row-gap` equal to its cap).
- Rule: a one-line hint is one line. Parts that do not fit wrap to a second line that must stay hidden (FLOW-168). The cap is line2's line (15px × 1.4 = 21px), but a row's hint is 13px on a 16.9px line, so the top 4px of the second line showed under the first (the pixel review of #1252, the Loans row at 320).
- Fix: the wrapped line starts a whole cap below the first, outside the box. The box keeps its height (the cap), so no row moves; a hint that fits on one line is unchanged.
- Check: `Loans, attention, one line at 320` asserts every hint part is wholly inside its box or wholly below it; it fails without the fix. The list-row and search stories pass.
