# FLOW-1156: a project's month payments say "קבוע"; its נכנס/יצא page names the project on a cold open

- PR: #TBD
- Kind: component
- Changed:
  - Project page, "תנועות החודש" (FLOW-1156): a recurring charge's payment says "קבוע" in its hint after the date ("10/11 · קבוע · ריבית"), so the paid mark is no longer the only sign; the mark stays. When the month's payments are in more than one currency, the group head shows the count and "מטבעות שונים" ("4 · מטבעות שונים") and no net, instead of a net of the first payment's currency.
  - The project's נכנס/יצא page (`/cash/:month/:side/:currency?project=`, FLOW-1157): opened from a link or a reload, with no name in the cache, it reads the project's row of the month (`projects_cash_month`, the read the project page already uses) and fills the name into the month line and the Back label. While that read is out a short skeleton bar holds the name's place at the start of the month line, so the line keeps its height. Back says "פרויקט" until the name is in.
  - Status bar cover (FLOW-1158): no visible change. It no longer measures the bands on every DOM change of the page: an IntersectionObserver over the status bar's strip says whether a band is under it, a MutationObserver on the screens' container (not the whole body) finds bands and the pull-to-refresh gap as they come and go, and a ResizeObserver gives the strip's height.
- Rule:
  - A row's kind (recurring or one-off) is said in its words, not only by an icon.
  - A group head over amounts in more than one currency shows the count and "מטבעות שונים", never a total of one currency.
- Source: UX rules review 2026-11-10 (the owner asked for the fixes); FLOW-1156, FLOW-1157, FLOW-1158.
