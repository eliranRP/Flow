- PR: #924
- Kind: screen
- Changed: `app/src/screens/project-returns-data.ts` (the first rent, the period's lines before it, the money in by part, all the cash out, where the purchase comes from), `project-returns-info.ts`, `project-returns-cards.tsx`, `project-returns-parts.tsx`, `project-returns-screen.tsx`, `project-returns-detail-screen.tsx`, `project-investment-cards.tsx`, `project-investment-screen.tsx`, `app/src/dev/returns-sample.ts`.
- Rule:
  - כסף בעסקה is shown by its four parts, each opening its lines: רכישה וסגירה, + שיפוץ לפני השכרה, + עלויות החזקה (caption "עד השכר הראשון ב־dd/mm/yyyy", or "עדיין לפני השכר הראשון"), − תקבולי הלוואות והחזרים (caption "נטו, אחרי עלויות ההלוואה"), = כסף בעסקה. The rows add up to it. The part page lists the transactions of all four. A line of the money in reads "מאז הקנייה", not the period.
  - The statement counts from the first rent; its hero meta says so ("משכר ראשון ב־dd/mm/yyyy") when the rent falls inside the period. Under מחוץ לחישוב, the holding and renovation costs before the rent are their own row, shown only when not 0; "לא נספר בתזרים" is captioned "רכישה, סגירה ותקבולי הלוואות". The cash flow ⓘ sheet carries a muted caption saying the same.
  - When all the cash came out, a per-cent figure reads "∞" at the size of the percent it replaces (40/400 on the total-return card, the row figure on מדדים, the hero on the metric page), with the meta "כל הכסף חזר · יצא $X יותר משנכנס". No signal colour: it is a good state. The sources keep their amounts and leave the per-cent column empty. The money in below zero is not drawn as a loss.
  - The purchase from the lines reads "מהתנועות · לפירוט" in נתוני הנכס and opens its line page, with no edit sheet; the ⓘ rows that use it read "מחיר רכישה (מהתנועות)".
- Source: owner, 2026-11-01, on a real rental after the FLOW-451 deploy ([0245](../../decisions/0245-returns-from-the-lines.md)): the net loan proceeds, money in from the lines, "∞" when all the cash came out, the first rent as the switch, "show the user the holding costs as a separate line".
