# FLOW-1042: pull to refresh brings the whole screen down, and re-reads Flow's data (ld-07)

- PR: #979
- Kind: component
- Changed: `app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx` (`PullRefresh` now wraps the routed screens in `App.tsx`; `PullRefreshView` replaces `PullRefreshMark`; new `pullTone`; `refreshAll` replaces `refreshActive`), CSS `62-pull-refresh.css` (new `.ui-pull-screen`, `.ui-pull-gap`; `.ui-pull-refresh` moves into the gap), `ui/index.ts`, DESIGN-RULES ld-07, CONTROLS.md. Story: Components/Pull to refresh (Home pulling at 320 and 393, armed, a past month's band, refreshing at 320 and 393, a plain screen pulling and refreshing).
- Rule:
  - While pulling, the routed screen itself comes down with the finger at half the move (up to 96px); the tab bar stays put. The gap above it takes the colour of the screen's top: `--color-band` where the screen starts with the band (Home, a project), `--color-band-past` for a past month's band, `--color-bg` elsewhere. The band looks stretched down.
  - On a notched iPhone the status bar covers the gap's top, so the screen also travels the top safe area in step with the progress (`--pull-y + --safe-top × progress`, no jump at the first move): at the 64px threshold the gap shows 64px under the status bar (with a 59px safe area the screen's top is at 123px).
  - The round mark (the edge-back mark's 40px `raised` circle with the inset ring and the shared spinner) sits in the middle of the gap's part under the status bar, turns and fades in with the progress, and takes the accent at 64px. From the threshold on it lies wholly inside the gap for any safe area; earlier in the pull, on a notched phone, it can meet the screen's top while it is still fading in.
  - Released at 64px or more, the screen, the gap and the mark settle at 64px (plus the safe area) on `--motion-fast` and the spinner spins (`--motion-spin`) until the reads settle, at least 400ms and at most 15s (a read still running after that goes on in the background, with no toast); then the screen slides back up on `--motion-sheet-out` with `--ease-exit`. Released before, it springs back the same way and nothing is read.
  - The screen moves by `top` on a relative box, not a transform: a transform would become the containing block of the bars the screen pins (`position: fixed`: the action bar, the card's step row, a save bar), and they would jump to the screen's end. So those bars stay put, as the tab bar, toasts and sheets do. An exception to "animate only transform and opacity" (2.7), for this one box; the gap and the mark move by transform. `top` changes on each touchmove only during a pull; idle, the box is not positioned at all. A Safari Web Inspector timeline of a pull on an installed iPhone (Home) is owed: FLOW-1040.
  - The gap and the mark are absolute at the page's top, so they scroll away with the screen if it is scrolled while a refresh runs.
  - Reduced motion: the screen does not move and there is no gap; the mark rests 24 to 64px under the top safe area and only fades in.
  - A refresh re-reads Flow's own data from the server: every cached read goes stale (`invalidateQueries()`), so the reads on screen read again, and so do a read nested in another and every other screen's reads when they next open. It starts no bank sync (the owner, 2026-11-05: "It should refresh the data from flow server."). A failed read shows "הרענון נכשל. נסו שוב." once.
  - This supersedes FLOW-1039's note that the refreshing mark rests over the screen's top row (on Home, the band's action row): with motion allowed nothing overlaps the screen's own controls any more. Under reduced motion the mark still rests over the top row, as before, and takes no taps.
