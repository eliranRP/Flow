- Rule:
  - The month chart's out bars are ink: `--color-chart-expense` is #D9D2E9 dark (11.7:1 on the card, was 2.18:1) and #3D3550 light (11.5:1, was 2.6:1). Against the purple in bars they differ by hue (the two are 1.64:1 against each other), and the legend names each. Every month chart takes it (Home's, the profit pages', the company's).
  - A bar's empty part is an outline, not a faint fill: `--color-track-outline` (#6F6787 dark, 3.2:1; #8F879F light, 3.4:1) on the budget's rest and the investment card's loan, and on the dashed no-budget card.
  - Over budget, the bar runs past a 2px, 16px tick in `--color-bad`, and the note leads with an alert icon: "חריגה של $1,800 · 106% מהתקציב". The bar is an image with "נוצל X מתוך Y · N%".
  - A project with no budget shows, in the budget's place, a dashed card ("אין תקציב לפרויקט", what a budget gives, הוספת תקציב), which opens the edit sheet with the cursor in the budget. Not on a new project, a finished one, or one whose investment card is on the page (a property), as the old link was not.
  - סיום הפרויקט finishes at once: the list opens with הסתיימו and a toast under the header offers ביטול for 5s (decision 0213). The finish row's hint names the items still waiting. Only delete still asks, on "מחיקת הפרויקט".
  - Finished rows are at full contrast (the 75% dim is gone; the line meets 4.5:1), with an outlined tick and "רווח כולל" under the total.
  - The project page's failed read and offline line are Home v2's (now `BandLoadFailed` and `BandOfflineLine`): "<name> לא נטען", נסו שוב, צריך עזרה?; "אין חיבור · מוצג המצב מ־09:41".
  - The project's waiting items are Home v2's filled button ("לאישור 2 פריטים · פחות מדקה"), first under the band.
  - The project chart's heading says the month's takeaway ("אוקטובר: יצא $14,253 יותר משנכנס"); the metric and its months are the 13px label above it. Every chart's image label now reads each month's two figures, then the forecast. Month labels are 13px (11px stays on a twelve-month chart).
  - יצירת פרויקט is never disabled: with no name the field is marked and says why.
  - The Projects header is one row: the title, חיפוש and a filled + (the screen's main action, `.ui-icon-btn-filled`). The over-budget flag leads its row's line, so a long type never cuts it.
- Source: owner approval of the Project v2 boards on 2026-10-26 (design canvas). Decision [0213](../../decisions/0213-project-finish-undo.md).

The board's ⋯ menu row that only closed the menu and its unreachable finished rows were the board's own: the app's השקעה והלוואות row and finished rows already opened their pages, and הסתיימו already had aria-expanded. The board's "rental" is a sample flag; the app has no rental kind, so the no-budget card keeps the old link's rule (no card beside the investment card).
