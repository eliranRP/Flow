# FLOW-327: the review card's pinned bar, aligned rows, Jev reason, flags and skipped cards

- PR: #175
- Kind: pattern
- Changed: `action-bar.tsx` (new), `toast.tsx` (`place: "bar"`), `review-card.tsx`, `suggest-tag.tsx`, `review-skipped-list.tsx` (new), `text-link` disabled styles, `ui.css` (`.ui-action-bar*`, `.ui-review-*`, `.ui-suggest-*`, `.ui-toast-host[data-place="bar"]`, `.ui-text-link:disabled`, `.ui-lsplit-*` disabled rest), `flow-screens.tsx` (`ReviewQueue`, `ReviewAllList`, `ReviewEmpty`, `ReviewScreen`), `line-split.tsx` (לתור on the open-review banner)
- Rule: A screen's repeated action sits in a pinned `ActionBar` above the tab bar (`place="tabbar"`), or on the screen edge with the safe area (`place="edge"`); the content scrolls above it and gets bottom padding of the bar's height.
- Rule: That screen's toasts sit just above the bar (`place: "bar"`, `--space-2` over the topmost `[data-toast-floor]`), never over it; one screen keeps one toast place.
- Rule: On the review card the label column has one width (`--review-label-w`) and the value column starts on one line; the Jev pill sits at the row's end and shows only ✦ below 18rem of card width.
- Rule: A disabled primary action says why in a hint linked with `aria-describedby` (אישור: "בחרו פרויקט וקטגוריה"; להשאיר כך: the mismatch line).
- Rule: A disabled text link is grey with a not-allowed cursor and no hover or press fill.
