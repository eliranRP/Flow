# 0258 · The band's type keeps v2's sizes; the boards' band type is superseded

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** accepted (the owner, on the phone after the shared pixel pass went live: "Everything looks scaled too much big", then "The same sizes that was in v2")
- **Context:** FLOW-1051 (#992) moved the top band's text to the `V3-Home` board's sizes: the company name 24/600, the month 20/600, the figure's label 17, the נכנס / יצא labels 17 and amounts 20/600, a 32px change pill at 16/500, the note 14, the forecast card's title 17/500 and figure 24. On the phone the band matched the board to within 5px, and the owner found the whole band too big.
- **Decision:** the band's text goes back to the sizes v2 used (the ones live before the pass): the company name 20/1.3/600, the month 16/500, the figure's label 16/500, the rows' labels and amounts 16/500, the change pill at the chip's 14/500 with its 3px 10px padding, the note 13, the forecast card's title 15 and figure 17/500. The board's spacing, radii and heights from FLOW-1051 stay (the 52px first row, the 48px month pill, the 44px rows, the 64px forecast card, the 28px radius, the 20px side padding).
- **Consequences:** the `V3-Home` board's band type is superseded by this decision; the per-screen pixel passes (FLOW-1052 to FLOW-1055) compare the band for layout only and leave its type alone; the other screens' bands keep the same shared blocks, so they move with Home. Decision 0257 stands for everything else on the boards.
- **Alternatives rejected:** one step down from the board everywhere (the owner asked for v2's sizes, which they had lived with); the figure and the rows only (the owner circled the whole band).
