# Logo

**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

[0023](0023-violet-coloured-top-band.md) set the wordmark in Rubik 700. [0024](0024-design-system-approved.md) approves the system. The wordmark and the app icon now have their own rules, separate from accent text.

## Decision

The main logo is the "Flow" wordmark in Rubik 700, letter-spacing −0.01em.

On light it is brand violet `#7B3FE4`. On dark it is `#B894FF`. On the band it is white.

A `logo` token was added. The in-app wordmark no longer uses `accent-text`.

The app icon is S1, the straight Rubik 700 F. White on violet is the installed PWA icon, the in-app spots, and the favicon. The light version is violet on white. The dark version is `#B894FF` on `#15111E`.

Earlier cursive and calligraphic directions were explored and rejected. See [explorations](../../design/logo/explorations/).

Evidence: [LOGO.md](../../design/logo/LOGO.md) and [flow-logo-usage.png](../../design/logo/board/flow-logo-usage.png).

## Alternatives rejected

Cursive and calligraphic marks in `design/logo/explorations/`. Using `accent-text` for the in-app wordmark.

## Consequences

Ship the files under [design/logo](../../design/logo/LOGO.md). Do not recolour, retype, or restyle the mark. The usage board is the picture of where each version goes.
