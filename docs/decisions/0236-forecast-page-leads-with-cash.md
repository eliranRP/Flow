# The צפי page leads with the cash its project card shows

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-10-30, FLOW-991: "the revenue and cashflow numbers are interchangeable, confusing", then "fix the terminology or calculation" on the existing screens, cash for both)
**Amends:** [0196](0196-next-month-forecast-card-and-chart.md) point 4 and its consequence that the page's expenses are lower than the card's

## Context

A project page's dashed card says תזרים צפוי (cash: the whole loan payment due next month, principal included, decision 0196). Its "פירוט ועריכה" opens the צפי page, which showed the profit forecast: the same month, a different net and lower expenses, with no word on why. On a project with a loan the two nets differ by the principal. The owner read it as a wrong number.

## Decision

1. **The page's figure is the card's.** The צפי page leads with תזרים צפוי, named so under the title: the forecast in cash terms (cashForecast), the number on the project page's card.
2. **The card's words.** The summary reads צפוי להיכנס and צפוי לצאת (cash), as the card does, and then, when they differ, the bridge to the profit: מזה לא נספר ברווח, then רווח צפוי.
3. **The profit, named.** When the loans' payments hold a part the profit leaves out, the summary adds רווח צפוי: the profit forecast, the number on the profit page's card, which also opens this page. The share of general expenses and the profit after it follow it, as before.
4. **The rows add up.** That part is one more row at the end of the list, shown even when no other row is expected, "לא נספר ברווח · לפי ההלוואה", with no edit page (it follows the loan's schedule; taking out every row of a loan still drops it, 0196 point 4).
5. **Nothing else moves.** The rows, edits and notes are unchanged; the profit page's card stays רווח צפוי and the company profit page stays profit; Home's and the Projects tab's cards (0235) are cash already.
