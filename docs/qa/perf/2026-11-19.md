## The 2026-11-18 outage

#1218 removed fields that the deployed app still read. Its migrations applied on main's push run, but its app deploy then waited: the e2e shards failed twice on "Wait for local Supabase", and the steward's worker restarted between the re-runs. For about 1.5 to 2 hours Company profit's figures did not load. The deploy of `d12881b` fixed it, and the steward checked that Company profit renders. Two lessons:
- A change that removes fields ships in two steps: the app that no longer reads them first, then the database. FLOW-1235 is written that way.
- The CI step is flaky enough to hold a deploy. It is now FLOW-1237.

The worker restart also cost about 7 hours of this run's time between the morning's measurements and the diff review.
