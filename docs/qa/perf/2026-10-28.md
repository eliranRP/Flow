## 2. Database

- Performance advisor: one unindexed foreign key, `legs (company_id, transaction_id)`. `legs_txn_idx (transaction_id, company_id)` already serves lookups by line, so no index is added.
- `company_next_month_inputs` is the slowest read by far. It called `project_category_months` once per active project: 14 projects, about 195 ms of its 440 ms. Its recurring part (`recurring_parties` about 95 ms, `recurring_stream_lines` about 30 ms) runs once.
- `recurring_parties` is shared by `recurring_schedule`, `expected_months` and `recurring_changes`; it is the next candidate.
