Profit's next-month card and year view, Home's months to come and the company's צפי and תזרים pages read the company forecast in one request each (FLOW-1308, slice 7 of the FLOW-1275 plan). `app/src/company-forecast-query.ts` calls `company_forecast` with the Israel date: `'totals'` for the card (next month) and the year view (every month left in the year), `'rows'` for one month's page, in place of the four reads the app made and built from (expected months, the loans, the company's inputs and the categories). `app/src/company-forecast-map.ts` turns the answer back into what the screens drew from the TypeScript builders: the month with its parts and rows (the general costs' part, which the database always lists, is dropped when it has no row, so it adds no caption, row or cash), the rows sorted as the builders sort them (Hebrew order, a changed row where Flow had it, the lines the user added in the edits' order), the rows taken out in the builder's order, and the card's and the year view's captions and income notes from the database's counts. Amounts become bigints and an unsafe integer fails the read; a failed read shows the usual failed state with retry. The kept data follows: the card's and the year view's totals are kept under `["month-forecast", preview, "company", "totals", day, months]` and the card draws at once on a repeat visit (FLOW-1294's case); the rows are not kept. The kept-row checks of `month_forecast` and `company_forecast` moved from `kept-rules.ts` into the lazy read-schemas chunk (same checks), which leaves Home's entry smaller than before (247.8 KB gzip, 62 bytes less). A skip, a reset and an undo change the cached company forecast of their month on the tap, as they changed the cached inputs before (see below). The overhead project's page keeps its own four reads until FLOW-1315; the TypeScript builders stay for the samples, the stories and `?preview=1`. The live smoke's read allowlist gains `company_forecast`. The sweep figures are pending after the deploy.
A skip, a reset and an undo move the cached company forecast of their month on the tap (forecast-month.ts calls the patch that company-forecast-query.ts registers once it is loaded): the rows, the month's totals, the captions and the income note counts (with the categories Home holds), so the Home band and the company page change at once; the cached totals of that month, Profit's card, are written from the same month, so the card draws at once with the skip applied (FLOW-1294), and the year view's totals, which hold no rows to move, are dropped. A failed write puts them all back. The kept IndexedDB row of the card's totals is not deleted by a skip; it is overwritten when the patched totals are kept, so on a cold start before that it may draw the figures from before the skip until the read refreshes it. A row of a kind the app does not know, or parts that do not add up to the database's month, fail the read instead of dropping out of a total. A kept `month_forecast` or `company_forecast` row is checked in the lazy read-schemas chunk, so if that chunk fails to load (offline on a cold start) the kept row is held back until it does, where the month forecast row was drawn before.
