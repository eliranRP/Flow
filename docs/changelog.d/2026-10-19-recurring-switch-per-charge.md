The recurring switch is the charge's, not the party's (decision 0188, amends 0185). "Recurring" on a line of a customer or supplier billed for several properties now makes that property's charge recurring, even with fewer than three months or only this month, and "not recurring" ends that charge alone; the pace works the same way. In קבועים, "הסתיים" on one property's charge ends that one and names it in the toast. MCP `set_line_recurring`, `set_line_pace` and `get_line_recurring` read and write the line's charge.
