Home is redesigned (FLOW-364). The band shows the company, the icons and a month pill that opens a month drawer; the figures show what came in and went out against the same day last month; the split card explains the month's net. A past month reads as closed, with a way back. The tab bar has four tabs: בית, פרויקטים, הלוואות and קבועים. The + moved into Home's band. The old paths still open their screens.

The tab-bar floor is 32px when the bottom inset is 0, as on the board. Decision [0183](../decisions/0183-tab-bar-floor-from-the-board.md) supersedes item 6 of [0045](../decisions/0045-phase-0-design-gaps.md) (the 8px floor); the other items of 0045 stand.
