FLOW-984, decision [0238](decisions/0238-review-loads-in-pages.md): Review loads its open items in pages of 50, ahead of the user, instead of all at once. The new `list_review_page` returns the page, the total open count and the next cursor; the page is chosen before the per-row parts run. Locally, on the largest company (1,371 open items), the first read falls from 1.29 MB (165 KB compressed) and about 172 ms in the database to 47 KB (6.9 KB compressed) and about 36 ms. The header, the segments, the list's subtitle and the minutes estimate show the true total ("פריט 12 מתוך 1,287"). A failed page leaves a quiet row with ניסיון חוזר; a project filter and a link to a card on a later page read through the server. `list_review()` is unchanged for the MCP tools.
