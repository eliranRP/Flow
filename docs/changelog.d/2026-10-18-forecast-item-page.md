The month forecast (צפי) opens a tapped row as a full page at `/forecast/item/<key>` instead of a bottom sheet, as the board's MonthForecastEdit and MonthForecastWhen frames draw it: a status pill (the month, or how many days late), the item's name, the amount as a big editable figure, a meta line, its project and category, a "מתי צפוי" row that opens a sheet of three choices with their explanations, a link to the source (the recurring charge, the invoice or the bill), a hint that only the forecast changes, and שמירה. Saving, undo, the overrides and the fold of an item moved to next month are unchanged; the forecast items now carry their project so the page can name it.
