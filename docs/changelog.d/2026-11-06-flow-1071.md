The investment page's metrics drill down (FLOW-1071). A renovation's מדדים tab keeps cash on cash, DSCR, cap rate and the cash flow per unit beside its own metrics. Every part of a metric's page opens what it is made of, down to the transactions: ROI per year opens the ROI, the time in the deal opens the purchase lines, and the holding costs open their own lines (new line `deal_holding` on `get_property_return_lines`, which adds up to the net profit's holding figure). The tab you chose stays chosen when you come Back from a metric or a loan, and a renovation's metric pages say "מאז הקנייה" instead of the last 12 months.
