A project's next-month forecast (ProfitNext, the ProfitProject card and the edit page) now takes each open loan's interest and escrow for that month from the loan's schedule, in the loan's interest and escrow categories, instead of those categories' averages, which diluted a young loan (decision 0190). The rows read "לפי ההלוואה" under their name, keep their `avg:<category>` key so earlier edits still apply, and several loans in one category sum into one row; principal, demand loans, closed or paid-off loans and months outside the schedule give nothing. The page's note and the card's basis mention the loan rows. The MCP doc of `get_project_forecast_edits` describes the new rows.
