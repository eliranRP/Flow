FLOW-371 builds the loading and empty parts of the redesign in the shared UI. Skeleton blocks wait 300ms before they show, pulse together at 1.2s on 6px blocks, and fade out as the content fades in over 200ms. Projects, the monthly profit page and the transaction page show their own designed skeleton while loading. A background refresh shows a sync strip: a 3px line, and after 200ms a pill with a spinner, with the content still usable under it. A long known job (a first sync or an import) shows an 8px bar, its count, its steps and a way to leave. A busy button keeps its width and its spinner turns at 800ms. A failure is one line with "נסו שוב"; under reduced motion the animations stop and the text still says what is loading. The empty state uses the States board's copy, its 64px icon circle and at most one action. The light job track is a placeholder until FLOW-372 sets the light palette. The month lines, Loans and קבועים skeletons are wired: the month lines in `cash-screens.tsx`, Loans in the loans section of `loan-setup.tsx`, and קבועים in `missing-bill-list.tsx`, each behind the 300ms delay.
