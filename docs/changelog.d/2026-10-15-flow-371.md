FLOW-371 builds the loading and empty parts of the redesign in the shared UI. Skeleton blocks wait 300ms before they show, and a screen's content fades in over 200ms when its loading ends. A background refresh shows a sync strip: a 3px line and a pill with a spinner, and the content stays usable under it. A long known job (a first sync or an import) shows an 8px bar, its count, its steps and a way to leave. The empty state uses the States board's copy and at most one action, a 52px primary pill for the next step or a 44px outline pill when it only helps. The skeleton, the spinner and the failure line still differ from the board where the screen files or the shared stylesheets outside this change draw them; the design log lists each difference.
