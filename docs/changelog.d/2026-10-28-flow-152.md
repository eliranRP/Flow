Balance-sheet accounts can now be managed from the MCP (decision [0232](../decisions/0232-ledger-only-books.md)): create, rename, describe, archive, restore and merge an account, with undo; the system accounts stay locked. Owner capital is an equity account. A journal entry can move only balance-sheet accounts, and get_balance_sheet shows assets, liabilities, equity and retained earnings as of a date, per currency and project, with a check that they balance and a warning while Suspense holds money.
