Reports v3: the Reports tab opens a hub with one card for each of the two reports (the net profit for the period and the equity today, each with a small chart and two facts) and three breakdowns. The profit and loss (`/reports/pnl`) shows its net, income and expenses on the violet band and breaks down by category, by project (the general expenses that belong to no project in their own group, so the groups add up) or by month; a project or a month row opens its own profit and loss on the same screen. The balance sheet (`/reports/balance`) shows assets, liabilities and equity, or each property's equity, and a property opens its own assets and liabilities. The view control docks into the bar as the page scrolls. Old links (`/reports?kind=balance`, a project's דוחות link) open the new pages with their choices (FLOW-1045).
