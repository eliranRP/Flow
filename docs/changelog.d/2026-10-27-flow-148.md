A loan payment's parts can each go to their own category. Through the AI tools, one payment's interest can move into the P&L (or out of it) while the payment stays on the loan, so its history and the interest paid stay right. A loan can also switch a part's category from a date on, for example a rehab loan whose interest is a holding cost until the rehab ends and mortgage interest after it; payments matched later follow the switch for their own date, and the ones already matched can be moved with it. Each change can be undone. A refused loan payment now says why.
