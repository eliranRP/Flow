Utility refunds count only exact matches, and Flow suggests nothing (FLOW-1143, the owner's decision, decision [0265](decisions/0265-utility-refunds.md) item 9): a bill is refunded by itself only by a money-in line of exactly its amount (or exactly the sum of the same month's bills of one category) filed to the bill's property and category. The usual rent plus bills, amounts in another category and payments with no property no longer match, and there are no suggestions (`suggested_count` is always 0). The owner can still pick a payment as the refund, close a bill as seen, mark a utility company-paid, refile and undo. `list_utility_refunds`, `count_utility_refunds` and their MCP tools ([TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md)).
