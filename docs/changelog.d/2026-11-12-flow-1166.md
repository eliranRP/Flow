FLOW-1166 (part 2b): a sync no longer changes a line in a closed period. A change to what the provider reports for a closed line is held (`private.closed_line_holds`), the stored line stays as it is, and the review queue shows it with the reason `closed_month_change`; the next sync applies it once the owner moves the closed date back. `leave_held_change` (MCP `mcp_leave_held_change`, undo `held_change_left`) leaves the line as it is. Filing the sync re-derives for a closed line keeps the stored value, and a new line dated in a closed period enters as before. `set_books_closed_through` refuses a close while a line the date would close is open for review. `docs/tech/state-machines.md` is updated.
