FLOW-938 (decision [0203](decisions/0203-mercury-connections-and-synced-accounts.md), amends 0086): Mercury takes several connections per company (one per organisation's key) and syncs only the accounts the owner chooses. After the key, step 3 asks which accounts to sync (checking, savings and cards checked, Treasury not) and from which date (מההתחלה, the start of the year or the month, or a day); the key form no longer asks the date for Mercury, and onboarding shows the same step after its key sheet. The connected page drops the accounts and card facts for an editable "חשבונות מסונכרנים" row, asks before syncing an account Mercury newly reports, and offers "+ חיבור Mercury נוסף"; with two connections the page opens on their list, and Settings › חיבורים says "2 חיבורים". A line on an unsynced account is skipped, a transfer to one counts as money out, and an account added later reads from its own start. **Migration:** `20261021060558_mercury_connections.sql` (`connector_connections.slot` and the per-connection columns, the key becomes `(company_id, provider, slot)`, existing rows keep syncing every account they list; `set_mercury_accounts`; a `p_slot` argument on `upsert_connector_lines`, `stamp_connector_sync`, `note_connector_failure`, `replace_connector_connection` and `disconnect_connector`; the daily refresh queues a company once; `mcp_bank_connections`, `mcp_set_bank_accounts`, `mcp_answer_bank_account` and the undo kind `bank_accounts`). Edge functions `mercury-connect`, `mercury-sync` and `flow-mcp` change and deploy with it. New MCP tools: `list_bank_connections` (each connection, its accounts, which sync and which are new), `set_bank_accounts` and `answer_bank_account` (owner only, through `set_mercury_accounts`, with undo).
