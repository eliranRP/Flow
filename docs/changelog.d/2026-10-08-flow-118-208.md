Undo puts a line's category back exactly as it was. `reassign_undo` and `review_queue` keep `prior_category_assigned`, and undoing a reassign, a split or a review write restores it, so a category that came from the bank or a supplier rule is no longer left marked as the owner's pick (FLOW-208). An `assign_expenses` retry with a split row's shares in another order now replays instead of `conflict`. `approve_split_review` refuses a category the company doesn't have with "category not found" (FLOW-118).
