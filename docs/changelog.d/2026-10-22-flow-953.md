The assistant can now book a closing wire, a refinance deposit or a mortgage payment with `book_line`, on a synced bank line or as a new line. Legs may sit on balance-sheet accounts: the purchase price on Property, the loan on Loan principal, escrow on Escrow, owner money on Owner capital, a tenant deposit on Security deposit held, and a transfer between own accounts on Transfers in transit (the two sides net to 0). Each such leg is filed under a kept-out category, so profit is exactly what the same booking with `split_line` gives; `get_line_legs` reads the legs back on those accounts. Two new accounts (Transfers in transit and Property), and each kept-out category now posts to one balance-sheet account: the built-in kept-out names bind by name (loan proceeds to Loan principal, purchase price and closing costs to Property, transfers to Transfers in transit, and so on), others stay on Suspense. `list_accounts` marks these accounts bookable and says where a kept-out category posts (`posts_to`). A loan principal or escrow leg does not move a tracked loan's balance; `attach_loan_payment` stays the tool for a loan's payment. VAT legs stay refused. No P&L figure moves.
