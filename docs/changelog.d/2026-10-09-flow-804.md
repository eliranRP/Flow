Home opens faster. The app used to download every screen before it showed Home. Now Home loads only its own code, about half of what it was (397 KB → 210 KB compressed), and the other screens load in the background once Home is up. On a mid-range phone on 4G, Home was ready in 1.4 seconds instead of 2.0 seconds in a local test. Nothing looks different.
