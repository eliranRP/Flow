Invites are capped and twelve redundant indexes are dropped (decision 0195, FLOW-928). `invite_member` creates at most 10 new invites per company and 20 per inviting user in any 24 hours; over a cap it refuses with `invite_limit` and writes nothing, the team screen's invite sheet shows a failure toast (שלחתם היום את מספר ההזמנות המרבי. נסו שוב מחר.), and flow-mcp's `invite_member` answers `refused` / `invite_limit` (TOOLS.md). The invite-email function claims each send with the new `claim_invite_email` RPC, at most 3 emails per pending, unexpired invite. The migration drops 12 single-column indexes whose lookups a `(company_id, …)` index already serves, and `foreign_key_indexes.test.sql` now requires a valid, usable index for every foreign key, with three known exceptions listed.
