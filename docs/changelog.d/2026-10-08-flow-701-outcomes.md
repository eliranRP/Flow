Jev phase 1, part 2: Jev's suggestions are compared with what the owner files ([0126](decisions/0126-jev-outcomes.md)). When a review line with a Jev suggestion is approved or changed, from the app, MCP or a batch, `jev_outcomes` records the project and category Jev chose against the ones the line was filed with; undo removes the row. Earlier approvals are backfilled. New MCP read `get_jev_accuracy`, the shadow accuracy report: how many lines matched, per field, at the company's threshold and by confidence band. The pending `record_jev_correction` is superseded (FLOW-701).
