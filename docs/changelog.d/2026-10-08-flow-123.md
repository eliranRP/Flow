Loan balances can no longer go below zero by a side door. Two app splits on the same loan at once now check the balance one after the other. A loan payment whose bank line was pending, or removed, and then posts or comes back past the balance has its parts flagged for review instead of counting. Lowering a loan's principal below what was already paid is refused with `loan balance exceeded` in MCP `update_loan`, its undo and the database (FLOW-123, decision 0121).
