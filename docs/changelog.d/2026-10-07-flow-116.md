The overhead project no longer takes a share of overhead (FLOW-116). The after-overhead view spreads overhead only over the other projects, by their income, and the overhead project's share is 0; company totals are unchanged. MCP `undo` kind `overhead_project` now returns `conflict` instead of `refused` when the project it would go back to was deleted. Decision [0117](decisions/0117-overhead-project-weights.md).
