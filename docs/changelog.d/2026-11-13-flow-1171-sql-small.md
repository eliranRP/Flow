**FLOW-1171 (perf queue).** Three small database reads now do less work, with the same answers. The P&L line states (`private.line_pnl_states`, both overloads) read the caller's company's lines only: a 1,856-line read on the QA company went from 159 ms to 21 ms, and service role still reads every company. The forecast helpers `private.pfe_hits`, `pfe_month_index` and `pfe_json` drop their `search_path` setting so Postgres can inline them, and `pfe_hits` is one expression with no FROM: a check over the QA company's forecast edits for the next 12 months went from 351 ms to 52 ms. The project list's waiting counts (`projects_cash_month`) count each project's waiting lines in one grouped pass instead of calling `project_waiting` per project: 76 ms down to 8 ms. `project_waiting` stays for the app and flow-mcp. Each result was checked equal (an md5 over the ordered output, or zero mismatches over every edit and month). No decision or screen changes.
