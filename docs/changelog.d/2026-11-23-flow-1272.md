FLOW-1272: Review's first card needs two waves of reads after the tap, not three. The Jev connector's read starts with the list (the company is the one Home's cached dashboard names, or the company's own read when there is none), so the suggestions go out the moment the list's answer lands, without waiting for the read schemas; the `jev-review` chunk and the card's categories are requested when the list's read starts, not after its answer. Home's Jev bookings count and Review's queue are one `list_jev_bookings` read under one key, so Home's read serves Review. On the perf build with the stub backend (CPU 4×, 85 ms, 9/1.5 Mbps, 412×915) a Review visit that waits on Jev draws its first card at a median of 612 ms against 771 ms (12 visits each), and sends 12 reads in 2 waves, not 3. The bundle total is unchanged (2 bytes less), the Home entry 50 bytes more; nothing on any screen changes.
