A category can be deleted even when it has lines: its lines go back to review with no category, a line split by category loses its split, and the delete can be undone (`delete_category`, `restore_category`, MCP `delete_category` with undo `category_delete`). Loan categories and categories a loan uses are refused. All lines of a category can be moved to another without hiding it (`move_category_lines`, `undo_category_move`, MCP `move_category_lines` with undo `category_move`), and merge now also moves split loan parts and remembered supplier categories ([FLOW-405](backlog/TASKS.md#flow-405), [0144](decisions/0144-category-delete-and-move.md)).
