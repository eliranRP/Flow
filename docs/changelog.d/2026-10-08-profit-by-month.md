Profit by month, server side ([0129](decisions/0129-profit-by-month.md)). `get_project` and `list_project_category` take an optional `p_from`/`p_to`, so a project can show its own period; with the same range a project's profit matches its `list_projects` row. New `get_profit_months` returns the company's or one project's profit per calendar month and currency, newest first, and its months add up to `get_totals` / `get_project` for the range. MCP: `get_project` takes `from` and `to`, and `get_profit_months` is a new read tool. FLOW-409: on the cash basis the overhead split now weights by paid income, not invoices.
