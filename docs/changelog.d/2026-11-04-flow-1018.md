A forecast item's שמירת השינוי בצפי that changes a line's כל כמה זמן and also its amount or date now shows one toast, "הצפי עודכן" (or "<name> הועבר לחודש הבא"), whose ביטול undoes both: this month's change, then the pace (FLOW-1018). Before, the pace write's toast replaced it, so its ביטול undid only the pace. A pace pick alone keeps its own toast, and an amount or date change alone is unchanged. If the pace write fails, its failure shows and nothing is sent to undo it.
