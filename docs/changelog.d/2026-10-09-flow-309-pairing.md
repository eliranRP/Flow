A customer's SUMIT invoice and the receipt that pays it are now one review card instead of two. The receipt waits with its invoice, one approval files both with the same project and category, and ביטול takes both back. A receipt that arrives after its invoice was filed takes the invoice's filing on its own. The review list now says, for each invoice, which receipts paid it, whether they cover it, and the date of the last one, so the card can show "✓ שולם". The Flow assistant's list_review carries the same fields. The card's new line comes in a later app update.
