**FLOW-1173 to FLOW-1175 (UX review: utility refunds).** On החזרי חשבונות, the bill's drawer, נכנס סביב החשבון and a property's bills by type, a line of parts joined by "·" now breaks only between whole parts, so no line starts or ends with a dot at 320. The drawer's history head names its category whole ("חשמל בנכס") with the bill count. Around a bill, the bill and each payment follow the row rule: a title (the supplier, the payer), the amount and one line, the payment's other parts a second line at most; when nothing came in, the page offers the 3-month range or the bill's transaction. On the bills by type, the supplier is the row's title and the date leads its line.
