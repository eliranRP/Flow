The server now has a monthly cash view (תזרים): for each month, the money that came in and went out, counted as it moved in the bank (with VAT), and the month's profit next to it. The whole loan payment counts as money out, including the principal that profit leaves out. Transfers between the company's own accounts, credit card bill payments and money received from a loan are left out by default, and a new income category, כסף שהתקבל מהלוואות, holds loan money. The owner can take a category or a single line out of the view, and choose whether a month counts by payment date (the default) or invoice date. Each figure opens a list of its lines. Home's new cash figure comes in a later app update.
