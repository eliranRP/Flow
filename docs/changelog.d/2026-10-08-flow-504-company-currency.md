Each company has a stored currency (`base_currency`, ILS or USD; all-dollar companies start as USD). Its row comes first in every per-currency list, it is the default for a new loan, a new project's investment and MCP `get_breakdown`, and Home's net profit, the previous period and the overhead share now come in it too (`net_profit_minor`, `prev_*_minor`, `overhead_share_minor`). The owner changes it with `set_company_currency`, the MCP with `set_company_currency` and undo `company_currency`. Nothing is converted ([FLOW-504](backlog/TASKS.md#flow-504), [0146](decisions/0146-company-currency.md)).
