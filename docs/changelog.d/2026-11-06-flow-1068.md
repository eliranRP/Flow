A property's investment page shows ROI and more of the book's deal metrics (FLOW-1068, [0261](decisions/0261-roi-and-deal-metrics.md)). A renovation's expected profit is now the net profit: the ARV less the costs of selling (8% of the ARV until the owner sets them), the purchase, the renovation and the holding costs. Its band shows the ROI on the money in the deal, and "מאיפה הרווח" shows all four costs. The מדדים tab groups the metrics: a rental's return (ROI since purchase, return on equity, cash on cash, equity multiplier), risk (DSCR, break-even occupancy, loan to value) and price (cap rate, gross rent multiplier, expense ratio, cash flow per unit); a renovation's return (ROI, ROI per year, margin on the ARV) and price (the 70% rule, the break-even sale price). Each opens its calculation page. MCP: `set_project_investment` takes `selling_cost_bp`, and `get_property_returns` returns `deal` and the new metrics.
