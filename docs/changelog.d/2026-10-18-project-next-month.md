A project's profit page (ProfitProject, by month) now shows a dashed card for next month above the month rows: the expected income less expenses, the two totals, and what the forecast is made of. It opens a new page, the ProfitNext frame (`/projects/:projectId/profit/next`), with the expected income and expenses, the project's average monthly share of general expenses (its share since the start over its months), the profit after it, and the expected rows. The rows are the project's recurring charges due that month (by their pace and next due month, on their typical day) and, for each expense category no recurring charge of the project files to, its average over the last six complete months. The card shows only when the project expects something next month. It reads `expected_months` and `project_category_months`; no new server read.
