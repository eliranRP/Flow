A recurring charge counts only its own parts of a split line, and the owner can set its usual amount (decision 0189). A rent transfer split into rent, electricity and water now counts its rent part for the property's charge, a kept-out part such as a tenant deposit never counts, and a part for another property counts for that property's charge; the usual amount, this month's arrival, the change percent, the line page's comparison, late bills and expected months all follow. On the recurring charge page (קבועים › a charge), "הסכום הרגיל" opens a sheet to set the usual amount ("קבעת" when set, "חזרה לחישוב" to clear it), with an undo toast. A charge fed only by another property's split transfer can be switched on, ended and paced from its own page: `set_payment_recurring` and `set_payment_pace` take an optional project, and MCP `set_line_recurring` and `set_line_pace` an optional `project_id`. An owner's amount in a charge's first month raises no change alert. New RPC `set_payment_amount`, MCP `set_line_usual_amount` (undo kind `line_amount`, optional `project_id`); `get_line_recurring` and `get_recurring_schedule` report `typical_amount_source`.
