A property's purchase price now counts a loan paid out at closing without passing through the bank, such as a mortgage taken over in a Sub2 deal or a hard-money loan the lender paid to the title company, so forced equity and the building cost are right for those deals; the money in the deal stays cash only (FLOW-454, decision [0246](decisions/0246-sub2-price-and-one-unit.md)). A property has one unit until the owner sets another number.
