**FLOW-1171 (perf queue).** Company profit's next-month read (`company_next_month_inputs`, also Home's future months, the year view, the overhead project's page and flow-mcp's `get_company_next_month_inputs`) answered 177 KB in about 365 ms in the database on the QA company; most of it was never read. Each project category now comes back as the four fields the forecast builds from (id, name, currency, months_minor) instead of eleven, and every project's lines are read in one pass (`private.project_category_months_lean`) instead of one call per project: the projects' part went from 102 KB and 93 ms to 50 KB and 54 ms, with the same ids, names and months (md5 of the ordered projection equal on 14 projects and 382 categories); `project_category_months` and the project page keep all eleven fields. A new `p_rest` argument (default true, the answer as before) lets next month's card ask without `rest_edits` (59 KB, about 120 ms): the answer is 66 KB and about 205 ms for the month view and Home's next month, and 125 KB and about 325 ms for the year view, the since-start view and any later month, which keep `rest_edits` under their own cache key, so a slim answer never reaches a view that needs it. The month view also reads next month's overrides only, not every month left in the year. flow-mcp's tool and its spec are unchanged. `company_next_month_inputs(date)` is replaced by `company_next_month_inputs(date, boolean)`; decision 0230 is the goal.
