The SUMIT sync checks for schema drift: when more than 1 in 20 of the CRM rows it should read cannot be mapped (a field SUMIT renamed or retyped), or a page comes back without its data or row list, it stops before it writes, so the full sweep does not void the documents those rows stand for. The last good ledger stays, `sumit_status` shows `sync_schema_drift`, the next try waits 15 minutes, and the function log names the broken fields (never values). Decision 0161 (FLOW-510).
