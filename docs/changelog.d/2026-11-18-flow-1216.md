FLOW-1216: the company's next-month inputs (the Company profit forecast read, `company_next_month_inputs`) are about a quarter faster in the database. Profiled as Flow QA, read-only: of its 178 ms (without `rest_edits`), about 58 ms is the projects' category months, about 115 ms the general costs, and the general costs' 98 ms was mostly finding which lines belong to a recurring charge, which it did by building every recurring charge in full (usual amount, last bill, loan flag, category) when it needed only which charges recur. A new private function, `recurring_charge_lines_of_projects`, reads the lines once and answers just that; on the QA company it returns the same 421 lines in 43 ms instead of 98 ms, and the whole read measured 141 ms instead of 194 ms with `rest_edits` and 130 ms instead of 183 ms without. The answer is identical, byte for byte, so no app change. The project category months (58 ms) and the 127 KB of bytes (`rest_edits` 61 KB, `projects` 53 KB) are left for their own tasks. Affects Company profit and the year view (decision 0230).
