FLOW-1216: the company's next-month inputs (the Company profit forecast read, `company_next_month_inputs`) are about a quarter faster in the database. Profiled as Flow QA, read-only: of its 178 ms (without `rest_edits`), about 58 ms is the projects' category months, about 115 ms the general costs. Inside the general costs, the step that finds which lines belong to a recurring charge took 98 ms as a whole (timed apart, its two pieces were the stream-lines read, 22 ms, and `recurring_parties`, 81 ms). It building every recurring charge in full (usual amount, last bill, loan flag, category) when it needed only which charges recur. A new private function, `recurring_charge_lines_of_projects`, reads the lines once and answers just that; on the QA company it returns the same 421 lines in 43 ms instead of 98 ms, and the whole read took 141 ms instead of 194 ms with `rest_edits` and 130 ms instead of 183 ms without. These after figures are an emulation (the new bodies run as DO blocks, means of 10, interleaved), because a read-only transaction cannot create the function; the steward re-measures the real function after the deploy. The answer is identical, byte for byte, so no app change. The project category months (58 ms) and the 127 KB of bytes (`rest_edits` 61 KB, `projects` 53 KB) are left for their own tasks. Affects Company profit and the year view (decision 0230).
