The company forecast in the database (FLOW-1307, slice 6 of the FLOW-1275 plan). New `public.company_forecast(p_months, p_detail, p_today)` (security invoker, execute for `authenticated` and `service_role`) builds Profit's next-month card and year view, Home's future months and the company forecast pages in one read, for any months of the forecast (next month to 12 months ahead, answered in the order asked). `'totals'` answers per month the income, expenses, net, the loans' cash no row holds and the counts the cards' notes read (projects and general costs with a row; income rows, recurring ones and rent), plus the income streams once over the months; `'rows'` adds each part's rows, removed, arrived and loan cash, the skipped rows and the split parties, rows listed by key for the app to sort. absent = null; a bad `p_detail` or month is refused (22023). It gathers `company_next_month_inputs`, `expected_months(0)`, the open loans with their balances, rates and stored schedules (FLOW-1306), the categories and the company currency, with `p_today` (null is today in Israel) for every read. `private.company_forecast_build(jsonb)` is the arithmetic of `nextMonthForecast`, `applyForecastEdits`, `cashForecast`, the loan parts, `skipEdits`, `companyNextMonthForecast`, `companyGeneralForecast` and each month's edits as one immutable function that reads no table, building each loan's payments left once per call (FLOW-1304's rule); it gives the figures the app's builders give for 63 shared vectors (`packages/shared/fixtures/vectors/company-forecast`, run by `app/src/company-forecast-vectors.test.ts` and the generated `vectors_company_forecast.test.sql`). `private.loan_payments_left` now holds the one port of the payments-left rule, and `private.loan_outlook_build` (FLOW-1310) calls it with the same answer. Locally, on Flow QA-shaped data (26 parts, 22 loans, 108 recurring parties), one month takes about 170 ms and 12 months of totals about 200 ms, warm.
