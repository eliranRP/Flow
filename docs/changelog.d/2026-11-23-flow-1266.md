FLOW-1266: a recurring charge can have an end date. On the charge's page (קבועים › a charge), a "תאריך סיום" row opens a sheet of presets (after the next charge, in 3 months, in 6 months, at the end of the year), "תאריך אחר" for an exact date, and "ללא תאריך סיום"; the toast offers ביטול. The charge is not expected on a due day after its end date (the forecast, next month and the late-bill alerts stop there), and once the date has passed it leaves קבועים. Marking it recurring again clears a passed end date. New: `recurring_overrides.ends_on`, `set_payment_end`, and the MCP tool `set_line_end_date` (undo kind `line_end`).
