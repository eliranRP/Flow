Home's pending card has a row for each thing that waits (FLOW-321, from the 2026-10-07 tap-count review). Before, the card linked only to Review while the queue had items, so unpaid invoices were out of reach from Home. Now, when both exist, one tinted card holds two rows: "N פריטים ממתינים לאישור" opens Review, and "N חשבוניות לא שולמו" with "₪x · טרם נגבה" under it opens Unpaid, each its own link with its own accessible name and pressed state, separated by padding with no hairline ([0120](../decisions/0120-income-green-type-scale.md)). When only one exists the card is the plain one-row banner. A count of 1 reads "פריט אחד ממתין לאישור" and "חשבונית אחת לא שולמה". The unpaid row stays hidden while the unpaid list loads or fails. The shared `BannerRows` component in `app/src/ui/banner.tsx` draws the card, with Banner and Home stories for both rows, review only, unpaid only and one of each, in light, dark and 320. No migration and no MCP change.
