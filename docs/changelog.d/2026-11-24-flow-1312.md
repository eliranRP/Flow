FLOW-1312: the MCP reads the forecasts from the database. `get_forecast` now reads `month_forecast` (one read, in place of the four it built the forecast from; the month's cash net still comes from `cash_months`) and answers exactly what it answered before: the same items in the same order, the company's currency on each, nothing expected on a day and nothing late. It takes an optional `today`, which goes to the database as the day the charges are counted against. A new read tool, `get_company_forecast`, gives the company's forecast for one or more future months (next month to 12 months ahead) from `company_forecast`: totals by default, and the rows of a single month with `detail: "rows"`. A bad month, a repeated month, or rows for several months is refused as `validation`, and the database's own refusals map to `validation`, `forbidden` or `refused`. The old tools, `get_company_next_month_inputs` and `get_expected_months` among them, are unchanged, so an assistant's saved instructions keep working.
