**FLOW-1204, FLOW-1205 (perf queue, the projects list and the project page).** Three reads stop computing or sending what no screen reads. What each screen shows, once its reads land, is unchanged.
- `projects_cash_month` returns `projects[].pending_count` and `projects[].deletable` (the project menu's two figures: two passes over the open review queue and suggested lines, and `project_is_empty`'s index probes per project) only with `p_project`. The Projects list asks without it and has neither. The menu reads its own row, which now always goes out when a project page opens, and starts on a project row's touch (with the project's own read, as the cash is): the list's row paints the page at once, and the menu's figures arrive with that read. Until it lands the menu shows a placeholder where the delete row goes and "בודקים שורות ממתינות…" in the finish sheet; if it fails, both say so with ניסיון חוזר. Also left out, with no reader: `projects[].group_id` and the top-level `month` and `basis`.
- `get_project` without lines (the app's read) leaves out `from`, `to` and `hidden` on each `investment.rehab_by_category` row; the rows stay, so the returns screen reads them from the same one lean read as the other pages. With lines (flow-mcp's default) the answer is exactly what it was.
- `expected_months` with `p_months` 0 (the app's read) leaves out `recurring[].months_seen`, `recurring[].source` and the top-level `project_id`. With 1 to 13 months (flow-mcp's `get_expected_months`) the answer is what it was.

Checked with pgTAP against pg_temp copies of the three previous bodies: the answers equal the old ones less exactly those fields, for a company with waiting lines, a shared line, a split, dollars, a period and both bases; and with one project the menu's two figures are the old list's, project by project.

Measured locally, not in production: a scratch company of 1,900 lines and 40 projects, 217 lines waiting in the review queue, called as the owner role on a shared machine; old and new bodies alternated, 8 rounds of 20 calls, the per-call minimum and median of the 8 rounds. `projects_cash_month` for the list: 26.6 to 23.4 ms (minimum) and 33.7 to 28.8 ms (median), and 16,413 to 14,023 bytes (-14.6%); for one project: 19.7 to 22.4 ms and 24.1 to 24.4 ms (no saving; it computes the two figures now as it did) and 554 to 496 bytes. `get_project` and `expected_months` 0 database times are the same within the noise (the medians moved by 1 to 2 ms either way; the machine's run-to-run spread was about that). The two menu figures cost about 0.85 ms (`project_is_empty` over the 40 projects) and 1.1 ms (the two waiting passes) timed alone. Bytes on the pgTAP fixtures: `get_project` lean for a house with three rehab categories 1,952 to 1,876 (1,666 to 1,574 with a period; the rehab rows stay, less their hidden flag); `expected_months` 0 with two charges 814 to 722 (484 to 394 for one project); the list of 4 projects 1,793 to 1,523. The production database time for `projects_cash_month` as Flow QA's owner, before and after, is for the perf steward after the deploy.
