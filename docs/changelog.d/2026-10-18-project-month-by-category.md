A project's month profit page (ProfitMonth) has a new "לפי קטגוריה" view, the ProfitMonthCategory frame, and the company profit page's "לפי קטגוריה" (ProfitByCategory) now uses the same rows: the counted lines in two groups, income then expenses, each head with its total, and one row per category, largest first, with its share of the group, its number of lines (and, on the company page, its number of projects when above one), its total (income with a plus, in the income colour) and a bar scaled to the group's largest category. Tapping a row opens it in place to its lines, newest first (on the company page each line names its day and project), and tapping it again closes it; one category is open at a time. On the month page it replaces the view where each category was a group head with its sum; on the company page it replaces the single list of categories ordered by size across income and expenses.
