A project's month profit page (ProfitMonth) has a new "לפי קטגוריה" view, the ProfitMonthCategory frame: the month's counted lines in two groups, income then expenses, each head with its total, and one row per category, largest first, with its share of the group, its number of lines, its total (income with a plus, in the income colour) and a bar scaled to the group's largest category. Tapping a row opens it in place to its lines, newest first, and tapping it again closes it; one category is open at a time. It replaces the earlier view, where each category was a group head with its sum on the page's side.
