FLOW-941 (decision [0205](decisions/0205-transfer-match-suggestions.md), builds on 0203): a transfer between two Mercury connections (two organisations) that Mercury could not match is suggested, and the user decides. When money goes out on one connection and the same amount comes in on another within 3 days, both lines still counted in the profit, Home's "דורש טיפול" shows "N העברות לבדיקה" (pairs from the last 90 days; the MCP lists every pair). It opens העברות לבדיקה (`/transfers`): one card per pair with both lines (account, organisation, day, amount) and "זו העברה בין החשבונות שלכם?". "כן, העברה" files both lines as העברות (created again if the company removed it), out of the profit and the cash view, and keeps them out of לאישור; "לא, צירוף מקרים" stops the suggestion; both toast with ביטול. The pairs are worked out on read (the sync does nothing new) and only for a company with two or more chosen connections. **Migration:** `20261021083034_transfer_matches.sql` (`transfer_matches`; `list_transfer_matches(p_all)`; `count_transfer_matches`; `answer_transfer_match`, owner or editor, null takes the answer back; `sync_review_queue` skips a confirmed pair's lines; `mcp_answer_transfer_match` and the undo kind `transfer_match`). Edge function `flow-mcp` changes and deploys with it. New MCP tools: `list_transfer_matches`, `confirm_transfer_match` and `dismiss_transfer_match` (with undo).
