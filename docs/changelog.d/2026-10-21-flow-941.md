FLOW-941 (decision [0205](decisions/0205-transfer-match-suggestions.md), builds on 0203): a transfer between two Mercury connections (two organisations) that Mercury could not match is suggested, and the user decides. When money goes out on one connection and the same amount comes in on another within 3 days, both lines still counted in the profit, Home's "דורש טיפול" shows "N העברות לבדיקה". It opens העברות לבדיקה (`/transfers`): one card per pair with both lines (account, organisation, day, amount) and "זו העברה בין החשבונות שלכם?". "כן, העברה" files both lines as העברות, out of the profit and the cash view, and sets their reviews aside; "לא, צירוף מקרים" stops the suggestion; both toast with ביטול. The pairs are worked out on read (the sync does nothing new) and only for a company with two or more chosen connections. **Migration:** `20261021083034_transfer_matches.sql` (`transfer_matches`; `list_transfer_matches`; `answer_transfer_match`, owner or editor, null takes the answer back; `mcp_answer_transfer_match` and the undo kind `transfer_match`). Edge function `flow-mcp` changes and deploys with it. New MCP tools: `list_transfer_matches`, `confirm_transfer_match` and `dismiss_transfer_match` (with undo).
