FLOW-449 (decision 0196): next month's forecast is one shared card and one income and expense chart. On a project's profit page (לפי חודש) the card replaces the one-line "נובמבר · צפי" row and the chart of the last six profit months and the forecast sits under it; on the project page they replace חודשים קודמים, with the project's cash months (the read now takes six), the net named תזרים צפוי and a "רווח לפי חודש ›" link to the profit page. The card's figures are the צפי page's forecast; its basis line ("2 קבועים ו־4 ממוצעים") is gone with the forecast's unused counts. New tokens `--color-chart-expense` and `--radius-chart-bar`; CONTROLS lists the card and the link.

On the project page the card's הוצאות צפויות and its תזרים צפוי net, and the chart's forecast pair, are now real cash: they add the rest of the project's loan payments due next month that the profit leaves out (the principal, and interest or escrow kept out of the profit, such as a flip's hard-money interest) to the צפי page's forecast; a loan whose every row the owner took out on the צפי page adds nothing (the owner's decision, recorded in 0196). The card's two figures step down from card size until each fits its column, so six-figure amounts fit at 320, and next month's chart label wraps under itself instead of running into the month before it. The צפי page and the profit page's רווח צפוי stay on the profit basis.
