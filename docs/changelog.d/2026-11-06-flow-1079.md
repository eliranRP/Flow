A recurring charge or income can be taken out of this month's forecast (FLOW-1079). On its page, a switch "לא צפוי ב<month>" takes it out of this month only; the next month expects it again. On Home, a row in נותר לשלם swipes to "לא החודש", with ביטול in the toast; the first time, the first row slides open by itself and back, to show the swipe. What was taken out moves to "הוצא מהצפי", crossed out, and swipes back from there. Both use the forecast's existing "לא החודש", so Home's band, the צפי page and the list agree.
