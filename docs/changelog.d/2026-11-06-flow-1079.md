A recurring charge or income can be taken out of one month's forecast (FLOW-1079), this month or any month ahead. On its page, a switch "לא צפוי ב<month>" takes it out of the month it is next expected in; the other months expect it as usual. On Home, a row in נותר לשלם, or in a month ahead, swipes to "לא החודש", with ביטול in the toast; the first time, the first row slides open by itself and back, to show the swipe. What was taken out moves to "הוצא מהצפי", crossed out, and swipes back from there. Both use the forecast's existing "לא החודש", so Home's band, the צפי page and the list agree.
