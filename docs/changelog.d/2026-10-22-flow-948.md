A double-entry ledger now runs underneath the books as a foundation, without changing a single P&L number. New `public.accounts` (typed income/expense/asset/liability, one bridge per category plus system accounts like Bank/Clearing, Loan principal, Escrow and VAT control) and `public.legs` (a signed `amount_minor`, debit positive / credit negative) mirror every posted line as balanced legs: each transaction's legs sum to zero with one bank leg carrying the statement movement. The legs are derived from, and reconciled against, the live P&L — a projection helper and pgTAP prove the legs project back to `private.pnl_lines` exactly, per line, category and project, across plain, split, loan, reversal, gross-up (normal and contra), kept-out, overhead, shared and VAT lines in ILS and USD. `private.pnl_lines` and every consumer (cash, breakdown, company P&L, projects) are byte-unchanged and stay authoritative; the legs stay in step through triggers on the existing tables, and the write RPCs are unchanged. A new MCP `book_line` tool books a line from a balanced set of legs, writing the equivalent legacy rows so the P&L reflects it, and refuses rather than mis-books.
