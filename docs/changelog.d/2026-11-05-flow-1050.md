FLOW-1050: pull to refresh now feels like iOS (DESIGN-RULES ld-07). The round mark is replaced by the iOS activity indicator, 28px as in the reference: 12 thin spokes that appear one by one as you pull and turn in steps while Flow refreshes, then fade and shrink. The screen follows the finger closely at first and then with growing resistance, with no hard stop; past the threshold a light haptic tick plays once where the phone supports it. Let go there and the screen eases to a short rest under the spinner, then glides back when the data is in; a touch during that glide catches the screen where it is. Two motion tokens join the system: `--motion-pull-spin` (880ms) and `--motion-pull-return` (400ms). A new Playwright check, `app/e2e/pull-refresh.spec.ts`, confirms the screen never moves faster than 12px per 60Hz frame over a pull and its release.
