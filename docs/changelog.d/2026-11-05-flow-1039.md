FLOW-1039: pull to refresh on every screen (DESIGN-RULES ld-07). In the installed app, a pull down from the top of any screen brings down a round spinner that follows the finger at half the move; let go past 64px and every read on screen refreshes, the spinner spinning until they settle (at least 400ms), and a failed read shows "הרענון נכשל. נסו שוב." once. Let go sooner and it springs back with nothing read. It never starts in a sheet, a field, a swiped row, a scrolled inner list, a sideways swipe or the edge-back swipe, and a browser tab keeps its own pull to refresh. With reduced motion the spinner fades in place. One shared `PullRefresh` (`app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx`), mounted once in the app shell. The app starts no bank sync on open (the server syncs on its own schedule), so a pull starts none either.
