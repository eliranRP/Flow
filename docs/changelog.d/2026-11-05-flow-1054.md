Reports now match the design to the pixel. The report pages set their text at the design's tighter line height, so names, lines and heads read smaller and closer together; the band's income and expenses are bold 20 with no plus sign; each row of a profit and loss or balance sheet shows its line on one line with a thin bar for its share of the group, and a profit reads in green; the bottom line sits under a heavy rule; the review card and the view control sit 16px apart. On the hub the period is an outlined button with its arrow, the cards' labels are in the secondary colour, both cards' charts are the same height and the breakdown rows keep their line on one line. The account and lines pages use the same type sizes.
