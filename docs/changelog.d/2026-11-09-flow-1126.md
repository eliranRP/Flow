The member report's forecast has its data and calculation (FLOW-1126, decision 0264):
- each property's starting point: the scheduled rent, the last 12 months of costs by category, and its loans;
- the assumptions, saved with the report;
- one calculation of each year and of a refinance in a chosen year, used by the app and by MCP `get_member_projection`.
