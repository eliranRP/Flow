FLOW-141: the month-end forecast (צפי). Home's forecast line opens a screen with the items still to come (recurring parties not yet seen, open customer invoices), two flat sections with totals, the small charges in one row, and "לא החודש". Tapping a row opens a sheet to change its amount, its day, or move it to next month; the change is saved per company until the month ends, never edits the invoice, bill or charge, and איפוס puts Flow's numbers back. MCP: `get_forecast`, `set_forecast_item` and `reset_forecast` (decision 0080 rules: idempotency, rate limit, undo kinds `forecast_item` and `forecast_reset`). Migration `20261016173728_forecast_overrides` adds `forecast_overrides` with RLS and the cross-tenant pgTAP test. Decision: the items and totals come from one shared builder (`packages/shared/src/month-forecast.ts`), so the app and the MCP read agree with Home's number.
