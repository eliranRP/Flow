FLOW-1221: Company profit's month view now has its search field (חיפוש בתנועות) in the header under the period switch, so it is visible at the top without scrolling and the found lines start right below the header. While a search shows lines, the order chips (לפי פרויקט / קטגוריה / תאריך / סכום), the next-month card and the chart are hidden; clearing the search brings them back and the chosen order is kept. The month figure and the summary stay. The year and since-the-start views are unchanged.
