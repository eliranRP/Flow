The pre-push gate now stops a hung phase instead of waiting forever. The Storybook tests get 10 minutes, the Storybook smoke 30, and the e2e specs 15 (30 under `--full`); past that, the gate names the phase and fails. When the gate ends for any reason (pass, fail, Ctrl-C, or kill), it also stops whatever it left running, such as a `wrangler pages dev` or a Playwright server, so the next gate can't hang on it. `FLOW_LOCAL_CI_BUDGET=0` turns the budgets off. No migration.
