Prepaid costs spread themselves (decision [0233](../decisions/0233-prepaid-schedules.md)). A payment that covers many months, such as a yearly insurance premium, is filed to Prepaid with one MCP call, create_prepaid_schedule, and Flow posts each month's share to the expense on the property as a journal entry, with no cash moved. A daily job posts what is due, a past start catches up at once, and refunds, changes and an early end touch only the months ahead. A books-closed date (set_books_closed_through, owner only) stops prepaid changes to months already closed.
