The ledger is now the only booking model (decision [0232](../decisions/0232-ledger-only-books.md)). A line's split by category and its loan payment parts are stored as its ledger legs. The old split tables, the legacy P&L view and the rollback diff are gone. A split that no longer matches its line is kept as a draft and the line counts whole, as before. No P&L, cash or loan figure moves; the migration checks every company leg by leg before it drops anything.
