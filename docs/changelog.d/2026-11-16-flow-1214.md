FLOW-1214: a forecast skip, reset or undo now moves the month's total at once. The write patches the `skips` the cached company inputs carry (the full answer and the slim one, seeded from it) with `setQueryData`, so the company next-month card and the overhead project's totals follow the tap, before the write answers. The app no longer refetches the company inputs after a skip, a reset or an undo (one `company_next_month_inputs` read fewer each time). A failed write puts the patch back, and the existing error toast and undo stay. A reset clears that month's skips, and the undo of a reset puts them back. Inactive company inputs are still dropped, as FLOW-1201 does, and the month forecast is still refetched. The change is in `app/src/forecast-month.ts`, with a new `apply` option on `app/src/use-write.ts` (the write's undo runs on failure). Tests: `app/src/forecast-skip-patch.test.tsx` (new, 5 cases) and `app/src/forecast-month-keys.test.tsx`, which now expects no inputs read on set or reset. All 5 new cases and those two updated cases fail on main.
