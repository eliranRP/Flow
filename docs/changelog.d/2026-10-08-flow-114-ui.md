The transaction card shows a loan payment as one row (FLOW-114, option B, the owner's pick). On a payment matched to a loan, the category row reads "תשלום הלוואה · <loan>" with "N חלקים" under it, and the "פיצול התשלום" list is gone from the card. The row opens a sheet with one amount per part (קרן, ריבית, מסים וביטוח, עמלות), the total, one "שמירה" that writes the whole split in one `save_loan_split` call, and a quiet "ביטול השיוך" that calls `clear_loan_split` ([0136](decisions/0136-loan-unmatch.md)). After an unmatch the category row and "שיוך להלוואה" come back, and the toast "השיוך בוטל · ביטול" puts the same parts back through `save_loan_split`. The category can't be changed while the line is matched, and "לפי קטגוריות" stays off. A new match also goes through `save_loan_split` instead of inserting the rows, and the one-tap "עדכון הפיצול" correction is replaced by the sheet, which opens with the schedule's split of a re-synced amount. The server's balance check shows as a toast. A match, an edit or an unmatch refreshes the P&L reads (Home, the project page, its category lines, the breakdowns and the months), the loan balances and the card. The card reads the split from `get_transaction`'s `loan_split` instead of a separate read; a viewer sees the row without the sheet.
