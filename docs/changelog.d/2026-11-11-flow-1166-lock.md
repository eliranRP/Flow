FLOW-1166 (part 2a): the books-closed date locks a closed period against a person's edits. A posted line dated on or before `companies.books_closed_through` takes no change to what a report shows, no new manual line and no delete from the app, the MCP tools or an undo (`private.books_closed_hit`, row triggers on `transactions`, `allocations` and `overhead`); a bulk action that reaches a closed line is refused whole. The app shows "הספרים סגורים עד {date}. כדי לשנות שורה מהתקופה הזו, הזיזו אחורה את תאריך הסגירה דרך עוזר ה־AI." with no retry. A sync still changes a closed line until the next part.
