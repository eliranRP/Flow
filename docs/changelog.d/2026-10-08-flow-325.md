Split a line by percent, not only exact amounts, and keep the rest where it was. MCP `split_line` (and the `save_line_split` RPC) parts now take `amount_minor`, `percent` (of the whole line, rounded together so the parts hit it to the cent) or `rest: true` (what the other parts leave, on the line's own category and project unless named). A part of the other kind is a reversal, so part of a refund can go back against the expenses it reverses on any project; it needs a project of its own. New refusals: `parts exceed the line`, `a part rounds to zero`, `nothing is left for the rest`, `line has no category for the rest`, `a reversal part needs a project` (FLOW-325).
