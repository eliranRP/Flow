Each loan has its own page under Settings › הלוואות (FLOW-106, FLOW-110, decision 0161). It shows the balance and status, the kind, the rate in force and its changes, the monthly payment, the project, the category of each part, and the last 3 attached payments, each linked to its bank row. Kind, rate changes, project, status and part categories each save from a small sheet with ביטול in the toast. Marking a loan paid off or closed picks a date from the last attached payment on. Delete names how many payments go back to their category, and ביטול restores the loan. The list keeps loans alphabetical and puts paid-off and closed loans under a collapsed "נסגרו (N)". A new loan's toast opens its page. A viewer can read the loan page.
