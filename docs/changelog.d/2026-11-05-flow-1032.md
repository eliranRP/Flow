FLOW-1032: Jev splits a loan payment instead of filing it under one loan category ([0254](../decisions/0254-jev-loan-split.md)). In auto mode, when Jev's category for a servicer's payment is a loan category, the tagging job finds the one open loan whose payment (or last attached payment) equals the line and fills the schedule's interest, escrow and principal; the line still waits in לאישור, and `undo_jev_prefill` takes the split back. Jev never fills a loan category on a whole line again. New service-role RPCs `jev_loan_candidates` and `jev_prefill_loan`; `jev_prefills.loan_id`.
