Team members, server side (FLOW-601, decision 0167). A user can own several companies and be an editor or a viewer of other people's. The owner invites by email; the invitee sees the invite after signing in with Google and joins, declines, or leaves it for later. Editors file and change the books; the team, the company's name and currency, connectors and Jev settings stay the owner's. The app picks the company it shows with an `x-flow-company` header, so two phones can show two companies. MCP: `list_team`, `invite_member`, `set_member_role` and `remove_member`, each write with undo. The screens come in the app PR.
