The loan schedule flags a balloon when a payment entered by hand is below the annuity on an `interest_only` or `balloon` loan (unless a rate change recast it), `ratioToPayment` compares principal and interest without escrow, and dates before the year 1000 keep four digits and the right leap years. On an `interest_only` loan whose interest-only months are the term, `list_loans` shows the interest plus escrow as `payment_minor`, not the bullet due at the term.
