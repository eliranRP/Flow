Three fixes for the AI tools. A journal entry that books income, such as a tenant's deposit kept as rent, now counts in the property's profit on both bases. A category kept out of the profit can now be tied to the balance-sheet account it belongs to (for example tenants' deposits to "Security deposit held"), and an entry that would have put money on Suspense is refused with a clear reason. A loan payment taken off a loan can be put back on it again.
