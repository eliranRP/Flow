FLOW-174: a posted journal entry can be corrected in place. MCP `update_journal_entry` (owner or editor) re-books its legs, date, description or reference under the same id, with `create_journal_entry`'s rules, `expected_version` and a required reason; the books-closed date and a prepaid month's entry are refused. Every version replaced is kept, and `get_journal_entry_history` lists them with who wrote each one, when and why. Undo kind `journal_entry_update` puts the version before back. `get_line_legs`, `get_expense` and `create_journal_entry` give the entry's `version` (decision 0234).
