FLOW-974: each on-demand screen's styles load with its chunk. The rules only an on-demand screen can match moved from `app/src/ui/css/` and `setup.css` into `app/src/ui/css-lazy/`, in the same order, and `app/src/ui/lazy.css` loads them. Every module the app loads with `import()` imports it first, so Vite loads the stylesheet before the screen renders; `scripts/check-lazy-css.mjs` fails a module that does not (in `pr (types)`). Home's first load goes from 244.4 to 216.0 KB gzip (its stylesheet from 47.5 to 18.9 KB), and `homeEntryGzipKB` from 246 to 221 (about 5 KB of room). A slow-network e2e check (`lazy-css.spec.ts`) proves three screens draw only after their styles arrive.
