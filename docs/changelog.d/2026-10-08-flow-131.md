MCP `list_loans` shows the loan payments waiting for review. Each loan now has `flagged_parts` (how many loan parts are flagged, for example a payment the bank sync posted past the balance) and `flagged_transaction_ids` (their lines, sorted, `[]` when none). A flagged part does not lower `balance_minor` until the split is corrected in the app. A new two-session pgTAP test (dblink) checks the loan lock order of decision 0121 (FLOW-131).
