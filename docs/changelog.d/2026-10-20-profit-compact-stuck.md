The company profit page's compact bar and chips panel (רווח • figure, לפי חודש | לפי פרויקט) no longer stay down at the top of the page after the 2026 or since-the-start view opens. The header's mark moves to a new node when the panel comes or goes (the year's skeleton before its read lands, back from a project's profit page, or since the start, which has no chips), and the shared `useScrolledPast` hook kept watching the removed node, which the browser reports as a zero rect above the bar. The hook now takes the mark node from a callback ref, so it observes the node on the page, ignores a removed one, and the bar shows only once the current mark scrolls off. Every stacked header with a Back (`ScreenHeader`), the Projects list and the project page share the fix.
