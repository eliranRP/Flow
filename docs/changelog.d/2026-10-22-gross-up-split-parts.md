A `split_line` part can now be a gross-up part: an opposite-sign item that nets against the line — gross loan proceeds (+income) minus a real closing cost (expense) on a deposit, or a positive credit on a payment — unlike a reversal/refund, which reduces the other side. A gross-up part keeps a positive `amount_minor` with a new `gross_up` flag; the parts' signed sum (a gross-up part subtracted) equals the line, its category is the opposite kind of the line, and it names its own project; v1 is an exact amount, VAT-free. The P&L counts a gross-up part as a real item of its category's kind (so a financed deal's closing cost raises the expense total, not lowers it), across the company P&L, cash view, projects and breakdown, with a "paid from deposit" tag in the drill-down. MCP `split_line` takes `gross_up` per part and `get_expense` returns it. Decision [0206](decisions/0206-gross-up-split-parts.md); FLOW-946. One migration (`20261022063554_gross_up_split_parts.sql`).
