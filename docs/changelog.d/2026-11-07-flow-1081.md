Taking a charge out of one month (FLOW-1079) now works on every screen that lists the month's forecast (FLOW-1081). On a project page, a row in the month's activity swipes to "לא החודש" and moves under "הוצא מהצפי", out of נותר לשלם; it swipes back from there. On תזרים צפוי, rows swipe out and back, the group of what was taken out reads "הוצא מהצפי" with "לא צפוי ב<month>" on each row, and "לא החודש" no longer says the charge moves to next month. A month ahead's פירוט ועריכה and a project's צפי page swipe a recurring row out of that month, and פירוט ועריכה lists what was taken out. On קבועים, a charge taken out of this month reads "לא צפוי ב<month>" and leaves עוד לשלם and the late cards.
