The investment page shows the IRR since the purchase (FLOW-1069): the yearly return on the owner's cash, by the date of every line in and out, with today's equity as the last flow (for a renovation with no value yet, the ARV less the selling costs and the loans). It is the first row of a rental's תשואה group and sits beside the ROI on a renovation; its page shows what went in, what came back and what is held, each opening where it comes from. `get_property_returns` returns `irr_bp` and the flows behind it. FLOW-1070: the holding costs of a renovation's net profit, and their line list, read a loan line's category before its part, as the rest of the returns do; sample mode rounds the ROI and the margin as the server does.
