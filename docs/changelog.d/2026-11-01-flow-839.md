A security scan agent: every day (Sunday to Friday) it checks the code that reached `main` since its last run, and on Saturday the whole repo. Stage 1 checks a list of known attacks written for Flow (`docs/security/known-attacks.md`); stage 2 applies the patterns behind past zero-days (`docs/security/zero-day-patterns.md`). It fixes what it confirms through the normal cycle, and keeps unfixed findings out of the public repo. Runbook: `docs/runbooks/security-scan.md` (FLOW-839).
