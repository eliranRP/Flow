The Loans list reads its outlooks from the database (FLOW-1316, slice 11 of the FLOW-1275 plan). The list and its band call `loan_outlooks(p_today)` (FLOW-1310) once, with today in Israel, instead of building each loan's schedule on the phone: a loan's next payment (the sum of its three parts) and its late mark come from the answer, and the band's 12-month figure is the sum of `ahead_minor` for the currency. A loan the answer leaves out (a demand loan, a closed one, or one with nothing left to pay) keeps its row with no next payment and no late mark. A failed read shows the usual failed state with retry. The list's `loans` read drops the rates embed (nothing on the list draws the rate) and the day each loan was added, and the `loan_unsplit_payments` read is gone from the list; `loans` and `loan_balances` stay. The loan page keeps its own schedule and the same loan list query for its loan choices. Kept data: the new key `["loans", company, "outlooks", day]` is kept and checked against the shared schema; a loan list kept before this change (its rows carry a next payment, late date and rate) is not used and is read again. The TypeScript builders stay as the reference the shared vectors check (and the dev samples build with). No screen changes. Docs: the plan's section 5 Loans row now says what stays and what leaves, and the `large-balance-near-1e15` vector's description says 10^15.
