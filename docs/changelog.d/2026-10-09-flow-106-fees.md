A loan payment can carry fees, cover several installments, or take exact parts ([0130](decisions/0130-loan-fees-installments.md), FLOW-106 part 3; amends [0128](decisions/0128-loan-part-categories.md)). A split may have a fourth part, `fees` (above zero, at most one). Fees have no default category (the owner's decision): they go to the attach call's `fees_category_id`, else the new `loans.fees_category_id`, else the attach is refused (`fees category required`). A fees category is any expense category, in or out of the P&L, and may flip sides; each fees part counts or stays out by its category. MCP `attach_loan_payment` takes `installments` (1 to 12 schedule rows from the first unpaid one), `fees` (taken off the line first), exact `parts` that must add up to the line (`parts don't add up`), and `fees_category_id`; `update_loan` sets the loan's `fees_category_id` and undo restores it; `list_loans` returns it with its name. The app's loan split shows a fees part as עמלות ("fees"). [TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md) describes the new inputs and refusals. FLOW-135 tracks the installments follow-ups.
