A loan payment can carry fees, cover several installments, or take exact parts ([0129](decisions/0129-loan-fees-installments.md), FLOW-106 part 3). A split may have a fourth part, `fees` (above zero, at most one), filed under the new `loans.fees_category_id` or the loan's interest category, and counted in the P&L like interest. MCP `attach_loan_payment` takes `installments` (1 to 12 schedule rows from the first unpaid one), `fees` (taken off the line first) and exact `parts` that must add up to the line (`parts don't add up`); `update_loan` sets `fees_category_id` and undo restores it; `list_loans` returns it with its name. The app's loan split shows a fees part as עמלות ("fees"). [TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md) describes the new inputs and refusals.
