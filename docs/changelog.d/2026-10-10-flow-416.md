Loan money counts in the cash view (תזרים) by default for a new company: its כסף שהתקבל מהלוואות category is seeded in cash, still out of the P&L, and a loan proceeds category created later starts in cash too. Existing companies keep their current setting; nothing is backfilled. Decision [0171](../decisions/0171-loan-money-in-cash.md) amends [0168](../decisions/0168-cash-flow-view.md) (FLOW-416).
