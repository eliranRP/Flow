While pulling, the screen follows the finger and the gap above it takes the colour of its top, so on Home the purple band stretches down; the spinner sits in the gap, the tab bar and the screen's own bottom bars stay put, and nothing covers the screen's controls while it refreshes. A pull now re-reads all of Flow's data from the server: every cached read goes stale, so the reads on screen, the ones nested in them and other screens' reads come back new.
