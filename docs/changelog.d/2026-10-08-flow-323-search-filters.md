MCP `search_expenses` and `search_transactions` filter by date, income or expense, project and category, with `none` for lines on no project or with no category. A project matches the line's own project, its share of a shared cost and a split part; a category also matches split and loan parts. The text now finds income by customer, and each filed row says its currency, whether it waits for review, whether it is kept out, and how it is split ([FLOW-323](backlog/TASKS.md#flow-323), [0139](decisions/0139-search-filters.md)).
