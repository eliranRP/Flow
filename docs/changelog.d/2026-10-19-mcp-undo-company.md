The MCP `undo` tool undoes only a write made in the company the token is for. Before, it found the write by user alone, so someone removed from a company could undo a forecast item or a month's reset from it with a token for another of their companies, and the old company's overrides were written into the other one. Every MCP write now records its token's company (older writes take it from their token), and an undo of a write from another company is not found. Undo in the same company works as before.
