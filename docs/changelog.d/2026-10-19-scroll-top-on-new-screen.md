A screen opened from a scrolled page now starts at the top (iPhone Safari bug: the project month profit page, "רווח אוקטובר", and other inner pages opened with their first line under the compact bar). The browser keeps the window scroll across an in-app navigation, and `ScrollMemory` (`app/src/ui/back.tsx`) only restored saved spots on Back, so a new screen came up scrolled to its own end. `ScrollMemory` now starts every pushed or replaced screen at the top, before paint; Back and forward still restore each entry's spot (decision 0069), and a new entry on the same screen (a sheet, a period or filter in the query) keeps the scroll. The saved spot is read from scroll events, not at the switch, so it is never the clamped value. The per-screen resets on the transaction card's prev and next and on the forecast item page are gone.
