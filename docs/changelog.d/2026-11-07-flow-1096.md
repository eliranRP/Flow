A charge taken out of one month (FLOW-1079) now leaves that month in the year view too: the company profit page's rest of the year and its next-month card count it out (FLOW-1096). A charge has one key on every screen, so putting it back once restores it everywhere. On פירוט ועריכה, a row put back returns to its project at once, with the sums. A project whose only charge was taken out of a month ahead still lists it under "הוצא מהצפי", so it can be put back.
