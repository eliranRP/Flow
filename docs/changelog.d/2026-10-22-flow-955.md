The ledger's composer and the review of Jev's bookings (UI PR B of the ledger screens the owner approved on 2026-10-22). A line's פקודת יומן card now ends with "עריכה ›" for an owner or editor, which opens the composer at `/transactions/:id/journal`: the bank line stays as it is ("שורה 1 · מהתנועה, לא משתנה"), and every other line is an account (the bookable accounts by type, with the system accounts in Hebrew), a project, an amount and חובה or זכות, with "+ שורה נוספת" adding the short side. The entry starts from "✦ הצעה של Jev" (the most common entry among the same supplier's or customer's last 20 booked lines with a split, scaled to this line, "על בסיס 3 תנועות קודמות של <party>"), from "כמו תנועה קודמת" (a sheet of that party's last 10 such lines with their חובה and זכות; a pick copies the entry, its amounts scaled, "הסכומים הותאמו"), or from scratch ("התחלה מאפס"). The status line says "מאוזן · $X בחובה ובזכות" or "חסרים $x בחובה|בזכות", and "שמירת הפקודה" stays off until the entry balances; it books the line through the new `save_line_journal`, which runs MCP `book_line`'s own rules (the body of `mcp_book_line` moved to a private function both call, its responses unchanged), and the toast's ביטול saves the split before back. Scaling rounds each leg to the cent and puts what rounding leaves on the largest leg, so the entry balances exactly. In לאישור, a booking an assistant made through the MCP (`book_line` or `split_line`) that still stands waits first, as "✦ בדיקת פקודת יומן של Jev" with the proposed entry: אישור keeps it, ביטול takes it back through MCP undo's own branch and the line returns to its normal assignment ("ביטול מסיר את הפקודה של Jev, והתנועה חוזרת לשיוך רגיל"). Bookings made before this change count as reviewed. New RPCs `save_line_journal`, `get_line_journal_suggestion`, `get_line_journal_copies`, `list_jev_bookings`, `approve_jev_booking`, `undo_jev_booking`; `private.mcp_writes` gains `reviewed_at` and `reviewed_by`.
