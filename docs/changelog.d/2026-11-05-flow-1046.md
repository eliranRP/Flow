The Loans tab follows the v3 design: a violet band says what is left to pay, with the next payment and the month's payments, and opens the summary; loans that need a check and payments due within ten days sit under דורש טיפול, which can be hidden; the פעילות / נסגרו switch docks into the bar as the page scrolls, and the loans sit in groups by kind. A loan's page leads with its band (the balance and two figures that fit its kind) and ⋯ to close it with ביטול; a loan whose payments wait for a check opens with הפרטים נכונים / עריכה; its payments page shows what was paid and what is still to come, year by year. The summary leads with what was paid, and a new loan starts with its kind (FLOW-1046).
