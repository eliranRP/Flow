A yearly, quarterly or 2-monthly recurring charge no longer shows in every month's תזרים צפוי (FLOW-930, decision 0199, amends 0175 point 1): the month forecast (app and MCP `get_forecast`) lists a recurring charge only when it is due that month, a late one included, by the same `dueInMonth` rule next month's forecasts and the expected_months cells use (now in `packages/shared`). And a non-monthly charge's usual amount and day come only from the months on its cycle, counted back from its last complete month (`recurring_parties`, `project_recurring_arrivals`; migration `20261020102512_recurring_cycle_usual_amount.sql`), so the monthly bills before a switch to yearly billing no longer pull it down. Monthly charges and the owner's usual amount are unchanged.
