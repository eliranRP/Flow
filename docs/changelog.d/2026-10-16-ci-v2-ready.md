CI v2, part 1: `bash scripts/local-ci.sh --part ready` runs the static, types and tests slices of a branch at the same time and writes a stamp for its exact head to `.git/flow-ci-stamp.md` (`scripts/ci-stamp.mjs`). A slice that fails, or never reports, fails the stamp. `CI_STAMP_PR=<number>` also posts the stamp as a comment on that PR. No GitHub check reads the stamp yet. See [ci-cd.md](../runbooks/ci-cd.md).
