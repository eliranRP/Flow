A loan can be deleted with `delete_loan` (owner) or MCP `delete_loan`. Its rate rows and the split parts of its payments go with it, so those payments count whole again under their own categories. `restore_loan` (the app's undo) or MCP undo `loan_delete` puts it all back. The loans list keeps a saved order (`reorder_loans`, MCP `reorder_loans` with undo `loan_order`), and MCP `list_loans` follows it ([FLOW-110](backlog/TASKS.md#flow-110), [0142](decisions/0142-loan-delete-and-order.md)).
