FLOW-865: every undo of a recurring switch puts back the end date the switch cleared, and leaves the owner's later word. Marking a charge recurring clears an end date that has passed (FLOW-1266). The app's ביטול on the transaction page and the charge page now writes that date back with `set_payment_end` after the switch. The MCP undo of `set_line_recurring` (kind `line_recurring`) no longer puts the date back when the owner cleared it after the write: `recurring_overrides.ends_on_set_at` says when the end date was last written or cleared. A handover yes (`answer_recurring_handover`) keeps the end date it cleared on the new payee's charge (`recurring_handovers.to_ends_on_prior`), and taking the yes back, as its MCP undo does, puts it back under the same rule and keeps the old charge's end date. [TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md) says so under set_line_end_date and answer_recurring_handover.
