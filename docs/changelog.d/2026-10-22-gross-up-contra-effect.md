A gross-up split part may now be a contra entry (decision [0207](decisions/0207-gross-up-contra-effect.md), extends [0206](decisions/0206-gross-up-split-parts.md)). It may carry either category kind: the opposite kind to the line stays a normal real item, and the same kind is now a contra entry that reduces its own category (an expense category can end as a credit, an income category is lowered), netting it. The effect is auto-derived from the category kind vs the line (no new column, no data migration) and reported by `split_line`/`get_expense` as `effect`; `split_line` drops the `category kind must match the part sign` rejection and accepts an optional explicit `effect`. The cash and signed-sum math are unchanged. Affects `split_line`, `get_expense`, the line-split editor, and the P&L drill-down.
