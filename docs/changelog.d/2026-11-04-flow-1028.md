The legal pages go live (FLOW-1028, decision [0252](decisions/0252-legal-pages-live.md)). `/terms` and `/privacy` show the full terms of use and privacy policy instead of one line each, and two pages are new: `/cookies` (Flow sets no cookies; what it keeps in browser storage and how to clear it) and `/accessibility` (the accessibility statement under IS 5568, with a contact). Sign-in says signing in accepts the terms and the privacy policy and links all four pages; Settings lists them under התנתקות. `OPERATOR_NAME` in `app/src/config.ts` names who runs Flow. The lawyer drafts in `docs/legal/` are deleted; `docs/legal/compliance.md` lists the Israeli laws Flow follows and the duties that are not pages.
