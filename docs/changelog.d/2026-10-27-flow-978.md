FLOW-978: a company's files go with it (decision 0226). Deleting a company left its files in the `transaction-files` bucket, and a deleted line's files stayed too. `public.thread_files_to_purge` (service role only) lists the objects no row names: a deleted company's at once, any other a day after upload. mercury-sync removes them through the Storage API, which deletes the bytes as well, every hour (`flow-thread-files-purge`), and only paths of the form `<company>/<line>/<name>`. Removed notes and files are kept 7 days for undo, then deleted nightly (`flow-thread-items-expire`). The legal drafts say so.
