FLOW-171 (#869): a balance-sheet account's movements from the start, and a long Latin account name that wraps. The owner opened a partner's equity account on the balance sheet and saw only this year's movements; the earlier years were inside the opening balance. `get_account_lines` (database and MCP) takes a start date (`p_from` / `from`, on or before the as-of date) or `p_from_start` / `from_start` for the whole history (no opening balance); with neither, the period is still 1 January of the as-of year. The output adds `from` (null from the start) and `from_start`, and keeps `year_start` for one release so the app already installed keeps parsing it during the deploy (FLOW-173 drops it). The account page (/reports/account) has a period choice over its summary, "השנה" (the as-of year; it names the year when the balance sheet is dated in an earlier year) or "מההתחלה" (`span=all` in the URL): from the start it shows the change since the start with no opening row, and the movements by month under a heading per year. A slash between letters in a stacked title or a report row is a line-break opportunity, and the reports' titles wrap inside the screen (the account page's on up to three lines), so "Contributions/Distributions" no longer runs past the left edge at 393px or breaks mid-word.
