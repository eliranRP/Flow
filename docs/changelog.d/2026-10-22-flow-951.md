The ledger can now stand in for the P&L, but the P&L still reads what it read before: no figure moves. The legs are posted from the P&L's own rules (`private.pnl_lines_legacy`), one item leg per P&L row, and keep what a row needs: the category the person sees (also on a kept-out leg), the loan-split part, the gross-up flag, the loan-split fallback and whether a split part named its own project. A new shadow view (`private.pnl_lines_ledger`) builds the P&L's 19 columns from the legs, and `private.ledger_pnl_diff` compares it with the rules, both ways. The migration re-posts every line and refuses to apply if any company differs. Posting is faster: an edit that touches only a line's description, dates or role no longer re-posts it, a bulk insert posts once per statement, and allocation edits post nothing (they are read when the P&L is read).
