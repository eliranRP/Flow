FLOW-406 server 3 (decision 0164): starter categories by field. `apply_starter_categories(p_set)` lets the owner replace the default categories with a starter set (השכרת נכסים, שיפוצים ופליפים or כללי) during setup, while the company has no lines and no category of its own. The sets, with their sub-categories, live in `private.starter_categories`; `packages/shared` mirrors the keys and labels for the setup screen.
