FLOW-406 server 3 (decision 0164): starter categories by field. New `apply_starter_categories(p_set)` lets the owner swap the default categories for a starter set during setup: השכרת נכסים (`rentals`), שיפוצים ופליפים (`renovation`) or כללי (`general`), with sub-categories under their parents. It works only while the company has no lines and no category of its own (`starter_locked` after that); the loan-part and kept-out defaults stay with their ids. The sets live in `private.starter_categories`; `packages/shared` exports `STARTER_SETS` (keys and labels) for the setup screen. One migration, no MCP change.
