A split line may now hold a normal part and a gross-up part on the same category and project, so a cost and its contra credit can share them (decision [0207](decisions/0207-gross-up-contra-effect.md), amended with D5; FLOW-949). Before, `split_line`, `save_line_split` and the `line_splits` unique constraint each allowed a category and project pair once, which refused 0207's own example; the key now includes `gross_up`, and two normal or two gross-up parts on one pair are still `same category and project twice`. A category merge follows the same key, and a shared line's project cash keeps two parts of one category apart (`projects_cash_month`). `split_line` and `assign_expenses` refusals now carry `detail`, the rule that failed, beside `code` and `message` `validation` ([TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md)). The line-split editor accepts the same pair. Affects `split_line`, `assign_expenses`, the line-split editor and the project cash view.
