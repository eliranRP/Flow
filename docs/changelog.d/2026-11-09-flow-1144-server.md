A property's utility bills by month (FLOW-1144, decision [0265](decisions/0265-utility-refunds.md)): the new read `list_utility_months` gives each month, newest first, from the first utility bill or rent line, with the rent that came in, the tenant-paid bills paid, the part not refunded, and the rent lines and bills themselves. Company-paid utilities are left out. MCP `list_utility_months` ([TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md)).
