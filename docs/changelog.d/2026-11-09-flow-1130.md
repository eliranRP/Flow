Utility refunds, the data and MCP (FLOW-1130, decision [0265](decisions/0265-utility-refunds.md)): Flow finds a property's utilities from the lines filed under חשבונות (or a supplier the owner adds) and looks, on read, for the tenant's refund of each bill: a money-in line from a month before the bill, within one unit of the bill, of the month's bills together, or of the usual rent plus the bills. A match in the bill's category counts by itself; the rest are suggestions for the owner. New RPCs `list_utility_refunds`, `count_utility_refunds`, `list_utility_payers`, `set_utility_payer`, `answer_utility_refund`, `mark_utility_refunds_seen`, `refile_utility_refund`, and the matching MCP tools with undo ([TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md)). The screens are FLOW-1131.
