One line out of the P&L (FLOW-108, decision 0112, the designer's option A). The transaction card's עוד sheet can take a single line out of the P&L, or bring one line of a kept-out category back in, without changing its category. An out line shows a ⊘ "מחוץ לרווח" pill and moves to the kept-out totals and the Home drill-down's kept-out group; nothing is hidden. The save happens on tap with a ביטול toast. Loan lines stay fixed. New column `transactions.in_pnl_override`, RPC `set_transaction_pnl`, and MCP `set_line_pnl` and `set_lines_pnl` (undo kind `line_pnl`, and `undo_batch`). `get_transaction` and MCP `get_expense` return `in_pnl`, `in_pnl_override`, `category_excluded_from_pnl` and `pnl_fixed`.
