**FLOW-1171 (perf queue).** Company profit's figures no longer wait for reads they do not draw. In the default order, לפי פרויקט, the page draws the month's summary, the year to date, the basis footer and the project rows, which come from the month's dashboard, the year's dashboard and the project breakdowns alone; the two category breakdowns and their counted lines now load only for לפי קטגוריה, לפי תאריך and לפי סכום, and switching to one of those orders reads them then. The company's expected months are read once for Home's forecast row, the תזרים צפוי screen and the profit page's next month (one `expected_months` request, with the same answer for `recurring`, which does not depend on the number of months asked for). A forecast or currency write refreshes that shared read as it refreshed the forecast's before. The one visible difference: the first switch to a category order waits for its breakdowns, as the page used to wait for them on every load.
