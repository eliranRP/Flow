FLOW-1210 and FLOW-1211: Company profit's date and amount orders get their lines sooner, from one lean read per direction, with no change a user sees beyond speed. FLOW-1210: the all-lines read of each direction no longer waits for the two `get_breakdown(category)` reads (each row names its group, so it needs no key); it starts with the page, once the company currency is known, and the rows are filed into the categories when both are in (the per-category caches are seeded in either order, from an effect). The two `get_breakdown(project)` reads no longer hold `ready` in the category, date and amount orders, which never draw them (`projectsDrawn`); they start after the first paint (`useAfterFirstPaint`), so a switch to לפי פרויקט finds them in, and לפי פרויקט itself still waits for them. FLOW-1211: `get_breakdown_lines` with no key (and not kept-out, no project) returns the whole direction in one computation and one call, where each page of 200 recomputed the direction (the migration `20261115190314_flow_1211_breakdown_lines_lean.sql` patches the function in place: `p_limit` and `p_offset` are ignored for that read, `has_more` is false). Its row keeps ten fields: `transaction_id`, `part`, `description`, `supplier_name`, `project_name`, `doc_date`, `amount_minor`, `source`, `gross_up` and `group_key` (the filing key); `category_id` (it is the key, null for none), `category_name` (the breakdown has it), `currency`, `kept_out` and `shared` are dropped. A keyed, kept-out or by-project read is unchanged and keeps every field (flow-mcp names them). The app parses the lean row with its own schema (`breakdownLinesAllSchema`) and reads each direction once; when it seeds a category's own cache it puts back `category_id`, `currency` and the breakdown's `category_name`, so the seeded rows equal the keyed read's, less `shared` (nothing reads it). pgTAP (`flow_1211_breakdown_lines_lean`, 23 checks, with 242 lines in one direction) checks the lean keys, parity per category against the keyed read less the dropped fields and in order, the seeded shape, and that keyed, kept-out and by-project reads equal a copy of the body as it was; `flow_1207_breakdown_lines_all` follows the lean row. Measured locally (a fixture of 1,800 expense lines in one year on the local database, mean of 5, one session, not Flow QA): the no-key read of the direction was 443.7 ms in 9 pages and 806,816 bytes (about 448 a row), and is 62.7 ms in one call and 546,304 bytes (about 303 a row). Production figures are the perf steward's.
