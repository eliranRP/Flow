The month forecast (צפי) has a second view, לפי קטגוריה (the board's update). Each side, out then in, lists its categories: what each already had this month, what is still expected, and the month-end figure, with a two-part bar. Opening a category lists its forecast items; a late recurring item links to its charge to confirm it is still recurring. Open invoices now carry their category (list_unpaid), and MCP get_forecast sends each item's category_id.
