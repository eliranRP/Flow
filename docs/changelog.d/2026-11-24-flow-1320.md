The company forecast reads the recurring charges once per call (FLOW-1320, after the 2026-11-24 production profiling: 12 months of totals were 318 to 354 ms, over the 300 ms budget). Before, `public.company_forecast` read them twice: `expected_months(0)` ran `private.recurring_parties` (plus the handovers, a second read of the lines), and `company_next_month_inputs` ran `private.recurring_charge_lines_of_projects` (the same lines and detection again) for the lines the general costs leave out; the overhead project's `project_forecast` did the same. New `private.recurring_charges` is `recurring_parties`' query with every charge, its order, whether it is listed and (when asked) its lines; `recurring_parties` is now its listed rows, so the detection stays one body. New `private.forecast_gather(today, project, inputs)` (security definer, the caller's readable company only, refused 42501 without one; executable by `authenticated` because the forecast reads are security invoker; not served by the API) gives `expected_months(0)`'s charges with the fields the builds read (no handovers) and `company_next_month_inputs`' answer from one pass; `company_forecast` and `project_forecast` read it. `expected_months`, `company_next_month_inputs`, `month_forecast` and `loan_outlooks` are untouched. Every public answer is unchanged: the vectors and pgTAP flow_1307, flow_1309, flow_1310, flow_1313 and flow_1318 pass, the new `flow_1320_recurring_once.test.sql` compares the gather with the two reads and the two forecasts with their old bodies, and on Flow QA-shaped local data 1,249 answers over 8 days (the forecasts, the month forecast, `expected_months`, `company_next_month_inputs`, the loan outlooks, the recurring reads and `recurring_parties` itself; 13.8 MB) were byte-identical before and after. Locally, warm: 1-month totals about 184 to 150 ms, 12 months 212 to 166 ms, 1-month rows 197 to 168 ms, the overhead project's forecast 162 to 86 ms; the first call on a new connection drops by the same amount (its extra cost, about 40 ms locally, is unchanged).
