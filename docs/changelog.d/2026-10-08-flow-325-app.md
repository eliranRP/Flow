The transaction detail has a "פיצול" section: "בין פרויקטים" opens the project split as before, and "לפי קטגוריות" opens a new full-screen editor that splits one line between categories and projects (FLOW-325, plan option A). Each part takes a category, an optional project, and a percent or an amount; the cents, the rest that stays on the line and the totals come from the server's preview (`save_line_split(..., p_preview => true)`). On an inflow, expense categories show as reversals, and a reversal part needs a project. Leaving saves (0075) with a toast and ביטול; an invalid split holds with ביטול השינוי. "הסרת הפיצול" clears the split after a confirm. A split line lists its parts and a סה״כ row on the detail, and its category and project rows say "מפוצל · N חלקים · לא נספר כאן". Every server refusal has Hebrew copy. A `split_mismatch` review card says the split no longer matches the bank amount, with "עדכון הפיצול" to open the editor (FLOW-312, decision 0125). While a line is split by category, "בין פרויקטים" stays visible but off with the reason, and the project split names that refusal instead of offering a retry. A line whose split no longer matches the bank amount saves its parts again on leaving, with no ביטול.
