The owner can delete a company and all of its data (FLOW-927, decision 0194). The business page in Settings (`/settings/business`, העסק) ends with an owner-only section אזור מסוכן ("danger zone") and a row מחיקת כל הנתונים ("delete all the data"). Its confirm sheet says that every project, transaction, loan, connection and setting of the company is deleted for everyone on the team and cannot be undone, and its delete button stays off until the owner types the company's name. The new RPC `delete_company` checks the owner and the name, deletes the company row so every table cascades, and clears the tables that have a `company_id` but no foreign key to `companies` and the company's flow-mcp rate counters. The user stays signed in and lands on setup's welcome, or on their other company. pgTAP `delete_company.test.sql` sweeps every table with a `company_id` after the delete and checks that another company is untouched. There is no MCP tool for it, by design.
