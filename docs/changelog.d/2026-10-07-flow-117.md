Reversals from the app (FLOW-117, design reviewer's option A under the owner's standing rule for UI tasks). The category picker has a closed section under the list, "הכנסה שהוחזרה" on an outflow and "הוצאה שהוחזרה" on an inflow, with the other kind's categories, so a bounced rent payment or a supplier refund can be filed from the review card, the change sheet and the transaction detail. Hidden, loan and kept-out categories stay out, and split or shared lines don't offer it yet. A line filed this way shows a החזר mark, and a reversal never saves a supplier rule. The server side shipped in [0103](decisions/0103-reversals-across-directions.md). No migration and no MCP change (`assign_expense` and the review tools already accept the other kind).
