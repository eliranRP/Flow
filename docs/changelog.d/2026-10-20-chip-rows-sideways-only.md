The organize chips on company profit, month lines and the compact panel, and the active filters on Search, scroll only sideways: a drag up or down over a chip row no longer nudges the chips inside it. Each chip's 44px hit area stuck 4px out of its 36px row, and `overflow-x: auto` alone made the row a vertical scroller for those 4px. The rows now set `overflow-y: hidden`, contain the sideways scroll, and get 4px of block padding (offset by negative margins, so nothing moves) so the hit area and the focus ring fit. `app/e2e/company-profit.spec.ts` checks it.
