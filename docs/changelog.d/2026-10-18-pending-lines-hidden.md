Mercury lines still pending at the bank are hidden until they post (decision 0184, supersedes 0086's pending rule; the owner asked to see only approved transactions). The sync stores them as before. A new migration (`pending_lines_hidden`) stops `sync_review_queue` from queueing a pending line, removes the open review rows of lines still pending (never the lines), and makes every read the app uses leave them out: לאישור and the Home review count, a project's waiting lines and transactions list, search and its month totals, `get_transaction` (not found), a loan's payments, a party's charges, the recurring reads, category line counts, receipt matching and both pushes. When the bank posts the line it is queued once, like any other. The ממתין mark on a party's charges, the unused בהמתנה chip on the statement row, and the `pending` and `line_status` fields only they read are removed. New pgTAP file `pending_lines_hidden`; nine pgTAP files that asserted the old visible behaviour now assert the hidden one.
