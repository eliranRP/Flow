A loan can be interest-only, balloon or on demand, and its rate can change ([0132](decisions/0132-loan-kinds-rates.md), FLOW-106 part 4). `loans.kind` is `amortizing` (the default; existing loans are unchanged), `interest_only` (`interest_only_months`), `balloon` (`amortization_months`, the term to 600) or `demand` (no term, payment or escrow). A new `loan_rates` table holds rate changes entered by hand; from the row a change takes effect, the schedule recasts the payment over the months left. Demand interest is the balance times the rate for the days since the last attached payment, actual/365, rounded half to even. MCP: new write `set_loan_rate` (undo kind `loan_rate`); `add_loan` and `update_loan` take `kind`, `interest_only_months` and `amortization_months`; `list_loans` returns them and `rates`; `get_loan_schedule` returns rows for every kind, and for a demand loan the attached payments and the interest `accrued` to `as_of`; `attach_loan_payment` works for every kind. Folded in: FLOW-135 N1 (`installments` start from the interest plus principal already paid, pending lines included) and N3 (exact `parts` need no schedule row); FLOW-132 (a closed loan's line that comes back or moves past `closed_on` is flagged for review, and `get_project`'s `loans[]` has `status`, `closed_on` and `kind`); FLOW-134 items 1 and 4 (`merge_category` moves loan part categories or refuses the merge; the part-category check runs only when a category changes). The app splits interest-only and balloon payments by their schedule and rate rows and does not offer a demand loan in the match sheet. [TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md) describes the changes.
