**FLOW-1207 (perf queue, Company profit).** `get_breakdown_lines` with no group key (`p_group_key` null, `p_excluded` false) now reads every counted line of the direction in the company currency, paged as before (`p_limit` up to 200, `p_offset`, `has_more`), newest first. Each row names its group in `group_key` (the key a keyed read is asked with: the category id, or `none`), a line split across two categories stays two rows, `total_minor` and `count` are the whole direction's, and `projects` is empty. A keyed read, a kept-out read and a read by project return what they did, with no `group_key`. Company profit's date and amount orders use it: one paged read per direction (pages of 200) instead of one per category (pages of 40), and the rows are filed under the breakdown's categories in its order, so the lines and their order on screen are the same. The first page's `count` says how many pages follow, and those are read together. When every page is in, each counted category's own lines are stored under the key the category order reads, so that order shows them at once after a date or amount visit, with no read. A failed read is the page's failure, with its retry. The category order still reads a category's lines when its row scrolls near, and the kept-out lines on a tap. Measured by call counts, not timings: the app test sends 1 read for the date order and for the amount order where the one before it sent 1 per counted category (2 in its fixture), and a direction of 201 lines is 2 reads and 401 is 3 (offsets 0, 200, 400, the last two together); a pgTAP test compares every category's rows, field for field and in order, with a copy of the previous body read one category at a time, on invented lines (a split line, a shared cost, a line with no category, a kept-out category, another currency).
