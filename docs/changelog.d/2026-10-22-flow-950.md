An MCP assistant can now use the ledger end to end. New read tools `list_accounts` (the company's accounts with the live category name, kind and P&L flag, and `bookable`) and `get_line_legs` (one line's signed legs and whether they balance) give it the account ids `book_line` needs and a way to verify a booking. `book_line` maps each category leg to a split part by its sign against the bank leg — the opposite sign a normal part, the same sign a gross-up part — so a contra credit (a property-tax credit on a payment) or an income credit on a payment can be booked, writing the split through `save_line_split` with every `split_line` rule. With the new `transaction_id` it books the caller's existing posted line (a bank line from a sync) instead of creating a duplicate, with `split_line`'s undo (`line_split`); a line with VAT, a loan split or an open review is refused. Bridge accounts now follow a category rename or keep-out, and the legs of that category's lines are re-derived, so the ledger keeps mirroring the P&L. Argument refusals name the field (`validation` with `detail`). No P&L figure moves. Decision [0208](decisions/0208-balanced-legs-ledger.md) (amended, item 9); FLOW-950.
