Team members, app side (FLOW-601). The owner finds "צוות" in Settings: everyone on the team with their role, and "הזמנה" to invite someone by email as a viewer (צפייה בלבד) or an editor. A member's role changes with a tap and can be undone; removing someone asks first. The company's name on Home opens a "חברה" sheet to switch companies, start a new one, and join or decline invites (a decline can be undone for a short while). Someone who signs in with invites and no company of their own sees them first, or chooses "אחר כך". Editors can file and change the books; the business details, connectors and the team stay with the owner. Each request names the company on screen, so two phones can show two companies.
