The app's money words now follow one glossary in plain Hebrew. Home says "רווח" (we count before tax, so not "רווח נקי"), and the rows under it say "הכנסות" and "הוצאות". Lines kept out of the profit say "לא נספר ברווח" in every screen. The investment card says "השבחה צפויה" and "הון עצמי בנכס", and unpaid customer invoices are "חשבוניות פתוחות" with an amount "לגבייה". The full table is in DESIGN-RULES §3.6.
