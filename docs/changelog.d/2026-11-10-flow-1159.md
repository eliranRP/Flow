The income around a utility bill (FLOW-1159, decision [0265](decisions/0265-utility-refunds.md) item 12): the new read `list_utility_income` gives a tenant-paid utility bill with its state and the money in on its property within 1 to 6 months before and after it (2 by default), newest first, each line with its split parts on the property and the bills it is the refund of. MCP has the same read (`list_utility_income`, [TOOLS.md](mcp/TOOLS.md)). The app's data layer for the V3-J boards: `useUtilityIncomeQuery`, the bill status line, a property's bills by type with what the tenant owes, the type's usual time to a refund, and the payment rows around a bill; a property's open bills on החזרי חשבונות run newest first.
