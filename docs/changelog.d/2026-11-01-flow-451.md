FLOW-451, decision [0244](decisions/0244-property-returns.md): a property's השקעה והלוואות page leads with the last 12 months' total return on the money in the deal, split into cash flow, principal paid, appreciation and tax savings, then the equity in the property (equity today, forced equity, money in the deal) and today's metrics (cash-on-cash, cap rate, DSCR, cash flow per unit). Every figure's ⓘ opens a sheet with its parts and operators; each part opens its source: the cash-flow statement ("איך מחושב"), a line with its categories and transactions, a part's page (the building cost, the money in the deal, the forced equity) with its rehab lines, a metric's page, Loans, or the input's edit sheet. A transaction opens its own page, where its category changes. A figure whose input is missing reads "חסר · הוספה"; נתוני הנכס gains land and units, and the tax bracket has its own sheet, each saved with an undo. The equity card with its bar is gone.
