Projects take the v3 layout. The Projects tab opens on the month's cash across the projects with its month arrows, then what needs a look (the lines with no project, a budget past 80%, a rent that did not arrive, a changed charge; each card can be hidden on this phone), then פעילים, הסתיימו or הכול, with the projects in groups by kind. A project's page keeps its band and shows the card its kind needs (the renovation budget, the rental's investment, the client's contract), links to the investment, the profit since the start and next month, and the month's recurring charges in groups; ⋯ adds רווח מתחילת הפרויקט and asks before finishing. A new project starts by picking its kind, and the fields follow it. The profit page shows the profit since the start in its band, by month or by category. The lines with no project have their own list, where שיוך assigns a line to a project with ביטול (FLOW-1044).
