The company and project forecasts build each loan's payments only for the months asked (FLOW-1318, after FLOW-1307's measured read on production was over its 300 ms budget). New `private.loan_schedule_window` builds a loan's rest of schedule up to the last month asked, refusing whatever the full build refuses (the payment below the interest at the first amortizing month after the window included) and taking the whole build when a rate change comes after the window; `private.loan_payments_window` wraps it as `private.loan_payments_left` is wrapped, and `private.loan_schedule_amortize_to` works out the pay-off months only when a rate change needs a new payment. `private.company_forecast_build` (which `project_forecast` runs too) uses it, and `public.company_forecast` and `public.project_forecast` now gather each loan's first due date on or after today and its period instead of every stored due date. `public.loan_outlooks` keeps the full build (it needs totals to the end). Every public answer is unchanged: the shared vectors and the FLOW-1307, FLOW-1309 and FLOW-1310 pgTAP files pass, a new pgTAP file compares the window with the full build over 1,440 generated cases (24 invented loan shapes, 10 days, 6 balances, 3 windows) and the build over 24 inputs, and on Flow QA-shaped local data 504 answers over 7 days were byte-identical before and after. Locally, the 22 loans' payments went from about 35 ms to about 2 ms per call; `company_forecast` 1-month totals from about 215 to 173 ms.
