# FLOW-1282 · Perf: Projects and Recurring draw only the rows on screen
- **Type:** MEDIUM (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** decision [0271](../../decisions/0271-half-second-by-kept-data-and-one-wave.md), item 4: the main-thread profile of 2026-11-23 ([2026-11-23-main-thread.md](../../qa/perf/2026-11-23-main-thread.md)); FLOW-1274, rows against render time.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/projects-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/recurring-list.tsx`, `app/src/screens/recurring-screen.tsx`, the list components they use, tests, stories.

React render and commit is the biggest part of a first visit (320 ms of 507 on Projects with 40 projects, 337 ms of 532 on Recurring with 60 charges). Varying the stub's list sizes with the rest unchanged (10 rows against 40, 3 runs each on the phone profile; the table is under "Row counts" in the profile doc) puts the cost at roughly 5 ms for each Projects row and 9 ms for each Recurring row, with a wide spread between runs (Projects at 40 rows ran from 321 to 630 ms), so the figures are rough. Every row is drawn at mount, each with its own figure fit (FLOW-1281), layout and effects. The first step of this task is to measure it again with enough runs. Review draws one card at a time and did not change with the queue's length (50 or 10), so it is not part of this.

- [ ] Draw the first screenful of rows (about 10) at once and the rest after the first paint, in idle time or as the list nears the end of the screen, in the same order, without a change in what the list shows, its scroll height or its find-in-page and screen-reader order (reserve the rows' height, or keep them in the DOM but cheap).
- [ ] The list keeps its swipe, section headers, totals and the open-from-query behavior.
- [ ] Measure Projects and Recurring at 40 and 60 rows before and after, and add a story with a long list.

**Acceptance:** the first-visit main-thread time of Projects (40 rows) and Recurring (60 rows) drops by at least 150 ms on the phone profile; every row is still reachable, and nothing a user sees changes once the list has drawn.
