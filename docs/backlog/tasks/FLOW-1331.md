# FLOW-1331 · Perf: the loan schedule tables keep only what their readers use
- **Type:** SMALL (database) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the [2026-11-25 daily report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-25.md), diff review (response fields row 12; indexes rows 5 and 6).
- **Files:** a migration on `public.loan_schedule_rows`, `public.loan_schedule_meta`, `private.loan_schedule_rebuild` and the `loan_rates_schedule` trigger (`20261123050701_flow_1306_loan_schedule.sql`).

- Every reader takes only `period` and `due_date` from `loan_schedule_rows` and `state` from the meta. `interest_minor`, `escrow_minor`, `principal_minor`, `payment_minor`, `balance_minor` and the meta's `balloon_minor`, `balloon_ratio`, `final_adjustment_minor`, `terms_hash`, `built_at`, `error` are written on every rebuild and read by nothing (only `database.types.ts` and tests).
- The `loan_rates_schedule` trigger is per row, so a statement writing N rates of one loan rebuilds it N times; a statement-level trigger with a transition table rebuilds once. A rebuild of a 360-month loan is an estimated 15 to 30 ms.

- [ ] Decide per column: keep it if a planned slice reads it (say which), otherwise drop it. Keep the `select` grant to `authenticated`: `public.loan_outlooks` is security invoker and reads both tables.
- [ ] Make the rates trigger statement-level.

**Acceptance:** smaller rebuild writes; loan outlook and forecast answers byte-identical.
