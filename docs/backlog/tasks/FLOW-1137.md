# FLOW-1137 · Skeletons in the content's shape: review, forms, sheets and settings
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress · **Depends on:** —
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09: "review the app and make sure we follow the skeleton concept for loading across the app". An audit of every loading state found these places drawing a blank, a generic list or a placeholder value while their data loads.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/review-screen.tsx`, `journal-composer-screen.tsx`, `line-split.tsx`, `change-form.tsx`, `project-next-edit-screen.tsx`, `add-sheet.tsx`, `add-entry-sheet.tsx`, `add-entry-view.tsx`, `recurring-charge-pick.tsx`, `transaction-recurring.tsx`, `transaction-thread.tsx`, `review-thread.tsx`, `loan-split-editor.tsx`, `notifications-screen.tsx`, `settings-screen.tsx`, `connections-screen.tsx`, `categories-screen.tsx`, `assistant-settings.tsx`; `app/src/ui/screen-skeletons.tsx` (a form skeleton); their CSS and stories.

- [ ] The review queue loads with its review card's skeleton (`ReviewCardLoading`), not list rows.
- [ ] A screen or sheet gated on the user's role (journal composer, line split, change assignment, next-month edit, add) draws its skeleton while the role loads, not nothing. The forms load with a form-shaped skeleton (label and control per field).
- [ ] The add sheet's projects and categories, and the recurring charge's pick sheet, show skeleton chips and rows while they load, not empty choices.
- [ ] The transaction's "קבוע" row, the thread section and the review card's thread button hold a skeleton while their reads are out, not "לא קבוע" or nothing.
- [ ] The journal composer's bank leg doesn't say "בנק" before the account's name is in. The loan split's fee category, the notifications switch, and Settings' team count and company row hold skeletons, not "טוען…", "off" or a missing row.
- [ ] Settings, connections, categories and the AI connection load with skeletons in their grouped shape.
- **Acceptance:** loading stories for the screens changed, in both themes.
