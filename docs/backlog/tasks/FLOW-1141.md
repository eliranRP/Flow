# FLOW-1141 · Utility refunds: rent and bills found by part of the category name; a ראיתי row folds away; dates with the year
- **Type:** BUG (owner's ask) · **Status:** in-progress (dev lane 1, 2026-11-09, claude/zen-keller-w90qi6) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1134](FLOW-1134.md), [FLOW-1135](FLOW-1135.md)
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09, from the phone, on the live החזרי חשבונות screen. Every bill read "חודש בלי שכירות", although the property's rent came in every month. The owner also asked for a disappear animation when a bill is marked, and for dd/mm/yy dates.
- **Why:** FLOW-1134 finds the rent and the bills by exact category names (`שכירות` for income, `חשבונות` for the bills' tree). A company's chart may name them otherwise, for example "הכנסות שכירות" or "12 חשבונות שוטפים". With no rent found, every month reads "no rent", and rule 3 (the usual rent plus bills) never matches.
- **Files:** a new migration (+pgTAP) that redefines `private.utility_category_ids`; `app/src/screens/utility-refunds-*`, `utility-refund-view.ts`, `project-utilities-screen.tsx`, `css/65-utility-refunds.css`, their tests and stories; decision 0265 (amended); a changelog fragment.

- [ ] Rent is every income category whose name contains "שכירות", with its children. The bills' tree is every expense category whose name contains "חשבונות", with its children.
- [ ] A bill row closed with ראיתי (or with סימון הכל כנראה, or by החברה משלמת תמיד) slides toward its ✓ and folds (the v3 row-card fold: 24px slide, `max-height` 220ms `--ease-exit`, opacity 160ms). With reduced motion it disappears at once. ביטול brings it back.
- [ ] Every date on the utility refund screens reads dd/mm/yy.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP with categories named "הכנסות שכירות" and "12 חשבונות שוטפים" (no-rent false, rule 3 matches); tests for the fold and its undo, and for the dates; clip-check at 320, 360 and 390.
