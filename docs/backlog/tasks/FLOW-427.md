# FLOW-427 · Loan pages: figures that agree, one row style, whole parts at 320
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** — · **Source:** cycle 20 phone review of production at cd4d7a9, 2026-10-10 (C20-1 to C20-4), on the FLOW-434 loan page (#536). Shots are in the project files under `reviews/ui-ux-cycle-20/`. The design lead signs it off; there is no owner card.
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **The future schedule disagrees with the balance (C20-1).** The mortgage page says יתרה $182,410, but its "עד סוף ההלוואה" principal is $195,382: `loanOutlook` builds the schedule from the loan's original terms, so any extra principal or a late start shows up as two different debts on one page. Start the rows still to pay from the current balance (re-amortize from the next due date with the balance and the payment), so the principal to the end equals the balance shown. Unit test with a loan whose balance is below its scheduled one; the interest-only and balloon kinds too.
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **Two row styles on the future page (C20-2).** The 12-month part rows have dark amounts and no chevron; "עד סוף ההלוואה" and the year rows have grey amounts and a chevron. Rows that open something keep the chevron and every row keeps the amount color of a normal row (dark), and rows that don't open keep the chevron's place, so the amounts line up (§3.7, FLOW-415). "לכל השנים" is a tint link (§2.8).
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **A part count includes parts worth $0 (C20-3).** On the bridge loan's details, each interest-only payment says "3 חלקים" though only interest (and fees) show. Count only the parts that have a row, by the FLOW-434 rule that a part worth $0 has no row.
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **A word alone on a line (C20-4).** At 320 the details' סוג line wraps as "ריבית בלבד • 12 מתוך 24 / חודשים". Build it with `HintParts` so it breaks only at the "•"; check the תשלום חודשי and payment-row hints the same way.
