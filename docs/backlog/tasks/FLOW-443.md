# FLOW-443 · The project's "לפי חודש" screen: year heads, and one consistent row style
- **Type:** PLAN FIRST · **Status:** plan-first · **Depends on:** — · **Source:** owner request 2026-10-11, with a phone shot of a project's "לפי חודש" screen (profit by month, project to date). The shot holds real data, so it is not in the repo. The owner calls it a design task.
- **What happens:** The screen lists every month since the project began, newest first, in one flat list. The current year's months show no year; older ones carry it in the row title ("דצמבר 2025", "אוקטובר 2025"). Rows don't share one style: some months carry a "הוצאות $X" hint and others none; months with nothing read "$0" rows; losses are coloured on rows but not in the subtitle's loss.
- [ ] **Years:** a year head above each year's months (newest year first), with the months listed under it, and the list scrolling through the years. Month rows drop the year their head already says. Follow the month head shape and the `heading` style (DESIGN-RULES: month heads in lists, and "a list grouped by something other than the month uses the month head's shape"). Decide whether a year head carries that year's total.
- [ ] **Consistency pass:** align the rows with the profit and cash month pages (FLOW-438) and the rules: one row shape for every month; when a hint shows and what it says (today's rule: הוצאות only when the month has income, no hint when income is 0, #367; check it still reads right here); whether $0 months before the first line show at all; how a loss looks on rows and in the subtitle; the open-month chip. List each mismatch found, with the fix, in the plan.
- [ ] Plan and mockup at 320 and 393 (light and dark), then the owner's approval written here before any build.
- [ ] Build in the UI lane that owns the project screens; the MCP tool behind the screen returns the months grouped the same way if its shape changes.
- **Acceptance:** stories with invented figures spanning two years at 320 and 393: one head per year, no year in month rows, one row style, no clipping. Design review against the approved mockup.
