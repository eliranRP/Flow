# FLOW-1345 · Perf: a skip keeps the kept forecasts of the projects it did not change
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the [2026-11-27 daily report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-27.md), diff review (response fields, row 3).
- **Files:** `app/src/forecast-month.ts` (the drop at the tap, `keptForecastOf`), `app/src/forecast-month-settle.ts` (`keepPatched`), `app/src/project-forecast-query.ts` (the patch).

A skip deletes the device's kept `project_forecast` of every project for the month at the tap (`forecast-month.ts:77`, matcher at `:94`), but at settle `keepPatched` keeps again only the answers the patch wrote, and the patch writes only the projects whose skips changed, normally one. For Flow QA that is up to about 135 KB of still-correct kept rows dropped and about 10 KB kept again (14 projects, 10,354 B average). After a skip, a cold start draws a skeleton on the forecast card of every other project visited that month until it reads again.

- [ ] Drop at the tap only the kept rows of projects whose skips change (or keep again the unchanged ones still in memory); the failure path keeps dropping everything.
- [ ] A test: a skip of one project's item leaves another project's kept forecast in place for a cold start.

**Acceptance:** after a skip, a cold start draws every project card of the month from kept data.
