# FLOW-1117 · Member share report: the data and the MCP tools
- **Type:** MCP · **Status:** in-progress · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09: a report where an LLC member sees their share after selling every property (less the selling costs and the loans) and after a refinance, with lender quotes compared to the US Treasury 5 and 10 year yields plus 1.5 or 2 points; concept A approved on the design canvas ("Approve", 2026-11-09).
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/<stamp>_member_report.sql`, `supabase/tests/database/member_report.test.sql`, `packages/shared/src/member-share.ts` (+test), `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/tools_member_share.ts` (+test), the MCP registry files, `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, a decision.

- [x] One company setting holds the report's inputs: the members and their ownership (basis points, adding up to 10000), the refinance assumptions (loan to value, closing costs, term), the rate rule (5 or 10 year Treasury, +150 or +200 basis points), the lender quotes (lender, rate, points, date, type, property or all) and the Treasury yields by date.
- [x] `get_member_report` reads it with each property's value, value date, selling cost and open loan balance; `set_member_report` (owner only) replaces the parts it names, with idempotency, the write rate limit and undo kind `member_report`.
- [x] One calculation in `packages/shared`, used by the app and by MCP `get_member_share`: sell everything (value less selling costs less loans), refinance (new loan at loan to value, less the loans and the closing costs, the monthly payment), the expected rate and each quote's verdict, per property and for the member.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP for the read, the write (validation, owner only, another company refused) and the undo; unit tests for the calculation; Deno tests for the tools.
