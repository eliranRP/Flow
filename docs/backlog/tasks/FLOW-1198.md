# FLOW-1198 · The loan page's סכום ששולם בסגירה row reads "כל ההלוואה" though the price counts the principal less the draws
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the code review of FLOW-1199 (#1180), 2026-11-14.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/loan-closing-paid-sheet.tsx`, `app/src/screens/loan-detail-screen.tsx`, their tests and stories, `docs/qa/CONTROLS.md` (rows "סכום ששולם בסגירה").

Since decision 0269, a loan paid out at closing with no amount set counts its principal less its draws (`taken_over_loans[].counted_minor`, `draws_minor`). The loan page row still reads "כל ההלוואה · <principal>", and the sheet's hint says "ריק = כל ההלוואה".

- [ ] With no amount set, the row reads what the price counts (`counted_minor`), and names the draws taken off when there are any.
- [ ] The sheet's hint says an empty field counts the loan less its draws, and that the amount is the settlement statement's Loan Amount less its Lender Hold Back.
