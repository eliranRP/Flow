# FLOW-1263 · Loans: a loan never paid in the books stays late
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** done (#1256) · **Depends on:** FLOW-1258
- **Source:** code review of #1252 (r2), the owner's go 2026-11-23. FLOW-1258 calls a loan with no payment in the books late only when the missed date is its first scheduled payment, so a loan added before its payments started shows "תשלום באיחור" for its first month and loses it once the second date also passes unpaid.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.ts` (`latePayment`), `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.test.ts`, `app/src/screens/loan-list.tsx` (`created_at` in the list's read only), `app/src/screens/loans-live.test.tsx`, `docs/qa/CONTROLS.md`, `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-23-flow-1263.md`.

- [x] With no payment in the books, a loan is late for its latest due date past the grace among those on or after the day the loan was added to the app (`loans.created_at`); a loan whose dates all predate that day is not called late. Unknown `created_at` is never late.
- [x] Tests: added before its first date and two dates missed (still late, latest date), added after its dates (not late), unknown `created_at`.
