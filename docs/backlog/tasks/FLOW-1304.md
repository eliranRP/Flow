# FLOW-1304 · Perf: the forecast builds each loan's payments left once per read
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 1 (owner approval 2026-11-24).
- **Files:** `app/src/project-next-loans.ts`; `app/src/project-next-month.ts`, `app/src/company-next-month.ts` and `app/src/company-year-forecast.ts` if the per-read map is passed down from them; tests (`app/src/project-next-month-loans.test.ts` or a new `project-next-loans.test.ts`). Not `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.ts` (FLOW-1283's).

`dueRows` in `project-next-loans.ts` calls `paymentsLeft` for every loan of a part, twice per project and month (once from `loanParts`, once from `loanCashOnly`), and each call builds two schedules: the loan's own (`scheduleRowsOf`) and the rest of it from the balance (`remainingFromBalance`). On Flow QA-shaped data (22 loans of its kinds, invented values) the company's next month takes 23 ms in Node, 0.8 ms of it without the loans; the year view's rest of the year in January (11 months) takes 275 ms, about 1.1 s on the FLOW-804 phone.

- [ ] Keep each loan's payments left in a per-read `Map` keyed by `loan.id`, `balanceMinor` and `today`. Not a `WeakMap` on the loan object: `companyGeneralForecast` copies the general and overhead loans on every month's build (`{ ...loan, projectId: GENERAL }`), so an object key misses them. The map lives for one build (one call of `companyNextMonthForecast`, `companyRestOfYear` or `companyMonthForecast`).
- [ ] Same rows and totals in every forecast test; a spy shows one `paymentsLeft` computation per loan per read, that is two `buildLoanSchedule` calls per loan, for a 12-month build, general and overhead loans included.

**Acceptance:** with the plan's Node method, `companyRestOfYear` for 11 months with Flow QA's loan shapes is under 30 ms (275 ms today) and next month under 15 ms (23 ms); no figure changes. Re-measured on the phone with FLOW-1284's realistic answers when that lands.
