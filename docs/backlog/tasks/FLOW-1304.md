# FLOW-1304 · Perf: the forecast builds each loan's schedule once per read
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 1 (owner approval 2026-11-24).
- **Files:** `app/src/project-next-loans.ts`, its tests (`app/src/project-next-month-loans.test.ts` or a new `project-next-loans.test.ts`). Not `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.ts` (FLOW-1283's).

`dueRows` in `project-next-loans.ts` calls `paymentsLeft` (a whole schedule, then the payments left from the balance) for every loan, twice per part (`loanParts`, `loanCashOnly`) and again for every month built. On Flow QA-shaped data (22 loans of its kinds, invented values) the company's next month takes 23 ms in Node, 0.8 ms of it without the loans; the year view's rest of the year in January (11 months) takes 275 ms, about 1.1 s on the FLOW-804 phone.

- [ ] Keep the payments left per loan and day (a `WeakMap` keyed by the loan object, then `today`), so a read builds each loan once for every month and part.
- [ ] Same rows and totals in every forecast test; a spy shows one `buildLoanSchedule` per loan for a 12-month build.

**Acceptance:** with the plan's Node method, `companyRestOfYear` for 11 months with Flow QA's loan shapes is under 30 ms (275 ms today) and next month under 15 ms (23 ms); no figure changes. Re-measured on the phone with FLOW-1284's realistic answers when that lands.
