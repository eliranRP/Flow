# FLOW-1325 · Perf: trim the month forecast, loan outlook and forecast totals answers
- **Type:** MEDIUM (perf, database and app) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the [2026-11-25 daily report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-25.md), diff review (response fields, rows 5 to 11).
- **Files:** a migration for `public.month_forecast`, `public.loan_outlooks`, `public.project_forecast`, `public.company_forecast` ('totals'); the app's readers (`month-forecast-query.ts`, `company-forecast-map.ts`, `project-forecast-map.ts`, `packages/shared/src/loan-outlooks.ts`), flow-mcp's `tools_forecast_read.ts`; the shared vectors.

Sizes on production as Flow QA (2026-11-25). Every item below is unread or recomputed by both readers, and removing it changes nothing users see:
- `month_forecast` (25.7 KB): `moved_out: false` and `overridden: false` on every item (2.6 KB); `usual` equal to `minor` (1.1 KB); the key lists `income`, `expense`, `moved`, `small`, the two totals and `changed`, which both readers rebuild from the items (3.5 KB); `overrides` sends nulls (strip them as `project_forecast` does).
- `loan_outlooks` (6.0 KB): `to_end_minor` and `end_date` are read by nobody (1.1 KB). They are why `loan_outlooks` still builds every schedule to the end (most of its 106 to 131 ms); without them it can use the 12-payment window of FLOW-1318. `next.amount_minor` is the sum of the three parts beside it (0.5 KB).
- `project_forecast`: `cash_expense_minor`, `cash_net_minor`, `today`, `net_minor`, `loan_count` (about 120 B).
- `company_forecast` 'totals': `months[].net_minor` and `cash_only_minor` (619 of 2,633 B for 12 months), the top-level `income` that repeats `months[0]` for one month, `today`.

- [ ] Omit or drop each field; the readers derive what they need; the vectors, the zod schemas and flow-mcp's descriptions follow.
- [ ] `loan_outlooks` uses the window build once the two to-the-end fields are gone, with byte-identical remaining fields.

**Acceptance:** the answers shrink by the bytes above for Flow QA; `loan_outlooks` well under its 146 ms; every reader's tests pass.
