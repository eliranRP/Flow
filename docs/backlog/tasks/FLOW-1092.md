# FLOW-1092 · Reports month drill: the pill shows the month but picking a period leaves the drill
- **Type:** SMALL CYCLE · **Status:** in-progress (#TBD)
- **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-07 (run 3, scope 925384f..ae9f7e4a: the v3 build). The owner chose (a) on 2026-11-07: "Move between months".
- **Files:** app/src/screens/reports-screen.tsx, reports-parts.tsx, reports-sheets.tsx, app/src/report-views.ts, app/src/ui/css/61-reports-v3.css.

**Finding:** Inside a month's drill the period pill reads the month, but it opens the period sheet and picking any period exits the drill (useLatePick sets drill: null).

- [x] Owner to choose: (a) in a drill the pill moves between months and keeps the drill, or (b) the pill is hidden in a drill and the back arrow leaves it. Chosen: (a). Built: the pill is the month between the earlier and later chevrons (the by-month view's months), and tapping it opens those months in a sheet. A step or a pick stays in the drill on the new month; "‹ לפי חודש" still goes back to the list.

**Acceptance:** the owner's choice is built, with its stories and tests.
