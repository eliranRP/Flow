# FLOW-1012 · A forecast item's כל כמה זמן saves with the page's save, or says it changes the project's line
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#TBD) · **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-01 (`docs/runbooks/ux-rules-review.md`, rules in `.claude/skills/ux-ui-design-review-rules/`). Severity: High (the copy misleads, and the change is permanent). From FLOW-1010.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/forecast-item-page.tsx` (`PaceRow`, the page hint).

- [x] Picking a pace in the כל כמה זמן sheet writes `set_project_forecast_edit` at once, while the amount and the date wait for שמירת השינוי בצפי. The hint under the list says "השורה בצפי של הפרויקט לא משתנה", but the pace pick changes that line from this month on. Rule: forms-and-requests § 4–5, feedback-and-states § 12 (one save model per form; the copy matches the outcome).
- [x] Fix: keep the pace in the page's draft and save it with שמירת השינוי בצפי. If it has to save at once, the sheet's subtitle says so ("משנה את השורה בצפי של הפרויקט מ{month} והלאה") and the hint for an added line no longer says the line is unchanged.

**Acceptance:** after a pace pick, nothing on the page says the project's line is unchanged; the story covers the pace pick.

Done: the pace is a draft on the page, and שמירת השינוי בצפי writes it with `set_project_forecast_edit` (a pace pick alone writes only the line, not this month's amount and date). After a pick the hint reads "משנה את הצפי של {month}, ואת השורה בצפי של הפרויקט מ{month} והלאה." Story: `Forecast · change an added line, pace picked` (a play test picks כל חודש). When the amount or the date also changed, both writes run and the pace write's toast (with its ביטול) replaces "הצפי עודכן", since one toast shows at a time.
