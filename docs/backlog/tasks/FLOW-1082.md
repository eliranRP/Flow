# FLOW-1082 · Reports: a row's second line is cut at 320, hiding expenses or the loan amount
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#1030) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-07 (run 3, scope 925384f..ae9f7e4a: the v3 build).
- **Files:** app/src/screens/reports-parts.tsx, app/src/screens/reports-balance-screen.tsx, app/src/ui/css/61-reports-v3.css, packages/shared/src/money.ts (`compactFrom`), docs/design/DESIGN-RULES.md, the reports stories.

**Finding:** On P&L by project / by month and balance sheet by project, the row hint ("37% · הכנסות $30,000 · הוצאות …") is one line with an ellipsis, so at 320 the expenses or the loan amount are cut. Shot: run-3 reports shots, by-project at 320.
**Rule:** feedback-and-states § 4 (long content), tables-and-responsive § 11 (numbers never truncate).

- [x] The owner's pick (2026-11-07, option A): the hint stays on one line. Below 360px the row is a grid: the name and the amount on the first line with the chevron at its end, the hint on a line of its own under them across the row (up to the chevron's column), its figures compact ("14% · הכנסות $30K · הוצאות $17K", "$1.2M"; "פחות מ־1%" stays). From 360 the hint stays under the name beside the amount with its figures in full. The exact figures are one tap away in the drill-down, and a screen reader reads the full figures once. No amount is cut at 320. (The first take, wrapping to two lines, was dropped: review r1 found a narrow row still clipped its third part; the second, compact figures beside the amount, still did not fit the 144–166px column at 320.)

**Acceptance:** at 320 and 393, dark and light, every figure in the by-project, by-month and balance-by-project hints is whole.
