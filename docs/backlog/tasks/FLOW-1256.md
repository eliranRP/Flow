# FLOW-1256 · The group picker's Back and Escape wait for a move to save
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** done (#1249) · **Depends on:** FLOW-1244
- **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-22 (scope c090b96a..9ee732a3): since #1237 (FLOW-1244) a group move closes the picker on its write, and the swipe and ✕ close return early while `move.isPending` (`app/src/screens/project-group-sheets.tsx`, `onOpenChange`). But the Back arrow (`IconButton label="חזרה" onClick={onBack}`) and `onEscape={onBack}` are not guarded: between the tap and the write, Back reopens the edit sheet, and the write's toast, or its failure toast, lands on a sheet that is no longer the picker. Severity: Low (a slow line makes it likelier). Rule: motion.md § 9 (optimistic UI: a failed write rolls back with a message, where the person made the change); hierarchy-and-actions § 10 (one sheet at a time: the picker should not hand over to the edit sheet while its write is open).
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/project-group-sheets.tsx`, its test.

- [x] While `move.isPending`, the Back arrow is disabled and Escape does nothing, like the swipe and ✕ close (the same guard on the new-group sheet's back, if it has one).
- [x] A test: with a move pending, Back and Escape leave the picker open; once it settles, Back works.

**Acceptance:** each box done.
