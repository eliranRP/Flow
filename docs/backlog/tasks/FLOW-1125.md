# FLOW-1125 · Member report: the portfolio in future years and the refinance then (plan)
- **Type:** PLAN FIRST · **Status:** done (plan approved; build in [FLOW-1126](FLOW-1126.md), [FLOW-1127](FLOW-1127.md)) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1124](FLOW-1124.md)
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09: "Plan a design for future years to see how the portfolio works so we can understand how refinance in 10 years from now will looks like and to insert some assumptions for capex and holding costs and tenant turnover (use default) allow the user to edit this".
- **Mockup:** the design canvas "Portfolio projection — 2 concepts" (A: a third tab, תחזית, in דוח שותף; B: its own report; C: the assumptions; D: one property year by year).
- **Approval:** approved option A by the owner, 2026-11-09, adding "include rent change and other cost raises each year". Rent and every cost type therefore rise by their own rate each year, and any year can take a different change.

## The plan

**Where.** דוח שותף gains a third tab, תחזית, after מכירת הכל and ריפייננס (`?mode=forecast`):
- **The band:** the member's share of a refinance in the chosen year, and their share of the cash flow until then.
- **A year stepper:** 1 to 10 years, 10 by default.
- **Bars:** equity and loan balance for each year.
- **החישוב** for the refinance in that year: value then, the new loan at the LTV, the balances then, the closing costs, then the cash out and the member's share.
- **עד אז:** the cumulative cash flow, the new monthly payment, and the DSCR it leaves.
- **לפי נכס:** each property, opening its year-by-year sheet (D).

Every row opens its parts (the FLOW-1121 pattern). The ריפייננס tab stays as today: a refinance now.

**Each year, per property** (one calculation in `packages/shared`, used by the app and MCP):
- **Starting point:**
  - **Rent:** the scheduled rent, not what was collected. The latest full month's rent × 12, so empty months aren't counted twice. A property rented for less than 12 months takes its latest full month's rent too.
  - **Costs:** the last 12 months of `opex` lines by category, after the first rent. Not `debt_service` (the loans), and not `capex` (the CapEx reserve takes its place). A category whose type is repairs is dropped, since maintenance at 5% of rent replaces it. A property rented for less than 12 months has its costs scaled to a year from the months it has.
  - **Loans:** each open loan's terms, so `buildLoanSchedule` gives the balance and payments in any month. Debt service is principal and interest only, never escrow: tax and insurance are already in the costs. A member's demand loan carries its balance unchanged; interest isn't added.
  - **Value:** the latest value.
- **Rent:** last year's rent × (1 + that year's rent change).
- **Vacancy:** each turnover leaves a unit empty for some months. Vacancy = monthly rent × empty months × (12 ÷ average stay).
- **Turnover cost:** units × cost per turnover × (12 ÷ average stay), with units from `projects.units`.
- **Costs by type:** property tax, insurance, HOA and other each = last year's × (1 + that type's change that year).
- **Maintenance and CapEx reserve:** a percent of that year's rent.
- **NOI** = rent − vacancy − turnover cost − costs − maintenance − CapEx. **Cash flow** = NOI − loan payments (principal and interest).
- **Value** = last year's × (1 + appreciation).
- **Refinance in year N:**
  - new loan = value × LTV;
  - cash out = new loan − that property's balances (a member's loan on the property included) − closing costs;
  - a property where the cash out is not above zero is not refinanced (as today);
  - the payment at the assumed rate and term;
  - DSCR = NOI ÷ new debt service. DSCR is shown, not used to cap the new loan.
- **Members:**
  - Each member's own loans (FLOW-1124) are repaid to them first:
    - a loan on a refinanced property, out of that property's payoff;
    - the company's own member loans, out of the total cash out.
  - When the cash out is smaller than the company's own member loans, the loans are repaid in proportion to their balances and nothing is left to split.
  - The rest is split by ownership.

**Assumptions and defaults** (one setting for the portfolio; every value starts at its default, and a changed one is marked):

| Assumption | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Rent change | 3% a year |
| Property tax change | 3% a year |
| Insurance change | 5% a year |
| HOA change | 3% a year |
| Other costs change | 3% a year |
| Appreciation | 3% a year |
| Maintenance | 5% of rent |
| CapEx reserve | 5% of rent |
| Average tenant stay | 24 months |
| Empty months per turnover | 1 |
| Turnover cost | $2,000 |
| LTV | 75% |
| Closing costs | 2% |
| Refinance rate | today's expected rate (Treasury + spread), labelled as today's rate, not a forecast |
| Term | 30 years |

Rent, each cost type and appreciation can take a different change in a given year: "שינוי שונה לשנה מסוימת". One button puts every default back.

**Cost types.** An `opex` category maps to tax, insurance, HOA, repairs (left out, maintenance replaces it) or other. The default is a guess from the category's name (tax/מס, insurance/ביטוח, HOA/ועד, repair/תיקון), else other. The owner can change it in the assumptions sheet.

**Bounds** (in `member_report_check` and the MCP schema):
- changes per year: −20% to +30%;
- maintenance and CapEx: 0 to 30% of rent;
- average stay: 1 to 120 months;
- empty months: 0 to 12, in halves;
- turnover cost: $0 to $100,000 (in minor units);
- LTV: 0 to 100%; closing costs: 0 to 20%;
- refinance rate: 0 to 30%; term: 1 to 40 years;
- by-year overrides: years 1 to 10 only.

**Properties left out, and why:** no value; no rent in the last 12 months (until FLOW-1126 decides to use the forecast's expected rent); another currency; loans in another currency.

**Data.**
- No new table. `member_reports.config` gains a `projection` part, which `private.member_report_check` validates and `set_member_report` and MCP set like the other parts; undo is kind `member_report`.
- `get_member_report` adds, per property:
  - the latest full month's rent and the months rented;
  - the last 12 months of `opex` by category, with the category's type;
  - its units;
  - each open loan's terms.
- MCP `get_member_projection` returns the calculation for a year and a member.

**Not in it:** taxes (depreciation, capital gains); buying more properties; one property's own assumptions (the portfolio shares one set; a later task can add a per-property override).
