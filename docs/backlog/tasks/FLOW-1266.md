# FLOW-1266 · A recurring charge can have an end date
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#1257) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-23, on the charge page (קבועים › one charge): a recurring charge should take an end date. Owner's choices: preset dates and an exact date; once the end date has passed, the charge leaves קבועים (it is no longer recurring; its lines and history stay).
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/<stamp>_flow_1266_recurring_end_date.sql`, `supabase/migrations.lock`, `supabase/tests/database/flow_1266_recurring_end_date.test.sql`, `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts`, `packages/shared/src/recurring-due.ts` and its callers, `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/` (tool `set_line_end_date`), `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, `app/src/screens/recurring-end-date.tsx` (new), `app/src/screens/recurring-charge-screen.tsx`, `app/src/recurring-schedule.ts`, stories and tests, `docs/design/log/2026-11-23-flow-1266.md`, `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-23-flow-1266.md`.

- [x] The owner's word on a charge takes an end date (`recurring_overrides.ends_on`): only the charge's own, as the usual amount (a charge with no project reads the row with no project).
- [x] A charge is not expected in a month whose due day (its usual day, capped at the month's end) is after the end date: the forecast, next month, the rest of the year and the late-bill alerts all stop there.
- [x] Once the end date has passed, the charge is not recurring: it leaves קבועים and its alerts.
- [x] On the charge page, in the קבוע card, a row "תאריך סיום" ("ללא" or the date). Its sheet offers presets (after the next charge, in 3 months, in 6 months, at the end of the year), an exact date, and "ללא תאריך סיום". The toast offers ביטול.
- [x] MCP `set_line_end_date` (idempotency key, write rate limit, undo kind `line_end`), listed in TOOLS.md.

**Built:** a charge that has not come this month and whose next due day is after its end date also leaves (nothing more is expected), so it can leave a few days before the date itself. The MCP refuses a date before today, as the app's date sheet does. Presets are dates: "after the next charge" is the last day of the month the charge next comes in. `get_expected_months` carries `ends_on` only on a charge that has one, so every other charge's answer is unchanged.

**Acceptance:** pgTAP for the column, the cap and the write; unit tests for the presets and the due rule; a story for the row and the sheet at 320 and 393.
