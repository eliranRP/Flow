# FLOW-367 · Redesign: open invoices, with a bank match and mark as paid
- **Type:** UI (design approved) · **Status:** in progress (UI half, claude/project-thread-ttdukn; server half 367-server in the dev lane) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-363](FLOW-363.md), [FLOW-364](FLOW-364.md) · **Source:** the owner's approved redesign, handed off 2026-10-11. Design: [Design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/9grt9spjshVCxerYWrRXiD), page "Ready for dev", section "Open invoices" (boards: prototype, bank match found, an invoice open 34 days with no match, all paid); rules on "Design guide", motion on "Flow & motion".
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/unpaid-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/match-text.tsx`, `app/src/ui/empty-state.tsx`, a new invoice detail screen beside it (`app/src/screens/invoice-screen.tsx`), `app/src/App.tsx` (its route), a new CSS file under `app/src/ui/css/` for the invoice blocks, their stories and tests.
- [ ] Entry from Home's דורש טיפול ("N חשבוניות פתוחות · $X · לגבייה"); header "← בית".
- [ ] Summary card: the total to collect (green, money in), "N חשבוניות · הוותיקה פתוחה N ימים", a two-part bar of the invoices issued since the 1st of last month, שולם (accent) vs פתוח (grey), with both amounts and "מתוך $X שהוצאו מאז …"; marking or linking moves the amount from grey to accent (300ms). Then, only when there are any, "● $X פתוחים יותר מ־30 ימים" (the screen's one signal colour).
- [ ] List oldest first. A card has two lines: customer ↔ amount (green), project ↔ "● פתוחה N ימים" (grey; over 30 days in the signal colour). Number and date live on the detail. A quiet "סימון כשולם" under a hairline at the bottom of the card.
- [ ] Bank match: when a deposit has the same amount and customer, the card shows "✦ נמצא תשלום תואם בבנק · $X · dd/mm · לבדיקה". The detail shows the deposit (date, account, raw description) with "זה התשלום" (links them; the card fades and collapses, 240ms) and "לא זה".
- [ ] Mark as paid (cash, cheque): the card stays, dimmed, with "סומן כשולם · ממתין לסנכרון · ביטול" until the next SUMIT sync removes it; toast with ביטול.
- [ ] Detail (push from the left): status, customer, amount, invoice number and date, project, how long it is open, a note that it isn't in cash until paid but is in the month-end forecast, the PDF from SUMIT, and סימון כשולם when there is no match.
- [ ] "רענון מ־SUMIT · לפני N דק׳" refreshes (shows מרענן…).
- [ ] Empty: "הכל שולם" with חזרה לבית.
- [ ] Loading and empty: use the shared skeleton, empty state, inline spinner and sync strip of [FLOW-371](FLOW-371.md).
- **MCP:** `list_unpaid`, `set_invoice_paid` and `sync_bank` exist. Linking an invoice to a deposit and "לא זה" are new writes: add them (or extend `match_lines`) with an idempotency key, the write rate limit and undo, and list them in TOOLS.md. `list_unpaid` returns the match candidate and the issued-since-last-month totals the bar needs.
- **Acceptance:** stories of the list with and without a 30-day line, a card with a match, a marked card, the detail with and without a match, and the empty state, at 320 and 393 with invented customers and round amounts; a sample test that the list adds up to Home's open-invoices figure; a pgTAP or unit test that a match needs the same amount and customer; a test that ביטול undoes the mark; design review against the boards.
