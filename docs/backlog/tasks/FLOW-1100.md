# FLOW-1100 · A cut hint always ends in "…", even when Safari measures it wider than its box
- **Type:** SMALL CYCLE · **Status:** done · **Depends on:** [FLOW-172](FLOW-172.md)
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-07, with a screenshot of רכישה וסגירה's lines: hints such as "עלויות רכישה וסגירה" and "קרן הלוואה – Sub…" stopped mid-letter at the amount, with no "…" ("Fix it").
- **Files:** `app/src/ui/css/02-fields-sheets.css`, `app/src/ui/list-row.stories.tsx`.

- [x] The drawn cut (FLOW-172's `data-cut` overlay) cuts itself again with CSS's "…" when it is wider than its box, so its end is never clipped mid-letter. Safari can measure a part it already cuts short narrower than it is, so the drawn cut came out a letter or two too long and lost its "…" at the clipped end.
- **Acceptance:** CutAtALetter checks every drawn cut has the fallback (fails without it); a cut forced too wide shows "…" (Storybook, 393, dark).
