# FLOW-1323 · Perf: a forecast skip reads only the overrides, and drops only the answers of its month
- **Type:** MEDIUM (perf) · **Status:** in-progress (#1299) · **Source:** a code review of FLOW-1308 and FLOW-1315 (2026-11-25).
- **Files:** `app/src/forecast-month.ts` (the three skip writes' refresh and the drops), `app/src/kept-reads.ts` (`dropKeptReads`), `app/src/kept-store.ts` (`ids`); tests `forecast-month-no-read.test.tsx`, `forecast-month-kept-drop.test.tsx`, `forecast-month-overlap.test.tsx`, `kept-reads.test.ts`, `forecast-month-keys.test.tsx`, `project-forecast-skip.test.tsx`. No migration.

Since FLOW-1308 and FLOW-1315 the company forecast rows and totals and every project forecast sit under the `month-forecast` key prefix, which the skip, reset and undo writes refetch. After the tap patched them, a skip on Home's future month or the company צפי page read the active `company_forecast` rows (about 102 KB) and `forecast_overrides_for`, the write stayed pending through the read (`use-write` awaits the invalidate and a skip has no `optimistic`), and the month's next swipe queued behind it. Before FLOW-1201, FLOW-1202 and FLOW-1214 a skip read nothing. Separately, with the patch modules not loaded (Home), one skip dropped every inactive project forecast and company total, whatever the month, though a skip changes only the answers that include its month; and the kept rows on the device were not deleted, so a cold start put the pre-skip figures back.

- [ ] A skip, reset or undo refetches only the month's overrides and its `month_forecast` answer, not awaited; the patched company and project forecasts are not read again.
- [ ] The drops on Home are by the skipped month: an answer for other months stays. A failure still drops every inactive one.
- [ ] The kept rows of the answers a skip changes are deleted with them (`dropKeptReads`, found by id with no row's data read).
- [ ] A forecast read under way at the tap, or begun while the month's writes are out, is cancelled or noted and read again once the writes settle, so a late pre-skip answer never stays.

**Acceptance:** a skip sends no `company_forecast` or `project_forecast` request and its write resolves without waiting for one; the patched figures stay; a skip of month M leaves the kept answers of other months, in memory and on the device.
