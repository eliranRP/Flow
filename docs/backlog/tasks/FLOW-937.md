# FLOW-937 · Under an iPhone notch, a page's title is never hidden under the compact bar
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (lane, 2026-10-20, fix/notch-header-padding) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-935](FLOW-935.md). **Source:** Owner report 2026-10-20 with a screenshot of Settings on the iPhone Home Screen app: the compact bar shows at the top of the page and cuts the email line under it.
- **Files:** `app/src/ui/css/07-band-home.css`, `10-settings-onboarding.css`, `10-settings-connect.css`, `35-team.css`, `28-forecast.css`, `47-company-profit.css`, `06-review-list.css`, `06-review-card.css`, `app/e2e/notch-header.spec.ts`, `app/e2e/spec-sources.json`.
- [x] Page rules that set the stacked header's top padding to a plain space dropped `.ui-page`'s safe-area inset, so under a notch the title sat in the notch and `useScrolledPast` (bar height plus the safe area) showed the compact bar at scroll 0, cutting the subtitle. Each override now adds `var(--safe-top)`: settings and its pages, team, the connection pages, the forecast screen, company profit, and the review list and queue. `.ui-page` and `.ui-band` read `var(--safe-top)` too, so one `:root` override emulates a notch.
- [x] `notch-header.spec.ts` sets `--safe-top: 59px` and checks seven pages open with the large title below the notch and the compact bar hidden; all seven fail without the fix. With no notch (tests, Storybook) every value is unchanged.
