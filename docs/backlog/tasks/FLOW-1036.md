# FLOW-1036 · Home v3: the month's activity on Home, dismissable attention rows
- **Type:** UI (design approved) · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** FLOW-1035 (reads its `recurring` flag; works without it)
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/home-parts.tsx`, `app/src/screens/attention-rows.tsx`, `app/src/recurring-schedule.ts` (the schedule read), `app/src/screens/month-drawer.tsx`, a new `app/src/screens/home-activity.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/43-home.css`, `app/src/ui/css/27-compact-header.css`, their stories and tests.
- **Source:** the owner approved the design on 2026-11-05: [canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/Rq2HmKi3E9WEQCFe9eXuyt), page **Ready for dev**, boards "בית v3 · כהה" and "בית v3 · בהיר", with the composition, interaction and token notes beside them. The owner's words: keep today's components and look, change only the composition.

The band is unchanged. Under it, in this order:

- [ ] **דורש טיפול as row-cards.** Each attention row (the rows `attentionRows` builds today) is its own slim card: 56px, surface with the line border, radius 16, icon, one-line title, chevron, and an X (44px, `aria-label` "הסתרה: <title>"). The X slides the card out and folds it (220ms); the section label shows "N הוסתרו · הצגה" to bring them back; when every row is hidden one line remains, "אין מה לטפל · הכול נסקר" with ביטול. A hidden row stays hidden on this device (localStorage, every read and write in try/catch) until its content changes (key it by row kind plus what it counts, so a new change shows again). The review button keeps its place.
- [ ] **תנועות החודש.** A segmented control (the app's `SegmentedControl`): הכול / קבועים / נותר לשלם. Then three groups, rows in the app's list-row style (64px, no dividers):
  - נותר לשלם: the schedule's `late`, `soon` charges (and income still due), a dashed circle, "צפוי ב-<day>" or the signal "איחר" for late, the usual amount muted.
  - קבועים ששולמו: the schedule's `arrived` charges, a check in the tint, the amount (income green with +).
  - חד-פעמי: the month's lines with `recurring = false`, a 6px dot.
  Each group's head shows its count and total. קבועים hides חד-פעמי; נותר לשלם shows only its group. A row opens what it opens elsewhere (the charge page, the transaction page).
- [ ] **The filter docks into the compact bar.** When the in-list control reaches the compact bar, the bar grows to hold it on the band (280ms) and the in-list copy fades; the selected pill slides between options (280ms). A pick while docked scrolls the list's top under the bar.
- [ ] **Sticky group heads.** Each group head sticks under the bar with a 1px line under it; the next group pushes it out.
- [ ] **Leaves Home:** the split card (ממה מורכב התזרים; profit stays a tap away in דוחות and on the forecast screen) and the next-month card. The month pill's next arrow on the current month opens the month drawer on next month, and the drawer shows that month's forecast card (the next-month card's read). Delete what this leaves unused.
- [ ] Reduced motion turns every animation off. Touch targets 44px. Light and dark.
- **Acceptance:** stories for: rows-to-review, some hidden, all hidden, each filter, docked bar, empty month, loading, a failed schedule read (the list says it and offers a retry; the band stays). Unit tests for the grouping and the dismissal key. Story shots at 320 and 393, dark and light.
