# FLOW-1079 · Take a recurring charge out of this month's forecast
- **Type:** SMALL CYCLE · **Status:** in-progress (#1023) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-06: "add the option to remove a transaction from the forecast of a specific month". Of three concepts (swipe, a switch on the charge's page, a row menu) the owner picked B, the switch, "but add a hint: on first time a mini interaction that starts the swipe automatically and returns".
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/home-activity.tsx`, `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/recurring-charge-screen.tsx` (and a small part file), `app/src/forecast-skip.ts` (new), their tests and stories, CSS.

- [x] On the charge's page, in the הכנסה/תשלום קבוע card, a row "לא צפוי ב<month>" with a switch, for a charge still expected this month. On: the charge is out of this month's forecast (`set_forecast_item` with `moved_out`, the forecast page's existing "לא החודש"); off: it is back.
- [x] On Home, a row in נותר לשלם swipes to "לא החודש"; the toast offers ביטול. The first time the list shows, its first row slides open by itself and back, once per device.
- [x] A charge taken out moves to a "הוצא מהצפי" group (crossed out, "לא צפוי ב<month>"), whose swipe brings it back. נותר לשלם's sums and the band's expected figure leave it out.
- **Built:** the forecast item key comes from the schedule row (`forecast-skip.ts`); the swipe is the existing `SwipeRow` with its one-time peek, on the first row of נותר לשלם, remembered per phone in localStorage. A viewer and a preview get no swipe and a locked switch.
- **Acceptance:** unit tests for the grouping, the key and the switch; stories for the swiped row and the group.
