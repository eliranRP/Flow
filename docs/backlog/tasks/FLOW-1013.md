# FLOW-1013 · Forecast rows: a noun for added lines, the date before the number, one צפי per screen
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-01 (`docs/runbooks/ux-rules-review.md`, rules in `.claude/skills/ux-ui-design-review-rules/`). Severity: Medium. From FLOW-1000 to FLOW-1006.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/forecast-parts.tsx` (`itemWhat`, `kindText`), `forecast-project-view.tsx`, `company-next-month-screen.tsx`, `forecast-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/51-next-month.css`.

- [ ] An added line's meta reads "הוספת · עד 10/11": a verb beside "חשבונית #1043" and "קבוע". Use the noun "שורה בצפי". Rule: hierarchy-and-actions (labels name the thing).
- [ ] In the לפי פרויקט view at 320 the date is cut ("חשבונית #1047 · עד 10/…"). Put the date first, or drop the document number in this view. The 3-way segment wraps to two lines at 320; shorter labels. Rule: tables-and-responsive § 1, § 12 (the key value is never cut on mobile).
- [ ] The company צפי page repeats "צפי" in every row hint and every section tag under the title "צפי נובמבר"; keep it only on the title. The kept (NOT_IN_PROFIT) row looks like its tappable siblings but has no link: no chevron or press state. Rule: hierarchy § 5, visual-system (affordance).
- [ ] Optional, low: with one project, the add button says where the line goes ("הוספת קטגוריה לצפי של {name}"); the "מזה …" bridge row on the צפי card is muted and smaller.

**Acceptance:** the 320 shots of the forecast stories show whole dates, one צפי per screen, and no tappable-looking row without a link.
