# FLOW-1013 · Forecast rows: a noun for added lines, the date before the number, one צפי per screen
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#930) · **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-01 (`docs/runbooks/ux-rules-review.md`, rules in `.claude/skills/ux-ui-design-review-rules/`). Severity: Medium. From FLOW-1000 to FLOW-1006.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/forecast-parts.tsx` (`itemWhat`, `kindText`), `forecast-project-view.tsx`, `company-next-month-screen.tsx`, `forecast-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/51-next-month.css`.

- [x] An added line's meta reads "הוספת · עד 10/11": a verb beside "חשבונית #1043" and "קבוע". Use the noun "שורה בצפי". Rule: hierarchy-and-actions (labels name the thing).
- [x] In the לפי פרויקט view at 320 the date is cut ("חשבונית #1047 · עד 10/…"). Put the date first, or drop the document number in this view. The 3-way segment wraps to two lines at 320; shorter labels. Rule: tables-and-responsive § 1, § 12 (the key value is never cut on mobile).
- [x] The company צפי page repeats "צפי" in every row hint and every section tag under the title "צפי נובמבר"; keep it only on the title. The kept (NOT_IN_PROFIT) row looks like its tappable siblings but has no link: no chevron or press state. Rule: hierarchy § 5, visual-system (affordance).
- [ ] Optional, low: with one project, the add button says where the line goes ("הוספת קטגוריה לצפי של {name}"); the "מזה …" bridge row on the צפי card is muted and smaller.

**Acceptance:** the 320 shots of the forecast stories show whole dates, one צפי per screen, and no tappable-looking row without a link.

Done: `ADDED_LINE` ("שורה בצפי") names an added line on its row and its page. Every forecast row's second line puts the date first, in all views. The chips are פריטים, פרויקטים, קטגוריות in the radiogroup "תצוגה לפי". The company צפי page drops the section tag (and its CSS), shows a dated row's hint as "05/11 · קבוע", and the kept row fades (`setAside`). Optional: with one project the add button reads "הוספת קטגוריה לצפי של {name}". Left open: the "מזה …" bridge row on the cards is `SummaryRow` in `profit-summary.tsx`, shared with the profit pages, outside this task's files; and the same kept row on a project's צפי page (`project-next-month-screen.tsx`) is not faded here.
