# FLOW-1239 · Perf: get_project totals only the project's loan balances
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** closed, no change (measured 2026-11-20: no gain) · **Source:** the perf steward's 2026-11-20 [report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-20.md), diff review row 2 (load scope).
- **Files:** none shipped.

The load-scope reviewer read `get_project`'s `loans` block, which joins `loan_balances` on `company_id` and `loan_id` only, and estimated that the view totals every loan of the company there (about 15 ms, from the view's own unfiltered total on 2026-11-19). A builder wrote the fix (`and b.project_id = p_id` in the join, with a pgTAP parity test) and measured it first, on production, read-only, as Flow QA, warm, 10 runs each:

- The loans block alone took about 0.7 ms per run without the predicate and 0.9 ms with it. The join on `loan_id` already keeps the view's work to the project's loans here; the 15 ms estimate does not hold.
- The whole `get_project` call took about 114 ms (lifetime) and 79 ms (current month) on the QA project with the most loans.

No measured gain, so nothing shipped. Reopen if a company with many more loans shows the block taking real time.
