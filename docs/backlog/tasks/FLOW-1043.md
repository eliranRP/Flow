Additive only: no existing prop changes meaning, every existing story still passes. Each part is used by no screen in this PR; the screens come in FLOW-1036 and the wave B and C tasks.

- [ ] **The compact bar docks a control** (plan §3.1): `dock?: ReactNode` and `docked?: boolean` on `CompactBar`. The dock row is always rendered when `dock` is set: closed it is `max-height: 0`, `opacity: 0`, `inert` and `aria-hidden`; open it is 60px, the height over `--motion-page` with `--ease-enter`, the opacity over 160ms. A bar with `centre` reports `band` (64px) to `html[data-compact-bar]`; an open dock sets `html[data-compact-dock="on"]`, which adds 60px to `--compact-bar-h`, so `--month-sticky-top` and `scroll-padding-block-start` follow. Story: the band bar and the list bar, each closed and docked, with a sticky head under them.
- [ ] **The docking hook** (plan §3.2): `useDockedFilter({ barShown, bar })` returning `markRef`, `docked` and `scrollUnderBar`, built on `useScrolledPast`; `DockSlot` fades the in-page control (160ms) and keeps its height. Story: a page with a band bar whose filter docks on scroll (a play test scrolls and asserts `docked`), and a pick while docked that scrolls the list's top under the bar.
- [ ] **The segment's selected pill slides** (plan §3.3): one `aria-hidden` indicator moves between options (`transform`, `--motion-page`, `--ease-enter`; a jump under reduced motion). Band tone: a white indicator, the selected label in the accent colour. Page tone: today's colours. The options' roles, `aria-checked`, `onRadioArrow` and `short` labels are unchanged.
- [ ] **One-line group heads** (plan §3.4): `summary?: ReactNode` on `GroupHead`: title at the start, summary at the end in `t-meta` secondary, 48px minimum, a 1px `--color-line` under, the page background behind, and `inset-block-start` transitioning over `--motion-page`. Without `summary` nothing changes.
- [ ] **Attention row-cards** (plan §3.5): `AttentionCards({ rows, storageKey, title })` with 56px cards (surface, line border, radius `--radius-card-tight`, icon, one-line title, chevron, a 44px X with `aria-label="הסתרה: <title>"`; a row with `button` is first and has no X). The X slides the card 24px toward itself then folds it (`max-height` over `--motion-sheet-out` with `--ease-exit`, opacity 160ms). "N הוסתרו · הצגה" restores; all hidden folds the section to "אין מה לטפל · הכול נסקר" with ביטול; `aria-live="polite"`. `attention-dismiss.ts` keeps `{ [rowId]: dismissKey }` under `flow.attention.<storageKey>`, every read and write in try/catch; a row is hidden while its stored key equals its `dismissKey`. Unit tests: hide, restore, a changed key shows the row again, storage that throws.
- [ ] **Status marks** (plan §3.6): `StatusMark({ kind })` for `due`, `late`, `paid`, `once`, 24px, `aria-hidden`, used as a `ListRow` icon. Story: the four marks on four rows, both themes.
- [ ] Reduced motion turns every new transition off. Both themes. The design log entry's `Rule:` lines name the four new parts and the dock offsets (0, 64, 124; 44 or 104; 56 or 116) for DESIGN-RULES §2.8.
- **Acceptance:** stories as listed, at 320 and 393, dark and light; `pnpm --filter @flow/app test` and `test:storybook` green for the touched stories; `node scripts/check-unused-app-code.mjs` passes (each new part has a story, and the attention cards' helper has a test, so the unused-code check sees them used).
