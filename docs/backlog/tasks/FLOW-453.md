# FLOW-453 · A property's returns from the real lines: rent start, holding costs, net loan proceeds
- **Type:** MCP + UI · **Status:** in-progress (#924) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-450](FLOW-450.md), [FLOW-451](FLOW-451.md) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-01, on a real rental after the FLOW-451 deploy: "for total cost it seems we miss the loan costs" and "how do you treat the −14k? this is more money we put into the deal". Answers on cards the same day: loan costs through the net proceeds received; money in from the actual lines, the purchase price too; "∞" when all the cash came out; rent start = the first rent payment (option A); "show the user the holding costs as a separate line".
- **What:** cash lines only (a journal entry such as depreciation is not cash). Money in the deal = what the property took from the owner that is not its running results: every line before the first rent (purchase and closing, renovation, holding costs), plus every kept-out line at any time (purchase, closing, loan proceeds, refunds), so loan costs held back at closing count and a cash-out refinance lowers it. The statement counts only the lines from the first rent on; a period's lines before it are shown apart. The purchase price comes from the lines (the typed one when there are none). Money in at or below zero shows "∞ · כל הכסף חזר" with the amount that came out.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP for each bucket and the first-rent split on invented lines that add up; the money-in sheet with its parts (רכישה וסגירה, שיפוץ לפני השכרה, עלויות החזקה, תקבולי הלוואות והחזרים); the holding-costs line page; the ∞ state; TOOLS.md and a decision amending 0244.
