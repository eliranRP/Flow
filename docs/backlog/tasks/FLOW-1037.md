# FLOW-1037 · A recurring charge that moves to another payee: the forecast stops expecting it, and Flow asks
- **Type:** MCP + DB · **Status:** claimed (dev lane, 2026-11-05, claude/flow-1037-loan-servicer-handover) · **Depends on:** — · **Related:** [FLOW-430](FLOW-430.md) (a renamed supplier, decision 0179), [FLOW-1038](FLOW-1038.md) (the app's question) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-05: a loan moved to a new servicer. The old servicer's monthly charge stayed in the month forecast while the new servicer's payment for the same property, at the same amount, was already in, so תזרים בסוף החודש counted the payment twice. FLOW-430's suggestion missed it: the names share no word, and the new servicer was not new (it already paid other loans). The owner chose, on a card on 2026-11-05: **hide and ask** (the forecast stops expecting the old charge at once; Flow asks; yes ends the old charge and makes the new one recurring, no brings the old one back).
- **Files:** a new migration in `supabase/migrations/`, `supabase/migrations.lock`, a new `supabase/tests/database/flow_1037_recurring_handover.test.sql` (and the registries it needs: `privilege_guards`, `cross_company_sweep`, `mcp_undo_company`), `packages/shared/src/forecast.ts`, `packages/shared/src/month-forecast.ts` and its test, `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts`, `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/` (`tools_write.ts`, `tools_schemas.ts`, `tools_specs.ts`, `tools_guide_data.ts`, their tests), `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, a decision record (`docs/decisions/0255-*.md` and the index), a changelog fragment.

- [ ] **A handover.** A recurring charge (one party, direction, currency and project, decision 0185) with no line this month has a likely handover when a posted line this month, up to today, from another party has the same direction and currency, the charge's project (a charge with no project never has one), an amount within 2% of the charge's usual amount, and that party had no posted line on that project before this month. The nearest amount wins, then the latest line. A party the company already answered for this charge is not offered again.
- [ ] **The forecast stops expecting it.** `expected_months`' `recurring[]` items carry `handover`: null, or `{ party_id, party_name, transaction_id, doc_date, amount_minor }` while unanswered. `buildMonthForecast` leaves a charge with a handover out of the items and the totals (the app, Home and the MCP's `get_forecast` read the same builder), so the payment is counted once, in today's cash.
- [ ] **The answer.** A new table of answers per charge and party (RLS on, written only through the functions). `answer_recurring_handover(p_direction, p_party_id, p_currency, p_project_id, p_to_party_id, p_same)`, owner or editor: yes writes the old charge "not recurring" and the new party's charge on that project "recurring" (the per-charge overrides of decision 0188), so the next months expect the new payee; no brings the old charge back into the forecast and stops the pair's suggestion; null takes the answer back and restores both overrides. It returns what it replaced, for ביטול.
- [ ] **MCP.** `get_expected_months` and `get_forecast` describe `handover`. A new write tool `answer_recurring_handover` with the idempotency key, the write rate limit and `undo` (kind `recurring_handover`); listed in TOOLS.md and the guide. `get_missing_bills` leaves out a charge that has an unanswered handover (it is not late: it came under another payee).
- [ ] **Acceptance:** pgTAP: a charge moved to a payee that already pays other charges, a different name, the same project and amount, is offered and leaves the forecast; 3% off, another project, or a payee with earlier lines on that project is not offered; yes ends the old charge and makes the new one recurring; no brings it back; undo restores both overrides; a viewer is refused; another company's charge is not reachable. A vitest that the builder leaves a handed-over charge out of the totals. A Deno test for the tool's schema and RPC body.
