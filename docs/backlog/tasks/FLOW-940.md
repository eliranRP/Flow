# FLOW-940 · Under a notch on a tall phone, the review queue's worst-case card ends above the bar
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (lane, 2026-10-21, claude/friendly-tesla-m8id0r) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-937](FLOW-937.md). **Source:** FLOW-937 follow-up: once the queue's header kept the safe area, the `fit=1` card (banner, reason line, a supplier that wraps) ended under the action bar on a notched phone at least 721px tall (393x852 with a 59px notch: card bottom 693, bar top 671; 390x844 with 47: 681 against 663). With no notch it fits (634 against 671).
- **Files:** `app/src/ui/css/06-review-card.css`, `app/src/ui/css/06-review-board.css`, `app/e2e/review-fit.spec.ts`.
- [x] On a tall phone under a notch, the safe area is the header's top air (`max(var(--safe-top), var(--space-3))`, so 12px less), and the title, counter, segments and card sit 6, 6, 4 and 4px closer. `--review-notch` (`min(1px, var(--safe-top))`) is 1px under a notch and 0 without one, so a phone with no notch keeps every value. The card now ends at 661 against 671 (393x852, notch 59) and 649 against 663 (390x844, notch 47); the back row starts at the notch's edge.
- [x] The title and segment gaps in `06-review-card.css`'s tall-phone block never applied (`06-review-board.css` sets both later), so they are deleted and the notch cut goes on the board's rules.
- [x] `review-fit.spec.ts` adds the two notched phones (`--safe-top` injected before load, as in `notch-header.spec.ts`); both fail without the fix.
- [ ] Not covered: 375x812 overlaps with or without a notch (card bottom 634 against a bar at 631 with none, 646 with a 44px notch, was 678). It overlapped before FLOW-937 too.
