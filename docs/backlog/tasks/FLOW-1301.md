# FLOW-1301 · Perf: restore the open screen's kept reads first, and keep them past an hour
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the 2026-11-24 daily report's diff review (load scope, row 9).
- **Files:** `app/src/kept-reads.ts`, `app/src/query-client.ts`, tests.

`restoreKeptReads` reads every row, deep-copies and validates each, and puts all of them in the cache at launch, whatever the route (kept-reads.ts:110-140); decision 0271 measured 60 to 125 ms for the read alone. Rows of screens not yet visited are then dropped from memory after 60 minutes (query-client.ts:12) and are not restored again that session, so after an hour a screen not yet opened shows its skeleton again.

- [ ] Restore the open route's rows first, then the rest at idle.
- [ ] Keep restored rows available past the 60-minute garbage collection (restore them again on a screen's first visit, or give restored queries a longer gcTime), without growing memory past the store's cap.
- [ ] Measure Home's time to its first kept frame before and after on the phone profile.

**Acceptance:** the open screen's kept figures draw no later, and a screen opened after an hour still draws from kept data; the gates pass.
