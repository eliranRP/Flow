# FLOW-1208 · Project profit: לפי חודש puts the months under a sticky head per year
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-15: on a project with months from two years the list ran them together ("ינואר" with no year sat right above "דצמבר 2025"), and only a month outside the newest year carried its year. Of three concepts on a canvas (sticky year heads, year chips, years first) the owner picked the sticky year heads.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/project-profit-screen.tsx`, its test and story; `docs/design/log/`.

- [x] Months from more than one year sit under a one-line sticky `GroupHead` per year, newest first ("2026" · "11 · −$10,037"), the same pattern as the loan's payments by year (FLOW-1046). Each year's head pushes the one before it out.
- [x] The month rows name the month only; the head names the year. A project whose months are all in one year keeps the one list with no year head.
- [x] The לפי חודש head and the next-month card stay above the years.

**Acceptance:** each box done; the unit test covers one year (no head) and two years (heads in order, each with its count and profit, December with no year).
