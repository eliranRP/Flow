# FLOW-1322 · Profit's year view: the rest-of-year card waits forever in December
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (#1298) · **Source:** a code review of FLOW-1308 (the company forecast read), 2026-11-25. Goes live on 2026-12-01.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/company-profit-screen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/company-profit-december.test.tsx` (new), `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-25-flow-1322.md`.

## Evidence

From December, `restOfYearMonths(today)` is empty (there is no month left in the year), so `useCompanyForecastTotals` is disabled (`enabled: months.length > 0`). A disabled TanStack query with no data reports `isPending`, so the screen's `nextWaiting` stayed true and the year view's card (`YearForecast`) drew its skeleton forever. Before FLOW-1308 the card was hidden in December.

## Changes

- [ ] In December the rest-of-year read has no months, so it is not waited for: the card is hidden, with no skeleton and no `company_forecast` read, as before FLOW-1308. The month view and the other months are unchanged.
- [ ] A screen-level test renders the year view in December (no skeleton, no `company_forecast` request) and in November (the card loads from one month left). It failed on main before the fix.

**Acceptance:** the year view in December shows its figures and no card skeleton; in November the card loads as before.
