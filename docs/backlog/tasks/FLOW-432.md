# FLOW-432 · Show a split line's parts on the list row
- **Type:** UI · **Status:** in-progress (#531) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-10-10: a rent deposit split into rent and two utility refunds shows only the refunds' sum under יצא, so he can't see at a glance that it adds up.
- [ ] Small UI, signed off by the design lead (option A; the hint takes up to three lines). On a split line in the cash lists (נכנס, יצא, לא נספר ברווח, and the project's cash lines), the row's hint names the parts that list counts, then the whole line: "חשמל $171.76 · מים וביוב $183.10 · מתוך $2,054.86". More than 2 parts: the first, "ועוד N", then "מתוך". The date goes last and drops first at narrow widths.
