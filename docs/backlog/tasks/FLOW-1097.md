# FLOW-1097 · דורש טיפול cards at 320: one line, a short title and a tag
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#TBD) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1085](FLOW-1085.md)
- **Lane:** UI/UX review.
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-07, after seeing FLOW-1085's two-line cards: "I don't like where you change one row to multiple rows… come with different solutions"; of three one-line options the owner picked B.
- **Files:** app/src/ui/attention-cards.tsx, app/src/ui/banner.tsx (BannerRow), app/src/ui/css/63-attention-cards.css, the rows' producers (screens/attention-rows.tsx, project-attention.tsx, loans-list.tsx, project-returns-cards.tsx, reports-screen.tsx, ui/recurring-list.tsx), their stories and tests.

**Finding:** FLOW-1085 (#1031) wraps a long card title to two lines under 360px, so one row becomes two. The owner wants every card on one line.
**Rule:** feedback-and-states § 4 (long content): nothing cut, and the card keeps its one-line shape.

- [x] Under 360px every card that has them draws a short title (one or two words: what is needed) and a tag with the count, amount or time, on one line: "לבדיקה" + "2 תשלומים", "לבדיקה" + "תשלום אחד", "הלוואת ציוד" + "בעוד 8 ימים", "לגבייה" + "$21,300", "חיובים קבועים השתנו" + "4" ("חשבוניות פתוחות" + "$21,300", the first proposal, does not fit beside the chevron and the X at 320, so the unpaid card says what is needed: לגבייה). Under 360px the card's parts sit 8px apart and its icon 12px in, to make the room. The tag is 13/500, `--color-accent` on `--color-tint`, a chip, 3px 9px. Details such as the loan's name or the amount stay on the page the card opens.
- [x] At 360px and up the card keeps its one-line full title. The X (44px) and the chevron stay.
- [x] The link's accessible name is still the full title (aria-label); the short title and tag are drawn from `data-text`, so the page has the words once.
- [x] `BannerRow` gets optional `short` and `tag`; every producer sets them (Home and a project's rows, the budget row, Loans, the investment's חסר לחישוב, קבועים' לא הגיעו, the report's לבדיקה). Plurals: 1 is "תשלום אחד", 2 and up "N תשלומים" (`countTag`). A row without both keeps its full title, cut at its end.
- [x] FLOW-1085's two-line clamp under 360px is removed from 63-attention-cards.css.

**Acceptance:** stories at 320 (components-attentioncards long-title-320, one-line-320; Home attention, one line at 320; Loans, attention, one line at 320) check each card is one line with nothing cut; unit tests cover each producer's short title and tag.
