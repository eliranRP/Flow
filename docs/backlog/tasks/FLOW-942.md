# FLOW-942 · Under an iPhone notch, no header line is ever cut in half by the top bar
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (lane, 2026-10-21, fix/header-clip-sweep) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-937](FLOW-937.md). **Source:** Owner report 2026-10-21 ("Still have some screens with the top clipping, can you make sure to review all screens to make sure we fix this bug") with a screenshot of a project's profit page on the iPhone Home Screen app: the compact bar ("רווח", "Edisto 2432 ›") is down and the subtitle under it is cut in half above the figure.
- **Files:** `app/src/ui/screen-header.tsx`, `screen-state.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/10-settings-onboarding.css`, `45-project-profit.css`, `25-investment.css`, `45-invoices.css`, `13-line-split.css`, `16-transaction.css`, `46-loans.css`, `app/src/styles/app.css`, `app/src/screens/project-profit-screen.tsx`, `project-month-profit-screen.tsx`, `project-next-month-screen.tsx`, `transaction-top-bar.tsx`, `transaction-screen.tsx`, `transaction-head.tsx`, `recurring-charge-screen.tsx`, `loan-detail-screen.tsx`, `loan-payments-view.tsx`, `loans-summary-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/recurring-charge-parts.tsx`, `app/src/ui/screen-header.test.tsx`, `app/src/screens/transaction-head.test.tsx`, `app/e2e/notch-header.spec.ts`, `app/e2e/spec-sources.json`.
- [x] A sweep of every `/e2e/` fixture and the app's routes with `?preview=1` (115 routes, at 393×852 with a 59px notch, 390×844 with 47px and 375×667 with none, every 4px from the top to 400px) found 31 routes where a header line straddled a shown bar's bottom edge, the bar showed on an unscrolled page, or a header line started inside the notch.
- [x] `ScreenHeader` put the compact bar's mark right after the title (when there was no panel), so the bar came down while the subtitle, the kicker or the row under the title was still on screen, and for a band of scroll positions that line sat half under the bar. The mark now ends the header, after the subtitle and the `below` row, so the bar comes down once every header line is under it. The settings pages' subtitle rules keyed on `.ui-compact-mark + p` now key on the title and the mark, so their spacing at rest is unchanged.
- [x] The project's profit pages draw their figure just under the header, so with the bar waiting only for the subtitle it came down over half the figure. `ScreenHeader` (and `ScreenState`) take `headEnd`, the end of a head that goes on past the header; the three profit pages pass their figure, and the bar waits for it.
- [x] The transaction-style top bar (a transaction, a recurring charge, a loan, its payments, the loans summary) took its line and title at a fixed 110px (60px on the summary), while the head's figure and last line were still under its edge. It now takes them once the page's head has scrolled under it; the 110px sentinel and `--txn-bar-scroll` are gone.
- [x] The project's profit pages (`.ui-pp-head`), its investment page (`.ui-pinv-head`) and the split page's top dropped `var(--safe-top)`, so under a notch the back row sat in the notch and the bar showed at the top. They add it now; the invoice pages read `var(--safe-top)` in place of the raw `env()`.
- [x] `notch-header.spec.ts` scrolls 14 pages 4px at a time under a notch (and three on a small phone with no notch) and checks no header line straddles a shown bar; 14 of the 25 checks fail without the fix. With no notch, every page's text sits where it did at rest.
