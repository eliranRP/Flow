# FLOW-440 · The profit page shows what it leaves out, with the cash month page's "לא נספר ברווח" row
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** [FLOW-438](FLOW-438.md) (done) · **Related:** [FLOW-442](FLOW-442.md) (that list names the loan part) · **Source:** owner request 2026-10-11, with phone shots of a project's month profit page and its cash month page. The shots hold real data, so they are not in the repo.
- **Owner (2026-10-11):** use the pattern the cash view already has: its "לא נספר ברווח" row and list. Keep the two pages consistent; no new design. (This replaces the earlier plan-first options.)
- **Owner (2026-10-11, later):** the profit figure is right; what's missing is that the reader can't see what was taken out of it. That covers any line that counts here only in part, not only loans: a payment split across projects is the same issue (a month listed a whole split payment while its הוצאות counted only this project's part).
- **What happens:** On a project's month profit page (רווח <month>, the FLOW-438 layout), a loan payment counts toward הוצאות only in part. Principal is not an expense; interest and escrow are (DESIGN-RULES §3.6). The list shows the whole payment with "3 חלקים", so the rows don't add up to הוצאות and the reader can't see how the profit was reached. Example with invented figures: rent +$2,000; a loan payment −$1,500 (principal $900, interest $500, escrow $100); electricity −$50. הוצאות reads $650 and profit $1,350.
- [ ] The profit page gets the cash month page's "לא נספר ברווח" row under הכנסות and הוצאות: the kept-out total (−$900 in the example) and its hint ("תשלומי הלוואה", or the part names once FLOW-442 lands). It opens the same kept-out list, scoped to the month and project.
- [ ] The transactions list counts each line as the page's figures do: a loan payment lists its counted part (−$600 in the example) with the whole payment in the hint, so the listed rows add up to הוצאות. The kept-out part shows only under "לא נספר ברווח".
- [ ] A payment split across projects lists this project's part as its figure, with the whole payment in the hint (DESIGN-RULES: "a project's split line shows the project's part, with the whole amount in the hint", FLOW-344). Example with invented figures: a −$3,000 repair split three ways lists −$1,000 here, "מתוך $3,000". Other projects' parts don't count here and aren't shown as "לא נספר ברווח".
- [ ] Same components, order and wording as the cash month page; no new pattern.
- [ ] The MCP tool behind the profit month returns the kept-out total with the counted figures.
- **Acceptance:** a story of the profit page with the examples' invented figures (the loan payment and the split repair) at 320 and 393: the "לא נספר ברווח" row reads −$900 and opens the list, the split row reads −$1,000 "מתוך $3,000", and the listed rows add up to הוצאות. A sample test of that sum. Design review against the cash month page.
