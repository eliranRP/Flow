# FLOW-1124 · Member report: each property's all-in and profit, and each member's own loans in their total
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1117](FLOW-1117.md), [FLOW-1121](FLOW-1121.md)
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09: "The problem with the calculation it does not include the all in today we have on each property, so when i look on the numbers they just take the arv minus the loan and selling costs. Please review the project metrics page to get a sense how the numbers should be. Also include each member loans so when i see the total for a member i know how much i get as a revenue and not just the equity (keep the equity line)".
- **Files:** a new migration (get_member_report, private.member_report_check) (+pgTAP), `packages/shared/src/member-share.ts` (+test), `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/tools_member_share.ts` (+test), `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, `app/src/use-member-report.ts`, `app/src/screens/member-report-*.ts(x)`, `app/src/ui/css/64-member-report.css`, the design log.

- [ ] `get_member_report` gives each property its all-in as the investment page counts it (get_property_returns: purchase, renovation, and the holding costs the renovation does not count) and the company's open loans (id, name, kind, project, currency, balance) so the owner can say whose each is.
- [ ] The setting's members take `loan_ids`: the loans a member gave the company (a loan belongs to one member).
- [ ] Sell everything: value − selling − bank loans − members' loans = equity (הון נקי, kept); a member gets their part of it plus their own loans back: the total in the band. Profit: value − selling − all-in, and each member's part; a property without a purchase is left out of the profit and named.
- [ ] Each property's sheet and row shows its all-in and profit; each row of the new lines opens its parts (FLOW-1121).
- [ ] The members sheet picks each member's loans; MCP `set_member_report` takes `loan_ids`, `get_member_share` returns the new figures.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP for the all-in and the loans list and loan_ids checks; Vitest for the calculation; Deno for MCP; stories for the band total, the profit card, the loans picker; a design log entry.
