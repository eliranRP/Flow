# FLOW-1144 · Utility refunds: a property's bills by month, with a drawer per month
- **Type:** SMALL UI (owner's ask) · **Status:** in-progress (dev lane 1, 2026-11-09, claude/zen-keller-w90qi6) · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1143](FLOW-1143.md)
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-09: "From each month we got a rent payment we need to see utilities payments as well." They asked for a simpler version they can drill into, in a drawer like the rest of the app.
- **Board:** `V3-Project-UtilityMonths` on the v3 canvas (In review page). **Approved by the owner on 2026-11-09** ("Approved, build it").
- **Files:** a new read (+pgTAP, + an MCP tool `list_utility_months`, TOOLS.md); a new screen opened from the property's חשבונות page, with its stories and tests; the design log; changelog fragments.

- [ ] Opened from the property's חשבונות · מי משלם page ("לפי חודש").
- [ ] One row per month, newest first. Each row shows the rent that came in and the bills paid, and a status: ✓ "כל החשבונות הוחזרו", "חסר $X" (the sum of that month's bills not refunded), or "לא נכנסה שכירות".
- [ ] Tapping a month opens a Sheet with the month as title and its status. It has two groups, each with its total: "נכנס מהשוכר" (the rent lines) and "חשבונות ששולמו" (each tenant-paid bill with its status mark, ✓ refunded or a dashed ring not refunded). Every line opens its transaction.
- [ ] A bill counts in the month of its date. Only tenant-paid utilities count, so a company-paid one (such as a property's water the company keeps) never shows.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP for the read (the months, the status, a company-paid utility left out, another company's property refused); app tests and stories at 320/360/393, dark and light, with clip-check; MCP tool test.
