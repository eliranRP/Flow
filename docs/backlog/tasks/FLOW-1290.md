# FLOW-1290 · Perf: `get_breakdown_lines` takes no currency, so Profit's date and amount lines start at mount
- **Type:** SMALL (perf, database) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** decision [0271](../../decisions/0271-half-second-by-kept-data-and-one-wave.md), item 3; FLOW-1271's per-view finding.
- **Files:** a new migration for `get_breakdown_lines`, `app/src/use-company-profit.ts`, tests.

Profit's month by date and by amount read every counted line of each direction in one `get_breakdown_lines` read with `p_currency` (use-company-profit.ts, `fetchDirectionLines`). The read waits for the company currency (`currencyKnown`), because a null `p_currency` reads as `'ILS'` in the function (`coalesce(p_currency, 'ILS')`). Kept, or already read by Home, the currency is known at mount; on a cold direct load of `/profit?by=date` the two lines reads go out one round trip after the others.

- [ ] A migration: a null `p_currency` reads the company's `base_currency`, with a pgTAP test.
- [ ] The page sends `p_currency: null` and drops the wait for the currency in the lines reads' `enabled`; the lines' cache key follows the answer's currency.

**Acceptance:** a cold load of the date and amount orders sends all of its first-view reads at once; the lines do not change.
