**Done (#846):** the view reads the line first and its item legs by the legs index (a lateral fenced with `OFFSET 0`), and takes `company_id` and `transaction_id` from the line. The shadow body's plain join of legs to lines was as fast as the legacy body with planner statistics, but with none (a fresh company before autovacuum) it nested-looped every leg against every line: `search_month_totals_speed` took 13 s and `get_dashboard` 6 s on 5,000 lines. No new index: a covering partial index on item legs made nothing faster. `legs_txn_idx` is rebuilt as `(transaction_id, company_id)`: led by `company_id` it costed the same as `legs_account_idx` when `legs` had few statistics, and depending on when autoanalyze ran the planner probed by the company alone (`search_month_totals_speed` 4 s). Timings on 5,000 lines in one company, legs body: with statistics `get_project` 101 ms, `get_dashboard` 95 ms, `project_cash_months` 22 ms (legacy 42, 51, 12 ms); with none, 133–185 ms, 87–108 ms, 66–85 ms (legacy 336, 55, 25 ms). The largest company has 1,608 lines, a few tens of ms of difference at most. The golden test covers 36 reads (both bases, both `get_project` overloads, the line states, a viewer); the full suite passes.
