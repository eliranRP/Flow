# FLOW-1015 · Home's קבועים tip: only for owners who had the tab, never over the band's controls
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-01 (`docs/runbooks/ux-rules-review.md`, rules in `.claude/skills/ux-ui-design-review-rules/`). Severity: Medium. From FLOW-987.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/home-parts.tsx` (`HomeBandActions`), `app/src/recurring-tip.ts`, `app/src/ui/css/07-band-home.css` (`.ui-band-tip`).

- [ ] The tip is gated by a local flag only, so a new owner sees "קבועים עברו לכאן" about a tab they never had, and "נקודה אדומה: חשבון קבוע לא הגיע" when no dot shows. Show it only to returning owners, and the dot sentence only when the dot is on. Rule: onboarding-and-empty-states § 4 (one coach mark, relevant to what is on screen).
- [ ] The bubble sits over the month pill and the figure with no timeout; it closes only on its ✕ or the icon, so a tap on the stepper hits the bubble. Close it on a tap outside or on scroll. `role="note"` is never announced; use `role="status"` once when it opens. Rule: feedback-and-states § 9, § 19 (a hint never blocks the content).

**Acceptance:** a first-time story shows no tip; a tap outside the bubble closes it and reaches the control under it.
