# FLOW-141 · Redesign: the month-end forecast (צפי), which the user can correct
- **Type:** UI (design approved) · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** [FLOW-363](FLOW-363.md), [FLOW-364](FLOW-364.md) (Home's forecast line opens it) · **Related:** [FLOW-367](FLOW-367.md) (open invoices), [FLOW-447](FLOW-447.md) (recurring charges), [FLOW-139](FLOW-139.md) (loan payments) · **Source:** the owner's approved redesign, handed off 2026-10-11. Design: [Design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/9grt9spjshVCxerYWrRXiD), page "Ready for dev", section "Month-end forecast (צפי)" (boards: prototype from Home, change an item sheet, after moving an item to next month); rules on "Design guide", motion on "Flow & motion".
- **Files:** `app/src/forecast.ts`, `app/src/forecast-sample.ts`, a new `app/src/screens/forecast-screen.tsx`, `app/src/App.tsx` (its route), `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (the band line's link only), `app/src/ui/css/28-forecast.css`, their stories and tests; one migration for the overrides.
- [ ] Back "בית" above the title. Title "צפי" 32/600, "סוף <month> · עוד N ימים", the forecast 34/400 (negative in the signal colour with U+2212; the same number as Home's line), one grey line "היום $X".
- [ ] Two flat sections with totals in their heads: צפוי להיכנס (+$X, green) and צפוי לצאת ($X), largest first. Row: initial, payee, "<what> · <when>", amount (income green with +). No card, bars or chips.
- [ ] The small recurring charges share one row under צפוי לצאת ("+N · חיובים קבועים קטנים · −$X") that opens in place to a short indented list.
- [ ] Tap an item → sheet: סכום צפוי, מתי (השבוע · עד <date> · לא החודש), a link to the source (the invoice, the bill, the recurring charge), שמירה. The big number and the line update at once; toast "הצפי עודכן" / "<name> הועבר לחודש הבא" with ביטול.
- [ ] "לא החודש": after the sheet closes the row folds away (max-height to 0 in 280ms ease-exit, fade 180ms, slide 24px toward the start in 220ms), then appears in a grey לא החודש section at the bottom with a 260ms fade-and-drop-in (8px, ease-enter); the totals update when it lands. Only the leaving row animates; the rows below just close the gap. A tap there brings it back.
- [ ] After any change, a grey line under the number: "שינית N פריטים בצפי · איפוס" (איפוס puts Flow's numbers back, with ביטול). Its 36px row is always there (hidden, not removed) and fades in or out over 200ms, so the list never shifts.
- [ ] A change edits only the forecast, never the invoice, the bill or the charge; it is saved per company until the month ends.
- [ ] Scroll: after 150px the compact bar slides down: בית · "צפי · $X".
- [ ] Footnote on what goes in: recurring charges and income not in yet this month (usual amount), open customer invoices (left to collect), open supplier bills due this month, loan payments due this month.
- [ ] Loading and empty: use the shared skeleton, empty state, inline spinner and sync strip of [FLOW-371](FLOW-371.md) (pending approval; until it is approved, keep today's parts and invent none).
- **MCP:** a `get_forecast` read (items, totals, overrides) and a `set_forecast_item` write (amount, when, or reset; idempotency key, write rate limit, undo) plus `reset_forecast`, listed in TOOLS.md; a `forecast_overrides` table scoped to the company and month, with a cross-tenant pgTAP test.
- **Acceptance:** stories of the forecast (positive, negative), the sheet, a moved item in לא החודש, the איפוס line, at 320 and 393 with invented payees and round amounts; a unit test that the big number equals the sum of the sections and Home's line; a test that moving an item updates the totals and ביטול restores it; a pgTAP test that overrides expire with the month and another company can't read them; reduced motion moves rows without the fold; design review against the boards.
