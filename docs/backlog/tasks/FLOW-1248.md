# FLOW-1248 · A charge taken out of this month is quiet everywhere
- **Type:** SMALL CYCLE · **Status:** in-progress (#1242) · **Depends on:** FLOW-1081, FLOW-1247
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-21, chose "Skipped means quiet" after asking why the recurring screen said עוד לקבל +$0 while the rent group said עוד +$2,500. A tenant's rent taken out of the month's forecast (FLOW-1081, "לא צפוי בנובמבר") was still counted in its group head, and still called late on Home, the project page and the late list.
- **Files:** `app/src/forecast-skip.ts` (`lateIsSkipped`, `withoutSkippedLate`, `useLateBillsQuery`), `app/src/ui/recurring-list.tsx` (`RecurringListRow.skipped`; `groupSummary` and `leftOf` leave it out), `app/src/screens/recurring-screen.tsx` (`recurringView` sets `skipped`), `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` and `app/src/screens/project-attention.tsx` (read the late list through `useLateBillsQuery`), `app/src/forecast-skip.test.tsx`, `app/src/ui/recurring-list.test.tsx`, `docs/qa/CONTROLS.md`, `docs/design/log/2026-11-21-flow-1248.md`, `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-21-flow-1248.md`.

- [ ] A skipped row is not part of its group head's "עוד …" (nor of "N מתוך M"); it stays in its group with "לא צפוי ב…".
- [ ] A late item (`missing_bills`) whose forecast item is moved out in the current month's overrides (Israel's month, decision 0182) is not late on Home or the project page: the count, the single-item name (`lateItemName`) and the link (`lateTarget`) all use the filtered list, so 2 late with one skipped names the other.
- [ ] Key rules are the existing ones (`forecastKeyOf`): `party:<direction>:<party>:<currency>`, with `:<project|none>` when split. A late row cannot say whether its party is split, so it is skipped when either key is moved out. A charge in another currency than the company's is never in the forecast and stays late. A row with no party id stays late.
- [ ] While the overrides load or fail, the late list is whole: an error never hides an alert.
- [ ] Tests: `lateIsSkipped` and `withoutSkippedLate` (split, whole, other project, other party, other direction, no project, other currency, no party id, no skips known); the group head's עוד drops a skipped expense and a skipped income row and keeps the row.

**Known limit:** no server change. The MCP `missing_bills` tool still lists a charge taken out of the month; only the app is quiet about it. The row is the same one `useMissingBillsQuery` reads on the charge page and קבועים, which keep their own handling (FLOW-1081).

**Acceptance:** each box done, with a test for each rule above.
