# FLOW-425 · Split rows keep their amounts whole, and the first sheet opens at once
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** done · **Depends on:** — · **Source:** cycle 19 phone review of production at dd76b5b, 2026-10-10 (C19-1, C19-2). Shots and harness copies are in the project files under `reviews/ui-ux-cycle-19/`. The design lead signs it off; there is no owner card.
- [x] (UI lane 1) **Split-row hints cut amounts at 320 to 375 (C19-1).** In cash lists, a split row's parts and its "מתוך" total are cut mid-glyph with no ellipsis when names are long or amounts reach thousands: "חשמל ומים משותפים $1,000" shows 62% at 320 and loses "$1,000"; next to a $12,345.67 figure the first name collapses to 0px and "מתוך $98,765.43" loses its "$9". Amounts are never cut: give each part's amount `flex: none` and let only the name ellipsize; with "ועוד N", keep about 3em of the first name or drop the part's amount before its name; size the מתוך line to its content so only the date drops. Add 320 stories with "שיפוץ והשבחה $1,234.56" and a $12,345.67 row. Rows at 393 and wider are fine and must stay as they are.
- [x] (UI lane 1) **The first sheet on a screen waits for the sheet code (C19-2).** Since FLOW-815, `Sheet` renders nothing until vaul arrives, so on a screen with no closed sheet mounted (a transaction opened from a link or push, among others) the first tap shows no response for the whole download (about 1.5 s on a slow line). Load the sheet code on idle after first paint (`loadSheetDrawer()` from `preloadScreens`), and also on `pointerdown` of a sheet opener. Home's entry budget must stay under its cap.
