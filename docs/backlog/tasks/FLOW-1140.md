# FLOW-1140 · A cold open and a screen's code: the screen's own skeleton, not Home's or a blank page, and an offline line
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress · **Depends on:** —
- **Source:** the owner's app-wide skeleton ask, 2026-11-09 ([FLOW-1137](FLOW-1137.md)). The audit found two places outside any one screen: a cold open of any signed-in route drew Home's skeleton, with Home's band, while the session loaded (`App.tsx`), and a full-screen route opened from a tab showed a blank page until its code loaded (`Suspense` fallback null). The owner decided the plan on 2026-11-09:
  1. A cold open onto a screen other than Home shows that screen's own skeleton from the first frame.
  2. A full-screen route opened from a tab shows the screen's title bar and grey blocks after the usual 300ms, so a fast load still shows nothing; a boundary already on screen keeps the previous screen up. A neutral bar where the title isn't known.
  3. Offline: "Please add a check for internet connection that recognizes when offline only then use the offline ui". A loading place that waits on a paused read shows a short offline line instead of its skeleton, and fills in by itself when the connection returns; online nothing changes.
- **Files:** `app/src/cold-open-route.ts` (new, the route-to-skeleton map), `app/src/cold-open.tsx` (new, the frames), `app/src/App.tsx`, `app/src/screen-suspense.tsx`, `app/src/connection.ts` (new, the connection check), `app/src/main.tsx`, `app/src/ui/offline-wait.tsx` (new, the offline line), `app/src/ui/skeleton.tsx`; `DelayedSkeleton offline` in `app/src/screens/loans-list.tsx`, `loans-summary-screen.tsx`, `unpaid-screen.tsx`, `recurring-charge-screen.tsx`, `project-unassigned-screen.tsx`, `report-account-screen.tsx`, `report-lines-screen.tsx`, `forecast-screen.tsx`; stories in `app/src/cold-open.stories.tsx`; tests in `app/src/cold-open-route.test.tsx`, `app/src/connection.test.ts`, `app/src/ui/offline-wait.test.tsx`.

- [x] A cold open draws the route's own loading frame, picked by its path, in the outlet it lands in: under the tab bar for a tab route (Home keeps its skeleton), without it for a full-screen route, with the band or bar the screen has.
- [x] A full-screen route opened from a tab shows its bar and blocks after 300ms while its code loads (FullScreen's Suspense fallback); a boundary already on screen still keeps the previous screen up.
- [x] One connection check: React Query's online state starts from the browser's own and follows its events, so a read sent with no connection pauses.
- [x] Offline, a loading place waiting on a paused read shows "אין חיבור · ננסה שוב כשהחיבור יחזור" in place of its skeleton: every ScreenState (the journal composer's bank-name wait included) and the page bodies marked `DelayedSkeleton offline`. Online, skeletons stay skeletons.
- **Not covered** (they keep their skeleton offline; a follow-up if the owner wants them): the skeletons drawn without ScreenState or a marked DelayedSkeleton, among them קבועים' list, the projects tab and a project's page, the reports pages' rows (ReportRowsSkeleton), the investment page, the member report, the + sheet's tiles and the change sheet's choices, and the blocks inside a band or a card (Home's band, next-month cards, charts). A screen whose read goes through `screenPhase` already shows its offline failure line, not a skeleton.
- **Acceptance:** cold-open stories per route family, a full-screen fallback story and the offline and online stories, in both themes; unit tests for the route map and the offline switch.
