# FLOW-1307 · Perf: the company forecast in one read
- **Type:** LARGE (perf) · **Status:** blocked (FLOW-1305, FLOW-1306) · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 4 (owner approval 2026-11-24).
- **Files:** a migration `<ts>_flow_1307_company_forecast.sql` (`private.company_forecast_build`, `public.company_forecast`); `packages/shared/fixtures/vectors/company-forecast/*.json`; pgTAP `flow_1307_company_forecast.test.sql` and the generated vectors file. No app change.

- [ ] `public.company_forecast(p_today date, p_months text[], p_detail text)`, security invoker: per month the totals; `'rows'` adds each part's rows, the removed, skipped and arrived rows, the loan cash and the split keys; `'totals'` adds the counts the cards' notes read. Rows come unsorted; the app sorts.
- [ ] Vectors from `project-next-month*.test.ts`, `company-next-month*.test.ts` and `company-year-forecast.test.ts`, plus month-length, 31 December and Israel-midnight cases.

**Acceptance:** equal to the TypeScript build for every vector and for the fixtures' companies; warm mean under 250 ms for Flow QA as its owner, read-only (else FLOW-1311 is built); size and time reported.
