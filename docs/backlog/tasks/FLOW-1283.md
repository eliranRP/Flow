# FLOW-1283 · Perf: build a loan's schedule only when something needs it
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** decision [0271](../../decisions/0271-half-second-by-kept-data-and-one-wave.md), item 4: the main-thread profile of 2026-11-23 ([2026-11-23-main-thread.md](../../qa/perf/2026-11-23-main-thread.md)); FLOW-1274, the Loans visit.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/loan-list.tsx`, `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.ts`, `packages/shared/src/loan-schedule.ts`, tests.

`readLoanList` builds each loan's whole schedule (`buildLoanSchedule`, up to 360 months of bigint math) when the list is read, for the next payment and the late check (FLOW-1260 builds it once per loan). With 12 invented loans the schedule math is about 25 to 30 ms of the Loans visit on the phone profile (`powBig` 8 ms, `buildLoanSchedule` 7 ms, `divHalfEven` 6 ms, `utcTime` 5 ms), so a company with 40 loans would pay about 90 ms before the list draws.

- [ ] Find the next due payment without building every row (walk to the first row after today, or compute that period directly for the level-payment kinds) and keep the whole schedule for the band's summary and the detail page, built on first use.
- [ ] Same next payment, late flag and band figures as today for every loan kind (amortizing, interest-only, balloon, demand, paid off), with a rate change in the middle; the existing loan tests are the check.

**Acceptance:** the Loans visit's schedule math falls under 10 ms with 12 loans, the list and the band show the same figures, and the loan page still opens with its schedule.
