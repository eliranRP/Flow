# FLOW-1251 · Home: a late charge taken out of the month does not show and then drop on a cold load
- **Type:** SMALL (owner decision: Home's attention box) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the perf steward's 2026-11-22 [report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-22.md), diff review (load scope), row 2. Follows FLOW-1248 (#1242).
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx`, `app/src/forecast-skip.ts`, or `missing_bills` on the server; their tests.

On a cold load Home reads `missing_bills` at mount, but the overrides that hide a charge taken out of the month arrive only after first paint (`useLateBillsQuery(true, afterPaint)`). Until they land the late list is whole by design (an error never hides an alert), so a skipped late charge shows as late and then drops; with two late and one skipped, the row changes from a count to a name and its link changes. Not visible on Flow QA today (no skips this month).

- [ ] The owner chooses: hold the late rows until the overrides settle (a short wait on the attention box), or filter skipped charges in `missing_bills` on the server (no wait, and it also closes FLOW-1248's known limit for the MCP tool).
- [ ] Build the chosen form; a test shows no row appears and then drops on a cold load with a skipped late charge.

**Acceptance:** a skipped late charge never shows on Home; an override read that fails still shows every late charge.
