# FLOW-1170 · Settings that move a closed month's figures are not locked
- **Type:** PLAN FIRST · **Status:** in-progress · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1166](FLOW-1166.md)
- **Source:** the owner's decision of 2026-11-11 on the closed-month lock ("Not now, file a follow-up"). The lock (FLOW-1166) refuses a change to a closed line. These settings move a closed month's reported figures without touching any line, so the lock does not see them.
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/20261114094800_books_closed_settings.sql`, `supabase/tests/database/books_closed_settings.test.sql`, the flow-mcp specs of `set_cash_basis`, `set_category_pnl`, `set_category_cash`, `set_category_account`, `set_overhead_project` and `set_books_closed_through`, `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, `app/src/company-role-cache.ts`, `app/src/use-write.ts`, `app/src/setup/steps.tsx`.

- [x] Decide, per setting, what happens while a period is closed: refuse the change, apply it to open periods only, or allow it and say that closed figures moved.
- [x] The cash basis: switching it re-dates every income line; say whether a closed period keeps the basis it was closed on.
- [x] A category's P&L or cash flag, its account, the overhead project: the same question.

**Acceptance:** a plan the owner approves, with the wording of any new message, before any build.

**Decided by the owner, 2026-11-14:** when one of the settings below would move figures in a closed period, the change is REFUSED, with a message that the books are closed and how to reopen, the same way as editing a closed line; it is refused only when it would actually move a closed figure; otherwise it goes through (approved option "Refuse it"). The app's sentence for it, approved as written (approved option "Shorter"): "הספרים סגורים עד {date}, וההגדרה הזו משנה אותם. כדי לשנות, הזיזו אחורה את תאריך הסגירה דרך עוזר ה־AI." ({date} formatted dd/mm/yyyy exactly as `booksClosedNote` formats it.) The settings: the company's cash/accrual basis, a category's P&L flag, a category's cash flag, a category's account, the company's overhead project.

**In this PR:** one migration. Row triggers `companies_lock_closed_month` and `categories_lock_closed_month` (BEFORE UPDATE, with a WHEN clause on `cash_basis`, `overhead_project_id`, `excluded_from_pnl`, `in_cash`, `ledger_account_id`) refuse a person's change (`auth.uid()` set, as the line lock) when a line it would move is closed by `private.books_closed_hit`, with `the books are closed through YYYY-MM-DD: this setting changes them`. The triggers cover every writer: the owner RPCs (`set_cash_basis`, `set_category_excluded_from_pnl`, `set_category_cash`, `set_overhead_project`), their MCP tools, `mcp_set_category_account`, `mcp_merge_account` and its undo (they rebind categories), `mcp_undo` for each kind, and the app's own ביטול. The lines each setting moves: the basis, a line that reads differently on the two bases (an income invoice, credit or receipt; an invoice or credit with no cash date; a line paid on another day than its document date); a category's P&L flag or account, the lines filed to it and those with a split or loan part filed to it (what `legs_category_sync_tg` re-posts); its cash flag, the same lines except one with its own cash override; the overhead project, the lines of the old and the new project (filed to it, a split part on it, an allocation to it). A change to the same value fires nothing. With no closed date the categories guard reads the company's row by its key and the companies guard nothing; with one, each check is an indexed existence query (`transactions_company_date_idx` and `transactions_company_cash_idx`; `transactions_company_id_category_id_fk_idx` and `legs_company_id_category_id_fk_idx`; `transactions_company_id_project_id_fk_idx`, `legs_project_idx` and `allocations_company_idx`). `private.mcp_refused` passes the new message as `refused`. A rename keeps the P&L flag without changing it and back: `rename_category` and the `category_name` undo name the category in the transaction-local setting `flow.rename_keeps_pnl`, which `categories_default_pnl` reads, and their write-back is gone. The service role and the jobs are not refused; none of them writes these columns today (no cron job, sync or edge function). The app says the approved sentence (`booksClosedSettingNote`, mapped by `booksClosedMessage`) in the date sheet's and the category sheet's toast, and in place of the setup business step's save error, with no retry; the control keeps its value. The tools' specs and `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md` say each is refused while it would move a closed figure, and to move the closed date back with `set_books_closed_through` first.

**Left:** the app writes no category cash flag, account or overhead project (they are the assistant's), so no screen of the app shows those refusals. The ledger rebuilds the line lock leaves (`post_lines`, `rebuild_legs`) and a journal entry's legs on a merged account are not locked. A category's P&L flag and account count a closed line with its own P&L choice too (its split parts and loan parts ignore that choice, so the check stays the category's lines).
