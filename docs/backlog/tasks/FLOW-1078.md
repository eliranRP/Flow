# FLOW-1078 · Back from the background, the tab bar sits mid-screen on iOS
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (#1022) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** the owner's screenshot of Reports, 2026-11-06: the tab bar floated in the middle of the page with the report showing under it. It happened after coming back to the app, and a scroll put it back.
- **Files:** `app/src/ui/keyboard-inset.ts`, its test.

- [x] On iOS, when the app becomes visible again (`visibilitychange`, `pageshow`), the page scrolls 1px and back a frame apart, so iOS places its fixed layers (the tab bar) on the current viewport again, as the owner's own scroll did. Other platforms are left alone.
- **Acceptance:** unit tests for the nudge on iOS and none elsewhere; on the owner's phone, the tab bar stays at the bottom after returning to the app.
