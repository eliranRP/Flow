**Type (Rubik 400 / 500 / 600; text is light, weight only for titles and headers)**
- [ ] Tokens for each role, and no other sizes in app CSS: Home band figure 52/500; detail amount 40/400; list and summary total 34/400; screen title 32/600 (also the first-run hero titles: welcome, done, invitation); card figure 28/400; detail name and step titles 24/600; empty-state title 24/600; sheet title 20/600; section and date header 20/500 (white, plain text, no caps, no grey labels); row name and body 17/400; primary button 17/500; secondary button and icon-row value 16/500; row second line 15/400; detail meta line 14/400; chips, segments and avatar initials 14/500; caption 13/400; list amount 17/400 with the cents at 11px, raised.
- [ ] The decision records the new scale and says what it amends ([0120](../../decisions/0120-income-green-type-scale.md) and today's `--type-*` weights, which put 600 on 17px titles and 500 on body).

**Colour**
- [ ] Tokens (dark drawn; light keeps the same token names): background #15111E, card #1E1929, inner surface #251F33, tint #2A2045, accent #B894FF (pressed or on tint #C3A5FF), band #7B3FE4 and past-month band #5A2DB0, money in #62CB8D (income only), signal #FF8A80 with a soft signal #E8A598, text #F1EDF8 / #B1A8C4 / #978EAB, lines #2E2740. Band change pill: grey #4A4458 when the change helps, #C3302B when it hurts.
- [ ] DESIGN-RULES says: green means money in and nothing else, and income gets a "+" everywhere; at most one signal colour per screen, only for what needs attention (a loss, a change that hurts, invoices over 30 days); good states stay neutral; status and tags are coloured text or a 6px dot, never a filled chip; bars are 6 to 8px.

**Right to left and numbers**
- [ ] Every amount is wrapped in `<bdi dir="ltr">`, the minus is U+2212 and sits left of the number, income gets "+" (the shared formatter in `packages/shared/src/money.ts` stays the one source, [0096](../../decisions/0096-currency-display.md)). Chevrons are icons, never the ‹ › characters the browser mirrors; path separators read "←".

**Motion (all off under `prefers-reduced-motion`)**
- [ ] Tokens and one utility each: `--ease-enter: cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)`, `--ease-exit: cubic-bezier(0.4,0,1,1)`; compact bar 240ms; sheet 280ms in / 220ms out (scrim 260ms); page enter: content rises 8px and fades, 280ms, the first blocks staggered 40ms; push to a detail page: slides in 24px from the left and fades, 280ms; press: opacity .72 and scale .985 for 120ms; thin bars grow from the right, 480ms, on enter.
- [ ] `reduced-motion.tsx` turns every one of them off (the end state shows at once).

**Shared patterns (each one component with a story; screens reuse them)**
- [ ] Flat list row for long lists: on the background, 20px side margins, no dividers or chevrons, 36px initial circle, name and one grey line, amount; about 64px tall. Cards (#1E1929, radius 16 to 24) for focused items and summaries.
- [ ] Header that shrinks into a compact bar: the big block fades with scroll, then a compact bar slides down (240ms, ease-enter; out with ease-exit) with Back, "<title> · <figure>" and the screen's actions; group heads stick under it. Extends the compact bar of [0156](../../decisions/0156-labelled-back-and-compact-bar.md) instead of adding a second one.
- [ ] Organize chips with a sticky compact panel (month lines and profit): one chip always selected, no filters, and when the chips scroll away the panel slides down with Back · "<title> · $X" and the chips; group heads then stick under the panel.
- [ ] Transaction-page icon rows (icon, name, value at the end, chevron; a switch row has no chevron) and the "נספר ב" row with its sheet (switches נספר ברווח, נספר בתזרים), reused by the charge page and the category sheet.
- [ ] Guided connection page frame (numbered steps, a tinted box for a problem, fields with העתקה in monospace LTR), reused by SUMIT, Mercury and MCP in FLOW-512.
- [ ] Loading and empty parts are specified in [FLOW-371](FLOW-371.md) (skeleton blocks, empty state, inline spinner, sync strip, progress bar). This task leaves room for them: the motion tokens include its pulse (1.2s) and crossfade (200ms), and nothing here adds a loader of its own.
- [ ] Undo toast: 3 seconds, with ביטול where the change can be undone ([0180](../../decisions/0180-instant-tap-notify-on-failure.md) keeps the failure toast).
- [ ] Remove what this leaves unused: type tokens, CSS blocks and components the new ones replace go in the same PR ([CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#remove-what-your-change-leaves-unused)).
- **Order for the UI lanes:** this task first; then FLOW-371 and FLOW-372; then Home (FLOW-364) and month lines with the transaction page (FLOW-366), which most screens reuse; then the rest by area: review and invoices; projects, investment and loans; profit and forecast; קבועים; search, add and split; Settings, categories and invitation; onboarding last (it reuses the connection pages, the new-loan form and the review card).
- **Acceptance:** stories of each shared part at 320 and 393, dark and light, with invented data; a unit test that the money formatter puts "+" on income and U+2212 on a loss inside a `bdi`; a test that every motion utility resolves to 0ms under reduced motion; `pnpm clip-check` clean; `node scripts/check-unused-css.mjs` and `node scripts/check-unused-app-code.mjs` pass; DESIGN-RULES and the implementation guide match the tokens; design review against the "Design guide" page.
