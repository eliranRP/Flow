- [x] **The horizon.** One constant: 12 months after the current month, from next month through the same month next year (in November 2026: December 2026 to November 2027), in `packages/shared`, used by the app, the MCP tools and (as the same 12) the database read.
- [x] **Server.** `company_next_month_inputs`' `rest_edits` (and their matches by month) cover every month in the horizon, not only the rest of the year. `expected_months` takes up to 13 months (this month and the horizon); its `recurring[]` was never limited by `p_months` (a pgTAP test says so).
- [x] **Client forecast.** `companyMonthForecast` builds any month in the horizon from the same reads; the year view's rest-of-the-year card (decision 0200) still sums this year's months only.
- [x] **Home.** Any month in the horizon is reachable: `?month=` clamps to the horizon's last month, the later arrow stays on until it, the earlier arrow on a future month goes one month back. The drawer's year stepping reaches the last month's year; every horizon month is a dashed צפי cell to pick, a month past it is faded and disabled. A future month shows what next month shows (band, figures, הצפוי ב<month> by project, פירוט ועריכה), built for that month; a month after next has the caption "הערכה · לפי קבועים, הלוואות וממוצע 6 חודשים".
- [x] **The expected profit on פירוט ועריכה** (the owner's ask above), with a story and a test.
- [x] **Edit pages.** `/forecast/next` and `/projects/:id/profit/next` (and its edit and add pages) take `?month=YYYY-MM` in the horizon (next month by default); Home's פירוט ועריכה and the rows pass it; the titles name the month; edits are saved for it.
- [x] **MCP.** `get_project_forecast_edits` and `get_forecast` take any month up to the horizon's last and refuse a later one (validation); `get_expected_months` takes 13 months; `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md` and the Deno tests.
- [x] Stories (Home on a far month, on the last month, the drawer past December), unit tests, the e2e spec.
- **Acceptance:** in November 2026 Home steps from December through November 2027 and no further; each month's band, rows and פירוט ועריכה are that month's forecast, the foot row with its expected profit, and an edit made there is saved for that month; the year view's card still reads סוף 2026 (December only); lint, typecheck, unit, Deno and pgTAP tests pass.
