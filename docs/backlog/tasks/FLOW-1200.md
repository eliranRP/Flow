# FLOW-1200 · A refinance closing's taxes, insurance and escrow count as the renovation's holding
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (#PR) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-14 ("Follow the book"), on a rental refinanced three weeks before its first rent.
- **Files:** a new migration (`private.refinance_transactions`, patching `get_property_returns` and `get_property_return_lines`), `supabase/tests/database/property_returns.test.sql`, `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/tools_property.ts`, `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, decision 0270.

A renovation's holding (0261) counted everything paid before the first rent, a refinance closing's rental-year taxes, insurance and escrow included.

- [x] In a refinance transaction, only interest and loan fees stay in `deal.holding_minor` and the `deal_holding` lines (decision 0270).
- [x] pgTAP: taxes before the refinance and the payoff interest at it stay; the refinance's taxes leave; the lines add up; the money in is unchanged.
