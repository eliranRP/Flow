# FLOW-1221 · Company profit's search: where it sits, and what it hides while it shows results
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** in-progress (#1211) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** UX rules review 2026-11-16 (scope 1321ffca..5cf22eb7): #1176 (FLOW-1194): the month view's search field (חיפוש בתנועות) renders after the period switch, the figure, the summary, the next-month card, the chart and the order chips, about 1,060px down at 393 (shot `screens-routes--search`), so it is found only by scrolling and its results start under the keyboard. While a search shows its flat result list, the order chips (לפי פרויקט / קטגוריה / תאריך) stay visible with one still selected but do nothing, and the summary, card and chart stay above the results. Severity: Medium. Rule: hierarchy-and-actions § 1, § 5.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/company-profit-screen.tsx`, `app/src/ui/css/47-company-profit.css`, `app/src/screens/company-profit-screen.stories.tsx`.

- [x] The owner picks where the field goes: (a) into the header, under the period switch; (b) where it is, scrolling itself to the top when it takes focus; or (c) another place. **Owner's pick, 2026-11-16: (a) "In the header".** The field is in `ScreenHeader`'s `below` slot right after the period switch, in the same slot whether or not a search shows, so typing keeps its focus.
- [x] The owner picks what hides while a search shows results: the order chips only, or the chips, the next-month card and the chart. **Owner's pick, 2026-11-16: "Chips, card and chart".** The chips (in the header and on the compact bar's panel), the next-month card and the chart hide while the found lines show; clearing the search brings them back with the chosen order kept. The month figure and the summary card stay (the owner did not name them; the figure is the month's own net and its subtitle says so). Open for the owner: hiding the summary card too would put the results right under the field at 393.

**Acceptance:** each box done, with a story or test for each fixed state at 320 and 393.
