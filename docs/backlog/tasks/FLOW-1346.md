# FLOW-1346 · Perf: cold Home sends its first-view reads ahead of the rest under the four-read cap
- **Type:** SMALL (perf; part of FLOW-1300) · **Status:** in-progress (#1321) · **Source:** the [2026-11-28 daily report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-28.md), diff review (load scope, row 1).
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (`useCashYearsQuery`, the attention counts, the `useAfterFirstPaint` gate), `app/src/lib/read-cap.ts` (only if a priority is chosen), their tests.

Since FLOW-1344 the app sends at most 4 REST and RPC requests at once, first in, first out. On a cold Home `cash_months` and `get_dashboard` go first (`home-early-reads.ts`). `cash_years` (`HomeScreen.tsx`, `useCashYearsQuery`), which only bounds the month picker's earliest month, takes slot 3. `month_forecast` (the band's forecast row) and the two activity lists wait for first paint by design (`useAfterFirstPaint`, FLOW-1019, the owner's decision of 2026-11-03), so when they start, the attention counts issued at mount are already queued ahead of them; `list_my_companies` (from the auth state, `team-queries.ts`) lands there too. They sit at positions 14 to 17 of 18 and start about 3 round trips (0.9 to 1.4 s on the FLOW-804 phone) later than before the cap. FLOW-1019 stays: the fix is in what waits, not in reading the forecast at mount.

- [ ] Issue `cash_years` after paint (or when the month picker opens).
- [ ] Let the after-paint reads (`month_forecast`, the activity lists) start ahead of the attention counts, and say in the PR which way: the counts also wait for first paint and start after them, or a priority in the cap for those reads (FIFO within a priority). Keep FLOW-1259 (the late bills and their overrides start at mount).
- [ ] A test that pins the cold Home issue order.

**Acceptance:** the next sweep's cold Home fresh-data time is back to 11-27's 3.36 s or better, with no other screen slower (with FLOW-1348, this carries FLOW-1344's "no worse stall-free times").
