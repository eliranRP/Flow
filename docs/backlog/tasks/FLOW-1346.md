# FLOW-1346 · Perf: cold Home sends its first-view reads ahead of the rest under the four-read cap
- **Type:** SMALL (perf; part of FLOW-1300) · **Status:** ready · **Source:** the [2026-11-28 daily report](../../qa/perf/2026-11-28.md), diff review (load scope, row 1).
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx` (the hook order and `cash_years`), `app/src/home-early-reads.ts`, `app/src/lib/read-cap.ts` (only if a priority is needed), their tests.

Since FLOW-1344 the app sends at most 4 REST and RPC requests at once, first in, first out. On a cold Home `cash_months` and `get_dashboard` go first (`home-early-reads.ts`), but the rest follow hook order: `cash_years`, which only bounds the month picker's earliest month, takes slot 3, and `month_forecast` (the band's forecast row), the two activity lists and `list_my_companies` (the role for write controls, the company name) sit at positions 14 to 17 of 18, behind four attention counts. They start about 3 round trips (0.9 to 1.4 s on the FLOW-804 phone) later than before the cap.

- [ ] Issue `cash_years` after paint (or when the month picker opens).
- [ ] Issue the reads the first view draws (`month_forecast`, the activity lists, `list_my_companies`) before the attention counts, by issue order or by a priority in the cap; keep FIFO within a priority.
- [ ] A test that pins the cold Home issue order.

**Acceptance:** the next sweep's cold Home fresh-data time is back to 11-27's 3.36 s or better, with no other screen slower.
