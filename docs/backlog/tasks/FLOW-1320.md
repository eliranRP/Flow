# FLOW-1320 · Perf: company_forecast reads the recurring charges once, and its first call on a connection costs less
- **Type:** MEDIUM (perf) · **Status:** claimed (dev lane 2, 2026-11-24, db/flow-1320-forecast-recurring-once) · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 6 acceptance ("warm mean under 300 ms for Flow QA"), and the 2026-11-24 production profiling after FLOW-1318 deployed.
- **Files:** one migration `supabase/migrations/<stamp>_flow_1320_forecast_recurring_once.sql` (private helpers added; `private.company_forecast_build` and the gathers of `public.company_forecast` patched in place); a pgTAP file `supabase/tests/database/flow_1320_recurring_once.test.sql`. No app change, no new table or trigger.

Measured on production as Flow QA, read-only (2026-11-24, after FLOW-1318): `company_forecast` 'totals' warm 1 month 250–263 ms (budget 300), 12 months 318–354 ms (over); the first call on a connection about 766 ms. `private.recurring_parties` runs twice per call: inside `expected_months` and again inside `company_next_month_inputs` for the general costs. The build reads only the recurring charges' fields of `expected_months` (never the handovers or `seen_this_month`). On the app's statement statistics, the first call per connection dominates: `expected_months` mean 506 ms, `company_next_month_inputs` mean 765 ms; the Profit year view's repeat fresh-data load went from 1,632 to 2,531 ms.

- [ ] `company_forecast` reads the recurring parties once per call and hands them to both the recurring charges and the general costs.
- [ ] The forecast path skips what its build never reads (the handovers, the supplier and customer names).
- [ ] The first call on a connection costs less: measure on a fresh connection locally; fewer distinct plpgsql functions per request, or inlinable SQL helpers, where that helps.
- [ ] Every public answer is byte-identical: `company_forecast`, `project_forecast`, `month_forecast`, `expected_months`, `company_next_month_inputs` and `loan_outlooks` (vectors, pgTAP flow_1307/1309/1310/1313/1318, and an equality capture before and after on Flow QA-shaped data). No grant widens; public reads stay security invoker.

**Acceptance:** 12-month 'totals' warm under 300 ms on production (about −20 to −60 ms); a lower first call on a connection. Local before and after timings, warm and on a fresh connection, for `company_forecast` 1 and 12 months 'totals', 1 month 'rows', `project_forecast` and `month_forecast`.
