# FLOW-1320 · Perf: company_forecast reads the recurring charges once, and its first call on a connection costs less
- **Type:** MEDIUM (perf) · **Status:** done (#1296) · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 6 acceptance ("warm mean under 300 ms for Flow QA"), and the 2026-11-24 production profiling after FLOW-1318 deployed.
- **Files:** one migration `supabase/migrations/20261123135127_flow_1320_forecast_recurring_once.sql` (`private.recurring_charges` and `private.forecast_gather` added; `private.recurring_parties`, `public.company_forecast` and `public.project_forecast` patched, same answers); a pgTAP file `supabase/tests/database/flow_1320_recurring_once.test.sql`. No app change, no new table or trigger.

Measured on production as Flow QA, read-only (2026-11-24, after FLOW-1318): `company_forecast` 'totals' warm 1 month 250–263 ms (budget 300), 12 months 318–354 ms (over); the first call on a connection about 766 ms. `private.recurring_parties` runs twice per call: inside `expected_months` and again inside `company_next_month_inputs` for the general costs. The build reads only the recurring charges' fields of `expected_months` (never the handovers or `seen_this_month`). On the app's statement statistics, the first call per connection dominates: `expected_months` mean 506 ms, `company_next_month_inputs` mean 765 ms; the Profit year view's repeat fresh-data load went from 1,632 to 2,531 ms.

- [x] `company_forecast` reads the recurring parties once per call and hands them to both the recurring charges and the general costs (the overhead project's `project_forecast` too).
- [x] The forecast path skips what its build never reads: the handovers (a second read of the lines) and `seen_this_month`. The names stay: the builds read them (rows, the income note).
- [x] The first call on a connection costs less: measured on fresh connections locally, it drops by what the warm call saves (one plpgsql and two SQL functions fewer per call, and one read of the charges' lines instead of three). Its extra cost over a warm call, about 40 ms locally, is unchanged: planning the SQL bodies takes 3 to 5 ms and compiling the build about 8 ms, so no helper rewrite would cut it much here; production's larger first-call cost needs a production-side breakdown.
- [x] Every public answer is byte-identical: `company_forecast`, `project_forecast`, `month_forecast`, `expected_months`, `company_next_month_inputs` and `loan_outlooks` (vectors, pgTAP flow_1307/1309/1310/1313/1318, and an equality capture before and after on Flow QA-shaped data). No grant widens; public reads stay security invoker.

**Acceptance:** 12-month 'totals' warm under 300 ms on production (about −20 to −60 ms); a lower first call on a connection. Local before and after timings, warm and on a fresh connection, for `company_forecast` 1 and 12 months 'totals', 1 month 'rows', `project_forecast` and `month_forecast`.
