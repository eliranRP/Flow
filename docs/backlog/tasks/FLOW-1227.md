# FLOW-1227 · Perf: a category's lines are read in pages of 200, not 40
- **Type:** SMALL (perf) · **Status:** in-progress (#PR) · **Source:** the perf steward's daily diff review, 2026-11-18. `fetchAllLines` in `use-company-profit.ts` read one category's lines 40 at a time, one page after another.
- **Files:** `app/src/use-company-profit.ts`.

`get_breakdown_lines` caps `p_limit` at 200 (`least(greatest(coalesce(p_limit, 40), 1), 200)` in `20261029190012_category_by_project_parts.sql`, which the later migrations keep; FLOW-1207 says "p_limit up to 200"). A keyed read with a limit of 200 is therefore accepted as is, and a category takes a fifth of the round trips.

- [x] Page the keyed read in `LINES_PAGE_ALL` (200) and delete `LINES_PAGE`.
- [x] Check the tests that count pages or calls (none count the keyed pages of 40; the direction read's tests are unchanged).

**Acceptance:** a category's lines are read in pages of 200 and come out in the same order and with the same rows; the tests pass.
