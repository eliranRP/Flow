# FLOW-1068 · Investment page: ROI, the flip's net profit, and new deal metrics

- **Design:** the v3 canvas boards `V3-Investment` (both kinds), `V3-Calc` and the new `V3-Calc-FlipProfit`, `V3-Calc-Roi`, `V3-Calc-Roe` (Ready for dev, "דף פרויקט ודפי המשקיע"; the canvas note "roiNote").
- **Files:** a migration (`projects.selling_cost_bp`, `set_project_investment`, `get_property_returns`); `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/tools_property.ts` and its tests; `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`; `app/src/screens/project-returns-*.ts(x)`, `project-investment-*.ts(x)`, `app/src/ui/investment-card.tsx`, their stories and tests; `app/src/ui/css/26-returns.css`; a decision, a design log entry and a changelog fragment.

A flip's "רווח צפוי" was ARV − purchase − rehab: it left out the holding costs, the loan's costs and the costs of selling, so the profit read high (Real Estate by the Numbers, Ch 42 counts all four cost buckets). The מדדים tab had four rows and no ROI.

- [ ] **Selling costs:** a new input, `projects.selling_cost_bp` (0 to 30%), set with `set_project_investment` (undo `project_investment`). Null reads as 8% of the ARV, shown as an estimate the owner can change.
- [ ] **Flip net profit:** ARV − selling costs − purchase − rehab − holding − financing costs. The band, "מאיפה הרווח" and "איך מחושב" show it; ROI on the cash in the deal sits under it.
- [ ] **Flip metrics** in two groups. תשואה: ROI on the cash in the deal, ROI per year (from the first purchase line to today), margin on the ARV. מחיר: the 70% rule (ARV × 70% − rehab, against the price paid), the break-even sale price.
- [ ] **Rental metrics** in three groups. תשואה: ROI since purchase, return on equity (ROE), cash on cash, equity multiplier. סיכון: DSCR, break-even occupancy, loan to value. מחיר: cap rate, gross rent multiplier, expense ratio, cash flow per unit.
- [ ] Each new row opens its calculation page with the formula rows; a missing input shows "חסר · הוספה" as the other rows do.
- [ ] Rows that open point left, back links point right (RTL).
- **MCP:** `set_project_investment` takes `selling_cost_bp`; `get_property_returns` returns the new figures; TOOLS.md lists both.
- **Acceptance:** stories for both kinds' מדדים tab and each new calculation page; unit tests of every formula, the missing-input cases and the 8% default; pgTAP for the new input and the returns' new fields; the existing returns tests stay green.
