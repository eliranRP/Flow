# FLOW-1025 · All the cash out but losing money: money in counts the losses, and ∞ only for a gain
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** done (#954) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-03, on a rental after a cash-out refinance: a negative cash flow under "∞ · כל הכסף חזר". **Approved:** the owner, 2026-11-03 ("yes do both"). Decision [0251](../../decisions/0251-money-in-counts-the-shortfall.md).
- **Files:** a migration (`get_property_returns`: `money_in_parts.shortfall_minor`, `equity.cash_out_cover_months`), `supabase/tests/database/property_returns.test.sql`, `app/src/screens/project-returns-data.ts`, `project-returns-info.ts` (`infiniteTotal`, `coverText`, `coverLine`, the shortfall row, cash on cash), `project-returns-cards.tsx` (the total), `app/src/dev/returns-sample.ts`, the stories and tests, `supabase/functions/flow-mcp/tools_property.ts`, `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`.

The money in the deal was fixed at the first rent (0245). After a cash-out refinance it sat below zero, and the total return read ∞ whatever the rental earned: the money the owner adds each month to cover a negative cash flow never counted, and a loss on nothing in read as an infinite return.

- [x] Money in adds the running cash flow since the first rent when it is below zero (`shortfall_minor`), so it climbs back toward the owner's money as the losses add up.
- [x] With all the cash out and a negative cash flow, `cash_out_cover_months`: how long the cash still out covers that cash flow. The card says it ("הכסף שיצא מכסה עוד כ־5 שנים של תזרים שלילי").
- [x] ∞ only for a gain. A negative total reads as the loss in money; cash on cash with a negative cash flow reads as the cash flow for a year.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP: the shortfall is 0 with a positive cash flow; a loss is a part of money in and gives the cover months; losses past the cash out bring money in above zero. Unit tests: the card shows the loss and the cover line, not ∞; the money in page lists the shortfall row.
