# FLOW-514 · Redesign: first-time onboarding in 7 short steps
- **Type:** UI (design approved) · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** [FLOW-363](FLOW-363.md), [FLOW-512](FLOW-512.md) (the connection pages), [FLOW-139](FLOW-139.md) (the new-loan form), [FLOW-365](FLOW-365.md) (the review card) · **Related:** [FLOW-506](FLOW-506.md) (setup follow-ups; its last open box, the iOS label check, carries over to this flow), [FLOW-513](FLOW-513.md) (an invited person sees the invitation instead) · **Source:** the owner's approved redesign, handed off 2026-10-11; it replaces today's 5-step setup ([0163](../../decisions/0163-setup-state-on-the-server.md) keeps the skip state on the server). Design: [Design canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/9grt9spjshVCxerYWrRXiD), page "Ready for dev", section "First-time onboarding (הגדרה ראשונה)" (boards: welcome, 1 העסק, 2 חיבור הבנק והספרים, 3 הפרויקטים, 4 הלוואות, 5 תיוג חכם, 6 אישור ראשון, 7 מסך הבית, done, skip all); rules on "Design guide", motion on "Flow & motion".
- **Files:** `app/src/setup/*` (`shell.tsx`, `steps.tsx`, `route.tsx`, `route-screens.tsx`, `model.ts`, `store.tsx`, `server-store.ts`, `copy.ts`, `card.tsx`, `facts.tsx`, `home.tsx`, `sample-review.tsx`, `demo-slot.tsx`, `settings-row.tsx`, `setup.css`), `app/src/screens/onboarding-screen.tsx`, `app/src/screens/company-form.tsx`, `app/src/ui/setup-demos.tsx`, `app/src/ui/setup-demos.css`, `app/src/ui/demo-player.tsx`, `app/src/ui/demo-player.css`, `app/src/ui/demo-pointer.tsx`, `app/src/ui/progress-bar.tsx`, `app/src/ui/stepper.tsx`, their stories and tests; remove the old steps and demos the new ones replace.
- [ ] Shown once, right after the first sign-in. Every step can be skipped, and "דילוג על הכל" is always at the top. What was skipped waits in Settings › הגדרה ראשונה (shown only until done).
- [ ] Frame: welcome and done are full screen with no progress. Steps 1 to 7: top row הקודם · "שלב N מתוך 7" · דילוג על הכל, and a 7-segment progress bar (done accent, current band purple, next #2E2740; 300ms). Title 24/600 (32/600 on welcome and done), one lead line; fixed bottom with the main button and "דילוג על השלב". Each step fades up 8px (280ms).
- [ ] The "movie" (welcome, connect, loans, smart tagging, home screen): a 212px panel with a soft band gradient and a small phone outline; three scenes looping every 2.4s (mini rows in the app's row style, an optional pill, a finger dot moving to what is tapped); scenes cross-fade (360ms) and slide 10px (420ms ease-enter); three dots (the current 18px wide), the scene's caption 15px, and pause / play. Under reduced motion it shows the last scene. Scenes: welcome (a bank line arrives → a suggestion → approved, the month's profit updates); connect (the bank's API token page → read-only, copy → paste in Flow, connected); loans (a loan payment arrives → principal / interest / escrow → only interest and escrow count in profit); smart tagging (a line with no project → a suggestion from past approvals → after a few, it happens by itself); home screen (open in the browser → Share › Add to Home Screen → opens like an app). All sample rows are invented.
- [ ] 1 · העסק: name; currency $ / ₪; רווח ותזרים לפי: תאריך תשלום (recommended, one line why) / תאריך חשבונית ([0170](../../decisions/0170-pnl-follows-company-basis.md)). No business-kind question: starter categories open as the general set. המשך disabled until there is a name.
- [ ] 2 · חיבור הבנק והספרים: Mercury and SUMIT rows with חיבור (FLOW-512's connection pages; "מתחבר…" → "מחובר ✓"); one line that Flow only reads; the main button reads "המשך בלי בנק" until one is connected.
- [ ] 3 · הפרויקטים: a name field and הוספה; added projects as chips with ×; once a bank is connected, "מהבנק נראה שיש לכם עוד:" with dashed suggestion chips. Names only.
- [ ] 4 · הלוואות: once a bank is connected, "מהבנק נראה שאתם משלמים ל:" with the lenders found in the lines; an added one shows "משלמים $X בחודש · מ־<date>" and "להשלמה". "+ הלוואה אחרת" opens FLOW-139's new-loan form. One line: rate, term and payment are completed in the Loans tab. With none, the button reads "אין לי הלוואות".
- [ ] 5 · תיוג חכם: the movie and one switch (on by default) "מציע פרויקט וקטגוריה לכל שורה חדשה. אתם מאשרים."
- [ ] 6 · אישור ראשון: one sample review card (invented supplier, project and category) with אישור; it turns to "✓ אושר · נכנס לרווח של <project>". A line says it is a sample and is not saved; the lead says real ones wait in Home › דורש טיפול.
- [ ] 7 · מסך הבית: the movie and a notifications switch (something to approve, the Sunday summary). Main "סיום", secondary "לא עכשיו".
- [ ] Done: "הכל מוכן" and what to expect (daily cash, monthly profit); a card listing each step with ✓ (green) or — and its result (מחובר / דילגתם / a count / "N להשלמה"), rows fading in 60ms apart; "מה שדילגתם עליו מחכה בהגדרות › הגדרה ראשונה."; "לבית".
- [ ] Skip all: a sheet "לדלג על ההגדרה?" (you can come back from Settings › הגדרה ראשונה; until a bank is connected Home shows sample data; FLOW-371 replaces that with Home's "חברו את הבנק" empty state, so follow FLOW-371), "דילוג ומעבר לבית" / "המשך בהגדרה". The business is named "העסק שלי" until renamed.
- [ ] Loading and empty: use the shared skeleton, empty state, inline spinner and sync strip of [FLOW-371](FLOW-371.md).
- **MCP:** `rename_company`, `set_company_currency`, `set_cash_basis`, `create_projects`, `add_loan` and `set_jev_mode` exist. The lenders found in the lines (step 4) and the project names suggested from the bank (step 3) are reads: add them to an existing read (or a `get_setup_suggestions` read) and TOOLS.md. Skip state stays in `setup_states`.
- **Acceptance:** stories of welcome, each step (with and without a bank), done and the skip sheet, at 320 and 393 with invented names; a test that each step's main button and skip move on and the skip state saves on the server; a test that המשך stays disabled without a name; a test that the sample approval writes nothing; reduced motion shows each movie's last scene; design review against the boards.
