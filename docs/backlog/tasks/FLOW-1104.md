# FLOW-1104 · A project month's profit shows a credit on an expense with a minus
- **Type:** BUG · **Status:** in-progress (#TBD) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-07, with a phone shot of a project's "רווח נובמבר" page: a RentRedi transfer that pays back water and sewage (מים וביוב) showed as −$164.59, though it is money in. The day's total (+$4,165) and the expenses line ($3,730) already counted it as a credit.
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/project-month-categories.tsx` (`MonthLineRow`), `app/src/screens/project-month-profit-screen.test.tsx`.

- [x] `MonthLineRow` took its sign from the line's category side (`line.side`), so every row in an expense category had a minus. It now signs by where the money went (`sideMinor(line, "kept")`): a credit on an expense reads +, as the day's total counts it. The same row draws the "לפי קטגוריה" view, so both views are fixed.

**Acceptance:** a test with an expense line of −$164.59 (a credit) shows "+$164" on its row and no minus; it fails without the fix.
