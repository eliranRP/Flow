# FLOW-1258 · Loans: a late payment, not a payment due soon, needs attention
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** done (#1252) · **Depends on:** none
- **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-22. The Loans screen's "דורש טיפול" showed a card for every loan with a payment due within 10 days ("תשלום … בעוד 9 ימים"), so five loans paid on time every month read as five items to handle. The next payment came from the schedule alone and never looked at the payments recorded, so a missed payment never showed either: the date just moved to the next month. Owner's decision: drop the payment-soon cards (the band already shows the next payment) and add a card only when a scheduled payment is late and no payment was recorded for it.
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/<stamp>_flow_1258_loan_last_paid.sql` (`loan_balances.last_paid_on`), `supabase/tests/database/flow_1258_loan_last_paid.test.sql`, `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts`, `app/src/screens/loan-reads.ts`, `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.ts` (`latePayment`), `app/src/screens/loan-outlook.test.ts`, `app/src/screens/loan-list.tsx` (`lateOn`), `app/src/screens/loans-list.tsx` (`loansAttention`, the row's late mark), `app/src/screens/loans-list.test.tsx`, `app/src/screens/attention-short.test.tsx`, `app/src/screens/loans-live.test.tsx`, `app/src/ui/attention-cards.stories.tsx` (the sample's soon card becomes a late one), `app/src/screens/loans-screen.stories.tsx`, `docs/qa/CONTROLS.md`, `docs/design/log/2026-11-22-flow-1258.md`, `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-22-flow-1258.md`.

- [x] A loan is late when a scheduled due date is more than 10 days past (`LATE_GRACE_DAYS`) and no posted payment is recorded on or after 15 days before that date (`EARLY_PAY_DAYS`, a payment made early counts). Only the latest such due date is checked; a loan with no payment in the books at all is late only for its first payment (its history may predate the books). One payment pays one date, so a missed month right after a late one shows a month later. Demand loans, closed loans and loans with no schedule are never late.
- [x] The card reads "תשלום באיחור · {loan}" (what is wrong first, as the check card does, so a long name is what the line cuts; the date and amount are on the loan's page), short title the loan's name, tag "באיחור", the alert icon; `dismissKey` `late:{dueDate}`, so a hidden card comes back for the next missed date.
- [x] Late cards come first (the more urgent). The payment-soon cards are gone, with `SOON_DAYS` and their tests; the waiting-for-review cards stay as they are.
- [x] The row's StatusMark shows "late" for a late loan (it read `next.dueDate < today`, which never happened).
- [x] `loan_balances` gets `last_paid_on`, the latest posted, not removed loan payment's date, appended last; pgTAP checks it.
- [x] Tests for on time, paid early (and the 15-day edge), late, no payment at all, inside the grace, the latest date only, a demand loan, and the read's `last_paid_on` to `lateOn` wiring. Stories: the attention state shows a late card.
