# FLOW-426 · The viewer toast wraps whole, and the update reload never drops the user's place
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** ready · **Depends on:** — · **Source:** cycle 19 phone review of production at dd76b5b and code review of FLOW-910 (#555), 2026-10-10 (C19-3, C19-4). Shots are in the project files under `reviews/ui-ux-cycle-19/`. The design lead signs it off; there is no owner card.
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **The viewer toast breaks inside a part (C19-3).** At 320 to 375 the FLOW-911 toast wraps as "צפייה בלבד · שינויים / נעשים על ידי בעל העסק": the "·" sits mid-line and a part is split. Build the message from its two parts so it breaks only at the "·" (each part nowrap, or `HintParts`). One line at 393 stays as is.
- [ ] (UI lane 3) **The update reload can drop the user's place (C19-4).** FLOW-910 reloads at once, silently, whenever no dialog is open and no field has focus, so a user scrolling a long list or holding state that is not in the URL (Home's period, a typed search) loses it with no word why. Design call (design lead): no toast or banner. Reload on the next in-app navigation, or when the page goes hidden (`visibilitychange`), whichever comes first; never while the page is in front and idle. Keep the dialog and focus guards.
