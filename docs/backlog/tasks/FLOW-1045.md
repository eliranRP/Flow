# FLOW-1045 · Reports v3: the hub, profit and loss by category, project and month with a drill-down, the balance sheet by project
- **Type:** UI (design approved) · **Status:** done (#984, #995) · **Depends on:** FLOW-1043 (the shared parts)
- **Files:** `app/src/screens/reports-screen.tsx`, `reports-parts.tsx`, `reports-sheets.tsx`, a new `reports-hub-screen.tsx`, `report-lines-screen.tsx`, `report-account-screen.tsx`, their stories and tests; `app/src/ui/css/61-reports.css`; `app/src/reports.ts`, `app/src/use-reports.ts`, `app/src/report-schemas.ts`; `app/src/App.tsx` (the reports routes only); `docs/design/log/2026-11-05-flow-1045.md`, `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-05-flow-1045.md`.
- **Source:** [HANDOFF §3 Reports](../../design/v3/HANDOFF.md#reports) and the canvas boards `V3-Reports`, `V3-Report`, `V3-Report-PLProj`, `V3-Report-PLMonth`, `V3-Report-BS`, `V3-Report-BSProj` (Ready for dev, "דוחות · אושר 2026-11-05"; the canvas note "reportsNote"). [BUILD-PLAN §4 and §5](../../design/v3/BUILD-PLAN.md). The owner's rule: there are only two reports, profit and loss and the balance sheet; every other view is a breakdown of one of them.

- [ ] **Routes** (plan §5): `/reports` is the hub; `/reports/pnl?view=category|project|month[&project=<id>|&month=YYYY-MM]` and `/reports/balance?view=assets|liabilities|project[&project=<id>]`; `/reports/lines` and `/reports/account` stay. Old links redirect with their state: `/reports?kind=balance` and the project band's דוחות link (`?project=`).
- [ ] **The hub** (Hub pattern): the page title, then one card for each report with its headline figure (net profit for the period, equity as of today), a decorative mini chart (`aria-hidden`, the figure written beside it) and two facts, then "פירוקים": P&L by project, P&L by month, balance sheet by project, each opening the report with that view picked. The period and date chips keep their sheets.
- [ ] **Profit and loss** (Overview): the band carries the period pill and the net profit, with income and expenses under it. A docking `SegmentedControl` קטגוריה / פרויקט / חודש. Category: today's statement sections (income, expenses, kept out) with one-line sticky `GroupHead`s. Project: one row per project with its net and, under it, income and expenses; general expenses that belong to no project in their own group, so the groups add up to the band. Month: `get_profit_months` rows the same way.
- [ ] **The drill-down stays on the screen.** A project or month row opens its own P&L on the same screen: the band changes to that project's or month's name and net (`get_pnl_statement` with `p_project_id`, or the month's `p_from`/`p_to`), the list splits into income and expense categories, "‹ לפי פרויקט" (or "‹ לפי חודש") returns, and a last row goes on, "לדף הפרויקט" or "תנועות החודש". The totals add up to the row tapped (a test).
- [ ] **The balance sheet** (Overview): the band carries the date chip and the equity figure, assets and liabilities under it. A docking segment נכסים / התחייבויות והון / פרויקט. By project: one `mcp_balance_sheet(p_as_of, p_project_id)` per property project through `useQueries` (plan §5), one row per property with its equity (value less loans and accumulated depreciation), and a property row drills into its own assets and liabilities the same way.
- [ ] Delete what this leaves unused: the old `/reports` segmented control between the two reports, `ByProjectView` cards if the row form replaces them, CSS blocks.
- **Acceptance:** stories for the hub, each P&L view, a project and a month drill-down, each balance view and a property drill-down, and the `V3-States` states that apply; a unit test that a drill-down's totals equal the row tapped and that the general-expenses group makes the by-project view add up; shots at 320 and 393, dark and light; the redirects tested.
