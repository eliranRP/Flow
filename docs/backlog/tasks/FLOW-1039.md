# FLOW-1039 · Pull to refresh on every screen (ld-07)
- **Type:** SMALL UI · **Status:** claimed (UI lane 1, 2026-11-05, claude/flow-1039-pull-to-refresh) · **Depends on:** — · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-05: pulling down on a screen does nothing in the Home Screen app (iOS gives a standalone web app no pull to refresh); add a loading spinner on pull, as other banking apps do. Design: DESIGN-RULES "ld-07 Pull to refresh" (no component yet); tech plan: pull to refresh does what opening the app does, with a 30-minute floor on a bank sync.
- **Files:** a new `app/src/ui/pull-refresh.tsx` and its story and test, a new CSS file under `app/src/ui/css/`, the app shell that wraps every routed screen (one place, not per screen), `app/src/ui/index.ts`, `docs/design/DESIGN-RULES.md` (the ld-07 row and the component), a design log entry.

- [ ] Pulling down from the top of any routed screen (the scroller at scroll top 0) shows a spinner (`.ui-spinner`, `--motion-spin`) that follows the finger with resistance; past a threshold (about 64px) a release refreshes, before it springs back with nothing done.
- [ ] A refresh re-reads every active query (TanStack Query) and starts what opening the app starts (the bank refresh, under its own floor); the spinner stays until the reads settle, then collapses. A failed read shows the app's usual error toast once.
- [ ] Never starts inside an open sheet, dialog or a horizontally swiped row, from a scrolled list, or during a period or card swipe or the edge-back gesture; native overscroll does not double it. Works by touch (iPhone) and does nothing with a mouse wheel.
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion`: no follow and no spring, the spinner fades in place. The spinner has an accessible busy label ("מרענן").
- **Acceptance:** a unit test for the threshold, cancel, and no start when not at the top or inside a sheet; a story in both themes at 320 and 393 px (idle, pulling, refreshing); a design log entry.
