# FLOW-1032 · Jev splits a loan payment instead of filing it whole under one loan category
- **Type:** feature (owner call) · **Status:** done (#972) · **Source:** the owner, 2026-11-05, on the Loans tab: "the payments we did on November did not change the loan numbers", then "Jev didn't split the payment, his suggestion was only one category. Can we add the ability of Jev to infer multiple categories specifically for loans, where most of the time things are recurring".
- **Files:** `supabase/migrations/20261103150910_jev_loan_split.sql` (new), `supabase/migrations.lock`, `supabase/tests/database/jev_loan_split.test.sql` (new), `supabase/functions/_shared/jev_tag.ts`, `jev_tag_plan.ts`, `jev_tag_rest.ts`, `jev_tag_loan.ts` (new) and its test, `packages/shared/src/database.types.ts`, `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`, decision [0254](../../decisions/0254-jev-loan-split.md) (amends 0145), `docs/changelog.d/2026-11-05-flow-1032.md`.

What happened: a servicer's monthly payment synced from the bank, and Jev answered the loan interest category for the whole line (its answer is one category). In auto mode at or above the threshold that filled the line as all interest, the owner approved it, and no loan split was ever written: the loan balance did not move and the P&L counted principal and escrow as interest.

- [x] When Jev's category for a line is a loan-part category (`categories.loan_part` set), the tagging job looks for the line's loan instead of filling the category: an open, non-demand loan in the line's currency that takes a payment on the line's date, whose payment (`loans.payment_minor`) equals the line amount, or, failing that, whose last attached payment had the same amount (a recurring payment after an escrow change). Exactly one loan must match, and not one that already has a payment attached in the line's month; none or several write nothing.
- [x] For that loan the job computes the split the way `attach_loan_payment` does (the schedule row for the date, `allocateLoanSplitWithFees`) and writes it with a new service-role RPC, `jev_prefill_loan`, under the same gates as `jev_prefill`: auto mode, the threshold, the line's review open, not assigned by the owner, the anomaly gate, the line not already split. The split's parts take the loan's categories, as any split does.
- [x] Jev never fills a loan-part category on a whole line: `jev_prefill` does not write such a category (it answers `category: false`), so a payment with no matching loan stays as the bank hint left it.
- [x] The fill is recorded in `jev_prefills` (new `loan_id`) and `undo_jev_prefill` (and MCP `undo_jev_prefill`) takes it back: the split is cleared, unless the owner changed or confirmed it since.
- [x] MCP: `get_jev_status`/`jev_suggestions` keep working; TOOLS.md says what a loan fill is and how to undo it.
- **Acceptance:** pgTAP for the RPC (writes the split, gates: a closed review, an assigned line, an already split line, parts that do not add up, a closed loan) and for `jev_loan_candidates` (no demand or closed loan, the last payment), for `jev_prefill` refusing a loan-part category, and for the undo; Deno tests for the loan match (payment amount, last payment amount, none, two) and the job's path; a decision record.
- **UI follow-up:** FLOW-1033 (the filled card says Jev split the payment).
