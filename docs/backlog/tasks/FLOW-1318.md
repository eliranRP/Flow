# FLOW-1318 · Perf: company_forecast builds each loan's payments for the months asked only, and gathers only the first due date
- **Type:** SMALL/MEDIUM (perf) · **Status:** in-progress (#1293) · **Source:** the FLOW-1275 [plan](../../tech/flow-1275-server-side-math.md), slice 6 acceptance ("warm mean under 300 ms for Flow QA … else FLOW-1311"), and the 2026-11-24 production profiling.
- **Files:** one migration `20261123094208_flow_1318_loan_window.sql` (`private.loan_schedule_amortize_to`, `private.loan_schedule_window`, `private.loan_payments_window`; `private.company_forecast_build` and the loan gathers of `public.company_forecast` and `public.project_forecast` patched in place); pgTAP `flow_1318_loan_window.test.sql`. No app change, no new table or trigger.

Measured on production as Flow QA, warm, read-only (2026-11-24): `company_forecast` 'totals' 1 month 324–433 ms, 12 months about 395–405 ms, against a 300 ms budget. Of the build's 104 ms (1 month) and 162 ms (12 months), the 21 calls of `private.loan_payments_left` take about 70–75 ms: for 16 of the 21 loans the books' balance differs from the stored schedule, so each rebuilds its whole remaining schedule (about 340 months). The loan gather also sends every stored due date (about 80 KB).

- [x] A window helper builds each loan's payments only up to the last month asked: it stops at the window's end, works out the pay-off months only when a rate change needs a new payment, skips the balloon and final-adjustment outputs, checks payment-below-interest once at the first amortizing period when no rate change comes after the window, and falls back to the full `private.loan_payments_left` otherwise. `company_forecast` and `project_forecast` use it; `loan_outlooks` keeps the full build (it needs totals to the end).
- [x] The loan gather passes each loan's first due date on or after today and its period number, instead of every stored due date.
- [x] Every public answer is byte-identical: the company-forecast, project-forecast, loan-outlook and loan-schedule vectors pass; pgTAP flow_1307, flow_1309 and flow_1310 pass; a new pgTAP compares the window path with the full path over many invented loan terms and dates (rate changes inside and after the window, interest-only, balloon, refused terms, zero balance, demand).

**Acceptance:** about −70 ms on production (about −25 ms locally on Flow QA-shaped data) for `company_forecast`; local before and after timings for 1 and 12 months 'totals', 1 month 'rows', and `project_forecast` for the largest project and the overhead project.
