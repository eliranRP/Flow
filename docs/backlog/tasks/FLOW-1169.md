# FLOW-1169 · Settings that move a closed month's figures are not locked
- **Type:** PLAN FIRST · **Status:** plan-first · **Depends on:** [FLOW-1166](FLOW-1166.md)
- **Source:** the owner's decision of 2026-11-11 on the closed-month lock ("Not now, file a follow-up"). The lock (FLOW-1166) refuses a change to a closed line. These settings move a closed month's reported figures without touching any line, so the lock does not see them.
- **Files:** to be planned: the setters `set_cash_basis`, `set_category_pnl`, `set_category_cash`, `set_category_account`, `set_overhead_project`, their MCP tools, and the app's settings that call them.

- [ ] Decide, per setting, what happens while a period is closed: refuse the change, apply it to open periods only, or allow it and say that closed figures moved.
- [ ] The cash basis: switching it re-dates every income line; say whether a closed period keeps the basis it was closed on.
- [ ] A category's P&L or cash flag, its account, the overhead project: the same question.

**Acceptance:** a plan the owner approves, with the wording of any new message, before any build.
