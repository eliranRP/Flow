## The plan

**What is a utility.** A utility is one supplier's bills for one property and currency (the same key as a recurring charge, [0185](../../decisions/0185-recurring-charge-per-project.md)). Flow finds it from the lines:
- an expense filed to a property in a category under **חשבונות** (חשמל ומים, גז, אינטרנט and their children), or
- paid with a card whose name names the property and a utilities word (the FLOW-1072 match, [0262](../../decisions/0262-jev-card-name-project.md)).

The owner can also add a supplier to a property by hand.

**Who pays.** Each utility is **השוכר מחזיר** (the tenant pays it back) by default, or **החברה משלמת** (the company pays). "The company pays" means none of its bills is ever checked. It is set on the property's page, in a small **חשבונות** section: each utility with its usual amount and a שוכר / חברה switch.

**The match** (worked out on read, like the transfer suggestions in [0205](../../decisions/0205-transfer-match-suggestions.md); the sync does nothing new). For each bill of a tenant-paid utility, from the first bill in the books, Flow looks for a money-in line:
- dated from one month before the bill to today;
- filed to the same property, or to no property yet;
- not already the refund of another bill;
- whose amount is, within $1:
  1. the bill;
  2. several of the property's bills from the same month together; or
  3. the property's usual rent plus one or more of those bills.

**What Flow does with a match:**
- The amount matches (1 or 2) **and** the line is filed to the bill's own category (a refund) → **counted as refunded by itself**. Undo puts the bill back.
- Any other match → a **suggestion**: "זה ההחזר?" with כן / לא. A "no" is never suggested again for that pair.
- When the owner says yes to a payment filed elsewhere (rent, other income), the books stay as they are. The card shows a note, "ייתכן שסווג לא נכון", with one button: **העברה ל<category> כהחזר**. Nothing moves until it is tapped. For a payment that is rent plus the bill, the button splits off the bill's part.
- No match 30 days after the bill → **לא הוחזר**.

**Closing a bill without a refund** (each one is the owner's call):
- **ראיתי, אין צורך בטיפול** (seen, nothing to do): closes this bill.
- **החברה משלמת תמיד** (company always pays): switches the utility to company-paid, which closes all its open bills.
- **סימון הכל כנראה** (mark all as seen), on each property's group: closes every open לא הוחזר bill of that property at once, with one ביטול. Suggestions are left open. New bills after that are answered one by one.
- A month in which the property had no rent at all shows **"חודש בלי שכירות"** on the card, and "ראיתי" comes first.

Every answer shows a toast with ביטול.

**Where it shows:**
- **Home**, in "דורש טיפול": one row, "3 חשבונות לא הוחזרו · ‎$412", only when there is one. Suggestions are counted in the row.
- **`/utility-refunds` (החזרי חשבונות):** a lazy screen with the bills grouped by property, the suggestions first. Each card shows the supplier, the bill's date and amount, the days since, and the suggested payment with its date, payer and category.
- **The property page:** the חשבונות section (who pays).

**Data and MCP ([0095](../../decisions/0095-mcp-first.md)):**
- `utility_payers` (property, supplier, currency, `tenant` or `company`, and `added` for a supplier the owner added). RLS on, written only through the functions.
- `utility_refund_answers` (bill line, then one of: `matched` with the payment line and `auto` or `owner`; `rejected` with the payment line; or `seen`).
- Reads: `list_utility_refunds(p_project default null)` and `count_utility_refunds()`.
- Writes: `set_utility_payer` and `answer_utility_refund` and `mark_utility_refunds_seen(p_project)` (owner or editor), each returning the prior answer for ביטול.
- MCP: `list_utility_refunds`, `set_utility_payer`, `answer_utility_refund`, `mark_utility_refunds_seen` (undo kind with every bill it closed) and `refile_utility_refund` (the one-tap fix), each with the idempotency key, the write rate limit and undo.
- A decision record and `docs/mcp/TOOLS.md`.
