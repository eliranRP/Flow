The signature of Flow: one confident violet area at the top of Home, white below. Same screen, light and dark.

Light

band #7B3FE4 · text #FFFFFF (5.7:1) · labels #F0E8FF (4.8:1)

Dark

band #7B3FE4 · text #FFFFFF (5.7:1) · labels #F0E8FF (4.8:1)

How the band works

  • Solid violet, no gradient, rounded bottom corners (28px).
  • Holds only the summary: logo, period, greeting, profit, change, income and expenses.
  • Used on Home and the Project header. All other screens are white with violet accents.
  • The change sits in a solid pill so red ▼ / green ▲ stay readable on violet.

In dark mode

  • The page becomes a violet-tinted near-black (#15111E), never pure black.
  • The band keeps the same violet as light mode (#7B3FE4) with white text, so Flow looks the same in both modes.
  • Accent brightens to #B894FF with dark text on it; the period pill and change pill become dark surfaces (#1E1929).
  • Good and bad lighten to #62CB8D / #FF8A80 so they pass 4.5:1.
