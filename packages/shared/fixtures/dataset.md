## Conventions used in SUMIT
- Every project document and expense is tagged `BudgetManagement_BudgetItem` = the project's budget section, and its description starts with `פרויקט: <name>`.
- Overhead expenses have no budget section and their description starts with `תקורה:`.
- The shared cost (site workers) has no budget section and its description starts with `עלות משותפת:`. Flow splits it by worker-days (table below).
- Receipts link to their invoice (`OriginalDocumentID`). The credit note links to the invoice it reduces.
- **SUMIT limitation 1 (dates):** income documents can't be dated more than 3 days back ("המערכת מגבילה הפקת מסמכים בתאריך עבר עד שלושה ימים אחורה"; the restriction can be lifted only in the SUMIT UI under accounting-portal settings). SUMIT also blocks dates that break the per-type numbering sequence ("...בתאריך השובר את רצף מספור המסמכים"), and the pre-existing invoice #20001 is dated 2026-09-26. So all new income documents are dated **2026-09-26**. Expenses (`addexpense`) accept old dates and are spread from April to September 2026.
- **SUMIT limitation 2 (expense VAT):** `addexpense` has no VAT field. SUMIT stores the expense with VAT line = 0 and `…WithoutVAT` = gross. Expenses are therefore entered at the **real gross invoice amount** (net × 1.18; insurance is VAT-exempt, so gross = net). Flow must derive net = gross / 1.18 for VAT-able suppliers (see expected-pnl.md).
